# EURO Historical European competitions
> r4 | Define historical European competitions and qualification.

## decisions
- EURO-1 [o] European Cup starts 1955; Champions League name starts 1992. UEFA Cup starts 1971; Europa League name starts 2009. Conference starts 2021 and drops Europa from its name in 2024.
- EURO-2 [o] Cup Winners Cup runs 1960-1998; companion Super Cup is an optional summarized tie from 1973. Fairs/Intertoto/regional and pandemic exceptional formats are excluded from the demo.
- EURO-3 [o] Era profiles preserve main field/format milestones: Cup16 then32; 1991 two groups after knockout; 1994 groups16; 1997 groups24; 1999 groups32 twice; 2003 groups32 then knockout; UEFA Cup64 then2004 groups40 then2009 groups48 then2021 groups32.
- EURO-4 [o] From 2024, UCL/UEL/Conference use 36-team league phases with 8/8/6 matches, top8 direct to the round of 16 and 9-24 in a seeded two-legged playoff (9th v 24th …); no league-phase drop to a lower competition.
- EURO-5 [o] Qualification uses the immediately preceding closed season's league, domestic cup and continental titles. A club enters at most one continental competition, preferring UCL, CWC, UEL, then Conference. Additional fictional representatives fill the remaining era field capacity; they never displace an eligible domestic club or titleholder.
- EURO-6 [o] Qualifying paths, draw pots, initial invitations and background representatives are normalized allocations, labelled as such; historical access before 2021 is normalized rather than an exact historical UEFA access list. Modern domestic access follows →!EURO-11/12/13.
- EURO-7 [o] All ties use the same seeded football model, affect recorded coefficient/honor/revenue, and archive own-club matches. Qualification occurs before the world calendar schedules Europe.
- EURO-8 [o] Display locked competitions before founding date, current historical name/format, qualification cause, the live league-phase or group tables with their qualification zones, the bracket as it is drawn with both legs' dates, own results and the history of finals (→WEB-40).
- EURO-9 [o] European Cup/Champions League and UEFA Cup/Europa League share lineage history; discontinued cups retain prior records.
- EURO-10 [o] Continental competitions run through the season, stage by stage, three days before the league weekend. ← knockouts settled at season close could not be followed

  - every tie before the final is two legs on its era's gap — two weeks before 2003, then one, the Champions League's last 16 three weeks apart 2003–2023 — and no leg shares a domestic cup midweek (5 11 16 23 28 35 42); the final is one match. A level aggregate goes through on away goals before 2021, then extra time and penalties from the world's stream. ← real UEFA calendars: 1985–86 first round 18 Sep/2 Oct, 2022–23 last 16 14 Feb/7 Mar, 2024–25 rounds a week apart
  - a round drawn after its planned first leg (a save from before rules 1.14.0) moves past today with its gap; matches archived on the earlier calendar (every return a round later) keep their stage.
  - after a phase the qualifiers are seeded (group winners first, or league rank) and the first knockout round pairs best against worst; a field that is not a power of two gives its top seeds byes (the playoff); later rounds are open draws.
  - qualifiers per group: 3 in the 40-club UEFA Cup, 1 in the 1991–92 eight-club groups, 2 with 16 or 32+ clubs, otherwise 1 plus the two best runners-up (24 clubs).
  - the Super Cup stays a single match at the season close; the archive keeps each final's runner-up, score and shoot-out.
- EURO-11 [o] From 2021, modern access uses association quotas including qualifying places; the demo admits those clubs directly to its existing field. England's fifth place is a UEL place with the ordinary four UCL berths, never a fixed Conference berth.
  - Association ranks 1–4 receive UCL/UEL/Conference quotas 4/2/1; ranks 5–6 receive 3/2/1 before 2024; from 2024 rank 5 receives 4/2/1 and rank 6 receives 3/2/1; ranks 7–8 receive 2/1/2. UEL quotas include the domestic cup winner. When that winner already qualifies for UCL or occupies a league UEL berth, the next eligible league club takes the vacant domestic place.
  - Baseline association order is ENG ESP ITA GER FRA POR NED BEL before 2024, ENG ESP ITA GER FRA NED POR BEL thereafter. With five complete recorded association-coefficient seasons ending two seasons before entry, rank the eight implemented associations by their five-year sum instead; ties use the latest season backwards, then baseline order. No real-world future result is imposed on a simulated career.
  - England has one simulated domestic cup, its FA Cup. Its unsimulated EFL Cup berth goes to the highest league club not already qualified for a higher competition; Dutch/Belgian domestic access playoffs are likewise normalized to league order. State these limitations in the competition view.
  - Sources: [UEFA access](https://documents.uefa.com/r/Regulations-of-the-UEFA-Champions-League-2026/27/Article-3-Entries-for-the-competition-Online), [domestic cup overlap](https://documents.uefa.com/r/Regulations-of-the-UEFA-Europa-League-2026/27/Article-3-Entries-for-the-competition-Online), [English allocation](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/373663/european-qualification-for-uefa-competitions-explained).
- EURO-12 [o] The preceding UCL winner qualifies for UCL from 1956; the UEFA Cup/UEL winner qualifies for UEFA Cup/UEL from 1972 through 2014 and UCL from 2015; the Conference winner qualifies for UEL from 2022; a CWC winner qualifies for CWC while it exists.
  - A titleholder already in the same or a higher competition creates no extra domestic league berth. Moving a domestically qualified club to a higher competition through its European title removes its lower berth; the vacant international field place is filled by a background representative rather than another domestic league club. A titleholder outside domestic access is an additional entrant. A continental title does not by itself increase a country's base league UCL quota.
- EURO-13 [o] From 2024, the two best preceding-season association coefficients among the eight implemented associations each earn an additional UCL European Performance Spot (EPS), assigned after titleholder qualification to the highest league club not already in UCL. EPS increases that association's total entrants by one; any vacated domestic berth cascades through its remaining lower competition entrants to the next eligible league club.
  - From the 2023 close, archive association coefficient totals in eighth-point integer units and distinct entrant counts. Count every completed continental match except the Super Cup: win 2, draw 1, defeat 0, with half points for qualifying matches. Shoot-outs award no match points. Include era phase and round bonuses; modern league-phase bonuses and round bonuses follow UEFA Annex D.5. Divide totals by entrant counts, truncate to thousandths, and break equal season coefficients by the preceding five-year totals then baseline order.
  - Missing older coefficient snapshots remain missing; do not invent an EPS from incomplete archived match detail. A completed season with complete current fixtures produces its snapshot even in an older save. Source: [UEFA coefficient calculation](https://www.uefa.com/nationalassociations/uefarankings/news/0252-0cda3876cf40-3b08507f16f2-1000--how-men-s-association-club-coefficients-are-calculated/).
- EURO-14 [o] Existing supported careers retain their active competition memberships, played fixtures, finances and all closed records. New qualification applies only when a new season is prepared. Optional qualification causes and coefficient snapshots survive save/export/import; their absence is valid for older saves. Do not replay missed historical titleholder entries or mutate an imported source file.
- EURO-15 [o] A newly allocated own-club entry records and displays its preceding season and concrete cause: league rank, domestic cup, continental title or EPS and its domestic cascade. Older entries without a recorded cause say it was not retained; their cause is never reconstructed using newer rules. The view states the applicable normalized qualifying-path and supported-association limits (→!EURO-6/11/13).
