# WEB Browser game delivery
> r78 | Validate football-enthusiast depth and repeat play in a responsive browser prototype before a paid mobile app.

## decisions
- WEB-1 [o] First release is a static, account-free Korean web game; Supabase and Flutter are deferred.
- WEB-2 [o] Play a fictional club founded in 1950, five seasons before the 1955 European Cup, in its country's amateur league below the professional pyramid (→WORLD-5); choose country, name, colors, seed, difficulty level (→WEB-3) and the club's direction (→WEB-15). ← clubs were founded at all times; starting close to the continental era keeps the road to Europe within reach
- WEB-3 [o] Four difficulty levels set only the start: 쉬움 2×, 보통 1×, 어려움 0.5× and 최상 0.25× the founding annual operating budget in cash, and a starting XI at the league average plus 0, −2, −4 or −6 (→CLUB-1). Every rule, coefficient and chance is the same at every level (→LEAGUE-13), and a shared seed makes the same world. ← the start decides how hard a career is; the dice never change
- WEB-4 [o] Dashboard, match observation, league/Europe, people (squad, market, manager and coaches), training, operations and history are playable views, with progress and actionable errors.
- WEB-5 [o] Advance one round, finish the current season, or follow automatic x1/x2/x5/x10 pacing; the public interface has no multi-season jump. Every mode lives through the same days and records the same facts (→LEAGUE-11). Manager resignation and saving failure interrupt unattended progress.
- WEB-6 [o] Free local play has no real-money purchases, identity collection, analytics upload, sign-in, or automatic cloud submission.
- WEB-7 [o] A new game goes into an empty game slot (→WEB-62); starting one over an occupied slot's game requires an explicit in-app replacement choice and offers export first.
- WEB-8 [o] A runtime error preserves a recoverable save and offers retry/export; do not replace failed loading with a new world.

- WEB-9 [o] Casual growth play follows docs/IMPROVEMENT-GUIDE.md: one-action matches, roster/tactic/fatigue tradeoffs, evidence-backed growth, and no guaranteed win claims.

- WEB-10 [o] Play uses a game HUD (menu, club crest, division/date, inbox, cash pinned top right) and, on mobile, a seven-tab safe-area bar with home centered and selected navigation marked by a persistent underline: league, cups (컵) and records (클럽 기록실) on the left; 선수단, 훈련 and 구단 on the right, the owner's three levers — who joins and stays (→WEB-43), how players develop (→WEB-70) and where the club is going with how it earns and spends (→WEB-39). The match view has no tab (→WEB-40). The HUD menu lists only what the tab bar does not show plus guide, intervention, club news (→WEB-51), the metrics glossary (→WEB-53), save tools and 게임 슬롯 (→WEB-62). Tapping the HUD cash opens the cash sheet (→WEB-44); the HUD shows cash in the money notation (→WEB-67), and on phones the club name wraps to two lines before it truncates. The document never scrolls: HUD, clock and tab bar stay fixed and one content region scrolls between them, and the shell around that region never scrolls, by code (focus, an element scrolled into view) either, so warning bands taller than a low screen never push the HUD off its top; the match theatre fits that region at 360x740 and 390x844 CSS pixels and home may scroll vertically.   - wider screens name the right tabs 선수단 (선수단·스태프), 훈련 (훈련·육성) and 구단 (구단 운영).
  - read-only ownership shows as a compact HUD badge that explains itself on tap, never a content banner.
  - the page background and fixed bars are flat charcoal surfaces with fine neutral dividers (→WEB-38).
  - phones pack rows: no view repeats its tab's name as a title, and panels keep tight padding.
  - wording follows Korean football media: continental competitions are 클럽대항전 (never 대륙컵, which reads as a national-team tournament), morale is 분위기 (a bare 사기 also reads as fraud), money runway is 자금 여력 in weeks or months, and the squad-support policy is 선수단 투자. ← football fans read the game in the words of their own media
