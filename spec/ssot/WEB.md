# WEB Browser game delivery
> r55 | Validate football-enthusiast depth and repeat play in a responsive browser prototype before a paid mobile app.

## decisions
- WEB-1 [o] First release is a static, account-free Korean web game; Supabase and Flutter are deferred.
- WEB-2 [o] Play a fictional club founded in 1950, five seasons before the 1955 European Cup, in its country's amateur league below the professional pyramid (→WORLD-5); choose country, name, colors, seed, and capital difficulty. ← clubs were founded at all times; starting close to the continental era keeps the road to Europe within reach
- WEB-3 [o] Capital presets are 2, 1, and 0.5 times the founding annual operating budget; all other initial conditions remain identical for a shared seed.
- WEB-4 [o] Dashboard, match observation, league/Europe, people (squad, market, manager and coaches), training, operations and history are playable views, with progress and actionable errors.
- WEB-5 [o] Advance one round, finish the current season, or follow automatic x1/x2/x5/x10 pacing; the public interface has no multi-season jump. Every mode lives through the same days and records the same facts (→LEAGUE-11). Manager resignation and saving failure interrupt unattended progress.
- WEB-6 [o] Free local play has no real-money purchases, identity collection, analytics upload, sign-in, or automatic cloud submission.
- WEB-7 [o] A new game goes into an empty game slot (→WEB-62); starting one over an occupied slot's game requires an explicit in-app replacement choice and offers export first.
- WEB-8 [o] A runtime error preserves a recoverable save and offers retry/export; do not replace failed loading with a new world.

- WEB-9 [o] Casual growth play follows docs/IMPROVEMENT-GUIDE.md: one-action matches, roster/tactic/fatigue tradeoffs, evidence-backed growth, and no guaranteed win claims.

- WEB-10 [o] Play uses a game HUD (menu, club crest, division/date, inbox, cash pinned top right) and, on mobile, a seven-tab safe-area bar with home raised: league, cups (컵) and records (클럽 기록실) on the left; 사람, 훈련 and 운영 on the right, the owner's three levers — who joins and stays (→WEB-43), how players develop (→WEB-70) and how the club earns and spends (→WEB-39). The match view has no tab (→WEB-40). The HUD menu lists only what the tab bar does not show plus guide, intervention, club news (→WEB-51), the metrics glossary (→WEB-53), save tools and 게임 슬롯 (→WEB-62). Tapping the HUD cash opens the cash sheet (→WEB-44); the HUD shows cash in the money notation (→WEB-67), and on phones the club name wraps to two lines before it truncates. The document never scrolls: HUD, clock and tab bar stay fixed and one content region scrolls between them; the match theatre fits that region at 360x740 and 390x844 CSS pixels and home may scroll vertically. Content never overflows the viewport or clips inside a panel; controls sharing a row share height and edges.
  - wider screens name the right tabs 사람 (선수·스태프), 훈련 (훈련·육성) and 운영 (구단 운영).
