## summary
The owner asked (261119, gusta-self-improve np19) for engine speed-ups on two symptoms: creating a new career takes very long, and on slow devices 10x automatic play stops now and then. At d999af70 a 1950 takeover with seed `generation-reference` takes 47.8 s in Node on a loaded M1 Max (13 seasons, 3.7 s a season; world `4161b5e0d829175a3b1fc1ad959a4d5243ea43c5509c266f1a1202dea25435ce`); unprofiled at lower load 35.9 s (2.76 s a season). Two read-only scouts profiled it. In a history season the NPC transfer market is 53–54% (its candidate scan alone about 24%), squad re-selection across all callers 16–17%, in-season NPC growth 7–10%; the results-only NPC fixtures of history compute no place terms. After the takeover on a lived 1950 world an ordinary day is about 25 ms of CPU, a league round day with the own fixture about 0.64 s without helpers (NPC aggregate 72%, its player-fact attribution 53%, the place-weight fitting 29%), an NPC market settlement day 0.3–0.65 s. At 10x a 7-day week is shown in 0.7 s while it costs about 1.0 s of CPU here, so a slower device runs out of its run-ahead buffer and every round day shows as a stop: only less CPU a week, or more parallel work, helps.
