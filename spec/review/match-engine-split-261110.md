# REVIEW match-engine-split-261110
> st:converted@261111 | scope:packages/engine/src/grid packages/engine/src/aggregate spec/ssot/MATCH.md | at:7be261d | base:ARCH@38

## summary
- The owner asked on 261110 whether the detailed match engine can be repaired until an observed match reads as real football, or needs a redesign, and whether a new engine could carry other sports. Verdict: the code is sound (pure `playGrid(setup) → record + trace`, a clean seam to playback frames, a complete aggregate path), but the model's structure caps what repair can reach. The owner adopted the proposed direction — facts settled by a probabilistic script, the pitch picture staged from that script — and asked for an isolated prototype first.
- Measured on this host under load (261110, release revision 10, `scripts/grid-bands.ts --matches 120 --candidate`, a small sample): 25 of 37 MATCH-23 rows pass; shots 20.2 a team (11–14), goals 3.58 a match (2.6–2.9), forwards 79.5% of goals (50–60%), offsides 0.96 a team (1.7–1.9), throw-ins 8.5 a team (17–23). The same 40 top-tier fixtures give 4.6 goals a match on the detailed engine and 2.83 on the aggregate path. Cost about 480 ms against 3 ms a match (main-thread CPU).

## findings
- F1 [o] P1 packages/engine/src/grid/engine.ts `playGrid`, decide.ts `actionMix`/`passOptions`, restarts.ts `offsideChance`, spots.ts `ROLE_SHIFT`: one simulation both settles the match facts and is the picture, so every fix of a scene that "reads as a bug" moves the calibrated statistics and is tuned back (T689: fifteen exploration windows from 261104, none entered production; `DECISION_CONFIG` shot scale re-tuned nine times). The carrier's choice is a draw from real football's marginal shares per cell with exception rules layered on (`pass.backPrior` multiplies every backward option, so a back pass in front of goal is by construction; revisions 4–10 each add exceptions). Time advances per touch: the outcome is drawn first and movement fitted to it, off-ball intentions update once a touch, so offside can only be a draw on a position and roles are a fixed offset plus action weights. Proposal: split responsibilities — a probabilistic result script (who, where, what, outcome) settles the facts for every match, and a staging layer that knows the script ahead draws the watched match from it, carrying no statistical burden. First prove the unproven half, staging, with an isolated ten-minute prototype under `scripts/match-proto/` that imports nothing from `packages/` or `apps/` and changes no production source. ← the owner judges signings on what he sees and counts; a picture generated from the counts cannot disagree with them, and role behaviour becomes constants in two small tables instead of emergent tuning →T791 →T792 →T793 →T794 →T795 →T796 →T797 →T798 →T799 →T800
- F2 [o] P2 scripts/match-proto/script/cast.ts `feetPoint`, stage/pass.ts `holdUntil`/`canReceive`, stage/world.ts `separated`: bug: teammates pass while standing almost on each other and stay together for seconds (owner, watching `artifacts/match-proto-261110` on 261111: a second or two reads fine, several do not). Measured on `demo#17`: 15% of completed script passes are aimed under 6 m (one 0.2 m), the passer walks toward his pass's aim for the whole hold, and `canReceive` counts the reaction time of a receiver already on the point, so a pass under about 4.2 m always waits the full extra hold; 22% of passes are kicked with the receiver within 5 m and 24 pairs of teammates stay within 3 m for more than 2 s in ten minutes (longest 7 s). Proposal: a least pass length in the script's aim points, one bounded touch for the passer, a least distance to the reception point, room around the man on the ball. ← the first thing the owner saw; a picture that reads as a bug decides the prototype's verdict whatever the script says →T814 →T815 →T818
- F3 [o] P2 scripts/match-proto/stage/world.ts `setPossession`/`defaultTarget`, core/shape.ts `nominalSpot`: bug: when the ball changes sides every player turns at the same moment, in ordinary play as much as in a counter (owner 261111: players seem to read only the ball, not the man they mark). Every place is a function of the ball and the side in possession, the side flips for all 22 in one tick and all run for `motion.transitionSeconds`; after a settled turnover 44–59% of moving players have reversed within 1 s and 75–85% are running after 1.5 s (counters 80–90%); marking is a 0.2 lean, so only 28–31% of attackers in the last 40 m have a defender within 4 m. Proposal: a turnover believed later by far players and re-shaped over seconds unless the script says counter, defenders assigned a man and moving with him, attackers stepping off their marker. ← a fast counter may turn everyone at once; settled play must not →T814 →T817 →T818
- F4 [o] P2 scripts/match-proto/stage/world.ts `step`/`defaultTarget`, stage/motion.ts: bug: all players move in one burst and stop together (owner 261111: one player doing so reads as a sprint, all of them does not). Twenty targets are one rigid function of one point that jumps to a pass's landing point at the kick, read in the same tick and reached at one of two speeds: 57–67% of starts from rest share a ±0.2 s window with at least seven others, a player stops and restarts 9.1–9.7 times a minute, two players' speeds correlate at 0.46–0.54. Proposal: each free player acts on what he saw a moment ago (by distance and 판단력), his place follows with a lag and his speed with the distance left, the back four staying one line. ← staging has no statistical burden, so natural movement costs the script nothing — the claim F1's split rests on →T814 →T816 →T818
- F5 [o] P1 scripts/match-proto/script `generateScript`, packages/engine/src/aggregate/players.ts `attributePlayerFacts`: the prototype's script is a hand-written grammar tied to no result, so the split's second unproven half is open: whether a script can be written from the aggregate path's settled result — its ledger of grouped moves, shots and fouls, and the players they were split between — that reproduces the record exactly and still reads as football (owner 261111, having settled that every match's result comes from the aggregate path and a script is written only for a watched match). The ledger is drawn as flows between cells, not as a chain of possessions, and the attribution keeps only each player's totals, so nothing yet says how much a script must invent between recorded events. Proposal: keep the attribution's own split as an optional trace and export ten settled matches as data, write a script that performs every recorded event exactly once, stage it bound to the record for the full ninety minutes, then measure the invention, the strain and the cost in a timebox and deliver one full match beside its record. ← production planning (ideation/match-script-and-staging) starts from that result →T819 →T820 →T821 →T822

## notes
- Owner decision 261110 (pair): adopt F1 as a prototype chain detailed task by task; target "ten minutes of staged football at a fixed path".
- Prototype scope: a hand-written possession grammar with probabilities stands in for the result engine's script; it is not yet tied to the aggregate path's totals. The question it answers is only whether a script with named actors can be staged without teleports, impossible interceptions or unexplained backward passes, with roles visible (inverted full-back inside in build-up, false nine dropping, poacher on the last shoulder, a low-판단력 runner flagged offside by a run started early).
- Not adopted here, the owner's later calls: (a) continuing T689's calibration windows while the prototype's verdict is open; (b) dropping the earlier match-model revisions' branches (about 110 `modelAtLeast`/candidate reads) once replays no longer resimulate under SAVE-23 — both are planning changes for gusta-update-ssot, not review tasks; (c) a shared multi-sport base: the script→staging split suits possession- and play-based sports, but contracts (16 attributes, 14 positions, `MatchRecord`) are football-shaped, so only keep the new kernel free of football terms and extract when a second sport starts.
- Reusable as they are if the split is adopted: `packages/engine/src/aggregate` (role-, trait- and attribute-weighted player facts, including offsides by 판단력), `grid/pitch.ts` cells, `grid/baselines.json`, `grid/record.ts` tallies, rules (eras, substitutions, discipline, shoot-out), the `MatchFrame` playback contract and the web renderer.
