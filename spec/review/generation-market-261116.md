## summary
The owner reported on 261116 that creating a new career (world generation before the clubhouse) has become very slow. At b431c2c1 a 1950 takeover with seed `generation-reference` takes 666.7 s in Node x1: 51.2 s a season on average, rising from 4.5 s (1931) to 117.5 s (1947/48), while takeover and club listings take under 0.1 s each. About 80–88% of each pre-takeover season is spent in `npcMarket`, which T864 (a85d3b99a) introduced on top of the squads T861/T863 persist; the later T899 rating cache did not change the loop shape.
