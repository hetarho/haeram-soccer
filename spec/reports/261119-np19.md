### Other owner decisions
- F9 (bug) save codec cliff: once a gzip save no longer fits the 8 MiB portable-file limit, every autosave switches to pure-JS Brotli quality 11 (SAVE-2/SAVE-4). Observed on the 1950 career played on: 1980 at 84.6% of the limit, 1985 at 92.9%, crossed in 1989 (+39; about 50k characters a season, people records are 60% of the world). Past it a checkpoint takes 6 min of CPU on a fast core (gzip 5.6 s) and 28 min on a slow core, beyond the codec worker's 10-minute timeout (`CODEC_TIMEOUT_MS`), so on a slow device every autosave fails with the save-worker error, using 0.8–1.6 GB while it runs. A bigger career (Europe, more transfers) crosses earlier. Recommended: apply the portable limit only at export, or never use quality 11 for autosaves; also on slow devices even the gzip checkpoint at 1985 takes about 27 s of CPU, nearly every 30 s autosave cycle, so the autosave interval or the codec job's size (D6, D7) matters there.
- F10 retention and F7 helpers as in D7/D8; F8/F11 view and records-room refresh as in D5.
- The 10 s days of the owner's 1980 career: not reproduced on this machine; the measurements above make a slow device plus late-era growth plus continuous save work the most likely cause. To confirm: the exported save and a 30 s Chrome Performance trace at x10 (records room open, then closed), and the device model; `artifacts/benchmarks/night-perf-261119/late/fromsave.ts`, `days2.ts`, `open.ts` read them, `cliff/` holds the played-on measurements.
