## goal
Construct one complete aggregate MatchPlayback whose incidents, team totals and named player lines describe the same match.

## acceptance
- [v] Implement `planParticipation`, `attributePlayerFacts` and `simulateAggregate`. Produce T273's complete numerical record and original matchday rosters, with empty observation frames and all applicable period/knockout fields.
- [v] Plan starters, legal era substitutions, injuries, cards and dismissals as one timeline before final team production; their availability/strength effects enter T279's kernel. A player cannot contribute before entering or after leaving.
- [v] Attribute basic metrics and every PD counter to actual eligible players by role and effective attributes, reconciling with team facts under each definition. Shot/key-pass/xG/xA attribution shares the same chances; no independent post-hoc scorer draw contradicts the score.
- [v] Honor bans/injuries in the input roster, legal player counts and bench limits, added time, extra-time participation and the existing ordered taker/keeper shootout rules. Historical eras without substitutes still produce valid man-short intervals.
- [v] Tests cover `reconciles full player and team totals`, `never credits an unavailable or off-pitch player`, `limits era substitution windows`, `couples a red card and man-short time`, `records an injury and replacement once`, `keeps regulation and shootout scores separate`, and `returns identical facts on repeated calls`.
- [v] The shared validator rejects every deliberately corrupted contribution/timeline fixture. Task checks follow ARCH-30; no large statistical corpus is embedded in unit tests.
