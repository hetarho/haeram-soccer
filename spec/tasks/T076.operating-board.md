## goal
운영 › 운영 전략 is one board: six areas, each with its card row and dials, one draft and one 적용 that acts at once, with locked cards saying when they free. 후원 carries the sponsors' staff switch.

## acceptance
- [ ] board.ts (pure, new):
  - `BoardDraft = { build?: ClubBuild; policy?: Partial<ClubPolicy>; ticket?: number; name?: string }`.
  - `draftChanges(w, d)`: the changed cards and dials.
  - `draftCommands(w, d)`: the `build` command first (with `name` for a preset or saved strategy), then one `policy` per changed key and `ticket` last.
  - `draftRefusal(w, d)`: the engine's `draftLock` text, or undefined.
  - `draftFromRequest(w, request)`: a `useBoardRequest` entry turned into a draft.
- [ ] StrategyBoard, in order (→WEB-60):
  - the strategy in force with its name and combined gains and costs;
  - 추천 전략 and 내 전략 cards (tap → draft), with 나만의 전략 만들기 and 지금 전략 저장;
  - six area rows (선수단, 영입, 매각, 유소년, 수익, 홈·팬), each a card row (tap → draft) beside the area's dials (선수단 투자; 영입 기조; none; 유소년 투자; 마케팅; 티켓 가격). A locked slot shows `<day>에 변경 가능` from `slotLocks` and its cards cannot be drafted;
  - 시너지 and 확률, as today.
- [ ] The draft bar is pinned while the draft differs from the world:
  - the combined card gains and costs;
  - for each changed dial its preview: cost per round and the projection at season end (`policyPreview`), and for the ticket the gate projection;
  - synergies lighting or dropping, and odds moving;
  - 되돌리기, and 적용, which is disabled with `draftRefusal`'s text when refused.
  적용 sends `draftCommands` in order, stops at the first refusal, and clears the draft when all succeed.
