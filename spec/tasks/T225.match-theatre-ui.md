## goal
Watching a match shows the grid engine as football: every touch at its place, commentary in football words, red cards and added time on the scoreboard, substitutions and injuries in the event line, and minutes that slow down where the game happens.

## acceptance
- [ ] Scoreboard shows red cards per side (a red mark per sent-off player, from `record.incidents` up to the presented minute) and the minute label `frame.label` (`45+2′`, `90+4′`, `105′`).
- [ ] The latest-event line shows `event.text` of the current event (MATCH-24); substitutions read `{out} ↔ {in}`, injuries `{player} 부상`, cards `{player} 경고/퇴장`.
- [ ] Pacing (WEB-32): `presentationClock` maps time with `frame.pace`: a minute takes `4000 ms × pace / speed`; speeds 1x–16x unchanged; pause/seek unchanged.
- [ ] Charts and moments: x axis over all frames with ticks at the first frame of period 2 (45′) and the last regulation frame (90′); goal marks by frame; `MatchMoments` adds red cards and substitutions to the list and labels added-time moments; the scrubber label reads `frame.label′ / {last label}′`.
- [ ] Player inspector lists the players on the pitch and the bench by id; shows the inspected player's state.
- [ ] Unit tests: `presentationClock.test.ts` `spends longer on minutes with a higher pace and four seconds on average`; `moments.test.ts` `lists red cards and substitutions with their labels`.
- [ ] E2E: `match-core.spec.ts` and `stable-controls-and-pacing.spec.ts` updated and passing (`pnpm exec playwright test --project=chromium tests/e2e/match-core.spec.ts tests/e2e/stable-controls-and-pacing.spec.ts`); typecheck and lint pass.

## impl notes
- Data: `MatchFrame.period/label/pace/onPitch` and `MatchEvent.text` (T218 contracts); incidents carry minute/added.
- `presentationAdvance(elapsed, speed, samples, minutes)` today divides evenly; change it to walk cumulative pace: precompute `start[i] = Σ_{j<i} pace_j × 4000`, then the presented minute index is the last i with `start[i] ≤ elapsed × speed`, and the sample within it is proportional.
- Keep the 1.9:1 pitch ratio and existing test ids (`pitch-theatre`, `match-score`, `match-latest-event`).
- Do not show `기록 요약` frames differently than today for archived replays.
