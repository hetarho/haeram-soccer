## goal
Watching a match shows the grid engine as football: every touch at its place, commentary in football words, red cards and added time on the scoreboard, substitutions and injuries in the event line, and minutes that slow down where the game happens.

## acceptance
- [v] Scoreboard shows red cards per side (a red mark per sent-off player, from `record.incidents` up to the presented minute) and the minute label `frame.label` (`45+2′`, `90+4′`, `105′`).
- [v] The latest-event line shows `event.text` of the current event (MATCH-24); substitutions read `{out} ↔ {in}`, injuries `{player} 부상`, cards `{player} 경고/퇴장`.
- [v] Pacing (WEB-32): `presentationClock` maps time with `frame.pace`: a minute takes `4000 ms × pace / speed`; speeds 1x–16x and detailed seek unchanged; preserve speed-only live controls and speed-to-resume behavior (WEB-32/50).
- [v] Charts and moments: x axis over all frames with ticks at the first frame of period 2 (45′) and the last regulation frame (90′); goal marks by frame; `MatchMoments` adds red cards and substitutions to the list and labels added-time moments; the scrubber label reads `frame.label′ / {last label}′`.
- [v] Player inspector lists the players on the pitch and the bench by id; shows the inspected player's state.
- [v] Unit tests: `presentationClock.test.ts` `spends longer on minutes with a higher pace and four seconds on average`; `moments.test.ts` `lists red cards and substitutions with their labels`.
- [ ] E2E: `match-core.spec.ts` and `stable-controls-and-pacing.spec.ts` updated and passing (`pnpm exec playwright test --project=chromium tests/e2e/match-core.spec.ts tests/e2e/stable-controls-and-pacing.spec.ts`); typecheck and lint pass. — e2e specs updated, run pending the pre-push browser batch (ARCH-30); typecheck and lint pass.

## impl notes
- Data: `MatchFrame.period/label/pace/onPitch` and `MatchEvent.text` (T218 contracts); incidents carry minute/added.
- `presentationAdvance(elapsed, speed, samples, minutes)` today divides evenly; change it to walk cumulative pace: precompute `start[i] = Σ_{j<i} pace_j × 4000`, then the presented minute index is the last i with `start[i] ≤ elapsed × speed`, and the sample within it is proportional.
- Keep the 1.9:1 pitch ratio and existing test ids (`pitch-theatre`, `match-score`, `match-latest-event`).
- Do not show `기록 요약` frames differently than today for archived replays.

## result
- outcome: The scoreboard shows one red mark per player sent off (`red-cards-home`/`red-cards-away`, red and second yellow from `record.incidents` up to the presented frame) and the meta and event lines read `frame.label′`; the latest-event line keeps showing the engine's `event.text`, whose incident texts are `{out} ↔ {in} 교체`, `{player} 부상` and `{player} 경고/퇴장`. `presentationClock` now keeps presentation time in ms at 1x: `paceStarts` gives each minute 4000 ms × `frame.pace`, `presentationAt` finds the last minute started (binary search) and the share played, `presentationTime` inverts it for seeking, and `presentationAdvance(elapsed, speed, paces)` maps real time at a speed; Pitch advances the clock by elapsed × speed and spreads each minute's motion samples evenly across that minute's time. Speeds 1x–16x, speed-to-resume, hidden-tab pause/resume and the detailed seek still work as before. Archived replays have pace 1 on every frame, so they advance as before. Chart ticks sit at the first period-2 frame (45′) and the last regulation frame (90′), and goal marks use the shared `goalFrame`. `MatchMoments` takes the frames and player names and lists goals, red cards and substitutions in frame order with their labels (`45+2′`, `{out} ↔ {in}`), each seeking to its own frame; `sampleAt` also accepts a minute label. The player inspector lists the players on the pitch now, by `frame.onPitch` ID, and keeps the selected player in the list after he leaves. Below the list, each side's bench is shown with why a player left (교체/부상/퇴장), and the state line for a player off the pitch gives that reason. LiveSeason now loads Pitch with `React.lazy` and Suspense (an empty `s.pitch` box as the fallback).
