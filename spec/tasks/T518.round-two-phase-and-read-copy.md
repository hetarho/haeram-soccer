## goal
Let a league round's other fixtures be computed by an injected simulator from a read-only copy, recorded in fixture order, with worlds identical to today's interleaved round.

## acceptance
- [v] `playRound` computes every fixture first and records them in the original order afterwards ("two-phase"); with no simulator injected the world is identical to a08e0dc1's interleaved path (day bench hash `098fdcae5ada357b`, and identical with `--matches 30`).
- [v] New `packages/engine/src/round-jobs.ts`: `FIXTURE_READ_KEYS` (the world keys an other-club fixture reads: calendar, clubs, discipline, managerMarket, matchModel, people, playerClub, seed, year), `readCopy(w)` building that copy, `roundJobs(w, fixtures)` and the job/result types; `simulateRoundJob(copy, job)` is the pure function a helper runs.
- [v] An injectable `RoundSimulator` (set per world session, default none) receives the round's non-own league fixtures on a day with our league fixture and returns their results; our match still plays on the engine thread meanwhile. Cup/continental days and days without our fixture keep the sequential path.
- [v] A round where any club appears twice falls back to the sequential path.
- [v] `round-jobs.test.ts` (unit, small fixture world): (a) an in-process simulator that solves jobs in reverse order through `readCopy` gives the same canonical world as no simulator over 60 days including own match days; (b) a Proxy-traced run of `simulateRoundJob` reads no world key outside `FIXTURE_READ_KEYS`; (c) a duplicated club falls back.
- [v] typecheck, lint/format on touched files, `pnpm exec vitest run --project unit packages/engine/src/round-jobs.test.ts` and `--project integration packages/engine/src/season.test.ts packages/engine/src/world.test.ts packages/engine/src/calendar.fast-path.test.ts` pass.

## result
- outcome: two-phase `playRound` (all fixtures computed, then recorded in fixture order; a round naming a club twice keeps the interleaved loop). `round-jobs.ts` holds `FIXTURE_READ_KEYS`, `readCopy`, `roundJobs`, `simulateRoundJob`, `jobPlayback` (rejoins a result's roster IDs to the world's squads; a mismatched or unresolved result is computed on the engine instead), the job/result types and the per-world `setRoundSimulator(w, sim)` (WeakMap, default none). `RoundSimulator` = `(copy, jobs) => collect`: it starts the jobs, our match plays on the engine thread, then `collect()` returns results in job order or `undefined` (engine computes them). Used only on a league day with our fixture. For T519's async host: `prepareRound(w, start, observe)` (returns false and changes nothing unless the next day is such a day; else begins the day, calls `start(copy, jobs)`, plays our match and leaves the world pending) and `completeRound(w, results | undefined, settlement)` (records, settles, club day; returns our playback, as `advanceDays(w, 1)` would). `advanceDays`/`advanceRound` throw while a round is pending.
