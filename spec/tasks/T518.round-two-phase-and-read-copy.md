## goal
Let a league round's other fixtures be computed by an injected simulator from a read-only copy, recorded in fixture order, with worlds identical to today's interleaved round.

## acceptance
- [ ] `playRound` computes every fixture first and records them in the original order afterwards ("two-phase"); with no simulator injected the world is identical to a08e0dc1's interleaved path (day bench hash `098fdcae5ada357b`, and identical with `--matches 30`).
- [ ] New `packages/engine/src/round-jobs.ts`: `FIXTURE_READ_KEYS` (the world keys an other-club fixture reads: calendar, clubs, discipline, managerMarket, matchModel, people, playerClub, seed, year), `readCopy(w)` building that copy, `roundJobs(w, fixtures)` and the job/result types; `simulateRoundJob(copy, job)` is the pure function a helper runs.
- [ ] An injectable `RoundSimulator` (set per world session, default none) receives the round's non-own league fixtures on a day with our league fixture and returns their results; our match still plays on the engine thread meanwhile. Cup/continental days and days without our fixture keep the sequential path.
- [ ] A round where any club appears twice falls back to the sequential path.
- [ ] `round-jobs.test.ts` (unit, small fixture world): (a) an in-process simulator that solves jobs in reverse order through `readCopy` gives the same canonical world as no simulator over 60 days including own match days; (b) a Proxy-traced run of `simulateRoundJob` reads no world key outside `FIXTURE_READ_KEYS`; (c) a duplicated club falls back.
- [ ] typecheck, lint/format on touched files, `pnpm exec vitest run --project unit packages/engine/src/round-jobs.test.ts` and `--project integration packages/engine/src/season.test.ts packages/engine/src/world.test.ts packages/engine/src/calendar.fast-path.test.ts` pass.

Fixtures are independent: each draws from its own seeded streams (`${seed}:match/form/discipline/injury/shootout:${f.id}`) and records only its two clubs' state, each club playing once a round.
- The engine stays synchronous: export a `prepareRound`/`completeRound` pair (or equivalent) so T519's host can await helper results between them; `advanceDays` keeps its sync path when no simulator is set. Shipping NPC squads to helpers was a net loss (40–100 ms on the engine thread) — the copy omits them and helpers rebuild via the per-year squad cache.
