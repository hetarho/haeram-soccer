## goal
Give candidate players persistent, role-shaped intentions instead of unrelated targets on every update.

## acceptance
- [ ] Implement the shared intention lifecycle, references and priority order from the contract in `grid/intentions.ts`; active runs use that lifecycle instead of a second competing planner.
- [ ] Feasible intentions survive ordinary updates. Arrival, infeasibility, a turnover, an urgent defensive event, restart, substitution, injury or dismissal can interrupt them with a recorded reason; stale references never survive a roster change.
- [ ] Role, traits, decision/composure attributes, pressure and fatigue affect choices and reactions using existing player/tactic rules. No new career attributes, owner controls or per-render decisions are added.
- [ ] Trace/playback expose the same active target, state and relevant partner used by the engine. Movement consumes T274's bounds and cannot jump when the intention changes.
- [ ] `grid/intentions.test.ts` covers `retains a feasible intention`, `interrupts on a turnover or urgent danger`, `cancels a lost receiving opportunity`, `clears departed-player references`, `keeps role differences observable`, and `is deterministic with observation disabled`.
- [ ] Task checks follow ARCH-30. Leave tactic/distribution calibration to T278 and record the changed behavior for it.

## impl notes
- All state is local to one match. Seed draws by fixture and decision reason; do not depend on a rendering frame or incidental map traversal order.
- Keep the existing formation and role definitions canonical. An intention may temporarily move a player within his tactical job but does not grant a new formation or tactical instruction.
- T275 and this task may share `grid/playback.ts`; they have no parallel lanes and must integrate serially if run through work tooling.
