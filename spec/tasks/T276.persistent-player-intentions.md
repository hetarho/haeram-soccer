## goal
Give candidate players persistent, role-shaped intentions instead of unrelated targets on every update.

## acceptance
- [v] Implement the shared intention lifecycle, references and priority order from the contract in `grid/intentions.ts`; active runs use that lifecycle instead of a second competing planner.
- [v] Feasible intentions survive ordinary updates. Arrival, infeasibility, a turnover, an urgent defensive event, restart, substitution, injury or dismissal can interrupt them with a recorded reason; stale references never survive a roster change.
- [v] Role, traits, decision/composure attributes, pressure and fatigue affect choices and reactions using existing player/tactic rules. No new career attributes, owner controls or per-render decisions are added.
- [v] Trace/playback expose the same active target, state and relevant partner used by the engine. Movement consumes T274's bounds and cannot jump when the intention changes.
- [v] `grid/intentions.test.ts` covers `retains a feasible intention`, `interrupts on a turnover or urgent danger`, `cancels a lost receiving opportunity`, `clears departed-player references`, `keeps role differences observable`, and `is deterministic with observation disabled`.
- [v] Task checks follow ARCH-30. Leave tactic/distribution calibration to T278 and record the changed behavior for it.

## impl notes
- All state is local to one match. Seed draws by fixture and decision reason; do not depend on a rendering frame or incidental map traversal order.
- Keep the existing formation and role definitions canonical. An intention may temporarily move a player within his tactical job but does not grant a new formation or tactical instruction.
- T275 and this task may share `grid/playback.ts`; they have no parallel lanes and must integrate serially if run through work tooling.

## result
- outcome: Done. With `GridSetup.model` set, `grid/intentions.ts` gives every player a `GridPlayer.intention`: kind, priority, target, an optional teammate/opponent roster index, `since`/`until`, and a run kind for runs. `GridPlayer.ended` keeps the last ended intention with its contract ending and reason; `INTENTION_REASONS` maps each reason to achieved, expired, infeasible or displaced. Without the model the pre-feature path is unchanged. Priority follows the contract: exit > restart > danger > committed > tactical. Tactical choices go only to free players, so ordinary updates keep every feasible intention. Runs are intentions, and `GridPlayer.run` remains their view for `decide.ts`. `startRuns` and the candidate share `runPlan`, so there is no second planner. Engine hooks: every new owner commits to receiving, with the passer as teammate, and a carry commits to its end point. A pass, cut-back or offside that misses its receiver cancels his chance as `lost`. A change of side interrupts both sides (`turnover`). A dead ball interrupts everything below exit; the taker and the scene then take restart intentions, achieved at the kick. Players who went up for a delivery hold their box spots as committed runs, replacing `holdSetPiece` on the candidate path. Substitution, injury and dismissal end the leaver's intention with that reason, and every intention that references him ends as `departed`. `updateIntentions` runs once per action, after it is applied and before anyone moves. The first version ran after the movement: pressers chased the passer while the ball flew, and on 600 fixtures tackles + interceptions fell to 21 and goals rose to 3.5. Choices: Danger: the carrier within 30 m of goal, or a runner beyond the second-last defender. The first presser and the markers then take danger priority and leave the line. Press: the lowest distance ÷ (role/trait press weight × freshness), within the tactic's press or counter-press reach. Cover: a covering role first, only from shape places within 10 m of the cover point. Mark: a runner is picked up within 20 m and given up past 28 m. Recover: for 6 s after a loss, when 14 × (1 − 0.4 × 판단력) m from his shape place. Support: a pressured carrier's teammate shows, chosen by distance ÷ `key` weight, with odds 0.45 + 0.45 × 침착성. 판단력 picks the side away from the presser. There is one draw per carrier and possession spell. Run odds are scaled by 판단력 timing (0.8–1.2) × freshness (minutes and 체력). Draws are named by seed, fixture, decision, side, player and moment. `TraceEntry.intentions` holds each slot's intention, or one that ended since the last entry; the slot's `targets` is the target its movement used, and its `states` come from the intention. `PlayerMotion.intention` carries an `IntentionObservation` with player-ID partners. The design doc covers this in §1.2, §4.5, §10.1 and §10.2. Measured for T278 on grid-bands' 600 equal-strength 2024 fixtures (all 16 tactic pairings), against T274's recorded candidate values. It is the same population but not a paired rerun: this run's pre-feature path gave goals 2.74, tackles + interceptions 28.79 and ball in play 57.11, against T274's 2.75, 28.61 and 57.31. Candidate changes: ball in play 58.50 → 59.51 min, goals 2.94 → 2.70 tackles + interceptions 27.60 → 25.53, throw-ins 17.00 → 16.14, possessions 95.2 → 95.35 forward passes 33.7 → 35.4%, added time 10.61 → 10.19 min passes 469.8 → 472.5, completion 76.4 → 77.2%, shots 11.62 → 11.90; take-ons 14.95 Outside their MATCH-23 bands: goal-area shots 11.0%, crosses 21.5, throw-ins 16.1, goal kicks 9.3, fouls 9.5, yellow cards 3.12, set-piece goals 19.2%, penalty goals 4.9%, FW goals 62%, ball in play 59.5.
