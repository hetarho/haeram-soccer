## goal
An autosave encodes once and checks its own bytes (SHA-256 after a gunzip) instead of decoding, unpacking and validating the whole world; the 6 MiB/8 MiB/32 MiB budgets become an export file limit of 256 MiB and a decompressed limit of 1 GiB.

## acceptance
- [ ] `checkpoint(w, generation, parentGeneration)` returns `{ raw, envelope }` after re-reading only the payload: gunzip it, compare its SHA-256 and byte length with what was encoded; it never calls `JSON.parse` on the payload, `unpack`, `validateWorld` or `decode`. A corrupted payload makes it throw (unit test).
- [ ] The checksum is the SHA-256 of the exact uncompressed bytes written; those bytes come from `JSON.stringify(...)` of what is stored today (`pack(w)`), not `canonical(...)`. `decode` still verifies the checksum and validates the world fully at load/import (unchanged behaviour, existing tests).
- [ ] `repository.ts` exports `PORTABLE_FILE_LIMIT = 256 * 1024 * 1024` and `DECOMPRESSED_LIMIT = 1024 * 1024 * 1024`; `INDEXED_CHECKPOINT_LIMIT` and every UTF-16 budget check are gone (`checkpointWriter.ts`, `persistence.ts`); the gzip stream in `compression.ts` stops at `DECOMPRESSED_LIMIT`.
- [ ] Every import-size check reads `PORTABLE_FILE_LIMIT`: `App.tsx:957` (already), `GameSlotScreen.tsx:103` (hard-coded 8 MiB today), `protocol.ts:249` inspect `raw` max.
- [ ] Task stage, owner implementer: `vibe check --tier task` passes.

## impl notes

- Size checks at write: `checkpointWriter.ts:22-26` keeps only `new TextEncoder().encode(raw).length <= PORTABLE_FILE_LIMIT` (base64 text is ASCII, so `raw.length` is enough); `persistence.ts:83-87` `readEnvelope` keeps its byte check against the new limit.
- Tests: rewrite `persistence.budget.test.ts` for the new limits (one test that a raw above `PORTABLE_FILE_LIMIT` is refused before decompression, one that the decompression limit stops a bomb — use a small injected limit if the helper allows, or test the stream helper directly); update `repository.test.ts` and `persistence.history.test.ts` uses of `INDEXED_CHECKPOINT_LIMIT`.

- e2e (maintain only): `tests/e2e/fast-checkpoint.spec.ts` asserts the old size limits — update to the new limits or remove the size cases.
