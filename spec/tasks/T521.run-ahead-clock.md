## goal
While the page is shown the worker computes up to N days ahead and the calendar reveals one day per pace interval, so slow match days are absorbed without skipping a shown day.

## acceptance
- [ ] `RUN_AHEAD_DAYS` per pace (x1 3, x2 3, x5 5, x10 7) in `progression.ts`; computing continues back to back while fewer than that many days are held, and the calendar shows the next held day one pace interval after the previous one, or as soon as it lands when late.
- [ ] No day is computed past one whose reply carries a new stopping event, a pending decision, a critical alert, bankruptcy or an own match eve that stops for observation; such a day stops the clock when it is shown, with the world on the shown date.
- [ ] The owner's stop reveals the held days at the pace, then stops; the management lock (→WEB-97) lasts until they are shown; the stop's save is requested after the last one is shown (→SAVE-8). Entering the owner office, and hiding the tab, show the held days at once; nothing is computed ahead while hidden or right after returning.
- [ ] A pace change applies to days computed from then on; held days are shown at the faster of the old and new pace.
- [ ] `progression.runahead.test.ts` (unit, fake worker with measured step times): x10 for 30 s shows no skipped day and fewer stalls than depth 0; stop drains then unlocks and saves once; a stopping event or match eve lands on its day with nothing computed past it; office entry and hiding reveal at once; x10 → x1 with a full buffer reveals at x10. `progression.test.ts` still passes.
- [ ] typecheck, lint/format on touched files and `pnpm exec vitest run --project unit apps/web/src/runtime/progression.test.ts apps/web/src/runtime/progression.runahead.test.ts` pass; `client.test.ts` (integration) listed in result limits.

## impl notes
- Port prototype commit `db28c045` (branch `worktree-agent-aeba6917277c9c905`, in `perf/integrated` as `b9a6956a`, with the client.ts merge against T520 resolved there): the client holds run-ahead views as `head` (requests and saves use it, `reveal()` publishes held views in order); its `RUN_AHEAD_PROTOTYPE` option becomes the fixed `RUN_AHEAD_DAYS`.
- Rolling back to the shown day is not used: a world clone costs 70–230 ms (7.9 MB at season end), beyond x10's 100 ms day. Held views share structure; N = 7 adds under about 2 MB.
- Measured 261027 (1950 ENG season, with T517–T520's speed-ups): x10 54 → about 10 stalls, 4.3 → about 8.9 days a second; x1/x2/x5 practically none. Without them, depth alone leaves about 34 at x10.
