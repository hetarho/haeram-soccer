## goal
The office opens on a lobby of six pressable area cards with live facts; each area opens full-screen with ← 사무실, and the report/delegation area is named 결재 라인.

## acceptance
- [ ] `/direction` resolves to the lobby; `/direction?view=strategy|build|finance|sponsor|identity|office` open their areas; older `/business` addresses and `facility`/`marketing` views still reach their areas; a non-canonical address is replaced by the canonical one.
- [ ] The lobby shows the office illustration over six cards in the order 운영 방침, 시설, 재정, 후원, 구단 디자인, 결재 라인, each a button with its name and one current fact: applied direction name; stadium step name + capacity, or `공사 중 n건` while works run; cash (money notation) + runway text; filled sponsor slots `n/m`; the club crest; `보고받기 n · 위임 m`.
- [ ] Selecting a card pushes the area's address and shows only that area, with the office bar reading `← 사무실` and the area's name; ← 사무실 and browser back (from a lobby-opened area) return to the lobby at its previous scroll; 나가기 shows only in the lobby. No page-tab bar remains in the office.
- [ ] A link from elsewhere (who-decides, match-event, sponsor/finance event, facility request, department `openHome`) opens its area directly; ← 사무실 then goes to the lobby and 나가기 from there returns to the origin (T491).
- [ ] The `office` area is titled 결재 라인 everywhere it is named (`BUSINESS_TABS`, office bar, cards, confirmation copy that named 구단주 사무실 as the page); `data-testid="owner-office"` stays on its content.
- [ ] Home's entrance opens the lobby (fresh entry), not the last area.
- [ ] `apps/web/src/ui/officeSummary.test.ts` covers each card fact from small world fixtures (works under way vs. none, delegation counts, sponsor slots); `apps/web/src/ui/state.test.ts` (new or existing route tests) covers `/direction` → lobby, each `?view=` area, `/business?view=facility` → build and the canonical addresses.
- [ ] tests/e2e: `openOffice`/`openOperations` helpers use the lobby cards; specs that clicked office page tabs use the helper; records of the 구단주 사무실 tab name in specs read 결재 라인. Left to the batch run.

## impl notes
- `state.ts`: `BUSINESS_TABS` keeps ids and order, label of `office` → `결재 라인`. New `type OfficeView = 'lobby' | BusinessView`; `businessTab(view)` → `officeView(view): OfficeView` returning `'lobby'` when no area matches. `Route.view` for business is `OfficeView`; canonical address `/direction` for lobby, `/direction?view=<id>` for every area (strategy no longer omitted). `openBusiness(view: OfficeView)`, `useBusinessView` carries `OfficeView`; the popstate and `returnToTask` branches default to `'lobby'`. `openOfficeLobby()` = `openBusiness('lobby')` and is used by the home entrance; `openOwnerOffice()` keeps opening `'office'`.
- `Operations.tsx`: replace `PageTabs`/`TabPanel` with `view: OfficeView` state from the address; lobby renders `OfficeLobby`, an area renders its existing content unchanged. Selecting a card does `history.pushState` to the area address and sets state; ← 사무실 does `history.back()` when the previous entry is this office's lobby (track a `fromLobby` flag set on card selection), else `replaceState` to `/direction`. Remove the old office-tab `scrollIntoView` effect. Keep the lobby's scroll in task memory (`useTaskMemory` scrolls keyed by `/direction`).
- `OfficeBar` (from T491): back `{label:'나가기'}` in the lobby, `{label:'사무실'}` in an area; title `구단주 사무실` or the area label.
- `officeSummary.ts`: pure `officeFacts(w, view): Record<BusinessView, string>`-style helpers — direction name via `strategyName(worldBoard(w), saved)` (saved = the device strategy library the board already reads; pass it in), stadium from `STADIUM_STEPS[w.stadium]` and `w.facilityWorks`, cash via the existing money notation helper and `cashState(w).value`, sponsors from `state.view.sponsors` (slots with a contract / all slots), delegation counts moved here from `OwnerOfficeView` (`RESPONSIBILITY_KEYS`, observations from `settingsOf(w)`), reused by `OwnerOfficeView` so both read one count.
- `OfficeLobby.tsx` (+ css): `FacilityArt kind="manager"` hero, a 2-column grid of card buttons (`data-ui` card treatment and theme card tokens like the team-state tiles, icon from `PixelIcon`/`NavIcon` families, 44px+ targets, pressed motion per WEB-35/46), crest via `ClubCrest`.
- No engine, contract or save change.
