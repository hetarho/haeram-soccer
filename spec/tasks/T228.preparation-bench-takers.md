## goal
Before a match the owner sees who the manager put on the bench and on set pieces, who is out and why, the era's substitute rule, and a tactic lab that reads the same numbers the engine plays.

## acceptance
- [ ] The preparation dialog shows, under the formation, the named substitutes (`benchFor` for the next own fixture), the penalty, direct free-kick and corner takers (`takersFor`), and unavailable players with reason (injury days or ban matches and scope) — all read-only (the manager decides, MATCH-20/CLUB-6).

- [ ] `compareTactics` uses `ownTacticalProfile(w, players, tactic, opponent)` so manager style and synergies are included (MATCH-9), and `TacticalLab` shows each tactic's formation with the lineup (WEB-21) using `FormationPitch`.
