## goal
Players negotiate when a transfer window opens: renewal in their final season, an improved contract with at most three seasons left when 25%+ below market; terms count by window; delegated staff also check the season projection.

## acceptance
- [ ] Talks open on the day each window opens (summer: the season's first day; winter: 1 January), never on other days; a player in his final season asks to renew at either opening until he has agreed or turned unhappy; a player with at most three seasons left (this one included) paid 25% or more below his market wage asks for an improved contract at most once a season; nobody else asks, so a promotion or growth changes nothing before then.
- [ ] Terms by window: an offer in the summer window runs 1–5 seasons, this one included; in the winter window the rest of this season and 0–4 more (shown as ½–4½ years); `until` is always a season whose close ends the contract (→CLUB-28 unchanged).
- [ ] An agreed improved contract replaces the running one from the agreement (no back pay); the talk kind is `renew` or `improve`; a declined talk of either kind makes the player unhappy (→CLUB-27 unchanged).
- [ ] Delegated: the floor is offered for 2 seasons (1 from age 32) only when within the pay ceiling, cash after the raise covers 13 rounds of costs and the season's cash projection (→ECON-18) with the new wage from the next round still ends the season covering 13 rounds of costs; otherwise declined with the reason named.
- [ ] contracts.test.ts: no talk on a non-opening day; a final-season player asks at the summer and winter openings; a player 30% below market with 3 seasons left asks once a season, with 4 seasons left never; a winter offer with term 0 ends at this season's close and term 2 two closes later; a delegated renewal declines when the projection would end below 13 rounds of costs and accepts when above.
- [ ] The contract-talk UI offers the window's terms (½–4½ in winter) and names an improved-contract talk as such; `pnpm run test:unit`, `pnpm run typecheck`, `pnpm run lint` pass.

## impl notes
- Contract talks are new in rules 1.16.0 and unreleased: change the 1.16.0 shapes in place (`ContractTalk.kind: 'renew' | 'improve'`; `raiseAsked` becomes `improveAsked`, the season year; `UnhappyReason` `'raise-declined'` becomes `'improve-declined'`) and update schema and fixtures; no released save holds the old names.
- Winter terms: keep `ContractTerm` 1–5 for summer; for winter accept 0–4 extra seasons (`until = year + term`), summer `until = year + term - 1`. Represent the offered term as a number of further closes so both share one field; the UI labels winter terms ½, 1½ … 4½.
- Window days come from `transferWindowDays(year)` in transfers.ts (summerOpen, winterOpen); trigger talks in the same daily step that already handles the 1 January talks.
- The season-end check uses the projection the owner sees (packages/engine/src/projection.ts): project with the player's wage replaced by the floor from the next round and compare the projected season-end cash with 13 rounds of `operatingCosts` after the raise.
- Clauses and agents are T068; keep the demand formula's hook (a multiplier) where the agent premium will plug in.
