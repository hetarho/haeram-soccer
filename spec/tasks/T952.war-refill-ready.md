## goal
A skipped war summer refills a squad to its standing size with players aged 19–24 drawn near the division's level (NPC_YOUTH.warRefill), so the 1946 restart and a 1951 start open near 80/70/60/50 instead of about 4 below.

## acceptance
- [v] Unit tests: a skipped summer's refill players are aged 19–24 with OVR near the division level less `NPC_YOUTH.warRefill.below` (starting 3) and potential a few points above their OVR (`NPC_YOUTH.warRefill.headroom`, starting 4); a normal summer's academy intake and refills are unchanged; prodigies still arrive on top in every summer (T947); the refill still stops at the standing size with line minima and a goalkeeper.
- [v] Measured 1930→1957 (fresh 1930 on the T916 seed and two more seeds, the probe or the T944 harness `--start fresh:1930 --seasons 27` or equivalent): per-tier mean strength at the 1946–1957 openings against 80/70/60/50, before and after. Reported, not gating; T950 tunes the constants.
- [v] The day-loop equivalence tests (calendar.progression, season, full-model-throughput) and the wartime tests pass; fixtures this intended change moves are regenerated and named in the result.
- [v] Task-tier checks pass through `vibe check --tier task`.

## impl notes
- From the T950 phase-1 diagnosis (coordinator measurement 261118): with T946/T947 every variant opened 1949–1955 about 4 below the levels (75.8/65/56.7/49), because seven skipped summers refill with 17–18-year-olds who take years to reach the level.
- The refill path is closeNpcSummer's skipped-summer branch (`matches=false`) in npc-careers.ts; draw the refill person through the existing youth-person path with an age 19–24 and an OVR target, the way T947's prodigy draw shifts attributes onto a drawn OVR (reuse that helper from prodigies.ts by import; do not copy it).
- Keep PRNG streams of other draws unchanged (own stream key for the war refill draw) and keep the ×3 prodigy rule and the T947 registration checks.

## result
- outcome: A skipped war summer (`closeNpcSummer(…, matches=false)` → `intakeNpcYouth(…, refillOnly)`) now refills a squad to its standing size with players drawn ready to play. `NPC_YOUTH.warRefill` = { ages [19, 24], below 3, headroom 4 } (npc-careers.ts). A new private `youthDraw` returns age, OVR and potential. For a war refill they come from the war refill's own stream `<seed>:war-refill:<id>`, which draws only the age: OVR = `divisionLevel(tier)` − below (15–92), potential = OVR + headroom. The person is still drawn through `generatedPlayer` with the same id, position (`refillPlace`), contract stream and graduate/joined/fatigue fields. His attributes are then shifted onto the drawn OVR with T947's helper, which this task exported from prodigies.ts as `landOnOvr` (the prodigy draw now calls it too, with byte-identical behavior), and his wage is re-quoted at that OVR. A played summer keeps the youth stream's draws in their old order (age, skewed potential, headroom), so academy intakes and refills, the window-close safety net and every other stream are unchanged. The loop still stops at the standing size with the line minima and a goalkeeper, under the registration checks. `drawProdigies` still runs after the intake in every summer, so prodigies come on top.
  - beyond the declared touches: prodigies.ts exports `landOnOvr` (no behavior change), and prodigies.test.ts's war-gap case now expects war refills near the level − 3 rather than at the bench (its old comment described the replaced rule).
  - correction (review 58eb0a11, P2 npc-careers.ts): the war refill's wage was quoted before his potential was replaced by the warRefill headroom. A player under 24 is quoted on his potential too, so those arrivals kept the generated potential's higher wage (16 of 22 in the review's 1940 fixture). The wage is now quoted after the final OVR and potential are set. Played summers still keep generatedPlayer's wage, unchanged.
