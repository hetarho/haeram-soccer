# T1067 Keep a busy engine worker alive on slow devices

## goal
Main CI 45417944693 (0.8.26) failed `tests/e2e/second-career.spec.ts:292`: choosing AUTO at a real season close answered "진행 방식이 변경되지 않았어요. 다시 시도해 주세요." A local repro on two cores (`taskset -c 0,1`, `CI=1` built preview, two workers, `--repeat-each=4`) failed 2 of 4 twice, and client diagnostics showed why: the client failed the engine worker as hung ("계산 Worker가 종료되었거나 응답하지 않습니다"). First a manual `next-event` across the season close stayed silent past its 30 s watchdog. With that limit raised, the `settings` command right after the close stayed silent past its 15 s one. Engine work is synchronous and reports only between days, so one season-close day can hold a busy device silent that long; a failed worker drops the unsaved days. A player on a slow device needs the same tolerance.

## acceptance
- [ ] Commands and reads share one engine silence limit long enough for a season close on a busy device (storage and generation keep theirs). A unit test with a held worker shows a manual event and a settings command surviving more than 60 s of silence, and a worker silent past the limit still being failed; the first two fail on the old limits (deterministic; worker, unit).
- [ ] A request queued behind an earlier request allowed at least as long still waits its turn (`waitsBehindLonger`), and once the request ahead is answered it gets its whole limit from then, so a read behind a slow command is not taken for a hang (deterministic; unit).
- [ ] The two-core repro of second-career.spec.ts:292 passes 4 of 4 with the fix (host, recorded in result); browser journeys otherwise run in CI (ARCH-30).
- [ ] Run the ARCH-30 task tier with `vibe check --tier task` before committing.
