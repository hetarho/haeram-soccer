## impl notes
- The ledger is an engine read the worker computes into `officeRead(w).effects` beside the training focus's label; UI files import its types only, so no engine orchestration enters the UI (ARCH-4's leaf rule). Labels, value formats, destinations and area rows live in `apps/web/src/ui/officeLedger.ts` (`officeEffects.ts` would differ from `OfficeEffects.tsx` only in case, which macOS resolves to one file).
- Values come from the engine's own functions where one exists; factors decompose them: per department `departmentEffects(w, role)` (new in staff.ts, with `YOUTH_DEVELOPER` exported), per build slot `cardOf`, per synergy `SynergyState.effects` (new), per policy row the row's effects alone (`policyEffects` with every other row at its default). Engine refactors keep results bit-identical: `moraleBaselineTerms` + `MORALE_BASELINE_BOUNDS` (moraleBaseline sums the terms in the old order), `ACADEMY_INTAKE` in academy.ts, `gateProjection` returning `formDemand`.
- Goodwill replicates `talkGoodwill`'s card terms (family +5, meritocracy −5, frugal −5, 스타 대우 +10 at OVR 70+, squad morale) for an OVR-below-70 player and is guarded by the test; match strength replicates match.ts's own-club terms (manager ability, morale, school, 승리의 부적, home-only build strength).
- Money factors split the investment rows and facility upkeep by their units, the remainder to the last weighted part, so the parts add to `operatingCosts(w).annual` exactly; 정치력's saving is its own negative factor.
- Navigation: `openControl(area, control)` (state.ts) sets the one-shot `useControlRequest` and opens the area; InvestmentView takes it on the next frame, scrolls the element into view and sets `data-control-focus`, removed on `animationend` (no mark with reduced motion). 결재 설정 uses `openDecisionSettings(key)` from `DECISION_POLICIES`/`DECISION_BUILDS`/`DECISION_DIRECTIONS`; areas come from `PASSIVE_POLICIES`/`PASSIVE_BUILDS`, facilities by part, the ticket to 경기장, coaches to 코치진, bases to 팬 · 마케팅.
- Area rows: factors flagged `only` (OVR 70+, home) stay out of an area's share; a value no product or sum makes (expected crowd, overseas marketing fans) lists the area's non-neutral, non-count factors alone; listed values per area are `AREA_EFFECTS` in officeLedger.ts.
- Row buttons keep the theme's control frame (image themes frame every button), separated by a gap; the home gallery reuses InvestmentView.module.css with a `homeGallery` variant.
- Sequencing: the copy follows the primary checkout's uncommitted localization work (resource-only copy, ESLint `i18n/no-hardcoded-copy`), numbered locally T693 and WEB r208, which collide with next's T693 and r208. The implementation exists on local branch work/office-effects on a snapshot of that work; it lands on main once the localization is published under its renumbered task and rev.
