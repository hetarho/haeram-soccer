## goal
감독 이력 lists an interim only once he has been credited with an own match or an outcome; the stored facts stay as they are.

## acceptance
- [v] In `managerHistoryPage`, a tenure with `interim: true`, no attributed match (`summary.record.played === 0`) and no credited outcome is left out of the rows, the total count and cursor pagination; this holds whether or not he is the current manager.
- [v] `sharedSeason` ignores hidden interim tenures, so a season is not marked shared because of a caretaker who took no match.
- [v] An interim with one attributed match is listed from his appointment, as before.
- [v] The manager-history facts (`w.managerHistory`) are unchanged; capture code is untouched.
- [v] If `ManagerHistory.tsx`/`managerHistoryView.ts` count or label tenures independently of the page rows (e.g. a total, a "current" fallback), they follow the same rule.
- [v] Tests in `manager-achievements.test.ts`: "leaves out an interim with no match or outcome", "lists an interim once he takes a match", "a hidden interim does not make a season shared", "pagination skips hidden interims".
- [v] Fast checks: typecheck, lint, format:check, unit tests of changed files.

## impl notes
- Decide visibility after the match and outcome sweeps (both are already computed per accumulator); filter the final ordered rows. Keep the order and cursor format (`manager-history:1:`) unchanged — a cursor naming a hidden row is invalid like any unknown id.
- A typical case: a departure while delegated appoints the interim and his successor on the same day (→STAFF-7/18).

## result
- outcome: `managerHistoryPage` computes the hidden set (interim, `record.played === 0`, no deduplicated outcome credited to the tenure) after the match sweep and before the outcome sweep; hidden tenures drop out of the sorted rows (so `total`, pages and cursors skip them — a cursor naming one is `invalid-cursor`) and out of `sharedSeason`'s overlap check. Outcome deduplication now runs once as a filtered list that the sweep reads. `ManagerHistory.tsx`/`managerHistoryView.ts` only render page rows and `page.total`, so they need no change. Facts and capture code are untouched.
