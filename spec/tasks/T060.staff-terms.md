## goal
Hiring and renewing a manager or coach takes a term of 1–5 seasons at a fixed wage, releases pay 25% per season left, and no manager renews himself.

## acceptance
- [ ] `hire-manager`, `hire-staff`, `renew-manager` and `renew-staff` take a term of 1–5 seasons, never past a manager's 70th year; the firefighter signs for 1. The wage is fixed for the term: coach wages follow ability at signing, and the manager's is quote(120 + ability) indexed (→ECON-26).
- [ ] Release or replacement pays 25% of the annual wage for each season left.
- [ ] Managers no longer renew themselves at the close: on 1 April a renewal reminder covers any manager whose contract ends at the close, and an unrenewed manager leaves at the close (interim, departure choice).
- [ ] Delegated coach hiring renews coaches for 2 seasons as now.
- [ ] Hire and renew sheets in ClubStaff choose the term and show its total and the release cost.
- [ ] Tests: staff.test.ts covers terms, the 70 cap, release per season left and no self-renewal; managerMarket.test.ts covers hiring with a term.
- [ ] `pnpm run typecheck && pnpm run test:unit` pass.
