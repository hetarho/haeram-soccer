## goal
Running playback at 1x is real time, 60 real seconds per 60 match seconds (18 before), every speed of the unchanged 1x–16x ladder 10/3× slower, the dead-ball beats, the 1.5 s rush cap at 1x and the goal hold unchanged; every browser journey that watches running play keeps its assertions and fits its time limit.

## acceptance
- [ ] `WATCH_MINUTE_MS` is 60000; `WATCH_SPEEDS`, `WATCH_DEFAULT_SPEED`, `DEAD_BALL_CONFIG` and `GOAL_FEEDBACK_CONFIG` are unchanged.
- [ ] `presentationClock.test.ts` pins the real-time mapping (60000 ms a minute at 1x, `60000 / speed` at every speed, 30 s a minute at the opening 2x, 100 ms at 16x = 1.6 match seconds); its throw-in and short-stoppage cases use spans that still show the rush factor under its cap and a beat longer than running play at real time.
- [ ] `goalReplay.test.ts` (15 s clip in 15000 ms plus the hold; the final-sample goal reached by one 16 ms frame at 16x), `goalFeedback.test.ts` (the run limit covers the first goal at 1x) and `stagedPitch.test.ts` (frame counts derived from `presentationMs`) pass.
- [ ] Each journey under `tests/e2e` that watches running play is either judged safe or changed so that it keeps what it asserts and fits its limit, preferring a higher speed or a seek over a longer budget; the list with expected wall times is in result.
- [ ] `tests/e2e/preparation.ts`' `PLAIN_RUNNING_MS` is 20 s of match time at 1x from `WATCH_MINUTE_MS`, so `set-piece-scene.ts` picks the same corner whatever the mapping.
- [ ] `pnpm exec vitest run --project unit apps/web/src/features/match-viewer apps/web/src/entities/match`, `pnpm run typecheck`, `pnpm run lint`, `pnpm run format:check` and the gusta spec lint pass; browser journeys are not run here (batch gate).

## impl notes
- One constant (`WATCH_MINUTE_MS`); every journey wait already derived from it or from `presentationMs` follows by itself.
- Journeys that watch to a goal at the opening speed reach it at 16x instead (the cue, scorer, score and hold assertions do not depend on the speed reaching it); clips speed up to 16x only after any assertion on their opening speed; the archived replay seeks to the minute before its first goal (it has no speed chips); assertions on the opening 2x minute, a 16x rate or a whole match at 16x keep their watch and add its clock-derived time to the test budget.
- No engine, record, frame, seed, save or copy change.
