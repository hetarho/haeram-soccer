## goal
A free player acts on what he saw a moment ago, drifts toward his place at a speed that fits how far it is, and keeps adjusting instead of dashing and standing: after a pass the twenty outfield players no longer start in the same tick, run at the same speed and stop together, while the back four still move as one line.

## impl notes
- from review/match-engine-split-261110 F4
- Follow T791's chain rules. Staging stays a pure function of the script (→ARCH-5): everything below is computed from positions, attributes and the clock; no random number, no hash of a seed.
- What was measured on `artifacts/match-proto-261110` (planner's scratch read, `demo`–`demo5`), so the cause is not re-derived: every free player's target is one rigid function of one point (`shapeRef`: the ball, or a flight's landing point from the tick of the kick), read in the same tick by all twenty, and reached at one of two speeds (`jog` 3.8 m/s inside `motion.jogWithin`, else `run` 6 m/s) with a hard stop. So 63% of starts from rest come within 0.5 s of a kick or reception, 57–67% of them share a ± 0.2 s window with at least seven other starters, a player stops and starts again 9.1–9.7 times a minute, and two players' speeds correlate at 0.46–0.54. Speed shares: still 0.09–0.11, walk 0.31–0.36, jog 0.36–0.40, run 0.16–0.20.
- The design in one paragraph: a free player has a mind. He acts on the shape's inputs (the reference point, the side in possession, its phase) as they were `reactionDelay` seconds ago; the place he wants follows from them exactly as today; his own place follows the wanted place with a first-order lag; he moves to that place at a speed proportional to the distance left, inside his pace. Players near the ball and with high `decisions` react sooner and move more briskly; the back four share one delay and one lag so the line stays flat. Players under an order or cue, the presser, the cover, the keepers, the last-shoulder role and everyone during a dead ball are direct: they behave exactly as today.
- Config, `PROTO_CONFIG.motion` gains:
  ```ts
  /** Free players (stage/mind.ts). */
  own: {
    /** Seconds before he acts on a change: `base + perIq * (100 - decisions) / 100 + perMetre * metres to the ball`; a back-line player: `line`. */
    delay: { base: 0.15, perIq: 0.35, perMetre: 0.008, line: 0.25 },
    /** Time constant (s) his place follows the wanted place with: `base + perMetre * metres to the ball`; a back-line player: `line`. */
    lag: { base: 0.5, perMetre: 0.02, line: 0.5 },
    /** The metres to the ball count up to this. */
    farCap: 50,
    /** Seconds he gives himself to reach his place: `near` at the ball, `far` from `farAt` metres. */
    arrive: { near: 1.2, far: 3, farAt: 40 },
    /** He stands within `hold` metres of his place; otherwise he moves at `minSpeed` at least. */
    hold: 0.6,
    minSpeed: 0.8,
    /** Seconds of inputs kept for the delay. */
    memory: 1.2,
  },
  ```
  `motion.jogWithin` and `motion.transitionSeconds` stay: direct players still read them.
- `stage/mind.ts`:
  ```ts
  export interface Seen { ref: Vec; team: TeamId; phase: Phase }
  export interface Mind { place: [number, number] }
  /** The point the shapes read (moved here from world.ts, unchanged). */
  export function shapeRef(state: StageState): Vec;
  export function reactionDelay(spec: PlayerSpec, ballMetres: number): number;
  export function placeLag(spec: PlayerSpec, ballMetres: number): number;
  /** Appends this tick's true inputs `{ ref: shapeRef(state), team: state.team, phase: state.phase }` to `state.seen`, keeping `Math.round(own.memory / tick) + 1` entries, newest last. */
  export function remember(state: StageState): void;
  /** The inputs player `index` acts on: the entry `Math.round(reactionDelay / tick)` ticks back, the oldest kept when there are fewer. */
  export function seenBy(state: StageState, index: number, ballMetres: number): Seen;
  /** One tick: `place += (want - place) * (1 - Math.exp(-tick / lag))`. */
  export function followPlace(mind: Mind, want: Vec, lag: number): void;
  /** 0 when `d <= own.hold`; else `Math.min(cap, Math.max(own.minSpeed, d / arriveIn))` with `arriveIn = own.arrive.near + (own.arrive.far - own.arrive.near) * Math.min(1, ballMetres / own.arrive.farAt)`. */
  export function freeSpeed(d: number, ballMetres: number, cap: number): number;
  ```
  - `reactionDelay`: `own.delay.line` when `lineOf(spec.position) === 'DF'`, else the formula with `Math.min(ballMetres, own.farCap)`. `placeLag` likewise with `own.lag`.
  - `ballMetres` is always `dist(snapshot[index], state.ball.pos)`: the true ball, this tick.
  - `mind.ts` imports `StageState` as a type only (as `ball.ts` does); `world.ts` imports `mind.ts`.
- `stage/world.ts`:
  - `StageState` gains `minds: Mind[]` (one per player, `place` = his kick-off position in `createStage`) and `seen: Seen[]` (`createStage` pushes the first entry).
  - `defaultTarget(state, index, snapshot, seen?)` returns `{ target, pace, direct }`. `seen` defaults to the true inputs. The nominal place `base` is computed from `seen.ref`, `seen.team` and `seen.phase`; which branch applies is decided by the true `state.team`, `state.live` and `state.tight` as today, and the pressing targets read the true `shapeRef(state)` as today. `direct` is true for: a dead ball (`!state.live`), slot 0, the presser and the cover, and the `onShoulder` role while his side truly has the ball; for a direct player `base` is computed from the true inputs whatever `seen` says. The marking lean applies only to a player who is truly defending and whose `seen.team` is the other side (he believes he is defending); a player who does not believe it yet stands on his `base`. `pace` keeps today's label for every branch (tests read it).
  - `step`, per player, after `remember(state)` once at the top of the tick:
    - under an order or cue: as today, and `mind.place` is set to his body's position;
    - else `want = defaultTarget(state, index, snapshot, seenBy(state, index, ballMetres))`;
    - `want.direct`: target `separated(state, index, want.target, snapshot)`, speed `paceSpeed(want.pace, spec)` as today, `mind.place` set to his body's position;
    - otherwise `followPlace(mind, want.target, placeLag(spec, ballMetres))`, target `separated(state, index, mind.place, snapshot)` (making room is applied after the lag, so it is immediate), speed `freeSpeed(dist(position, target), ballMetres, paceSpeed('run', spec))`.
    - the pitch clamp, `keepOut`, `advanceBody` and `atBoards` follow as today.
  - Nothing else reads `mind.place`; handlers, contests, cues and the offside line keep reading bodies.
- `advanceBody` and `timeToReach` are not changed. A speed of 0 makes `advanceBody` brake to a stand.
- Expected side effects to record, not to fix here: the defending shape trails the play by up to its lag, so `events.recastRate` and `events.stretchRate` may rise and `stage.pressVisible` may move; the role metrics average places that now trail the ball. If a hard metric (`offside.consistency`, `kinematics.*`) fails, find the frame, reproduce it in a unit test with a short hand-written script and fix the cause (most likely a player who must be direct and is not); do not shorten a delay to hide it.
- Not in this task: what happens at a change of possession beyond the delay and lag above, and marking (T817); values are tuned in T818 — choose none by trial here beyond the ones given.
