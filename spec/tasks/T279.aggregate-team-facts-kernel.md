## goal
Build a fast probabilistic kernel whose coupled facts supply every numerical team counter with the detailed engine's meanings.

## acceptance
- [v] Define and export `AggregateInput`, `AggregateTeamFacts`, `buildAggregateInput` and `generateTeamFacts` as specified in the contract. Consume prepared strength/effective skills, lineups, roles, tactics, era and participation intervals without duplicating the strength formula.
- [v] Generate all Metrics and TD counters, team xG and compact shot/flow facts through a coherent ledger. Possession, passes, chances, xG, entries, transitions, set pieces and spatial classifications are coupled; denominators and successful/attempted counts reconcile.
- [v] Use named candidate-model seeded streams and grouped aggregate draws. Neither a 22-player trajectory simulation nor a loop over every match minute/action is used; bounded iteration over actual shots/incidents/participants is allowed.
- [v] The kernel accepts participation/man-short intervals, extends period facts for extra time and preserves the existing conditioning factors. It does not create player attributions or activate production dispatch before T280/T281.
- [v] Tests cover `produces every team counter`, `derives shot xg and classes from the same chances`, `derives spatial counters from shared flow facts`, `conserves attempts and completions`, `responds to tactic and strength inputs`, `accounts for man-short intervals`, and `is deterministic for its named streams`.
- [v] Ledger tests reject inconsistent facts rather than zero-fill/clamp unrelated counters. Task checks follow ARCH-30 using compact synthetic contexts.

## result
- outcome: `packages/engine/src/aggregate/` holds the kernel. `types.ts` defines `AggregateInput`, `AggregatePrepared`, `AggregateSkillAccess`, `AggregateParticipation`/`AggregateStint`, `AggregateStreams`, the ledger (`AggregateMove`, `AggregateShot`, `AggregateFouls`, `AggregateLedger`) and `AggregateTeamFacts`. `input.ts` has `buildAggregateInput(prepared, access, participants?)`, which copies the prepared strength, possession chance, tactics and profiles unchanged and reads effective skills only through the injected accessor (no `match.ts` import, no second strength formula, no new squads). It also has `fullParticipation`, `participationIssues`, `sideUnits` (time-weighted units over stints, including players short) and `sideCards`. `team.ts` has `aggregateStreams(seed, fixture, revision)`, which names streams `<seed>:<revision>:aggregate:<family>[:<period>]:<fixture>` for the families latent, flow, fouls, shots and setPieces. It also has `generateTeamFacts(input, streams, participation)`. That function draws one latent match state, then grouped binomial/multinomial/Poisson draws over the 40 grid cells and their reference tables: pass flows by direction and kind (short, long, through, cross, cut-back) with completions, line-breaking passes and failure fates (offside, interception, touchline, byline, corner won), then carries and take-ons, then fouls placed on those actions (penalties in the box, at least one foul per card shown for a foul), then shots from completed final-third arrivals (key pass = the arrival's pass) with cell-based xG in hundredths, then penalties, free kicks, and corners delivered until each won corner is taken. It visits only the actual shots and corners one by one. Extra time is a second period on its own streams, so the regulation facts stay identical when a tie is extended. `ledger.ts` derives every Metrics and TD counter, team xG, the score and the regulation score from the ledger under MATCH-13's grid definitions. Its `ledgerIssues`/`assertLedger`/`teamFactsIssues` checks refuse inconsistent ledgers or facts instead of filling or clamping them. `config.ts` (`AGGREGATE_CONFIG`) is the single tuning surface. Nothing calls the kernel yet: no player attribution and no dispatch.
