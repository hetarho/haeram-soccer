## goal
In the summer and winter windows (CLUB-17) clubs nobody owns buy, sell and loan among themselves at market value within their books and for need, and their bids for our players come from the same need.

## acceptance
- [ ] `packages/engine/src/npc-market.ts` runs on window days: each club ranks its needs (a line below minimum, an XI slot whose best player sits `NPC_MARKET.upgrade` below the club's affordable level) and its surplus (beyond `NPC_SQUAD.size`, blocked behind better players, last contract season); buyers in stature order take the best affordable listed player at CLUB-46 value (fee from cash or within debt rules of ECON-36), the seller's wage bill and need allowing; young surplus goes on loan.
- [ ] A player two clubs want joins the higher-stature club, then the higher wage (WORLD-16).
- [ ] Each completed move updates `w.squads`, both books, the player's contract (new wage/until) and records the move (clubs, fee, loan, date) on the player for T865; matchday caches and club strength refresh.
- [ ] Bids for our players (CLUB-22) come only from clubs whose need the player fills and whose books afford the fee and wage; stance and appeal rules for the owner's answer stay.
- [ ] A window's market is deterministic for a seed (own random streams) and runs in time proportional to listed players × buyers' needs, not clubs × all players.
- [ ] Tests: `packages/engine/src/npc-market.test.ts` — a club short of a keeper buys one; a club over its wage limit cannot buy; stature wins a contested player; a winter move splits his club totals; repeated runs on one seed give the same moves; an incoming bid needs a real need. Affected tests and goldens updated.
- [ ] `pnpm run typecheck`, `pnpm run lint`, `pnpm run format:check` and the changed unit tests pass.

## impl notes
- `NPC_MARKET` (upgrade margin, listing rules, loan ages, moves per club per window) is a named config defined here; T866 tunes it.
- No negotiation screens or counter-offers between clubs nobody owns; one settled price at value.
- Moves are not world-level history (a century would hold hundreds of thousands): they live in the player's record (T865) and in news only when T865's link rules allow.
