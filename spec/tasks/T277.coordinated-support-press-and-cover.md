## goal
Make individual intentions respond to teammates and opponents under one shared tactical purpose.

## acceptance
- [v] `grid/coordination.ts` computes side-level support, pressing and cover assignments from one snapshot, with stable-ID tie breaks and explicit handover; independent players cannot all claim the same tactical job by accident.
- [v] Carrier decisions consume actual receiving/overlap/run intentions. Support and defensive reactions remain legal under phase, role, offside line and rest defence, and use the same continuous movement scheduler.
- [v] Scenario tests cover `shows to receive while another opens the lane`, `waits for and releases an overlap`, `presses with cover behind`, `tracks and hands over a runner`, and `abandons a combination after the opportunity closes`.
- [v] Tests also cover `does not depend on player iteration order`, `preserves four tactic differences`, `does not change the world when only inspected`, and deterministic comparable role/trait differences.
- [v] Existing substitutions, unavailable-player rules and manager authority remain intact; raw outcome and target-band differences are handed to T278 rather than hidden by probability overrides.
- [v] Task checks follow ARCH-30 using compact situation fixtures, not full-season setup.

## impl notes
- Consume T276's intention lifecycle; this task owns the shared coordination module and is the first consumer that adds coordinated team jobs.
- Build tactical assignments before committing movements, then let each eligible player weigh his job through the existing attributes and traits. Do not let earlier processed players see a different snapshot.
- Keep commentary grounded in the recorded choice; no new label or story claims an unperformed combination occurred.

## result
- outcome: Done. New `grid/coordination.ts` plans both sides' jobs from one snapshot after each action (`planCoordination`, pure; `applyCoordination` in intentions.ts gives them out, so the order they are given out changes nothing): press count by tactic (`COORDINATION_CONFIG.pressers`, press 2, others 1, second within 0.6 × reach, counter-press reach after a loss), one cover per carrier behind the presser nearest him, runners matched to markers nearest pair first, an explicit `handover` (achieved; the ended record names the teammate) when a runner enters a free teammate's zone (`shapePlace` within 12 m and 6 m nearer than the marker's own), recover, one show per pressured carrier plus a `support: 'open'` lane clearance by a teammate standing within 3 m of the pass line, and runs with one overlap per flank, each overlap naming the carrier as teammate. Cost ties break by player ID, a claimed player takes no second job, and the danger escalation now raises the presser nearest the carrier (was lowest roster index). Legality: shows and clearances are clamped onside (offside line, ball or halfway), the rest defence (CB, DM) neither shows nor runs while the ball is in the opponent's half, defensive bounds and the bounded movement scheduler are unchanged because every job is an intention. Combinations close: a show or an overlap ends `closed` (infeasible) when an opponent stands within 2.5 m of its target, an overlap ends `moved` once its carrier plays on, and no job of that kind follows for 3 s. `updateIntentions` now reviews every intention against one state before ending any, and a cover or mark whose press or run just ended ends in the same update. `decide.ts` consumes intentions (`combination`; `DECISION_CONFIG.combination`): a showing teammate scores +0.6, an overlap level with or ahead of the carrier +0.9, and while it is still behind him an unpressured carrier carries 1.5×; there are no intentions on the pre-feature path, so production is unchanged. `shape.ts` splits `targetOf` into `targetOf` + `shapePlace` (same values). Design doc §1.2, §4.5, §4.6 (new), §6.3.
