## goal
A staged playback is judged by numbers before anyone watches it: no teleports, the ball where its owner is, receivers and winners at the ball, honest offside, a team shape that does not swarm, the showcase roles where they belong, and a reason behind every backward ball near goal — printed by one command for any seed.

## impl notes
- from review/match-engine-split-261110 F1
- Follow T791's chain rules. Metrics are pure functions of a `Playback` (plus `PROTO_CONFIG`); they import `offsideLineX`, `toAttack`, `lineOf`, `PASS_KINDS`, `isShotLike` and the vector helpers, nothing from `stage/` except in `report.ts`.
- Add to `PROTO_CONFIG`:
  ```ts
  limits: {
    duration: 599.5,
    ballSpeed: 45, outside: 2.5,
    ownerDistance: 1.2, ownerShare: 0.99,
    reception: 1.6, receptionShare: 0.98,
    contest: 1.6, contestShare: 0.95,
    recastRate: 0.25, stretchRate: 0.1,
    swarm: { radius: 10, count: 7, share: 0.02 },
    teamLength: [22, 52],
    spacing: { distance: 1.5, share: 0.03 },
    ifbInside: 8, ifbPivotGap: 8, falseNineDrop: 5, poacherGap: 3.5,
    backPass: { fromX: 80, back: 8 },
    pressedShare: 0.9, pressedSlack: 0.5,
  },
  ```
- Types and helpers in `metrics.ts`:
  ```ts
  export interface Metric { id: string; value: number; limit: string; pass: boolean; detail?: string }
  export const METRIC_IDS: readonly string[];
  export function computeMetrics(playback: Playback): Metric[];
  ```
  - `pos(frame, i) = [frame.p[2 * i], frame.p[2 * i + 1]]`; the frame of an event = `frames[Math.round(event.t * playback.hz)]` (clamp the index into range); the beat of an event = the performed beat whose `index` equals `event.beat`; `outfield(team)` = that team's player indexes without slot 0; "live frames" = frames with `live` true; `value` is rounded to 3 decimals; `limit` is a short text such as `<= 0.02` or `22..52` built from `PROTO_CONFIG.limits`.
  - a share with an empty denominator is 1 and passes (rates with an empty denominator are 0 and pass).
- The metrics, in this order (`METRIC_IDS`):
  | id | value | passes when |
  |---|---|---|
  | `duration` | `playback.duration` | ≥ `limits.duration` |
  | `kinematics.maxStep` | the largest player displacement between consecutive frames, divided by `tick` (m/s) | ≤ `motion.sprintBase + motion.sprintPerPace + stage.lunge + 0.2` |
  | `kinematics.outside` | player-frames more than `limits.outside` metres outside the pitch | = 0 |
  | `ball.maxSpeed` | consecutive-frame ball steps (x, y) faster than `limits.ballSpeed` m/s | = 0 |
  | `ball.ownerDistance` | share of frames with `owner >= 0` whose ball is within `limits.ownerDistance` of the owner | ≥ `limits.ownerShare` |
  | `events.reception` | share of `receive` events whose `other` is within `limits.reception` of the event's `at` in its frame | ≥ `limits.receptionShare` |
  | `events.contest` | the same share over `intercept`, `tackle`, `save` and `block` events with `limits.contest` | ≥ `limits.contestShare` |
  | `events.recastRate` | events with `recast` ÷ `intercept` + `tackle` + `foul` + `block` events | ≤ `limits.recastRate` |
  | `events.stretchRate` | events with `stretch` ÷ the same | ≤ `limits.stretchRate` |
  | `offside.consistency` | violations over `kick` events (below) | = 0 |
  | `shape.swarm` | share of live frames, phase not `setPiece` and the ball in neither box, with at least `swarm.count` outfield players of both teams within `swarm.radius` of the ball | ≤ `swarm.share` |
  | `shape.teamLength.H`, `shape.teamLength.A` | median over live frames of the team's outfield attack-x spread (max − min) | inside `limits.teamLength` |
  | `shape.spacing` | share of live frames, phase not `setPiece`, where two outfield teammates are closer than `spacing.distance` | ≤ `spacing.share` |
  | `role.invertedFullBack.inside` | for the team that has the role: mean over its live in-possession frames in `buildUp` or `progression` of `|y_other - 34| - |y_ifb - 34|`, `other` = the full-back of the other flank (slot 4 for slot 1 and the reverse) | ≥ `limits.ifbInside` |
  | `role.invertedFullBack.pivot` | mean over the same frames of `|attack x of the ifb − attack x of the team's slot 5|` | ≤ `limits.ifbPivotGap` |
  | `role.falseNine.drop` | for the team that has the role: mean over its live in-possession `progression` frames of `offsideLineX(frame positions, team) − his attack x` | ≥ `limits.falseNineDrop` |
  | `role.poacher.shoulder` | for the team that has the role: mean over its live in-possession frames in `progression` or `finalThird` of the same gap | ≤ `limits.poacherGap` |
  | `script.backPassReason` | performed beats of kind `pass` whose attack `from` x ≥ `backPass.fromX`, attack `to` x ≤ that − `backPass.back`, and no `reason` | = 0 |
  | `stage.pressVisible` | share of `kick` events of beats with `reason: 'pressed'` where an outfield opponent is within `stage.pressVisible + limits.pressedSlack` of the actor | ≥ `limits.pressedShare` |
  | `score.consistent` | goals in `goal` events | `playback.score` equals the last `goal` event's `score` (or `[0, 0]`), and the count equals the performed beats with outcome `goal` whose `goal` event exists |
  - a role metric of a role no team has is left out of the result and of the pass count; with the demo squads all four exist (`METRIC_IDS` lists them).
  - `offside.consistency`: for every `kick` event whose beat is pass-like and not shot-like and has a receiver: in the event's frame, with `T` the beat's team, the receiver is in an offside position when his attack x is beyond `HALFWAY`, beyond the ball's attack x and beyond `offsideLineX(fixture, frame positions, T)`, each by more than 0.05. A violation is (a) a beat with outcome `offside` whose receiver is not in an offside position, or (b) a beat of kind `pass` with another outcome whose receiver is. Restart kinds are exempt from (b).
  - the boxes for `shape.swarm`: world `x <= 16.5` or `x >= 88.5` with `y` inside `BOX_Y`.
- `report.ts` (CLI, `tsx`): `--seed <s>` (default `demo`), `--no-showcase`, `--duration <seconds>`, `--json <file>` (writes `{ info, stats, metrics }`), `--strict` (exit code 1 when a metric fails; default exit 0). Export `formatReport(info, stats, metrics): string` (pure, tested) and print it. Lines are `key=value` and table rows of identifiers and numbers only. Add the command to `README.md`'s `## commands`.
- Test helper (`check/playback.testkit.ts`): `fakePlayback({ frames, events, beats })` fills the rest (`demoFixture()`, `hz` from config, `duration` = last frame's `t`, `score` from the events); `frameOf(t, overrides)` starts every player on his kick-off place and lets a case move single players or the ball. Cases stay a handful of frames long.
