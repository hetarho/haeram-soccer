## impl notes
- The four formations' places are T823's tables; this task makes the stage use them for press and balanced sides and settles what the prototype decided by its two styles: every `style === 'possession' | 'counter'` branch becomes a read of a named per-tactic value in `STAGING_CONFIG` (`shape.block[tactic]`, `stage.press[tactic]`: how far the presser comes, whether the side counter-presses for `stage.counterPress.seconds` after a loss — possession and press do, counter and balanced do not, →MATCH-8's table). Start press from possession's values with a longer press reach, balanced between possession and counter; they are tuned in T840.
- Where each number acts (name the new values in `STAGING_CONFIG.motion.tendency`; 50 is neutral and reproduces T828's behaviour exactly, so T828's tests keep passing):
  - `anticipation` (예측력, 0–100): the reaction delay's `perIq` term reads it (the prototype's `iq`).
  - `discipline` (규율): scales the place lag down and the hold at his place up; at 100 the lag is `tendency.discipline.min` (0.6) of neutral, at 0 `max` (1.5).
  - `pressing` (압박 성향): scales the distance from which he is chosen presser or cover (`tendency.pressing.reach` 0.7–1.3 of neutral).
  - `freedom` (자유도): blends his role's `ROLE_SHAPE` override in — at 0 the slot's anchor, at 50 the override as today, at 100 `tendency.freedom.over` (1.3) of the way past it, clamped inside the pitch.
  - `pace`: his top and carrying speeds are the prototype's values × `tendency.pace` (0.85–1.15 from the slowest to the fastest outfield player of the match); in-match fatigue takes up to `tendency.fatigue` (8%) off by the final whistle, linearly with his minutes on the pitch.
- Where the numbers come from is T833's business (`playerTendencies`, the players' pace and 체력); tests here build `StagingPlayer`s by hand.
- A 3-5-2's back line: marking and the offside line read three centre-backs; the back-line group that shares one delay and lag (T816's back four) is the side's defenders by slot line, whatever their number.
- Keep the picture rules of →MATCH-17 as tests state them; do not tune limits here.
