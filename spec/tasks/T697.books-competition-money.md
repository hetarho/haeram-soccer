## impl notes
- Order: the mechanism and its unit tests (about 30 min); the screen on four worlds (T696 lived eight worlds in about 3 minutes each at eight jobs); the fit's two worlds; the eight gate worlds with the released extras re-read; the careers in one background run only after the three parts pass. At most 10 jobs on this shared host. Claude: one shell background run with its completion notification; Codex: one blocking call. No polling.
- Where the season's matches are at the close: `booksClose` runs after the archive and before promotion (`season.ts` 633, the results-only path 806); `w.cups` and `w.europe` still hold the closing season (`prepareEurope`/`prepareCups` run at the next start, `season.ts` 147–148). Read each club's cup ties from its country's `DomesticCup` (`rounds[].ties` with `winner`, `fixtures` with scores) and its continental matches from every `EuropeTournament` (`fixtures`, `rounds[].ties`, `winner`). Index them once a close beside `SeasonIndex` (T669) so no book scans every tie of the world.
- One rule for both payers: price each prize from a helper beside its config, used by the owner's credit and by the book — `cupTiePrize(code, year, left)` and `cupTitlePrize(code, year)` in cups.ts, `europeMatchPrize(code, year, result)`, `europeTiePrize(code, year)` and `europeTitlePrize(code, year)` in europe.ts — and the result bonus from a `resultBonus(code, year, tier, result)` in finance.ts that `matchBonus` uses. The owner's payments stay exactly as released. If importing cups.ts or europe.ts into books.ts trips a module-boundary or read-leaf test, move the helpers into a leaf both re-export instead of copying the arithmetic.
- Home gate of a cup or continental match: the book's league gate is the midpoint of `gateProjection(view)` (which carries `leagueMatchFactor`) per league home match; a cup or continental home match is that midpoint ÷ `leagueMatchFactor(view)`, the scale-1 gate the owner's `gate()` books for non-league matches. Hosting costs stay outside the books (wages and operating expenses only, as decided for ECON-36).
- ECON-30 needs nothing new: the next season's operating share reads the book's income as turnover, competition money included once in force. No stored field is added to `ClubSeasonBook` (SAVE-27 unchanged); the amount is summed into `income` only when in force.
- Screen counterfactual: `MEANS_LEVEL.observe` gives every hold (club, from, to); `BOOK_COMPETITIONS.observe` gives each club's amount at the close; the script searches the level the book would pay with budget + amount through `squadCostAt`, the same search as `meansLevel` (export it from dynamics.ts if needed, no second copy). The quarter is a go/no-go for spending world and career CPU, not a gate: with fewer released, the GER and POR champions T696 read cannot move back.
- `--cups` in `scripts/era-wage-spread.ts` and `scripts/owner-balance.ts` turns competition money on beside `--spread` and `--means`; overrides are measurement-only, the commit sets the production constants. Every table says which worlds it reads.
- No subsidy, no new scale: the amounts are the owner's own. Continental prizes follow each club's own country's broadcasting index (→ECON-31), so beside a small league's distribution they may read tiny; if the screen shows that, it is a finding for a later update-ssot of EURO-7/ECON-31 on UEFA's published distributions, not a change in this task.
- Champions comparison: production has not changed since T696, so T696's `released-{0..3}.extra.json` (in `books-rows.tar.gz`) are the released side; if a re-lived released world is needed, it must equal them.

Task checks: `books.test.ts`, `dynamics.test.ts`, `market-prices.test.ts` and other changed or affected unit-tier files, typecheck, lint, format. `cups.test.ts`, `europe.test.ts` and `finance.test.ts` are integration-tier (`tests/config/test-tiers.ts`): left to the host batch with the contract test, integration reading books, prices or dynamics, e2e on prepared worlds and build, listed in limits.
