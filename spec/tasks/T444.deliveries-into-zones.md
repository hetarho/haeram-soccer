## goal
On the candidate model, a cross or cut-back is played into a zone a teammate attacks — driven to the near post, lofted to the far post, pulled back to the spot or the edge, or squared across the goal area — to the runner arriving there, and the carrier does not deliver blind (MATCH-33 r28).

## acceptance
- [ ] `BOX_CONFIG.delivery` sets the delivery kinds by zone: `near` (driven, low, to `nearPost`), `far` (lofted, to `farPost`), `back` (a cut-back to `spot` or `edge`) and `square` (low across the goal area to the far-post runner, only from the goal-area band or the cut-back zones). The target is drawn among the zone runners of T443's scene by `exp(score / τ)` with the carrier's existing temperature, where score = `delivery.free` (1) × how free the runner arrives (no opponent within `delivery.freeRadius` 2 m of his zone) + `delivery.suit` (0.8) × his suitability (0.6 공중볼 + 0.4 몸싸움 for a lofted ball, 0.7 슈팅 + 0.3 침착성 for a low one, on the 0–1 share) + his zone's xT.
- [ ] No blind delivery: with no attacker in or loading for any zone the carrier's cross and cut-back weights are zero for that decision (he carries, passes or shoots by the rest of the table).
- [ ] The ball is played into the zone point, not to the runner's feet (as a through ball into the runner's path, T324): the delivery's path and the zone are contested first by an opponent `reachOnPath` (T441) finds — he cuts it out or heads it clear at his reach point by `DUEL_CONFIG.cross` (the existing intercept share and loose ball); otherwise a lofted ball goes to the existing aerial duel and keeper's claim, and a low ball (`near`, `back`, `square`) reaches the runner's feet for a shot with no aerial duel (a `cutBack`-flagged shot for `back` and `square`).
- [ ] Tests: `box.test.ts` "a lofted ball to the far post prefers the better header", "a low ball prefers the better finisher"; `decide.test.ts` "a cross goes to a zone a teammate attacks", "no cross or cut-back with nobody attacking a zone"; `resolve.test.ts` "a defender who reaches the delivery's path first cuts it out", "a low ball across the goal area is shot with the foot, not headed"; every new record passes `validateFullMatchRecord`; candidate digests re-recorded where they change, with the measured change in the test comment; the pre-feature path is unchanged (its digests pass untouched).
- [ ] The result reports, on one calibration round against the pre-change source (the next unused round in docs/CALIBRATION.md), per team a match: crosses and completion, cut-backs, deliveries by kind, key passes, big chances, box shots, shot place shares, xG per shot, headed goals, shots, xG and goals (reported, not gated: MATCH-28 r23).
