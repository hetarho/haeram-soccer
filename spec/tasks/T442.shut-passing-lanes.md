## goal
On the candidate model, the presser closes the carrier on a curve that keeps his most dangerous nearby option in his cover shadow, and a free midfielder or defender screens the lane into a receiver between the lines or in the half-space (MATCH-32 r28).

## acceptance
- [ ] `lanes.ts` exports `SHADOW_CONFIG` and `dangerousOption(s, side, carrier)`: among the carrier's teammates within `SHADOW_CONFIG.near` (25 m), the one with the highest xT gain whose path `reachOnPath` (T441) finds unreached, or none.
- [ ] Press with a cover shadow: the presser's tracked target moves from 1.2 m goal-side of the carrier toward the carrier–option line by up to `SHADOW_CONFIG.curve` (1.5 m), so that he approaches between them; with no dangerous option, or when the carrier is within `danger` of goal (the urgent press), the target is unchanged.
- [ ] Screening: one free defending player per carrier, the nearest midfielder or defender whose shape place is within `SHADOW_CONFIG.screen.reach` (10 m) of the lane to a receiver between the lines or in a half-space (MATCH-15's tags), takes a tactical `screen` job: he stands on that lane at most `SHADOW_CONFIG.screen.step` (6 m) from his shape place, inside his line's bounds, never the rest defence's last man nor a marker of a runner; it ends `cleared` when the carrier plays the ball or the receiver leaves the lane. It is a new `INTENTION_KINDS` entry `screen` (observation only, never archived) with its reason in `INTENTION_REASONS`, its trace mapping and its inspector label 길목 차단 in `Pitch.tsx`, one job per player (§4.6).
- [ ] Each tactic shuts lanes from its own block: the screen and the shadow apply only where its press reach applies (`SHAPE_CONFIG` press and counter-press reach by tactic, MATCH-8).
- [ ] Tests: `lanes.test.ts` "the dangerous option is the unreached teammate with the highest threat gain"; `coordination.test.ts` "the presser approaches between the carrier and his dangerous option", "one free midfielder screens the lane to a receiver between the lines", "no screen leaves the last defender or a marker's runner", "an urgent press keeps its goal-side target"; `intentions.test.ts` "a screen ends cleared when the ball is played"; every new record passes `validateFullMatchRecord`; candidate digests re-recorded where they change, with the measured change in the test comment; the pre-feature path is unchanged (its digests pass untouched).
- [ ] The result reports, on one calibration round against the pre-change source (the next unused round in docs/CALIBRATION.md), per team a match: passes and completion, passes into the space between the lines completed, interceptions, tackles + interceptions, PPDA and high turnovers, final-third passes, front-of-box and box entries, shots, xG, goals and MATCH-29's tactic rows (reported, not gated: MATCH-28 r23).

## impl notes
- The jobs go through `planCoordination`/`applyCoordination` like every other job (one snapshot, ranked by cost, ties by player ID, draws from named streams), so a plan changes nothing until applied.
- Shutting a lane is what makes T441's reach matter in play: the screen puts a reaching opponent on the path, it does not change the duel. No interception share or completion number changes here.
- T432 later moves free players from their slots on revision 2; the screen's step stays bounded by the shape place so the two do not fight.
- If bands.test's coarse guards fail, widen them only to the measured level with this task's evidence in the comment; T437 restores them before T286. Do not request a decision for that.
