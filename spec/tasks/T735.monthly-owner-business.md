## goal
The owner's business settles on the 1st of every month from September to June and on the season's close day, succeeds 60% of the time (70% with 성공한 사업가) and wins or loses 5% of the staked share each time, with the season's living costs unchanged in total.

## acceptance
- [ ] OWNER_BUSINESS lists the ten monthly dates (1 September–1 June) with the close, `chance` 0.6, `swing` 5 (percent of the staked share); 성공한 사업가 succeeds with 0.7; OWNER_LIVING states 0.8% a season, each settlement paying the season's amount divided by the settlement count without losing minor units over a season.
- [ ] Engine and web figures agree: `settlementDays`/`nextSettlement` name the next monthly date, the close or next season's 1 September; `stakeAmount` is fortune × stake × swing; the 구단주 sheet's 성공/실패 amounts and the trait's start-screen line read the new values; the war gap (`ownerSettleGap`) settles every monthly date it skips.
- [ ] The 50% dividend path is checked: a season's positive net result (income less expense, injections and the dividend excluded) pays the dial's share, capped by cash above zero; a unit case covers a season with an injection.
- [ ] The sheet tells the owner the cadence and swing in one translated sentence in every locale pack; the influence map's quarterly wording says monthly.
- [ ] Unit tests for owner, owner-config, ownerFigures and startChoices pass with typecheck, lint, format and `pnpm run validate:i18n`.

- The season runs from 1 August (day 0, never processed) to the close day (SEASON_END_DAY + 1); no settlement falls in the skipped summer.
- No save migration (not public yet); an owner's saved stake keeps its percent.
