## goal
The chief scout's list (`transferOffers`, the shortlist's source) holds only existing players — other clubs' first-team players and the world's free agents within his reach — and no player is ever generated for it.

## impl notes
- New module `packages/engine/src/scout-search.ts`: `SCOUT_CONFIG = { reachBands: [{ below: 60, home: 'adjacent', perLine: 2 }, { below: 80, home: 'country', perLine: 3 }, { home: 'country', perLine: 4 }], abroadCountries: [0, 1, 2, 4, 7], abroadPlaces: [0, 1, 1, 2, 2], youngAge: 23 }` and `scoutReach(w)`. `home: 'adjacent'` = our league plus the divisions directly above and below in our country; `'country'` = every division of our country. Abroad countries are the other playable countries ordered by league strength (the existing European coefficient/league-strength read; ties by country code), their top flight only. Free agents count as home when their nationality is ours, else as abroad for their nationality's country.
- Card effects become search effects in `build-cards.ts` (or where `buildEffects` lives): `scoutPerLine` (성골 유스 −1, 데이터 스카우팅 +1, 광역 네트워크 +1, 지역 인재 우선 −1), `scoutAbilityShift` (데이터 스카우팅 −2, 즉시 전력 +4, applied to both band ends), `scoutPotentialFirst` (데이터 스카우팅), `scoutNeverPotentialFirst` (광역 네트워크), `scoutAbroadExtra` (광역 네트워크 +1 country), `scoutNoAbroad` (지역 인재 우선). Policy `globalScouting` keeps its spend and 글로벌 인지도; its level only indexes `abroadCountries`/`abroadPlaces`.
- `transferOffers` keeps its name and array shape (callers index it); move its body to `scout-search.ts` and re-export from `ops-squad.ts` only if that avoids touching callers, otherwise update imports. Fees for club players reuse the world market's club-offer fee/loan computation (extract it from `world-market.ts` into an exported helper rather than duplicating). `available` = not ours and not already bid on by us for the same terms, as today.
- Exclusions: our players, players on loan at their club, announced retirements, and players the reach excludes. Squads come from `squad(w, club)` (cached per year); memoize the list per (world revision, year, day, settings signature, pool size) in a module WeakMap so the daily view (`host.ts` `transfers: transferOffers(w)`) and the 단장 stay cheap.
- `isForeignCandidate` becomes "his club's country (or a free agent's nationality) differs from ours", used for adaptation (PLAYER-11).
- `transfers.ts`: replace the `transferOffers(w, bid.year, true)` lookups with `worldMarketOffer`-style resolution by player id (club squads, then pool); delete the `legacy` parameter.
- STAFF-11's chief scout effect moves from candidate count/potential to `scoutReach`; drop `scoutCandidates`/`scoutPotential` from `staffEffects` and their consumers; the spotter trait feeds `scoutPotentialFirst` for players aged `SCOUT_CONFIG.youngAge` or under.
- Old saves: pending bids on `market:` ids lapse on load-free first read (no migration step needed beyond the lapse rule); shortlist entries with missing players drop off as CLUB-33 already does.
