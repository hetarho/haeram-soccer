## goal
Under revision 11 every action the aggregate path credits to two men — a ball won and the man who lost it (tackle, interception, a loose ball recovered in the high-turnover zone), an offside's passer and the man caught, an unbooked foul's man fouled and its fouler — is drawn as a pair of men on the pitch together in its period, the trace names each pair, and an own match's detail lays the rows out as drawn, so no record of the scripted model credits a man with a ball won from someone he never played beside and no detail has to trade who lost the ball to whom.

## impl notes
- Revision 11 only (`attributePlaced`, `AggregatePlan.placed`): the unplaced path, its trace and the pinned revision-10 digests (`players.test.ts` `revision 10 attribution is untouched by the place rule`, `full-model-throughput.test.ts`, `grid-throughput.test.ts`) do not move. Revision 11 is unreleased, so its draws may change freely; team facts never change (attribution only splits them).
- `sharedMinutes(participation, sideA, lineA, sideB, lineB, period)` in `aggregate/participation.ts`: the record minutes `m` of the period (`AGGREGATE_CONFIG.periods[period]`, `from < m ≤ to`) in which each of the two lines holds a stint with `s.from < m ≤ s.to` — detail.ts `onIn`'s rule, so the aggregate and the detail agree minute for minute. Cache it per side, line pair and period inside `attributePlaced`.
- Paired weights, in `SideLines` beside `placedWeights`: `pairedWeights(period, role, cell, inPossession, first)` sums, over the second man's exposures, `exposureShares[at] × term(slot, cell, inPossession) × (minutes that exposure shares with the first man) / e.minutes` — `share` is linear in minutes, so this is his base weight with his minutes replaced by the minutes the two share (→MATCH-16); `first` is `{ participation side, line }`. The unplaced form (no place term) serves the loose-ball recoverer. When every weight is 0, fall back to the men on the pitch with the first by those shared minutes, as `weights` falls back to minutes.
- Balls won: for each won group, its losers' expected share is the group's attempt weights (the fitted `attemptsOf[g].u`, else the taker weights): `n[g,p] = won[g] × a[p] / Σa`. `pairGroups` turns each `wonOf[g]` into one `PlacedGroup` per loser line `p` with `n[g,p] > 0`: class as the group's, `w` the unplaced winner weights (so `fitPlaced`'s expected totals stay T824's), `u` the paired weights given `p`. `fitPlaced` runs unchanged on these sub-groups (fractional `n` is fine). Draws: a non-pass group's losers are its failed attempts; a pass group's interception losers are drawn among its failed attempts left after the offside passers (`within`); each loser's winner by `weightedIndex` on the sub-group `(g, loser)`'s fitted `u`; `creditWon` as today.
- Loose balls (pass groups whose `to` is in the high-turnover zone, `looseBalls`): each recovered ball's passer is drawn among the group's failed attempts left after offsides and interceptions, its recoverer by the unplaced paired weights given him, still drawn after the fouls as today; outside the zone nothing changes (no recoverer is named).
- Offsides: `drawExcept` on `pairedWeights(period, ROLES.offside, move.to, true, passer)` (the passer's own side). Fouls: a booked foul keeps the carded fouler and the fouled man picked at the card's minute (already together); each unbooked foul draws its fouled man by today's placed weights, then its fouler by the opponent's paired weights given him — unit by unit instead of two independent multinomials. Offsides and fouls stay unfitted, as in T824.
- Trace (`aggregate/types.ts`): `AggregateTraceMove.wonPairs?: [loser, winner][]` and `recoveredPairs?: [passer, recoverer][]`, `AggregateTraceFouls.pairs?: [fouled, fouler][]` (unbooked fouls), each in draw order, absent on revisions up to 10; `won`, `recovered`, `fouled` and `fouler` stay and agree with them.
- Detail (`script/detail.ts`, `script/build.ts`): `expandGroup` assigns results and named men from the pairs when present (order in acceptance 3), keeping today's line-order expansion for a trace without them; the unbooked fouls' expansion moves from build.ts into `expandFouls(t)` in detail.ts (pairs when present, else today's ascending zip); `detailIssues` adds `<at>: side a line x and side b line y never share a minute of period p` for an event row with a named man and an unbooked, non-penalty foul row. `settlePairs`, its call in build.ts and its export go: a row whose men never met is now the attribution's bug (named by `detailIssues`, fixed here with a unit test naming the case), never a trade. `settlePenaltyFouls` stays (a booked penalty foul's card minute is a separate matter); a trade it makes keeps both rows' men together in the period.
- Revision-10 traces (the exporter's `s01`–`s10`, 1964, no substitutes) carry no pairs and expand as before; with no substitutes every pair already met, so removing `settlePairs` changes none of them.
- `T827` resumes after this task at its acceptance 2 (`script/sample.test.ts` un-skipped); its committed writer, validator and count are not touched here beyond the `settlePairs` case in `incidents.test.ts`.
- Not in this task: shots, key passers, cards, set-piece takers, penalties' kick minutes, the throughput of the paired fitting (T837 reads it).
