## goal
A coach's, chief scout's or 단장's career is recorded only when he moves, and opening it shows each tenure's league finishes read from the archived standings.

## acceptance
- [ ] Employment records open and close only on a move: hired or released by us, joining or leaving a free pool, a STAFF-43 placement; a derived holder gets a person record the first time he moves, his first job starting in the season his holder count started.
- [ ] `personCareer` (runtime read) adds, per coaching/management/director employment, the league finish of every season he held it (club, season, division, place) computed from `w.history` standings with `archivedFinish` at read time; nothing new is stored in the world or the save.
- [ ] `PersonCareer.tsx` lists each tenure with its seasons' finishes; seasons outside the archive's retention show as unavailable rather than guessed.
- [ ] `apps/web/src/runtime/personCareer.test.ts` covers a moved coach's tenures and finishes, a derived holder with no record, and a tenure crossing the archive horizon; existing person-career tests pass.
- [ ] typecheck, lint, format:check and the changed/affected unit tests pass.
