## goal
A scripts-only harness lives worlds for short spans (about five seasons) on the results-only path, many seeds in parallel, and reports transfer counts by type, window, reason and tier flow plus each division's strength, so the market and level work can be tuned by measurement.

## acceptance
- [ ] `pnpm exec tsx scripts/market-seasons.ts --start fresh:1951 --burn-in 2 --seasons 2 --seeds 0..1 --jobs 2 --out <scratch>/smoke.json` completes and writes JSON holding, per run and season: paid/free/loan/loan-return move counts, per-club in and out percentiles (p50, p90, max, share with none), season-start XI players lost by fee, a from-tier × to-tier flow matrix per country plus a cross-country count, lower-division top-3 players moving up, releases vs renewals, retirements, youth intake count and mean potential per tier, per-tier mean club strength (LEAGUE-10's stored strength) and its maximum, mean academy level per tier, and books sums per tier (wages, income, cash). A merge (`--merge a.json b.json`) gives mean and spread per season.
- [ ] Starts: `fresh:<year>` (historyWorld) refuses a burn-in under 2 and reports burn-in seasons separately; `lived:<year>` builds through liveHistory from 1930 once per seed and stores a gzipped snapshot via `scripts/lib/start-worlds.ts` under `.tmp/market-seasons/` keyed by measurementIdentity, seed and year with a sha256 check and an entry cap; `file:<path>` loads a snapshot. A round-trip unit test proves the stored world equals the canonical one.
- [ ] `--config <json>` merges overrides into exported tunables (the pattern of scripts/persisted-world-read.ts:47-51 and scripts/calibrate-transfers.ts:66-71) and echoes the applied values; `--lucks K` reruns each seed with RESULTS_LUCK.season set per season (results-only.ts:65, precedent scripts/era-wage-spread.ts:284); `--jobs N` runs child processes that each load the engine once. Unit tests cover the pure aggregation on synthetic moves (`scripts/lib/market-seasons.test.ts`).
- [ ] Task-tier checks pass through `vibe check --tier task`; no engine source changes.

- Observers: PLAYER_MOVE_FLOW.observe (npc-market.ts:275-282, called before the move so tiers, XI and wages are readable), FREE_AGENT_FLOW.observe (free-agents.ts:34), BOOK_COMPETITIONS.observe (books.ts:47). If the observer receives an optional reason argument (added by T945), count by it; otherwise infer and label reasons as inferred (seller: sell-down, surplus, blocked, expiring; buyer: short line, refill, upgrade) using exported functions only.
- Per season: `w.history.at(-1).standings`, tier change across the close for promotion, `club.books.seasons[0]`, mean `club.strength` and XI by country and tier (pattern persisted-world-read.ts:236-253), academy levels from the club's level fields.

- Wrap long runs in `caffeinate -ims` when run by hand; default `--jobs` = 7.
