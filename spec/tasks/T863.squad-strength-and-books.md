## goal
A club nobody owns is as strong as its squad and pays it from its books: scripted rise/fall goes, wage bills are contracts, spent cash moves its academy/ground/coaching levels, contracts are renewed or released into the free agents, and free agents are signed for need.

## acceptance
- [ ] Strength (→LEAGUE-10): a playable club nobody owns's `strength` is `xiRating` of its XI (the mean 실효 OVR at its slots, before 조직력 and fatigue), written by `clubStrength(w, club)` in `world.ts` whenever its XI is chosen (→WORLD-16) and after a growth settlement; every reader of `club.strength` sees that value.
- [ ] `evolveClubs` no longer touches a playable club: no convergence, success, means-level selling, project, takeover, crisis or summer luck. `MEANS_LEVEL`, `meansLevel`, `squadCostAt` and their uses in tests and scripts are deleted. A representative club keeps its scripted strength as it is (→LEAGUE-10), so `Club.project` stays for it alone.
- [ ] The project inbox report, which only a club of the owner's country could raise (`announceProject`; `copy.dynamics.d1735c6a1e`, `copy.dynamics.f3616ce2a8`), is removed with its entries in `packages/contracts/src/source-messages.ts` and every registered locale pack, or the entries stay only if `pnpm run validate:i18n` requires otherwise; `CareerClubDetail.tsx` shows its project line for a representative club only.
- [ ] LEAGUE-12: the takeover/project 0.15 cohesion term is gone; promotion/relegation 0.10 stays.
- [ ] ECON-36 books: the wage bill is the sum of the squad's contract wages at the close; transfer fees paid/received enter the books (fees arrive in T864); cash spent on the club at the close raises `levels` by `CLUB_LEVELS.investment` and levels decay by `CLUB_LEVELS.decay` a season without spending; no project/takeover debt clearing remains.
- [ ] Contracts (→WORLD-16), in the summer routine's place: a club nobody owns renews an expiring player it still needs — in its best XI, or his line or the squad would fall below its minimum without him (→CLUB-5) — under its wage limit, at the CLUB-25/48 wage of its division for the new season, for 1–5 seasons by age; a retiring player needs no renewal; the others join the free agents (→CLUB-49). The old "two least able aged 21+" rule (`lettingGo`), the 20% refresh join (`joinChance`) and the world's first-day pool (`seedFreeAgents`) are gone.
- [ ] Wage limit (→WORLD-17): a signing or renewal that would take the wage bill above last closed season's income less operating expenses is refused; before a club's first close the limit reads its opening books' season income. Youth intake is never refused by it. A club that keeps no books (`keepsBooks` false, or a world whose clubs hold none) has no limit and no level change.
- [ ] Currency (→WORLD-17, ECON-6): at a country's currency change every stored wage of its clubs' squads converts as `settleNpcBooks` carries cash, in both closes.
- [ ] Free agents: at each refresh (every 4 league rounds) and in the close's order after contracts, a club below `NPC_SQUAD.size` or below a line minimum signs the ablest affordable free agent of the line it lacks, the higher-stature club first when two want him (→WORLD-16); the pool's cap of 200 applies after the close's signings; one unsigned through a whole season still retires.
- [ ] `persisted-world.integration.test.ts` (T862) extended: lived 1930 → 1960; no club signs or renews above its limit; the pool holds at most 200 after every close; a club's strength changes only on a day its players or their attributes changed.
