## goal
A club nobody owns is as strong as its squad and pays it from its books: scripted rise/fall goes, wage bills are contracts, spent cash moves its academy/ground/coaching levels, contracts are renewed or released into the free agents, and free agents are signed for need.

## acceptance
- [ ] `evolveClubs` no longer converges strengths, applies success, means-level selling, projects, takeovers, crises or summer luck; a playable club nobody owns's `strength` is the round mean main OVR of its matchday XI, refreshed at the close and after each window/signing (`clubStrength(w, club)` in `world.ts`), and every reader of `club.strength` sees that value. Representative clubs keep their existing strength rules.
- [ ] LEAGUE-12: the takeover/project 0.15 cohesion term is gone; promotion/relegation 0.10 stays.
- [ ] ECON-36 books: the wage bill is the sum of the squad's contract wages over the season; transfer fees paid/received enter the books (fees arrive in T864); cash spent on the club at the close raises `levels` by `CLUB_LEVELS.investment` and levels decay by `CLUB_LEVELS.decay` a season without spending; no project/takeover debt clearing remains.
- [ ] Contracts (WORLD-16): at the close a club nobody owns renews an expiring player it still needs (in its best XI or below its line minimum without him, under its wage limit), at the CLUB-25/48 wage for 1–5 seasons by age; others join the free agents (CLUB-49); the old "two least able aged 21+" rule and the 20% refresh join are gone.
- [ ] Wage limit (WORLD-17): a signing or renewal that would take the wage bill above last closed season's income less operating expenses is refused; clubs without a closed season use their founding books.
- [ ] Free agents: at each refresh (every 4 league rounds and the close) a club below `NPC_SQUAD.size` or below a line minimum signs the ablest affordable free agent of the line it lacks, the higher-stature club first when two want him (WORLD-16); the pool cap of 200 stays.
- [ ] Tests: `packages/engine/src/squad-strength.test.ts` — strength follows a signing and a retirement; no strength change without a squad change; `books.test.ts` cases for the contract wage bill and level investment/decay; `free-agents.test.ts` cases for release on expiry, need-based signing and stature preference. Affected tests and goldens updated.
- [ ] `pnpm run typecheck`, `pnpm run lint`, `pnpm run format:check` and the changed unit tests pass.

## impl notes
- `CLUB_LEVELS.investment` and `.decay` are named configs defined here; T866 tunes them with the LEAGUE-10 spread.
- The means level and the scripted rise/fall code are deleted, not flagged off (owner chose a direct switch, 261113).
- Stature affects income only through the existing gate/commercial terms (ECON-8/9) and the preference tie-break; never add it to strength.
- Keep CLUB-48's star pay spread for every wage.
