Keep the existing career, books and solvency gates. Run `vibe check --tier task` once after the edits and before the commit.
- [ ] exit: At 45 minutes, record completed measurements and measured gaps in ECON-29, and record missing cases and reasons in result limits. An out-of-band figure is a finding. Leave any unverified or larger correction as a follow-up with its evidence and expected football mechanism.
