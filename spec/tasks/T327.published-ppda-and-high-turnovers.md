## goal
Make PPDA and high turnovers comparable with the figures fans read elsewhere, on both paths, with sourced MATCH-30 bands.

## acceptance
- [v] Both paths count a high turnover as a sequence that starts with an open-play ball won within 40 m of the opponent's goal, and PPDA as the opponent's pass attempts in its own 60% of the pitch divided by the side's tackles, interceptions, challenges and fouls there (MATCH-13 r18), through T322's shared `COUNTER_ZONES` rule in `grid/record.ts` read by the aggregate ledger.
- [v] Both paths report possession as a side's share of both sides' attempted passes (MATCH-3 r19) instead of time on the ball; MATCH-29's possession ordering and every view reading possession get the new share unchanged in form, and `possession.share` in the grid tally and the aggregate ledger agree on the same synthetic sequence (`reads possession as the share of attempted passes`).
- [ ] `docs/MATCH-BASELINES.md` gains both counters' bands from published per-team-per-match figures under these conventions (Opta high turnovers; Opta/StatsBomb/Understat PPDA), with sources, leagues and seasons; `ADVANCED_COUNTER_TARGET` moves them from unbanded to banded.
- [v] Tests: `counts a high turnover from an open-play win within 40 m` and `divides PPDA by every defensive action in the opponent's 60%` in `grid/record.test.ts`, and the same synthetic actions through both paths in `aggregate/engine.test.ts`; `full-model-throughput.test.ts`'s digests re-recorded with a comment that the counting rule changed them; pre-feature golden facts unchanged.

## result
- outcome: (1) Counting (MATCH-13 r18) through `COUNTER_ZONES` in grid/record.ts, read by the aggregate ledger and attribution. High turnovers are every open-play ball won from band 5 on: the grid's `regain` (tackle, interception, loose ball, keeper's claim; restarts never reach it, rule unchanged) and on the aggregate its opponent's tackled carries and take-ons, intercepted passes and now loose balls (`looseBalls` in ledger.ts: a failed pass neither offside, intercepted nor out, read as the opponent's recovery; players.ts credits their winners with draws appended after every other split of the period, so all other attribution keeps its draws). PPDA's denominator (`pressActions`) is tackles, interceptions, challenges lost (an opponent's won take-on where it started: grid `dribble(side, idx, from)`, aggregate completed `takeOn`) and fouls (grid `foul(..., at)`, aggregate the opponent's `fouled` cells) from band 3 on; the numerator stays the opponent's passes from below band 5. MATCH-14 has no edge at 40 m from goal (65 m) or at 60% (42/63 m), so both paths read the nearest band edge (70 m, 35 m from goal; the two-thirds edge), documented on `COUNTER_ZONES` and in MATCH-BASELINES. The pre-feature tally keeps tackles and interceptions only and its time-based possession (golden facts unchanged). (2) Possession (MATCH-3 r19): `possessionShare` (record.ts) gives the home part of 90 from both sides' attempted passes on the candidate grid tally and in `deriveTeamFacts`; `Tally.running()` hands playback the pass counts on the candidate model, so frame possession is the same share with grid/playback.ts untouched; every view keeps its form. (3) Bands: none — no published per-team-per-match figure under the new conventions is in the repo (Opta's about 15 high turnovers a match for both sides has no league or season; PPDA is held only as press-style ranges 7–10/11–14/15–20 with estimated ends; T322's SB16 figures are under the old definitions). Both stay unbanded in `ADVANCED_COUNTER_TARGET` (now v2) with the reason "published figure not in the repo; fetch in a supervised session"; MATCH-BASELINES' unbanded rows and rule list say the same. (4) Outcomes unchanged: a scratch digest of 40 aggregate records' scores, xG, incidents, passes and shots, and of two candidate rounds' scores, own-match outcomes and discipline, was identical before and after; `full-model-throughput.test.ts` digests re-recorded (aggregateRecords a3c1c7230ca5bc28, candidateRounds 2955d6675157a6aa). Smoke levels only, no evidence: aggregate core 300 matches high turnovers 13.6 a team, PPDA 13.1 (T322 tree 4.1 / 26.5); detailed core 60 matches 5.8 / 18.3 (6.2 / 38).
