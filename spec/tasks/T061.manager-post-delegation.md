## goal
With the manager post delegated the staff appoint and renew by the owner's rule without stopping the clock, and the manager market opens from the manager's 교체 like a coach's.

## acceptance
- [ ] On a vacancy (resignation, retirement, an unrenewed contract, the firefighter leaving) with `delegation.manager`, the staff pick the best affordable free agent by `managerRule`:
  - school: the departing manager's school, else best overall;
  - best: by OVR;
  - strategy: the school the aimed synergy needs, else best overall.
  They hire for 2 seasons, report it, and the clock runs on (→STAFF-7).
- [ ] Affordable means cash after the fee still covers 13 rounds of costs with his wage.
- [ ] Delegated renewal: an expiring manager is renewed for 2 seasons unless an affordable free agent ranks 3+ OVR above him under the rule.
- [ ] Poaching stays an owner decision.
- [ ] The 위임 tab shows the 감독 선임 toggle and the three rules, defaulting to 같은 학파 우선.
- [ ] STAFF_TABS drop 감독 후보. The manager card's 교체 opens the market list as a coach's 교체 does; `?view=candidates` opens 감독 with the market open, and the departure alert opens it too.
- [ ] Tests:
  - staff.test.ts: each rule picks the expected candidate on a seeded market, and an unaffordable best is skipped;
  - managerMarket.test.ts: a delegated renewal is taken against a stronger free agent;
  - staff.spec.ts (Chromium): 교체 opens the list, and a resignation under delegation does not stop the clock.
- [ ] `pnpm run typecheck && pnpm run test:unit` pass.
