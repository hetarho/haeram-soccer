# T061 Delegated manager appointment and the manager market behind 교체
> st:todo | ssot:STAFF-18 STAFF-7 STAFF-17 WEB-40 WEB-45 | base:STAFF@12 WEB@51 | dep:T060 | touches:packages/engine/src/staff.ts packages/engine/src/managerMarket.ts packages/engine/src/ops-staff.ts packages/engine/src/staff.test.ts packages/engine/src/managerMarket.test.ts apps/web/src/ui/ClubStaff.tsx apps/web/src/ui/state.ts tests/e2e/staff.spec.ts | lane:staff

## goal
With the manager post delegated the staff appoint and renew by the owner's rule without stopping the clock, and the manager market opens from the manager's 교체 like a coach's.

## acceptance
- [v] On a vacancy (resignation, retirement, an unrenewed contract, the firefighter leaving) with `delegation.manager`, the staff pick the best affordable free agent by `managerRule`:
  - school: the departing manager's school, else best overall;
  - best: by OVR;
  - strategy: the school the aimed synergy needs, else best overall.
  They hire for 2 seasons, report it, and the clock runs on (→STAFF-7).
- [v] Affordable means cash after the fee still covers 13 rounds of costs with his wage.
- [v] Delegated renewal: an expiring manager is renewed for 2 seasons unless an affordable free agent ranks 3+ OVR above him under the rule.
- [v] Poaching stays an owner decision.
- [v] The 위임 tab shows the 감독 선임 toggle and the three rules, defaulting to 같은 학파 우선.
- [v] STAFF_TABS drop 감독 후보. The manager card's 교체 opens the market list as a coach's 교체 does; `?view=candidates` opens 감독 with the market open, and the departure alert opens it too.
- [v] Tests:
  - staff.test.ts: each rule picks the expected candidate on a seeded market, and an unaffordable best is skipped;
  - managerMarket.test.ts: a delegated renewal is taken against a stronger free agent;
  - staff.spec.ts (Chromium): 교체 opens the list, and a resignation under delegation does not stop the clock.
- [v] `pnpm run typecheck && pnpm run test:unit` pass.

## result
- outcome: Every vacancy goes through `resign` (ops-staff.ts): a resignation, retirement at 70, an unrenewed contract or a firefighter whose season ends. With `delegation.manager` set, the staff now appoint at once through `appointDelegatedManager`. `delegatedManagerPick` (managerMarket.ts) takes only free agents the club can afford (`affordable`, now exported from staff.ts: cash after the signing fee still covers 13 rounds of costs with his wage in place of the current one). It sorts them by OVR and takes the first of the schools the rule asks for, else the best overall. The rules: `school` is the departing manager's school; `best` asks for no school; `strategy` is the schools of `aimedSynergy(w)` in synergy.ts. The staff never pick another club's manager. The appointee signs for 2 seasons (`DELEGATED_TERM`), 1 for a firefighter, never past his 70th year. The staff pay his fee, put out a `manager-hire` event (… · 스태프 위임 선임) and a `staff-report` inbox item, and set no `w.critical`, so the clock keeps running. When nobody is affordable, the interim stays without a stop, an attention `staff-report` names the vacancy, and `dailyStaff` appoints as soon as someone is affordable. That step also fills the post when an interim is in charge at the moment delegation is switched on. The owner's `hire` and the staff now share `installManager`. On 1 April a delegated expiring manager is let go if the rule's pick is ≥ `MANAGER_RENEWAL_MARGIN` (3) OVR better: a non-attention report with `ref` goes out, and he leaves at the close, where the staff appoint. Otherwise he is renewed for 2 seasons as in T060. `manager-rule` is implemented: 'school' clears `managerRule`, and unknown rules are refused. `MANAGER_RULES`/`MANAGER_RULE_INFO` hold the labels 같은 학파 우선, 능력 최고, 운영 전략 우선. UI: `STAFF_TABS` is now 감독 · 코치진 · 위임, and the new `StaffRequest` type adds `candidates` for links from elsewhere. The manager card has 교체 (감독 선임 under an interim), which opens the market as a wide dialog (감독 교체 / 감독 선임) with free agents, then other clubs' managers. Hiring confirms on top of it and closes both. `?view=candidates`, the departure alert and the candidate remedies all open 감독 with the market. The 위임 tab adds the 감독 선임 toggle and a three-step rule dial (default 같은 학파 우선). Under the dial a line says whom the staff would appoint today and, under 운영 전략 우선, which synergy and school they aim at.
