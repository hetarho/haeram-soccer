# T525 Reads wait for the computed days at the pace
> st:todo | ssot:WEB-18 | base:WEB@163 | dep:T524 | touches:apps/web/src/runtime/progression.ts apps/web/src/runtime/progression.runahead.test.ts | lane:- | explore:-

## goal
A read during automatic play no longer makes the calendar jump: instead of showing the computed days at once, it waits until they have been shown at the pace, so no view shows a result early and the shown calendar still never skips a day.

## acceptance
- [ ] With the shown clock running and days computed ahead, a read (`ProgressionController.beforeRead`, T524's gate) computes nothing ahead from its start and is posted once every held day has been shown at the pace and no step is in flight; no held day is revealed early for it, so every shown day stays one pace interval after the previous one.
- [ ] During the owner's draining stop a read waits the same way; the stop ends, unlocks and saves when the last held day is shown, as without a read (→WEB-97, SAVE-8). Office entry and hiding still show the held days at once, which also releases a waiting read.
- [ ] Computing ahead resumes when the read is answered or fails; stopped, hidden or manual play and a running clock with nothing held or in flight post the read at once as before; dispose releases a waiting read.
- [ ] `progression.runahead.test.ts` (unit, fake worker): T524's cases now assert that a read with days held is posted only after they are shown one interval apart (no two shown in one turn), that nothing is posted ahead meanwhile, that a read during an in-flight step waits for it and the held days, that a read during a draining stop leaves the stop's timing unchanged, and that office entry releases a waiting read at once. The other run-ahead and `progression.test.ts` cases pass.
- [ ] typecheck, lint/format on touched files and `pnpm exec vitest run --project unit apps/web/src/runtime/progression.test.ts apps/web/src/runtime/progression.runahead.test.ts` pass; `tests/e2e/records.spec.ts` (manager history refresh during x1 play, which failed on T524 with the date jumping from 8월 1일 to 8월 5일) listed in result limits for the batch.

## impl notes
- T524's gate (`beforeRead` → `{ ready, settled }`, `reads` counter that blocks `compute()`) stays; only `ready` changes from `showAhead()` to a promise that resolves when the held list is empty and nothing is computing, checked wherever a day is revealed, a step lands, the clock flushes or the controller is disposed.
- A records view re-reads on a new `managerHistoryKey` (a settled match, a closed season, an appointment, a cup final), so while one is open during play the run-ahead depth shrinks around each read; that is the accepted cost.
