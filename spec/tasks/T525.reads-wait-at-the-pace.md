# T525 Reads wait for the computed days at the pace
> st:todo | ssot:WEB-18 | base:WEB@163 | dep:T524 | touches:apps/web/src/runtime/progression.ts apps/web/src/runtime/progression.runahead.test.ts | lane:- | explore:-

## goal
A read during automatic play no longer makes the calendar jump: instead of showing the computed days at once, it waits until they have been shown at the pace, so no view shows a result early and the shown calendar still never skips a day.

## acceptance
- [v] With the shown clock running and days computed ahead, a read (`ProgressionController.beforeRead`, T524's gate) computes nothing ahead from its start and is posted once every held day has been shown at the pace and no step is in flight; no held day is revealed early for it, so every shown day stays one pace interval after the previous one.
- [v] During the owner's draining stop a read waits the same way; the stop ends, unlocks and saves when the last held day is shown, as without a read (→WEB-97, SAVE-8). Office entry and hiding still show the held days at once, which also releases a waiting read.
- [v] Computing ahead resumes when the read is answered or fails; stopped, hidden or manual play and a running clock with nothing held or in flight post the read at once as before; dispose releases a waiting read.
- [v] `progression.runahead.test.ts` (unit, fake worker): T524's cases now assert that a read with days held is posted only after they are shown one interval apart (no two shown in one turn), that nothing is posted ahead meanwhile, that a read during an in-flight step waits for it and the held days, that a read during a draining stop leaves the stop's timing unchanged, and that office entry releases a waiting read at once. The other run-ahead and `progression.test.ts` cases pass.
- [v] typecheck, lint/format on touched files and `pnpm exec vitest run --project unit apps/web/src/runtime/progression.test.ts apps/web/src/runtime/progression.runahead.test.ts` pass; `tests/e2e/records.spec.ts` (manager history refresh during x1 play, which failed on T524 with the date jumping from 8월 1일 to 8월 5일) listed in result limits for the batch.

## impl notes
- T524's gate (`beforeRead` → `{ ready, settled }`, `reads` counter that blocks `compute()`) stays; only `ready` changes from `showAhead()` to a promise that resolves when the held list is empty and nothing is computing, checked wherever a day is revealed, a step lands, the clock flushes or the controller is disposed.
- A records view re-reads on a new `managerHistoryKey` (a settled match, a closed season, an appointment, a cup final), so while one is open during play the run-ahead depth shrinks around each read; that is the accepted cost.

## result
- outcome: `beforeRead` keeps T524's `{ ready, settled }` gate and its `reads` counter, which blocks `compute()` from the read's start. If nothing is held and no step is in flight, the read is posted at once. Otherwise `ready` is a promise in `waitingReads`. `postReads()` resolves it once `ahead` is empty and `inFlight` is false. It is checked at the end of every `reveal()` (the paced reveal on a beat, the office, hiding, blocker and stop flushes, and a reveal the client refused), after every step lands in `send()` (which covers a refused, cancelled or failed step that brought no day), and on `dispose()`, which posts whatever still waits. `showAhead()`, the `computing` promise and T524's `announce` parameter on `reveal` are removed, and `shown()` takes `paced` again. Reads no longer show days at once, so our result among the held days is announced when its day is shown at the pace, as without a read. Fake-worker cases (x10, 20 ms steps, days 1–3 shown and 4–10 held at 0.35 s): the read goes at 1.0 s with the last held day, showing 10, held 0, and all nine gaps are 100 ms. With a step in flight at 0.31 s it lands, is held, and the read goes at 1.0 s. With only a slow 250 ms step in flight, the read goes when that day is shown at 0.75 s. The owner's draining stop with and without a read gives the same shown times (+50…+650 ms), unlock and single save at +650 ms; the read goes at +650 ms. Office entry and hiding at 0.55 s release the read at once (showing 10). Dispose releases it. At x1 with a round-keyed view, both round reads go from days 10 and 17 with nothing held, and 19 days are shown exactly 1 s apart (T524 showed 3 days early per round). A running clock with nothing held or in flight (depth 0, x1) posts at once, and computing waits for the answer. The nine read types each wait 650 ms for the held days
