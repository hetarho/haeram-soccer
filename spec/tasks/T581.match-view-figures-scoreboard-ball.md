## goal
In the game's match view (and so in the lab), figures are drawn at 85% of today's size, the ball carrier is the same size as everyone else, the scoreboard halves show each side's shirt colour, and the ball looks like a football; the lab's watched matches wear their real kits.

## acceptance
- [ ] Figure scale (MATCH-36): the torso is `FIGURE_SCALE.torso` (0.85) of the marker diameter at every width; `markerRadius` itself, number-tag size, ring geometry and every other user of the marker (tactical/analytical diagrams) are unchanged. figurePainting.test.ts "torso is 85% of the marker diameter" (compare the painted body bounds at a fixed radius before/after through the painter's scale input).
- [ ] Carrier never grows (WEB-95): the carrier's body is painted with no outline growth (`outlineWidth` 0 for every figure, or the outline drawn inside the silhouette), and his number plate uses the same scale as everyone's (`CARRIER_MARK.tagScale` removed / 1) — only the plate colour and the ring under him mark him. figurePainting.test.ts "carrier body bounds equal a non-carrier's in the same pose"; numberTags.test.ts "carrier tag same size". A header's jump scale (pose.jump, →MATCH-37) is a recorded action, not a carrier cue, and stays.
- [ ] Scoreboard halves (WEB-50/49): the scoreboard's left half behind the home side and right half behind the away side fill with that side's selected shirt base colour from `matchLook(record).sides[side].kit` (the unrecorded stand-in look's colours when unrecorded), each half's team text and crest area in `inkOn(colour)`; the centred score keeps its own plate; height, possession slots and phone layout unchanged. A pure helper `scoreboardHalves(look)` in apps/web/src/ui/scoreboardHalves.ts returns `[{ background, ink }, { background, ink }]`; scoreboardHalves.test.ts covers recorded kits, the unrecorded look and a light and a dark shirt's ink (contrast ≥ 4.5:1).
- [ ] Ball (MATCH-36): `paintPitchBall` draws a white ball with dark classic panels whose rotation follows the ball's travel (an angle accumulated from its track distance, passed in by Pitch), a thin dark edge, and the existing height-based shadow and size; the pixel skin draws the same idea on its pixel grid. The ground ball stays smaller than a torso (radius < 0.85 × marker radius at every width). pitchPainting.test.ts "ball draws panels and an edge", "pixel ball stays pixel", "ground ball smaller than a torso".
- [ ] Lab kits (LAB-9): `watchLabMatch` sets `record.appearance = appearanceForFixture(lab.w, fixture)` so the lab's match wears the host identities' selected kits and shows no 유니폼 미기록 tag; observing still changes no football (sides.test.ts "watched match equals the unobserved one" compares the record without `appearance`, and a new case checks the appearance is the fixture's).
- [ ] tests/e2e/match-themes.spec.ts asserts the scoreboard halves' backgrounds differ and match each side's kit colour in every theme; tests/e2e/lab.spec.ts asserts no 유니폼 미기록 tag after 관전. Browser execution belongs to the batch gate.
- [ ] typecheck, lint, format:check, build and the affected unit tests pass; check by eye at 1920×1080 and 390×844 (screenshots of game match view and lab 관전) and note what was seen in result.

## impl notes
- Today's carrier cue: `CARRIER_MARK = { tagScale: 1.3, outlineWidth: 1.5, ringScale: 1.45, ringGap: 3 }` in Pitch.tsx; the painter grows the silhouette by `outlineWidth / scale` (figurePainting.ts ~594). Keep the ring (`ringScale`, `ringGap`) and the coloured `carrierTagStyle`; drop the outline growth and the tag scale.
- Put `FIGURE_SCALE` beside `PAINT` in figurePainting.ts and apply it where the body scale is computed (`2 * options.radius` at ~269 and `width` at ~588) so `figurePoint` (ball socket) and drawing agree; number-tag placement keeps using the marker radius.
- The ball's travel angle: accumulate distance along `track` in Pitch's timed samples (memoized per playback) and pass `spin` to `paintPitchBall(ctx, x, y, radius, skin, spin)`; reduced motion keeps a fixed angle. Panels: one central pentagon and five partial pentagons around it, clipped to the circle, `skin.ballSeam` for the panels and an edge of the same; keep `skin.ball` as the leather colour so themes still recolour it.
- Scoreboard: today `.scoreboard` has one background (Pitch.module.css top). Use two absolutely positioned half layers or a `linear-gradient(90deg, home 50%, away 50%)` background with per-half ink via CSS custom properties set inline from `scoreboardHalves`.
- `appearanceForFixture` is in packages/engine/src/match-appearance.ts; the lab fixture is `labFixture(lab, seed, 0)`.
