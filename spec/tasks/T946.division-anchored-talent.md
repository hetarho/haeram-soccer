## result
- outcome: Divisions are anchored at founding and in youth supply. `divisionLevel(tier)` (world.ts) gives 90 − 10t, so 80/70/60/50, and founds every club at that level + stature ± 3, within 15–92. `tierLevel`/`tierStrengthRange` stay as the market's price reading, so CLUB-25/46 are unchanged. Levels follow the division (persisted-squads.ts `levelTargets`/`followLevels`, called from books.ts in place of `investNpcLevels`). The target is `facilityStage`'s ladder + stature. A level moves toward its target by `CLUB_LEVELS.moveAfterChange` (1) a season. Surplus investment accumulates progress that lifts a level at most `CLUB_LEVELS.aboveTarget` (1) above the target and holds it there while investing; without investment, progress decays by `decay`. Academy intakes (npc-careers.ts) centre potential on `divisionLevel` + (academy − the division's ladder academy) × `NPC_YOUTH.divisionCentre` (3). The spread is one uniform draw through the `NPC_YOUTH.skew` quantile table: 2/3 below, 1/3 at or above, 5% at +10. Youngsters beyond the academy count, the standing-size/line refills, centre at level − `NPC_YOUTH.refillBelow` (10). Counts, ages, headroom draw, PRNG keys and draw order are kept: the old one-draw potential became one skew draw. The takeover listing returns `club.strength` for clubs nobody owns. `SQUAD_SPREAD` stays `flat` (see limits).
