# T058 Contract talks with goodwill, floor and delegation
> st:todo | ssot:CLUB-26 ECON-16 STAFF-13 | base:CLUB@17 ECON@14 STAFF@12 | dep:T057 | touches:packages/engine/src/contracts.ts packages/engine/src/ops-squad.ts packages/engine/src/season.ts packages/engine/src/contracts.test.ts packages/engine/src/season.test.ts | lane:players

## goal
Players ask to renew in their final season and for raises when underpaid, accept any offer at or above their floor, and delegated staff answer within the squad-investment ceiling.

## acceptance
- [v] Triggers:
  - 1 January: every player in his contract's final season opens a `renew` talk;
  - any day: a player paid 25%+ below his market wage opens a `raise` talk, once a season;
  - each talk is an inbox item `contract-talk`.
- [v] Demand = market wage × the build's player-wage effects (→ECON-22). Floor = demand × (1 − goodwill), with goodwill:
  - squad investment −5/0/+5/+10/+15% (→T052 policy);
  - 가족 같은 클럽 +5, 스타 대우 +10 for players rated 70+, 철저한 경쟁 −5, 긴축 재정 −5;
  - (morale − 60)/4 %;
  - within −10 to +30%.
- [v] `contract-offer {player, wage, term}` at or above the floor signs a wage fixed for 1–5 seasons and books the change; below the floor it is refused with the floor stated. `contract-decline` closes the talk; T059 handles what follows.
- [v] Delegated (`contracts`): the staff offer the floor for 2 seasons (1 from age 32) when it is within the pay ceiling × market wage and cash after the raise still covers 13 rounds of costs; otherwise they decline, and the report gives the reason.
- [v] The silent 3-season renewal at the close (season.ts, `p.until <= w.year` block) is removed; expiry is handled by T059.
- [v] contracts.test.ts: floors for each goodwill source; a delegated club at squad investment 5 renews a star a level-1 club declines; a refused low offer.
- [v] season.test.ts: no contract renews itself at the close.
- [v] `pnpm run typecheck && pnpm run test:unit` pass.

## result
- outcome: Players now negotiate their contracts (→CLUB-26). The new `contractTalks` in `packages/engine/src/contracts.ts` runs each club day from `clubDay`. On any day, a player paid 75% or less of his market wage opens a `raise` talk once a season (`raiseAsked`). Loan players and announced retirements do not open talks. A talk stores its demand, floor, year and day as named. Demand = `marketWage` × `buildEffects.wage`, and floor = demand × (1 − goodwill). `talkGoodwill` returns each source with its percent: squad investment, 가족 같은 클럽 +5, 스타 대우 +10 for players rated 70+, 철저한 경쟁 −5, 긴축 재정 −5 and (morale − 60)/4, clamped to −10…+30. The parts are there for T059's sheet. Without delegation the talk is an attention `contract-talk` inbox item with the demand, floor and goodwill. With `contracts` delegated, `staffTalkVerdict` decides at once. Otherwise they decline and give the reason, and the talk's item becomes a non-attention report. `contract-offer` at or above the floor sets the wage and `until = year + 1 + term` (the rest of this season plus `term` seasons). An offer below the floor is refused with the floor in the message and the world unchanged. `contract-decline` closes the talk with a `contract-decline` event; the staff's declines go through the same `declineTalk`, where T059 can attach CLUB-27. The silent 3-season renewal at the close is gone. `operatingCosts` now pays wages as signed: the build's player-wage multiplier moved from the bill into the demand, so it is not applied twice and an agreed wage stays fixed (→ECON-4).
