# T058 Contract talks with goodwill, floor and delegation

## goal
Players ask to renew in their final season and for raises when underpaid, accept any offer at or above their floor, and delegated staff answer within the squad-investment ceiling.

## acceptance
- [ ] Triggers:
  - 1 January: every player in his contract's final season opens a `renew` talk;
  - any day: a player paid 25%+ below his market wage opens a `raise` talk, once a season;
  - each talk is an inbox item `contract-talk`.
- [ ] Demand = market wage × the build's player-wage effects (→ECON-22). Floor = demand × (1 − goodwill), with goodwill:
  - squad investment −5/0/+5/+10/+15% (→T052 policy);
  - 가족 같은 클럽 +5, 스타 대우 +10 for players rated 70+, 철저한 경쟁 −5, 긴축 재정 −5;
  - (morale − 60)/4 %;
  - within −10 to +30%.
- [ ] `contract-offer {player, wage, term}` at or above the floor signs a wage fixed for 1–5 seasons and books the change; below the floor it is refused with the floor stated. `contract-decline` closes the talk; T059 handles what follows.
- [ ] Delegated (`contracts`): the staff offer the floor for 2 seasons (1 from age 32) when it is within the pay ceiling × market wage and cash after the raise still covers 13 rounds of costs; otherwise they decline, and the report gives the reason.
- [ ] The silent 3-season renewal at the close (season.ts, `p.until <= w.year` block) is removed; expiry is handled by T059.
- [ ] contracts.test.ts: floors for each goodwill source; a delegated club at squad investment 5 renews a star a level-1 club declines; a refused low offer.
- [ ] season.test.ts: no contract renews itself at the close.
- [ ] `pnpm run typecheck && pnpm run test:unit` pass.
