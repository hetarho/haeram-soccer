## goal
Every contract carries its own promotion and relegation clauses drawn at signing and tilted by the player's agent, applied when the club moves; every player has an agent rated 1–5 that raises his demand.

## acceptance
- [ ] Every player has `agent: 1–5`, drawn when he is created (generated squads, market candidates, academy prospects, free agents) from his own stream, stronger more often for better players; it never changes.
- [ ] Every contract signed (renewal, improved contract, transfer in, loan excluded, free agent, academy promotion) settles `clauses: { promotion?: number; relegation?: number }` (whole percent) from the signing's stream: a promotion rise in 30% (agent 1) to 80% (agent 5) of contracts, +10% to +60%, larger with a stronger agent; a relegation cut in 80% (agent 1) to 40% (agent 5), −25% to −50%, smaller with a stronger agent; values come from a config object T062 may tune within these ranges.
- [ ] At the close, a club that goes up applies each running contract's promotion rise, one that goes down its relegation cut, to the wage from the new season's first round; nothing else changes; the inbox reports the squad's total wage change.
- [ ] The demand (→CLUB-26) is multiplied by 1 + 0.03 × (agent − 1).
- [ ] Activation from rules 1.15 (→SAVE-10) gives every existing player an agent drawn deterministically from his id and leaves existing contracts without clauses until their next signing.
- [ ] The contract talk shows the agent rating and the clauses the new contract would carry (settled before the owner answers, so the answer cannot reroll them); the player sheet shows the agent and the running contract's clauses.
- [ ] contracts.test.ts: clause shares and sizes stay within the ranges and lean with the agent over many draws; a promotion applies rises only to contracts that carry one; a relegation applies cuts; the agent premium; activation assigns agents and no clauses. `pnpm run test:unit`, `pnpm run typecheck`, `pnpm run lint` pass.

## impl notes
- Streams: agent from the player's creation stream (`${w.seed}:agent:${player.id}` for activation); clauses from `${w.seed}:clauses:${player.id}:${year}:${day}` at signing, so a talk's clauses are known when it opens and an offer does not reroll them (store them on the talk).
- Agent weighting: P(agent = k) shifts toward 5 with ability: draw u in [0,1), add (ability − 60)/100 clamped to ±0.3, map to 1–5 evenly.
- Clause odds and sizes interpolate linearly by agent level between the endpoints in the acceptance; sizes draw uniformly within an agent-dependent sub-range (e.g. promotion size from 10 + 5·(agent−1) to 35 + 6.25·(agent−1)).
- These are rules 1.16.0 additions (unreleased): add the fields to the 1.16.0 shapes and the activation from 1.15; portable goldens are regenerated by T062.
- Loans keep the parent club's contract; no clauses on loan deals.
