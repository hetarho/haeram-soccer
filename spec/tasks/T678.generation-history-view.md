## goal
While a later decade's world is generated, the start shows each season reached with the Club Elo world top 10, the champions, the European winners and the 발롱도르, and a self-closing card for the season's greatest feat (→WEB-115).

## acceptance
- [ ] engine `seasonDigest(w)` returns, for the season just closed: the season reached, the Club Elo world top 10 (id, name, country, Elo), each playable association's league champion, the European winners of that season, the 발롱도르 winner (none before 1955/56) and at most one feat, the greatest of the season in WEB-115's order; `packages/engine/src/generation-digest.test.ts` covers the Elo order, each feat kind on hand-built archives (war, treble, European Cup three-peat, third 발롱도르 in a row, back-to-back, first European Cup, league run of five), the greatest-first choice when one season holds several, and no 발롱도르 before 1955/56.
- [ ] every generation progress reply carries that season's digest; `generateHistory`'s callback hears the world with the season; a runtime host test on a 1940 generation (war crossed) checks each progress reply's digest and that the 1946 reply holds the war feat.
- [ ] the start's generation view shows the season, the Elo top 10, the champions, the European winners and the 발롱도르 from the latest digest; a feat card stays `GENERATION_CARD.ms` and closes by itself, one at a time, an unshown card dropped when the next season's digest arrives; after the last season the club list follows once an open card closes; generation never waits for the view. `apps/web/src/ui/generationCards.test.ts` covers the schedule (one at a time, the drop rule, the hand-off to the club list).
- [ ] the view fits 360x740 and 390x844 without document scrolling (lists may scroll inside their region, →WEB-63); `tests/e2e/start.ts` (a 1950 generation) checks the season, the Elo list and a champion on screen while it runs — confirmed by the batch run.
- [ ] every presentation locale pack carries the new strings (`pnpm run validate:presentation`); `pnpm run build`, typecheck, lint, format:check and the affected unit tests pass.

## impl notes
- Code base: the owner takeover (T653–T668) as merged into main. The path today: `liveHistory` (packages/engine/src/history.ts) calls `onProgress(w.year)` once a season; the host's `generate` handler (apps/web/src/runtime/host.ts) turns it into `this.progress({ requestId, ok, progress, season })`; `client.received` copies `season` into `state.start`; `StartFlow.tsx`'s generation view (세계를 생성중…, `data-testid="world-generation"`) shows it.
- Engine: new leaf `packages/engine/src/generation-digest.ts` exporting `seasonDigest(w: World): GenerationDigest`, reading only `w.clubs` (Elo by `eloOf` from elo.ts) and `w.history` (the `SeasonArchive` of the season just closed and the ones before it: `champions[]` league/cup winners per country, `europe[]` winners by competition, `ballonDor[]` podium). No simulation, no new state, nothing saved. `onProgress` becomes `(season: number, w: World) => void` in `generateHistory`/`liveHistory`; existing callers keep reading the season only.
- Contract: `GenerationDigest` and `GenerationFeat` in packages/contracts/src/types.ts with their zod schemas in schema.ts; the progress reply in apps/web/src/runtime/protocol.ts gains an optional `digest`. Clubs travel as id + name + country so the UI needs no world.
- Feat kinds, greatest first: `war` (the digest of the 1946 restart, the first after the 1938/39 close, →WORLD-14; one card for stop and restart), `treble` (one club holds its country's league and cup and the European Cup lineage in the same season), `europe-three` and `europe-two` (the European Cup lineage — European Cup, Champions League from 1992, →EURO-1/9 — won by the same club in consecutive archived seasons), `ballon-three` (the same player first in three consecutive podiums), `europe-first` (the club's first European Cup lineage win in the archives), `league-run` (a country's champion for its fifth consecutive archived season; afterwards only when the run becomes that country's longest in the archives, so a dominant club is not carded every season). Consecutive means consecutive archives, so the war gap does not break a run. Ties within one kind: the first association in WORLD-1's order. Names come from the archive's own `name` fields (era names included).
- Only the European Cup lineage counts for treble and the European feats; the UEFA Cup and Cup Winners' Cup winners appear in the background list only.
- UI: pure schedule in new `apps/web/src/ui/generationCards.ts` (`GENERATION_CARD = { ms: 3000 }` defined there; state = shown card + at most one pending card of the latest season); StartFlow's generation view renders background and card from client state; the card is an overlay with no input (no buttons, `aria-live="polite"`), honouring `prefers-reduced-motion` (no slide, still shown). A 1930 takeover never shows the view's history (no seasons).
- Layout: season and 발롱도르 on top; Elo top 10 and the champions as two compact text lists side by side from 390 px, stacked at 360 px inside a scrolling region; rows are text, not touch targets.
- Strings: card templates and labels in every pack under apps/web/public/presentation/locales (ko-KR first; the other packs in their language as earlier packs do); competition and club names are data, never translated.
- Integration and e2e are edited with the code and run by the coordinator's batch; list them in `- limits:`.
