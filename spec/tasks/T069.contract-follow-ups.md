## goal
Delegated renewals extend past this season and never shorten a contract, founding squads and close refills carry clauses, and the UI names improved-contract talks.

## acceptance
- [ ] Delegated offers run 2 seasons past this one (1 from age 32), never ending before the running contract: summer term 3 (2 from 32), winter term 2 (1 from 32), raised to the running contract's remaining seasons when longer.
- [ ] The founding squad and the players who refill a squad at the close settle clauses (→CLUB-29) from their own streams when created, like other signings.
- [ ] Inbox and stop labels name both kinds: '선수 계약 협상' (progression) and '계약 협상' (event center), and the stop-setting copy says 재계약·계약 개선 협상; the talk sheet and inbox item name a renewal or an improved contract.
- [ ] contracts.test.ts: a delegated summer renewal of a 33-year-old in his final season ends at next season's close; a delegated improved contract with three seasons left never ends earlier than the running one; a new world's founding squad and a close refill carry clauses within CLUB-29's ranges.
- [ ] `pnpm run test:unit`, `pnpm run typecheck`, `pnpm run lint`, `pnpm run format:check` pass; the e2e specs that read these labels pass in Chromium with `BROWSER_TEST_PORT` other than 4173.

## impl notes
- Terms use T067's `TalkTerm` (summer `until = year + term`, winter `until = year + 1 + term` where `year` is the season); the delegated term is computed from the target `until`: max(running `until`, season + 2 (or + 1 from 32)).
- Clause streams for founding and refills follow T068's `${seed}:clauses:${id}:${year}:${day}`; agents are already drawn at creation.
- These change rules 1.16.0 behaviour (unreleased); goldens are regenerated by T062.
