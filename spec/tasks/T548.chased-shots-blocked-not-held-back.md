## goal
Open the match-model revision that ships in version 0.7 (`continuous-aggregate-7`) and, on it, let a forward with nobody ahead of him shoot even when a defender chases him from behind or beside, while any outfielder within a few metres of the shot can close it down — block or deflect it — so goal volume falls through those blocks and through how often such chances arise, never through the forward holding back. Measure every step against the previous revision and MATCH-23/MATCH-30's tolerance and keep upgrading inside the timebox.

- Config, defined here: `DECISION_CONFIG.candidate.revision7 = { clear: { corridor: 10 } }` (the rest of `revision6.clear` reused), `BOX_CONFIG.shot.close = { radius: 6, block: 0.35, skill: 60 }` read on revision 7 only. The explore may tune them, never turn a rule off.
- Start from T542's numbers (600 matches on revision 6: goals 3.71, shots 13.26, xG/shot 0.13, 25/37 rows inside): its probe found about 7.7 chances a team a match with nobody ahead and a chaser behind — the closing block is where those goals are held.
