## result
- outcome: Contracts: `World.managerHistory?: ManagerHistoryFacts` and `MatchRecord.managerTenure?`. T304's two modules are exported from the contracts barrel. The world schema validates the facts with `managerHistoryFactsSchema` and rejects facts of another club and a record naming a tenure the facts lack. `versions.ts` adds `MANAGER_HISTORY_CHECKPOINT_SCHEMA = 4` (supported) and `carriesManagerHistory`. The engine adapter `packages/engine/src/manager-history-capture.ts` holds `ensureManagerHistory`, `captureManagerChange`, `attributeOwnMatch`, `captureCupTitle` and `captureSeasonClose`, built only on T304's `createManagerHistoryFacts`/`recordManagerTenureChange`/`attributeManagerMatch`/`attributeManagerOutcome`. It records nothing for a career without facts, and its dates come from `careerDate`. Hooks: `createWorld` tracks from the founding, whatever the release switch. In `ops-staff.ts`, `installManager` covers owner and delegated hires (the replaced tenure, an interim's included, ends `replaced`), and `resign` takes `resigned` (tactics), `retired` or `expired` and records the interim's tenure at once, so a zero-match interim stays recorded; renewals and tactic requests record nothing and keep the tenure. In `season.ts`, `recordMatch` freezes the tenure on the kept own record, and `closeSeason` credits the final finish in the division and group played and a promotion only on an actual tier move, after `promote` and before `yearlyStaff`'s close departures, both dated the close day, outcome first. `cups.ts` credits the domestic cup (`competition: 'cup'`) and `europe.ts` `crown` every continental title including the Super Cup (its tournament key), as each final is decided. Appointment keys are manager@date#serial, so a same-day reappointment is a new tenure; T304's source keys dedupe outcomes, and a frozen record is never reassigned. Packing: `apps/web/src/adapters/managerHistoryPacking.ts`, called by `pack`/`unpack`, keeps the facts out of the compact world as section `managerHistory` v1: a header `[club, tracked year, day, revision, coverage, serial]`, 14-column tenure rows, 12-column outcome rows (unused columns zero) and record runs `[tenure + 1, length]`. Every string is a shared-dictionary ref and an absent field its documented zero. Reading checks widths, refs, zero columns and runs, then T304's strict validator. A newer section is a compatibility error and anything else malformed is corruption. `checkpointContainerFor` writes container 4 whenever history exists, with or without model data; container 3 holds model data only, and 2 neither. `readCheckpoint` refuses history outside container 4 and container 4 without it. Container 4 never qualifies for the legacy reset. The host's writer load adds `adoptManagerHistory` after the identity and model adoptions. It shares T402's safe boundary (`settledAhead`), is independent of the switch, moves the revision once and keeps the manager's known service year. It records no appointment date and leaves earlier records unattributed. Read-only loads and inspections never track. Views omit `managerHistory`. Smoke run (tsx): two delegated seasons, 62/62 own matches attributed, two league finishes credited, valid world.
