## result
- outcome: clubs nobody owns trade by pull (packages/engine/src/npc-market.ts). Each settlement day every club with a need, in stature order, bids for up to `NPC_MARKET.targetsPerNeed` reachable targets by slot gain. Targets come from an index by country, division and line, sorted from the ablest down. Each list is read from the buyer's wage ceiling (the highest OVR whose wage at the club, at the cheapest age of 14 or 41 with no promise, fits its headroom). The read stops once nobody left can beat both the need's threshold and the kept targets at the slot; a slot rating stands at most `searchSlack` 6 above main OVR, and `targetsPerNeed` × `searchSpare` targets are kept. The fixed 24-entry depth is gone. A player whose club cannot sell now, whom the buyer meets on another day, or whose wage and lowest bid the buyer's envelope cannot hold never takes a target's place. This affordability check is pure and fires no wage-decision observer. Reach is the own country's division and below, a higher division only on loan, other top flights on a seeded day. The bid is the asking fee × a seeded share (0.5–1), or the fee itself for a buyer holding 3× it. The seller answers through CLUB-52 (`npcSaleContext`: relegated-starter and above-division rows) and the player through CLUB-53 (`npcTermsContext`, 100% of his market wage at the buyer). The bid, the seller's draw and the player's draw are keyed `<seed>:npc-bid|npc-sale|npc-terms:<year>:<summer|winter>:<buyer>:<player>`, and a buyer meets each target on one seeded settlement day of the window (`<seed>:npc-bid-day:<year>:<window>:<buyer>:<player>`, among the days the era's caps open to bids, `npcBidDay`), so it bids for a target at most once a window, with no saved state. CLUB-52's contract rows count half before 1995 for every seller, our club included (`saleContext` supplies the year). `NPC_MARKET_ERA.anchors` / `npcMarketCaps` cap bids, buys and sales per club and window; `compareSellDown` orders sell-down; the move observer gets a `MoveReason` and nothing new is stored.
