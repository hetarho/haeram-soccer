## result
- outcome: clubs nobody owns now trade by pull (packages/engine/src/npc-market.ts). On each settlement day every club with a need, in stature order, picks up to `NPC_MARKET.targetsPerNeed` targets by slot gain. Targets come from an index by country, division and line, searched within a rating band around the buyer's affordable level. Reach is the buyer's own country, same division and below; a higher division's player comes down only on loan, and a top flight also looks at the other top flights on a seeded day (`NPC_MARKET.abroad`). Listed players come first only on ties. The bid is the asking fee × a seeded share in `bidShare` 0.5–1, or the full asking fee for a buyer whose envelope holds 3× it. The seller answers through `saleAnswer` with the new `npcSaleContext` (sale-answer.ts: relegated-starter and above-division rows, contract rows half before 1995, no 단장). The player answers through `termsAnswer` with `npcTermsContext` (personal-terms.ts: 100% of his market wage at the buyer, "our" = the buying club). A counter at R is taken when the buyer can pay it. An equal-stature rival who reaches the player and pays a higher wage takes the bid. `NPC_MARKET_ERA` / `npcMarketCaps` (transfer-policy.ts) cap bids, buys and sales per club and window; bids are spread over the window's three days by `bidsOnDay`. Sell-down lists by `compareSellDown`: value per wage, then 30+, final season, highest wage. `PLAYER_MOVE_FLOW.observe` now gets a `MoveReason` (pull, sell-down, surplus, blocked, final-season, loan, loan-return, free-signing, offer). free-agents.ts tags free signings with `withMoveReason`. Nothing new is stored.
