## result
- outcome: clubs nobody owns trade by pull (packages/engine/src/npc-market.ts). Each settlement day every club with a need, in stature order, bids for up to `NPC_MARKET.targetsPerNeed` reachable targets by slot gain. Targets come from an index by country, division and line, searched in a rating band around the buyer's means; players whose club cannot sell now never take a place of the bounded search. Reach is the own country's division and below, a higher division only on loan, other top flights on a seeded day. The bid is the asking fee × a seeded share (0.5–1), or the fee itself for a buyer holding 3× it. The seller answers through CLUB-52 (`npcSaleContext`: relegated-starter and above-division rows) and the player through CLUB-53 (`npcTermsContext`, 100% of his market wage at the buyer). The bid, the seller's draw and the player's draw are keyed `<seed>:npc-bid|npc-sale|npc-terms:<year>:<summer|winter>:<buyer>:<player>`, and a buyer meets each target on one seeded settlement day of the window (`<seed>:npc-bid-day:<year>:<window>:<buyer>:<player>`, among the days the era's caps open to bids, `npcBidDay`), so it bids for a target at most once a window, with no saved state. CLUB-52's contract rows count half before 1995 for every seller, our club included (`saleContext` supplies the year). `NPC_MARKET_ERA.anchors` / `npcMarketCaps` cap bids, buys and sales per club and window; `compareSellDown` orders sell-down; the move observer gets a `MoveReason` and nothing new is stored.
