## goal
Entering stage 3 deducts 10 league points (from the next season's table when the league season has ended), at most once a season, and writes off half the club's debt; league tables show the deduction.

## acceptance
- [ ] Unit tests: standings rank a club with a deduction by its points after it; a second stage-3 entry in the same season deducts nothing; entry after the last league round deducts from the next season's table; half the debt is written off once per entry and booked as a write-off line, not income.
- [ ] The deduction is data on the club's league row for that season (points deducted and the stage that caused it), so history and the web table (T980) read it.
- [ ] Task-tier checks pass through `npx @haeram/gusta vibe check --tier task`.
