## goal
Replace the owner-only points rule and the 30% drift of clubs nobody owns with one standing rule for every club.

## acceptance
- [ ] At closeSeason every club except representatives (`representative` clubs and `euro:` ids) gets reputation = clamp(rep + 0.2 × (level − rep) + finish, 0, 100): level = 8 when the club's next tier ≥ its country's division count, else max(8, 75 − 15 × next tier); finish = 3 × (1 − 2 × (rank − 1) / (clubs − 1)) in its own league or group of the season just played, 0 for a club without a played table.
- [ ] The `table.points / 25 − 1` line (season.ts, after `w.year++`) and the 30% reputation drift in dynamics.ts evolveClubs are removed; nothing else changes reputation at the close.
- [ ] A domestic cup winner gains +2 when its cup is decided (cups.ts, every club alike); each settled continental match gives both non-representative clubs +0.2, and a continental title (not `super`) +8 to its winner, whoever they are (europe.ts, today own-club only).
- [ ] Owner capital −2 (ops-commercial.ts) and the supporter drift (fans.ts, ECON-23) are unchanged and read the new reputation.
- [ ] packages/engine/src/reputation.test.ts: level per tier and amateur; finish for champion/middle/last and inside a group; a promoted and a relegated club pull toward the next division's level; a club without a table gets only the level term; 30 identical mid-table seasons settle within ±1 of the level; cup +2 and continental +0.2/+8 for a club nobody owns; clamp at 0 and 100; representatives unchanged.
- [ ] Existing season.test.ts/dynamics.test.ts/europe.test.ts cases that asserted the old rules are updated to LEAGUE-14; tests/fixtures/portable-v1.json is regenerated with `pnpm exec tsx scripts/update-golden.ts --write` only after checking that every changed fact is reputation- or supporter-derived.
