## impl notes
- New `ScriptBeat` kinds beside the prototype's (`kickOff`, `pass`, `carry`, `takeOn`, `shot`, `throwIn`, `goalKick`, `corner`, `freeKick`): `penalty` (a restart kind whose only action is the kick), `stoppage` (a dead ball with no restart of its own: it carries the incidents below and play resumes with the restart owed), `shootoutKick`. A beat gains optional `incidents: { kind: 'yellow' | 'secondYellow' | 'red' | 'sub' | 'injury'; side; line; other?; reason? }[]` (the record's own values, →MATCH-19/20/21) and `period: 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5` (5 = the shoot-out).
- Where incidents go: a booked foul's card rides the foul's beat (its minute is already fixed, T826). A substitution, an injury or a card without a foul is fixed to its recorded minute like a shot: the writer ends the possession at the first dead ball of that minute — an out, a foul, an offside or a goal already there, else a `stoppage` it opens when the ball is next with a keeper or dead — and attaches the incident there. Incidents of one minute and side share one stoppage in the record's order; a half-time substitution (the record's minute 46, 91 or 106 stamps) rides the half's kick-off.
- A man short: from a red card's beat on, the side's `present` (T823's `ShapeSide`) loses his slot; a keeper sent off or injured is replaced as the record's stints say (a substitute keeper or an outfielder taking the gloves — the stint's `slot` tells). The writer never invents who covers.
- Penalties: the foul row flagged `penalty` (T825) is played as an ordinary foul beat inside its box cell, then a `penalty` beat: taker = the shot row's shooter, outcome = the shot's (`goal`, `save`, `block` never, `miss`), a goal followed by the kick-off, a save by the keeper's distribution or a corner as the next recorded row allows, a miss by a goal kick. Its minute is the shot's. Direct free-kick shots stay as the prototype writes them.
- Extra time: periods 3 and 4 (minutes 91–105, 106–120 plus `added`) are written like halves from the rows of period `extra`; the sides change ends at each period (the script only numbers the period; ends are T830's).
- The shoot-out: after the last whistle one `shootoutKick` beat a recorded kick, in order, `side`, `actor` = the taker's line, outcome `goal` or `saved`/`wide` (a kick not scored is `saved` when the stream says so with `SCRIPT_CONFIG.shootout.saved` 0.7, else `wide`: the record holds only scored or not). Its kicks count toward `record.shootout`, never toward goals or shots.
- `countScript` counts cards, substitutions and injuries from beats' `incidents`, penalties from `penalty` beats and the shoot-out from its kicks, so T825's whole record comparison holds.
- If some fixture of the sample cannot be written, that is this task's bug to fix with a unit test naming the case — not a row to drop, move off its minute or give to another player (→MATCH-38).
- T826's ten fixtures hold seven yellow cards no foul was booked for (s03 2, s05 2, s08 1, s09 1, s10 1), which its helper (`script.testkit.ts` `loadDetail`) leaves out unless `allCards`; once cards without a foul are written here, `write.test.ts`'s `every fixture is written exactly` also reads the ten with `allCards`, while T826's cost line keeps its card-less read (decided 261111).
