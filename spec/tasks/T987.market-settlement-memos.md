## goal
Cut the NPC transfer market further (about 6 points of a history season together) with bit-identical worlds by reading each settlement fact once: per-player slot-rating rows in `needs`, per-candidate asking fee and price, per-club sellable lines, array-based `listings`, and same-stature rival groups.

## result
- outcome: Parts A–D are in, and the world is bit-identical. The final code is faster on both workloads.
  - Part A (`SettlementRatings`): `rowOf(p)` finds or adds a player's row and keeps the last player and row; `forget` clears that memo. `bests(players, slots)` resolves each slot's place once, reads each player's row once and keeps a running maximum per slot, seeded with +0. A NaN slot best stays NaN (`Math.max` semantics), and a -0 rating leaves +0. A slot outside `SLOT_PLACES` takes the old `Math.max(0, ...of)` expression. `needs` uses `bests` while a settlement is open and the old expression otherwise.
  - Part B (`npcMarket`): `Candidate` keeps `asking` (read once through `askingFor`; a listing's loan flag is fixed for the settlement) and, for a buyer of the seller's country, `home` = converted fee, converted least bid and wage per buyer tier. `priceOf(buyer, cand, loan)` reads those; a buyer from another country computes as before. `attempt` reuses the candidate's asking fee.
  - Part C: `sellableLines(players)` is the squad side of `npcCanSell` by line: squad above `max(SQUAD_MINIMUM, minimum)` and line count above `LINE_MINIMUM`. `npcCanSell` reads it, unchanged in result. `targetsFor` reads a per-settlement `Map<Club, Record<Line, boolean>>` and keeps the per-player `loanUntil`/`retiring` checks. The map is cleared for the seller and the bidder next to `settlement.clubs.delete`, where `settleMove` changes the only two squads.
  - Part D: `listings` fills each club's main OVRs, lines and sellable lines once, and sorts an index array with the same comparator values over the same starting order. "Blocked" is counted from the arrays. The rival loop in `attempt` walks `peers.get(stature)`, the buyers of the same stature in `buyers` order, with the existing checks.
  - New unit (npc-market.test.ts): `bests` equals `Math.max(0, ...of)` for an empty squad, a real squad, an all -0 squad (+0 kept) and a NaN read after an abler player. It also checks that a slot outside the formations takes the plain path and that cached rows are reused. The NaN and -0 ratings come from a pass-through `vi.mock` of `freshSlotOvr` keyed by player id.
