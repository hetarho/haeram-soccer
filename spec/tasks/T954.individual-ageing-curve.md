## goal
Every player draws once an ageing onset shift (−4 to +5 years) and a decline rate (×0.6–1.8, mode ×1) that move his groups' grow-until/from ages and scale their per-season decline, so players fade at different ages and speeds.

## acceptance
- [ ] Unit tests: the onset shift and decline rate are fixed per player across seasons, saves and reloads; shift shares over 20,000 drawn players match PLAYER-17's table within 1 point each; the rate stays in ×0.6–1.8 with its mode near ×1; a +5 player's pace holds until 34 and a −4 player's falls from 25; growth before the shifted grow-until age is unchanged.
- [ ] Own players and persisted players of clubs nobody owns use the same curve (WORLD-16 development and ageing).
- [ ] Simulation results move on purpose: affected fixtures this change moves are regenerated and named in the result; the portable golden (`pnpm exec tsx scripts/update-golden.ts --write`) and integration expectations are handed to the batch gate in result limits, not run here.

## impl notes
- Derive both values deterministically from the player's identity stream at ageing time (a keyed draw such as `<seed>:ageing:<player id>`), so no save field and no schema change are needed; the values are fixed for his career because the key is.
- Constants as module constants beside the ageing table (e.g. `AGEING_CURVE.shiftShares`, `AGEING_CURVE.rate {min, mode, max}`), not config.ts leaves (a new config leaf fails the web lab tunables test).
- Apply the shift to both 'grow until about' and 'from' of every group, and the rate to every 'per season after'; the ±0.5 seasonal noise stays.
- Where: `AGEING` (player.ts L538-567, `{growUntil, declineFrom, perSeason}` per attribute — keep the name, apps/web catalog.test.ts refers to it); `developAnnually` (training.ts L313-364, skips growing attributes under 27 — use the shifted grow-until age) and `ageAttributes` (L366-383); free agents age in `declineFreeAgent` (free-agents.ts L101-113) and its reference copy free-agents-reference.testkit.ts L97-110; scout-verdict.ts:34 reads the pace decline age — read the player's own shifted age. Callers season.ts:739, npc-careers.ts `closeNpcSummer`, wartime.ts:63 need no change if the curve is read inside the ageing helpers.
- Stream convention: one new stream per fixed draw (world.ts L163); use `${seed}:ageing:${id}`.
