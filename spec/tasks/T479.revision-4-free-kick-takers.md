## goal
Open match-model revision 4 for this round's football changes and, on it, let the manager's named taker take only free kicks within shooting or crossing range, any other free kick going to the nearest suitable player.

## acceptance
- [ ] `MATCH_MODEL_REVISIONS` lists `continuous-aggregate-4` after 1–3 and `MATCH_MODEL_REVISION` is 4, which `MATCH_MODEL_RELEASE` names; the doc comment says what revision 4 adds (free-kick takers, corner numbers, shot choice, its calibration — T479–T482). Tests that name `continuous-aggregate-4` as an unknown future revision name `-5`.
- [ ] A world on revision 3 (or 1, 2) moves to revision 4 through the existing activation writer at the next safe boundary: tracking boundary and coverage kept, settled records keep their revision, only unsettled fixtures play revision 4.
- [ ] On revision 4 (`modelAtLeast(s.setup, 4)`) `playRestart` gives a free kick that is shot directly to the named direct-free-kick taker and one delivered into the box (band 5+) to the named corner taker, as today; every other free kick (a pass restart) goes to the nearest outfielder on the pitch to the spot, except that in the own third (attack-frame x below `RESTART_CONFIG.ownThird`) it goes to whichever of the side's centre-backs and keeper on the pitch is nearest. Revisions 1–3 and the pre-feature path keep today's choice draw for draw.
- [ ] Tests (`grid/restarts.test.ts`): "revision 4: an own-third free kick goes to the nearest centre-back or keeper" (the named taker a forward 60 m away), "revision 4: a midfield free kick goes to the nearest outfielder", "revision 4: direct and delivered free kicks keep the named takers", "revision 3 keeps the named taker for every free kick"; `match-model.test.ts` "a revision-3 world moves to revision 4 at the safe boundary".
- [ ] Measured on the 16 probe fixtures of `spec/ideation/done/dead-balls-and-finishing.md` (scratch script, not kept): own-third free kicks taken by forwards ≤ 5% (was 59%) with a median walk ≤ 15 m (was 34 m); reported in result.
- [ ] The candidate facts and positions digests of `grid-throughput.test.ts` and `full-model-throughput.test.ts` are re-recorded on revision 4 with the old values in their history comments and a one-line reason, with a `scripts/grid-bands.ts --candidate --quick` read in the result as their evidence.
- [ ] ARCH-30 task tier: typecheck, lint, format check and the changed unit tests pass.

## impl notes
- Follow T466's pattern for a revision: the revision string list and doc comment in `packages/contracts/src/match-model.ts`, the activation writer unchanged (`activatesMatchModel` compares revision numbers), `docs/MATCH-MODEL-CONTRACT.md` names revision 4. Revision gates read `modelAtLeast(s.setup, 4)` from `grid/rules.ts`.
- Define `RESTART_CONFIG.ownThird = 35` (metres from the own goal line in the side's attack frame, MATCH-14's band edge) in `grid/restarts.ts`; T480–T482 do not change it.
- The nearest taker is chosen by distance from each candidate's current position to the spot (ties by roster index) before `place(taker)`; it draws nothing, so revision 4 changes no other stream by itself.
- Archived replays rebuild from stored goals (`apps/web/src/ui/replay.ts`), and a queued playback replays under the revision it settled with, so gating keeps every settled match reproducible.
