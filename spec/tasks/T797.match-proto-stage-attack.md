## goal
Every beat the script can hold is staged: a runner timed onside as the ball is played or caught going early when the script says offside, shots that score, are saved, blocked or go wide with the keeper acting on them, deliveries met by runners in the box, corners with both sides set — and `producePlayback(seed)` returns ten staged minutes of the showcase script.

- Add to `PROTO_CONFIG`:

  // inside stage
  through: { setBack: 2.5, runLead: 0.8, earlyBy: 0.3, onsideGap: 0.3, window: 1.6, offsideGap: 0.6, flagDelay: 0.9, maxWait: 3 },

- `stage/through.ts` — `performThrough(state, ctx)`; `performPass`, `performPassIntercepted` and `performPassOut` start with `if (ctx.beat.through) return performThrough(state, ctx)` (after the restart wrapper), and it is registered for `pass:offside`.
  - names: `T = beat.team`, `runner = beat.receiver`, `line = () => offsideLineX(fixture, positionsOf(state), T)` (attack frame of `T`), `rx = () => toAttack(T, runnerPos)[0]`, `[ax, ay] = toAttack(T, beat.to)`, `offside = beat.outcome === 'offside'`, `lead = through.runLead + (offside ? through.earlyBy : 0)`.
  - `setPossession`; `ensureOwner`; the actor drifts as in `holdForPass`. The runner's order (pace `run` while waiting, `sprint` once he goes) has a target function of the hold time left (`left = beat.hold - held`):
    1. `left > lead` → attack `[line() - through.setBack, his current attack y]` (he waits a couple of metres short of the line);
    2. else, before the kick, when not `offside` → attack `[min(ax, line() - through.onsideGap), lerp(his attack y, ay, 0.5)]` (on his way, never past the line before the ball is played);
    3. else (the kick has happened, or the script says offside) → `beat.to`.
  - the kick, checked each tick once `held ≥ beat.hold`: an onside outcome kicks when `line() - through.window <= rx() < line() - 0.05`; `offside` kicks when `rx() >= line() + through.offsideGap`. After `through.maxWait` more seconds kick anyway (an onside runner is capped behind the line by rule 2, so he is onside).
  - at the kick: `pushEvent kick`; when `offside` also `pushEvent({ kind: 'offside', actor, other: runner, at: runnerPos, line: toAttack(T, [line(), 0])[0] })`.
  - the ball: `Rw = clampToPitch(beat.to, 0.5)` (for `out`: `beat.to` itself, outside the pitch); `eta = timeToReach(runnerBody, Rw, his sprint)`; `speed = clamp(dist(ballPos, Rw) / max(0.4, eta - stage.receiveSlack), ball.minSpeed, ball.through)` so ball and runner arrive together; `startFlight(state, Rw, speed, beat.loft)`.
  - `complete`: `cueNext(state, ctx, Rw)`; `runUntil` the flight ends; `giveBall(runner)`; `pushEvent receive`.
  - `intercepted`: `r = resolveCut(state, beat.opponent, ballPos, Rw, flight time)`; replace the flight with `startFlightTimed(state, r.plan.point, r.plan.time, apex scaled by r.plan.param)`; the resolved opponent sprints to the point (with `boost: stage.lunge` when `r.stretch`); at the end `giveBall(opponent)` and `pushEvent intercept` with `recast`/`stretch`.
  - `out`: `runUntil` the flight ends; ball `dead`, `state.live = false`, `pushEvent({ kind: 'out', actor, at: clampToPitch(Rw, 0) })`.
  - `offside`: `runUntil` the flight ends or `through.flagDelay` seconds pass, whichever is first; then the ball is `dead` where it is (no owner, `z = 0`), `state.live = false`, `pushEvent({ kind: 'whistle', actor, at: ballPos })`.
  - end with `state.orders.clear()`.
- `stage/pass.ts` — deliveries into the box: for a lofted pass-like beat whose `to` is in the box (`inBox(toAttack(team, beat.to))`), the receiver's hold order is the run-up point (attack `to` with `x - stage.cross.runUp`) instead of `R`; at the kick he sprints to `R` as before. `canKick` is unchanged (it reads where he is).
- `stage/pass.ts` — the onside guard for ordinary passes (kind `pass`, not `through`; restarts are exempt): with `line = () => offsideLineX(fixture, positionsOf(state), team)` and attack-frame x values, while the ball is not yet played the receiver's order target is `R` with its attack x capped at `line() - shape.onsideGap` (pace `sprint` while he stands beyond the line), and the kick also waits until the receiver's attack x is at most `line() - 0.05` or not ahead of the ball (still bounded by `stage.maxExtraHold`); at the kick `R` itself gets the same cap when it lies in the opponents' half. So no ordinary pass is played to a man standing offside.
- `stage/shot.ts` — `performShot` for `shot:goal | saved | blocked | wide` (registered through `withRestart`, so a free kick struck at goal gets its dead-ball scene):
  - `setPossession`; `ensureOwner`; `K` = the other team's keeper; `G = beat.to`; `speed = beat.header ? ball.header : ball.shot`; `apex = beat.header ? ball.headerApex : 0`.
  - hold `beat.hold` seconds: the actor steps toward goal (`target: () => actorPos + norm(G - actorPos) * shot.setStep`, pace `walk`); for `blocked` the scripted blocker is ordered `{ target: () => actorPos + norm(G - actorPos) * shot.blockDist, pace: 'sprint' }`. Keepers need no order here: their default target already stands them between ball and goal.
  - `pushEvent({ kind: 'shot', actor, at: ballPos })`; `from = ballPos`.
  - `goal`: `E = G + norm(G - from) * shot.netDepth`; `startFlightTimed(state, E, flightTime(from, E, speed), apex)`; keeper order `{ target: G, pace: 'sprint', speedCap: shot.beatenKeeper }`; `runUntil` the ball has passed `G` (elapsed ≥ `dist(from, G) / dist(from, E)` of the flight); `state.score[T] += 1`; `pushEvent({ kind: 'goal', actor, at: G, score: [...state.score] })`; `state.live = false` at once; `runUntil` the flight ends; ball `dead`.
  - `saved`: the save point is `closestOnSegment(from, G, keeperPos)` with its parameter clamped to `[shot.saveMin, 1]`, `time = param * flightTime(from, G, speed)`. While `timeToReach(keeperBody, point, his sprint + stage.lunge) > time + stage.cutSlack` (at most six times) move the aim toward him: `G = [G[0], lerp(G[1], keeperPos[1], shot.saveNudge)]` and recompute. `startFlightTimed(state, point, time, apex)`; keeper order `{ target: point, pace: 'sprint', boost: stage.lunge }`; `runUntil` it ends; `pushEvent({ kind: 'save', actor, other: K, at: point })`. When `ctx.next?.kind === 'corner'` the ball is parried behind: target = world of `T`'s attack `[105.8, clamp(y + side * shot.parryWide, 2, 66)]` with `y` the point's attack y and `side = y >= 34 ? 1 : -1`; `startFlightTimed(state, target, shot.parryTime, 0.5)`; `runUntil` it ends; ball `dead`, `live = false`, `pushEvent({ kind: 'out', actor, at: clampToPitch(target, 0) })`. Otherwise `giveBall(K)`.
  - `blocked`: `L = dist(from, G)`; `r = resolveCut(state, beat.opponent, from, G, flightTime(from, G, speed), [shot.blockMin / L, min(0.9, shot.blockMax / L)])`; `startFlightTimed(state, r.plan.point, r.plan.time, apex)`; the resolved blocker `{ target: r.plan.point, pace: 'sprint', boost: r.stretch ? stage.lunge : 0 }`; `runUntil` it ends; `pushEvent({ kind: 'block', actor, other: blocker, at: r.plan.point, recast: r.recast, stretch: r.stretch })`. Then by the next beat: a `corner` → deflected behind exactly as the parry above (from the block point); a `throwIn` → to the nearer touchline, `shot.wideRun` m beyond it at the block point's x, flying `distance / shot.deflectSpeed` seconds, then `dead`, not `live`, `pushEvent out`; anything else → the ball runs `min(shot.looseRoll, distance)` metres toward the next beat's actor in `shot.looseTime` seconds and becomes `loose` (the next handler's `ensureOwner` collects it); with no next beat it becomes `loose` where it was blocked.
  - `wide`: `E = G + norm(G - from) * shot.wideRun`; fly there at `speed`; `pushEvent({ kind: 'wide', actor, at: G })` when the ball passes `G`; at the end ball `dead`, `live = false`.
  - end with `state.orders.clear()`.
- `stage/scenes.ts` — the corner scene inside `runDeadBall` (kind `corner`), with `s = +1` when the spot's attack y is above 34, else −1, and `C(x, off)` = world of the taking team's attack `[x, 34 + s * off]`:
  - taking side: the receiver → his run-up point (attack `to` with `x - stage.cross.runUp`); the other outfield players except the taker, sorted by `heading` descending (lower index first on ties), take `corner.attack` in order (four in the box, two at its edge, two back); their keeper keeps his default target.
  - defending side: keeper → `C(...corner.keeper)`; the scripted opponent, when he is an outfield player → `corner.goalSide` metres goal-side (attack x + `goalSide`) of the receiver's run-up point; of the remaining outfield players by index: the first two → `corner.zonal`, the last two → `corner.up`, the others mark the attackers on `corner.attack[0..]` in order, each `corner.goalSide` goal-side of his man's place.
  - all with pace `run`; these orders stay in `state.orders` after the scene (do not clear them for a corner) so both sides hold their places through the delivery; the pass handler replaces the taker's, receiver's and opponent's and its final `orders.clear()` releases everyone. `keepOut` still applies (`restart.distance.corner`).
- `stage/pipeline.ts`:
  ```ts
  export interface ProduceInfo { seed: string; attempts: number; staged: number; check: QuotaCheck }
  export function producePlayback(base: string, options?: { duration?: number; showcase?: boolean }): { playback: Playback; info: ProduceInfo };
  ```
  - `showcase: false` → `script = generateScript(base)`, stage it, `check = checkQuotas(playback.beats, fixture)`, `attempts = 1`, `staged = 1`.
  - default (`showcase` true): take scripts from `showcaseScripts(base)` one by one, stage each, `check = checkQuotas(playback.beats, fixture)` on the **performed** beats; return the first whose `missing` is empty; stop after `grammar.showcase.maxStaged` staged candidates (or when the generator ends) and return the one with the fewest missing quotas; when the generator yields nothing, stage `findShowcaseScript(base).script`.
