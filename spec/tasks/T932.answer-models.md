## goal
Two pure, extensible answer models that every transfer path can share: the selling club's answer to a bid (CLUB-52) and the player's answer to our terms (CLUB-53), each a list of factors over one seeded draw, so a later prestige or club-history factor is one more registry entry and no caller changes. No caller uses them yet.

## acceptance
- [ ] `packages/engine/src/sale-answer.ts` exports a context type, a factor registry (`SALE_FACTORS`: id, read(context) → share delta with a message ID and named parameters, or nothing), `saleAnswer(context, draw)` returning accept / counter (fee) / refuse with the factors applied, and `saleOutlook(context)` returning the exact chances of acceptance and of a counter. CLUB-52's table is the starting registry; constants are module constants (not `config.ts`). Unit tests in `sale-answer.test.ts`: a bid at or above the asking fee accepts for every draw; at or above R accepts; in [R − 0.25A, R) counters at R, never below the bid and never above A; lower refuses; our club's R never below 80% of value; outlook chances match the answers over a fine uniform grid of draws; acceptance is monotone in the fee; adding a test-only factor changes the share and leaves the draw unchanged. Deterministic, stage unit, owner implementer.
- [ ] `packages/engine/src/personal-terms.ts` exports a context type, `TERMS_FACTORS` (CLUB-53's table, ambition weights 0.6/0.8/1/1.25/1.5 halved from 31), the wage steps `[1, 1.25, 1.5, 2]`, `termsChance(context)` = σ(1.5 + Σ) with its factor lines, and `termsAnswer(context, draw)`. Unit tests in `personal-terms.test.ts`: the chance rises with each wage step and saturates beyond 2.5×; each division below and a lower reputation lower it, scaled by ambition; Europe entries and leaving Europe; would start / loses his place; free agent; same draw same answer; a test-only factor plugs in without caller changes. Deterministic, stage unit, owner implementer.
- [ ] World readers build both contexts from a world: `saleContext(w, …)` (his place in the seller's XI: three best starters / starter / outside; contract seasons left; age and prospect; the seller's cash crisis or runway and our sales line, a club nobody owns selling down; our stance listed, transfer request or 언해피; our 단장's ability, trait or vacancy; the window's last 3 days) and `termsContext(w, …)` (demand = today's quote at the buying club, offered step, division gap to his club or, for a free agent, to the division his ability suits, reputations, this season's European entries of both clubs from `w.europe`, would start for us, starts now, free agent). Tests read a fixture world for at least the place, contract, Europe and playing-time factors. Deterministic, stage unit, owner implementer.
