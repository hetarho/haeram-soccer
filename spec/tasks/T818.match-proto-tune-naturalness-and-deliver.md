## impl notes
- from review/match-engine-split-261110 F2 F3 F4
- Follow T791's chain rules; nothing outside `scripts/match-proto/` and `artifacts/match-proto-261111/` changes. `artifacts/` is not linted or formatted. The page is not opened in a browser by this task and no browser automation is run (→ARCH-30); say so in the result.
- What the numbers are for: the owner's eyes are the judge. The ten naturalness metrics of T814 are his three complaints in measurable form and their limits are first targets set at planning time from the first page's baseline, not football facts; the 21 older metrics guard what the first page already held (contact at receptions and contests, honest offside, roles, no teleports). A round is good when the three complaints fall without the older ones breaking.
- Order of work inside the timebox (keep a short log with clock times in the artifacts README):
  1. baseline reports (first acceptance item); note the time — the two hours start here.
  2. hard failures first, because they are bugs rather than tuning: `duration`, `kinematics.*`, `ball.maxSpeed`, `score.consistent`, `offside.consistency`. Find the frame or event, reproduce it in a unit test with a short hand-written script, fix the handler.
  3. the showcase: `demo` must meet every showcase quota on its performed beats (`report.ts` prints `missing=`); if T815's `grammar.minPass` cost one, restore it through `grammar.weights` or `grammar.p` only.
  4. complaint 1, pairs that stick (`events.shortKick`, `shape.huddle`, `shape.huddleLongest`, and `shape.spacing` with them). Levers: `grammar.minPass`, `stage.kickGap`, `stage.ownerRoom`, `stage.holdDrift`, `stage.maxExtraHold`, `stage.separation`. For every episode of `shape.huddleLongest` over the limit read which beats it spans before touching a number: a pair made by two consecutive beats is the script's or `roomyAim`'s matter, a pair of free players `separated`'s.
  5. complaint 3, moving as one (`motion.restarts`, `motion.startSync`, `motion.speedSync` and its `bands=` detail). Levers: `motion.own.delay.*`, `motion.own.lag.*`, `motion.own.arrive.*`, `motion.own.hold`, `motion.own.minSpeed`.
  6. complaint 2, turning as one and marking (`turn.reverse`, `turn.run`, `turn.counterRun`, `mark.near`). Levers: `motion.turn.*`, `shape.mark.*`, `shape.unmark.*`.
  7. what the new movement cost the older metrics: `events.recastRate`, `events.stretchRate`, `events.contest`, `stage.pressVisible`, `shape.swarm`, the role metrics. A defending shape that trails the play is the usual cause; shorten `motion.own.lag.line`, `motion.own.lag.base` or `motion.turn.lag.settled` before touching `stage.*` contest values, and record the trade when a naturalness metric and a contact metric cannot both pass.
  8. delivery files and README, leaving at least fifteen minutes for them.
- One change at a time, five seeds each time; the round's score is the summed pass count over the five seeds (155 at most). Do not tune on `demo` alone. Screening several values with a scratch runner that calls `producePlayback` and `computeMetrics` in memory is fine; the baseline and final reports come from `report.ts`.
- Read the picture as well as the count, in numbers: for the final `demo` playback record the `bands=` shares, the number of completed passes that go straight back to the man who passed (a statistic for the owner, no limit) and the script volumes of the performed beats against T800's sanity ranges (passes 80–350, completion 72–94%, shots 2–12, goals 0–4, offsides 0–3, corners 0–4, throw-ins 1–10, tackles + interceptions 4–40, each team's share of beats 30–70%).
- If a limit cannot pass without breaking the design's rule that staging never changes a beat's outcome, actor or receiver, or without a contact metric failing, leave it failing and say so: that is a finding about the split the owner needs.
- If the criteria themselves need changing (a limit, a new rule for what staged football must hold, a script rule such as fewer return passes), that is a planning change for the owner, not something to decide here.
