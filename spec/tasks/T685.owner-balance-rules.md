The owner takeover plays by OWNER r2, ECON r43 and LEAGUE r13: reach by price rank with every cheaper club buyable, living costs 0.25%, gentler dividend and star-sale drops, books that live within their means and a ten-season takeover loan.

- [v] 팬 민심 (OWNER-12): dividend −0.5 per 1% of turnover; a star sale −4, at most once a transfer window (the window of the last drop kept on the owner state); `fan-mood.test.ts` covers two star sales in one window (one drop) and one in the next window (a second drop).
- [v] books (ECON-36): in the world's first season every club nobody owns opens with `CLUB_BOOKS.opening` × its season income in cash; at each close cash above `CLUB_BOOKS.cashSeasons` × the season's income leaves the books and debt beyond `CLUB_BOOKS.debtSeasons` × the season's income is written off; `books.test.ts` covers the opening, both bounds and a club inside them untouched; a 1930 takeover starts with that opening cash (takeover unit test).
