## goal
The owner takeover plays by OWNER r2, ECON r43 and LEAGUE r13: reach by price rank with every cheaper club buyable, living costs 0.25%, gentler dividend and star-sale drops, books that live within their means and a ten-season takeover loan.

## acceptance
- [ ] reach (OWNER-3): each tier's reach is its band of the price ranking of every club of the playable countries in the takeover season, dearest first (`OWNER_TIERS[tier].band`); the anchor stays the reach's median pound price; every club whose price the fortune pays is affordable, a 석유 재벌 paying for every club below its reach. Unit tests in `club-value.test.ts`/`listing.test.ts`: band membership on a hand-made price list, a three-division country holding clubs of 지역 유지's band, a 석유 재벌 fortune paying for the cheapest club.
- [ ] living costs (OWNER-8): `OWNER_LIVING.rate` 0.0025; the owner-settlement unit test reads the constant.
- [ ] 팬 민심 (OWNER-12): dividend −0.5 per 1% of turnover; a star sale −4, at most once a transfer window (the window of the last drop kept on the owner state); `fan-mood.test.ts` covers two star sales in one window (one drop) and one in the next window (a second drop).
- [ ] books (ECON-36): in the world's first season every club nobody owns opens with `CLUB_BOOKS.opening` × its season income in cash; at each close cash above `CLUB_BOOKS.cashSeasons` × the season's income leaves the books and debt beyond `CLUB_BOOKS.debtSeasons` × the season's income is written off; `books.test.ts` covers the opening, both bounds and a club inside them untouched; a 1930 takeover starts with that opening cash (takeover unit test).
- [ ] takeover loan (ECON-38): `TAKEOVER_DEBT.seasons` 10; the installment test reads the constant.
- [ ] typecheck, lint, format:check, `pnpm run build` and `pnpm exec vitest run --project unit --changed "$HAERAM_DIFF_BASE" --passWithNoTests` pass; integration and e2e cases whose numbers move with these rules are updated with the code and listed in `- limits:` for the batch.

## impl notes
- Constants live in `packages/engine/src/owner-config.ts` beside the ones they join: `OWNER_TIERS[tier].band: readonly [from, to]` as shares of the price ranking (oil [0, 0.08], chaebol [0.08, 0.2], industrial [0.2, 0.35], midsize [0.35, 0.55], local [0.55, 0.8], selfmade [0.8, 1]); `OWNER_LIVING.rate` 0.0025; `FAN_MOOD.dividend` −0.5 per percent and `FAN_MOOD.starSale.change` −4; new `CLUB_BOOKS = { opening: 0.5, cashSeasons: 1, debtSeasons: 1.5 }`; `TAKEOVER_DEBT.seasons` 10. Nothing else hard-codes these numbers.
- Reach: replace `REACH_BANDS`/`inReach`/`divisionPlaces` in `club-value.ts` by the price-rank band inside `reachOf(w, tier, priced)` (rank by `pricePounds`, ties by club id; a club at a band edge belongs to the band whose `from` its rank share reaches). `tierAnchor`/`tierFortune` keep their signatures; callers (`listing.ts`, `scripts/owner-balance.ts`, the start flow's tier cards) keep working. The tier cards keep naming their typical clubs (OWNER-3's column).
- Star-sale window: keep the last drop's window on `OwnerState` (optional field, contract type and zod schema); no save migration — older saves are not supported before launch.
- Books: in `books.ts`, the opening cash uses the same season-income figure the close sums for that club (division distribution, gate, commercial income) computed on the world's first day; the bounds apply in `settleNpcBooks` after the season's result, against that season's income. The removed cash is not recorded anywhere else (it is spent on the club).
- Equivalence digests, golden worlds and cached test worlds that include books or prices change; regenerate them as T671 did and say so in the result.
- Integration and e2e are edited with the code and run by the coordinator's batch.
