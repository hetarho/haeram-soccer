## goal
Every playable country's money stands at its real 1999 rate against the pound (ECON-35): one level per country in the catalog, catalog 2026-demo-4. English careers on older catalogs move their pin; other countries' careers on them are a compatibility failure (SAVE-10).

## acceptance
- [ ] COUNTRIES' `scale` holds ECON-35's levels, documented; at 1999 every euro country's quote converts at €1.518 per £ within 0.1% (unit test)
- [ ] a country's quotes keep their ratios between years and between the price, football and broadcasting indices (unit test against the old levels' ratios)
- [ ] a foreign player's asking fee and wage do not depend on his club's country (world-market unit test)
- [ ] CATALOG_VERSION 2026-demo-4 with a regenerated fingerprint; 2026-demo-3 joins PRIOR_CATALOG_HASHES; an English world pinned to 2026-demo-1/2/3 loads and moves its pin on activation; a world of another country pinned to them is a SaveCompatibilityError (persistence tests)
- [ ] calibrate-economy ESP FRA GER ITA (default build, reference club) before → after: ECON-29's bands in price units match up to minor-unit rounding, none bankrupt that was solvent before
- [ ] validate:catalogs and the unit, integration and contract tiers pass with updated vectors

## impl notes
- quotes.ts multiplies by `country(code).scale` and nothing else reads it, so the constant re-levels every price, football and broadcasting quote of a country and nothing across countries.
- Players at foreign clubs and scout offers are already quoted in the buyer's money (world-market.ts `clubOffer`, ops-squad.ts `transferOffers`); keep it so.
- 1999 €1.51805 per £ is the ECB's 1999 average EUR/GBP (0.65874); the levels are the old ones × 1.51805 / the old 1999 rate, rounded to four significant figures.
- The refusal belongs where the world's own country is known: persistence `decode` after validation, before activation.
