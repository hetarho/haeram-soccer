# T055 League distribution, local-supporter gate, no supporter income

## goal
Competition money is a league distribution (equal share and merit, with broadcasting) and the gate comes from local supporters bounded by the catchment; no income grows with supporters alone.

## acceptance
- [ ] Equal share per division, paid in league-round installments through `roundShare`; merit by final position at the close in place of `seasonPrize`; champion total 1.6× the bottom club's in the top flight and 1.3× below; a relegated club keeps 50% of the share it lost for one season; amounts × the football index (→ECON-26), per country and division, in one config table.
- [ ] Per-match result bonuses (`matchBonus` income) end for league games; sponsor bonuses stay (→ECON-14).
- [ ] Global fan income (`fanIncome`, `fan-income` ledger line) ends; the following no longer pays.
- [ ] Gate: attendance from local supporters bounded by a catchment of city, division and stature (→LEAGUE-10), form, opponent and price, capped by capacity; ticket presets × the football index; `gateProjection`, previews and the cash projection use the same model (→ECON-13, →ECON-18).
- [ ] Ledger lines `league-share`, `league-merit` and `parachute` are named in financialBreakdown; FinanceOutlook projects them.
- [ ] Tests: the distribution sums and the merit order (finance.test.ts); parachute for one season only; a club with 2M followers in the third tier draws no more than its catchment (fans.test.ts); no `fan-income` entries after this rule (business.test.ts).
- [ ] `pnpm run typecheck && pnpm run test:unit` pass.

## impl notes
- Constants are first estimates. T062 calibrates them against ECON-29, ECON-9 and CLUB-25 on 50-season runs; keep them in one exported config object so T062 tunes them in one place.
- Cup and continental prizes keep their rules (→LEAGUE-5, →EURO-7) and are only scaled by the football index.
