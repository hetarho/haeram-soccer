## goal
Allow wealthy clubs to spend their cash through operating losses according to the director's temperament and the owner's request. Replace bankruptcy by duration with a scaled debt amount.

## acceptance
- [ ] Balanced/default retains twenty years of current fixed costs; bold/freely releases cash down to zero; intermediate requests and temperaments have ordered reserves.
- [ ] Signing and renewal checks use released capital, update after spending or an injection, account for pending bids and new wages, and do not spend the same capital twice. Rich clubs are not pressured to sell solely to fund an allowed wage bill.
- [ ] A club can remain in shallow debt beyond eight rounds; bankruptcy occurs strictly below minus one year of fixed costs; recovery resets warnings and a bankrupt career still refuses mutations.
- [ ] Plans, inbox reports and cash warnings explain reserve funding and the debt limit in all registered locales; no countdown remains in club warnings.
- [ ] Focused unit tests, typecheck, i18n validation, lint, formatting, spec lint and build pass; integration and Chromium journeys are registered for the final batch gate.

## impl notes
- Reuse live `operatingCosts`, the existing plan and request/temperament readers, and integer money. Do not store a separate grant or change signed contracts, personal-owner bankruptcy or the save schema.
- Add available capital to squad budgets after subtracting historic fees from income money; reserve pending bid fees separately from live cash. Youth receives its configured share of capital. Wage affordability recomputes capital with the added annual wage.
- Cash protection retains the season-end nonnegative projection. The bold/freely exception applies to that decision's immediate four-round buffer only.
- `pnpm run verify:batch` is the final coordinator gate; browser and integration suites run there once.
