# T213 Cards in the match: yellow, second yellow, red, dissent, ten men
> st:todo | ssot:MATCH-19 MATCH-18 | base:MATCH@11 | dep:T212 | touches:packages/engine/src/grid/discipline.ts packages/engine/src/grid/engine.ts packages/engine/src/grid/record.ts packages/engine/src/grid/discipline.test.ts | lane:- | explore:-

## goal
Fouls draw cards by their nature, a second yellow or a red sends a player off and his side plays a man short, and every card is an incident.

## acceptance
- [ ] `grid/discipline.ts` exports `CARD_CONFIG` (design §7.5 values) and `cardForFoul(s, foulerSide, fouler, foul, r)` returning `'none' | 'yellow' | 'secondYellow' | 'red'` with a `reason`, plus `dissentAt(s, side, r)` drawn once per side at the end of each period.
- [ ] The engine applies cards: yellow sets `gp.yellow`; a second yellow or red sets `sentOff`, `onPitch = false`, `offAt`, removes him from his slot (the slot stays empty; ten men); `TD/PD.yellowCards|redCards` counted (a second yellow counts one yellow and one red); an `Incident` is pushed (`minute`, `added`, `side`, `kind`, `player` id, `reason`); a card adds 20 s of added-time credit and 25 s dead time.
- [ ] Tactical fouls (phase `counter`, fouler goal-side of the ball) and DOGSO (fouled player `inBehind` or in central bands ≥ 6 with no other defender goal-side) are recognised as design §7.5.
- [ ] `discipline.test.ts` cases: `books tactical fouls far more often than ordinary ones`, `sends off for denying an obvious goal-scoring opportunity, less often inside the box`, `turns a second yellow into a red`, `makes a booked player foul less`, `plays on with ten men after a red card`, `draws dissent rarely`, and a rate check over 300 equal-strength matches: yellows per match (both sides) 2.0–6.0, reds per match ≤ 0.4 (T215 tunes to the MATCH-23 band).
- [ ] `pnpm run typecheck`, `pnpm run lint`, the test files pass.

## impl notes
- The foul itself comes from T209/T210 outcomes (`kind: 'foul'`); this task adds the card decision right after it, before the free kick is placed.
- Booked players: before a challenge resolves, a booked defender's foul probability is ×0.6 (apply in `resolveTakeOn`/`resolveCarry` through a parameter, do not duplicate the duel code).
- Sent-off goalkeeper: if slot 0 empties and a substitute keeper is unavailable (T214 handles replacing), the outfield player with the highest 선방 among those on the pitch moves to slot 0 (`enterSlot`).
- Incident minute: match minute capped at the period end (45, 90), `added` = minutes beyond it.
- Cards exist in every era (MATCH-19); `setup.era.cards` is only for wording later.
