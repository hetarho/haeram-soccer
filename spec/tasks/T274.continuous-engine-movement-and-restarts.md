## goal
Give the candidate engine enough match time for every ball route and player relocation, including stoppages.

## acceptance
- [ ] Add shared `GRID_MOVEMENT_CONFIG` and `GRID_BALL_CONFIG` in `grid/movement.ts` using the contract defaults. Movement uses pace/fatigue bounds, bounded acceleration/turning and actual elapsed match time.
- [ ] Replace candidate direct position resets for restart takers, set-piece participants, receptions and possession changes with scheduled preparation/movement. The ball returns to its restart spot through a recorded retrieval/placement interval; a kick cannot occur before arrival.
- [ ] Ball travel determines a feasible action duration instead of being compressed into a fixed deadline. Dead time, in-play time, added time, incidents and participation all use the same elapsed-time accounting; preparation is not double counted. A side's player minutes never exceed eleven times the played length (T273 found 991 > 990 from per-line rounding), so every candidate record passes `validateFullMatchRecord` without loosening it.
- [ ] Observation traces expose semantic movement knots and explicit period/entry/exit transitions. Candidate simulation with and without observation yields identical settled records.
- [ ] `grid/movement.test.ts` covers `bounds speed acceleration and turning`, `does not teleport the restart taker or set-piece group`, `records retrieval before the kick`, `waits for a long flight to land`, and `keeps continuity at minute boundaries`; engine/restart cases cover substitution, injury, dismissal, goal and extra-time transitions.
- [ ] Task checks follow ARCH-30. Record any resulting target-band drift for T278; do not weaken distribution targets in this task.

## impl notes
- Use T273's candidate revision and path contract. Preserve the pre-feature path for unsupported/missing model metadata until prospective activation; do not rewrite previously settled playback.
- Motion logic belongs in the engine and its trace, not in a renderer-only fake route. Restarts and shape consume the shared scheduler rather than each maintaining their own relocation shortcut.
- Retain actual incident/outcome destinations and roster identities, including the frozen match appearance added by the identity feature.
