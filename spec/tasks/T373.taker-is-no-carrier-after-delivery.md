## goal
At a corner or delivered free kick, the candidate's update at the kick no longer treats the taker as the man on the ball once he has played the delivery, so no defender leaves his job to press him while the ball is in the air.

## acceptance
- [ ] Reproduce T367's census: in the update that reads a delivered set piece's kick, `inDanger` and the urgent press read the taker as the carrier, so the nearest defender (no reach limit, a committed mark counted as free) presses him at the flag or the spot; in T367's 96 observed matches 311 of 445 urgent presses at a kick went to the taker and 219 of those were man-markers leaving a runner who still held his spot.
- [ ] On the candidate model only, a taker who has played the delivery is no carrier for that update until the delivery is resolved or lands (whichever the engine's sequence makes the right boundary), so no urgent press goes to him; the delivery's outcome rules (header, second ball, out) are unchanged; the pre-feature path and its golden facts are unchanged; outcome-critical values use `s.math`.
- [ ] `grid/coordination.test.ts`: `nobody presses a corner taker once the ball is in the air` and `the second ball's winner is pressed as usual`; T366/T367's playback case in `grid/playback.test.ts` updated so no urgent press at a kick goes to the taker.
- [ ] Re-record the `CANDIDATE` digests (and the candidate rounds digest if it moves) with a comment; `PRE_FEATURE` unchanged; rescan knockout fixtures if a tie no longer reaches extra time or penalties.
- [ ] A paired candidate run on one fresh validation round (as T358/T367 did) shows no MATCH-29, MATCH-23 core or curve row moving from inside to outside; report set-piece goals, headed goals and second-ball recoveries before and after. Task checks follow ARCH-30.

## impl notes
- From T367's question: the header itself is resolved at the kick, so this changes second-ball positions and what playback shows, not the delivery's outcome; roughly one delivery in seven had a marker drawn off a runner. T367 chose to make only committed set-piece marks count as taken in the urgent pairing; this task removes the press's false target instead of widening that rule (its option b). Read T367's and T366's records.
