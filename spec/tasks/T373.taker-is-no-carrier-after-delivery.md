# T373 Stop the kick's update from pressing a set-piece taker who has already delivered

## goal
At a corner or delivered free kick, the candidate's update at the kick no longer treats the taker as the man on the ball once he has played the delivery, so no defender leaves his job to press him while the ball is in the air.

## acceptance
- [v] Reproduce T367's census: in the update that reads a delivered set piece's kick, `inDanger` and the urgent press read the taker as the carrier, so the nearest defender (no reach limit, a committed mark counted as free) presses him at the flag or the spot; in T367's 96 observed matches 311 of 445 urgent presses at a kick went to the taker and 219 of those were man-markers leaving a runner who still held his spot.
- [v] On the candidate model only, a taker who has played the delivery is no carrier for that update until the delivery is resolved or lands (whichever the engine's sequence makes the right boundary), so no urgent press goes to him; the delivery's outcome rules (header, second ball, out) are unchanged; the pre-feature path and its golden facts are unchanged; outcome-critical values use `s.math`.
- [v] `grid/coordination.test.ts`: `nobody presses a corner taker once the ball is in the air` and `the second ball's winner is pressed as usual`; T366/T367's playback case in `grid/playback.test.ts` updated so no urgent press at a kick goes to the taker.
- [v] Re-record the `CANDIDATE` digests (and the candidate rounds digest if it moves) with a comment; `PRE_FEATURE` unchanged; rescan knockout fixtures if a tie no longer reaches extra time or penalties.
- [v] A paired candidate run on one fresh validation round (as T358/T367 did) shows no MATCH-29, MATCH-23 core or curve row moving from inside to outside; report set-piece goals, headed goals and second-ball recoveries before and after. Task checks follow ARCH-30.

## impl notes
- From T367's question: the header itself is resolved at the kick, so this changes second-ball positions and what playback shows, not the delivery's outcome; roughly one delivery in seven had a marker drawn off a runner. T367 chose to make only committed set-piece marks count as taken in the urgent pairing; this task removes the press's false target instead of widening that rule (its option b). Read T367's and T366's records.
- Runs beside T368 (offside and runs) on `grid/coordination.ts`: keep the change local to how the kick's update reads the carrier so the merge stays small; T370 waits for both.

## result
- outcome: On the candidate model a delivered taker is no carrier for the update that reads the kick when his delivery handed the ball to nobody. Nobody presses him while the ball is in the air, and the scene keeps its set-piece jobs. A delivery that reaches someone is read as before. The pre-feature path is unchanged.
  - Census reproduced on 217a861 (`<scratch>/census.mts`, the 96 observed candidate matches of the playback world T367 read, all sixteen tactic pairings), exactly as T367 recorded it:
    - 445 urgent presses at a delivery's kick, 311 of them to the taker: 209 after a header or shot, 92 after a ball out and 10 at short corners;
    - 219 of those 311 came from a man-marker whose runner still held his box spot.
    - Why: a delivery whose outcome hands the ball to nobody at the kick leaves the taker recorded on the ball (`apply` calls no `setOwner` for a header resolved there, a ball out or a foul). `inDanger` then read him within 30 m of goal, and the urgent press, which has no reach limit and counts a committed mark as free, sent the nearest defender to him.
  - The fix (dd7c7f0), candidate only. The pre-feature path never updates intentions.
    - `engine.ts`: the update that reads a restart's kick passes `inFlight` when the restart is a delivery (a corner, short or crossed, or a crossed free kick) and `apply` wrote no new owner (`setOwner` always writes a new pair, so the same pair means nobody took the ball).
    - `intentions.ts`: `updateIntentions(s, inFlight)` then reads no carrier: no urgent danger, and no plan. With no carrier `planCoordination` plans nothing, so it is not asked, and `coordination.ts` is untouched (T368's file).
    - The boundary is that update. The next update comes with the next action, after the delivery is resolved or lands, and for these outcomes that is a restart, whose `interruptAll` ends every job anyway.
    - A delivery that reaches someone is read exactly as before: a receiver, an interceptor, a second ball's winner, or the taker himself on a loose ball. The outcome rules and draws are untouched, and no new arithmetic is added.
  - Census after (the same 96 matches, `<scratch>/census.mts` on the branch):
