# T051 Contracts for sponsors by slot, player talks, terms and new delegations
> st:todo | ssot:ECON-8 CLUB-26 CLUB-27 STAFF-13 STAFF-14 STAFF-18 WEB-45 SAVE-10 | base:ECON@14 CLUB@17 STAFF@12 WEB@51 SAVE@21 | dep:T050 | touches:packages/contracts/src/types.ts packages/contracts/src/schema.ts packages/contracts/src/schema.contract.test.ts apps/web/src/runtime/protocol.ts apps/web/src/ui/ProgressControls.tsx | lane:base

## goal
Every type, schema field, command and delegation the three feature lanes need exists up front, with older saves migrating into it, so the lanes never edit contracts.

## acceptance
- [v] types.ts and schema.ts (strict, bounded), all optional for older saves:
  - `sponsors`: one contract per slot `{ slot, kind, annual, bonus, from, until }`;
  - on players, `talk` (demand, floor, asked day, kind renew or raise), `unhappy` (since day, reason) and `transferRequest`;
  - on manager and staff, the chosen term;
  - delegation keys `contracts` and `manager`;
  - `managerRule` ('school' | 'best' | 'strategy');
  - inbox kinds `contract-talk`, `unhappy`, `transfer-request`, `sponsor-ending`;
  - ledger kinds `league-share`, `league-merit`, `parachute`, `sponsor:<slot>`, `free-transfer-compensation`.
- [v] Commands are added in types.ts and protocol.ts:
  - `contract-offer {player, wage, term}`;
  - `contract-decline {player}`;
  - `sign-sponsor {slot, offer}`;
  - `renew-manager {term}` and `renew-staff {role, term}`;
  - `hire-manager` and `hire-staff` with `term`;
  - `manager-rule {rule}`.
  The engine may reject them until their lane implements them.
- [v] Load migration (schema.ts, like legacyVision): a pre-1.16 `sponsor` becomes the 메인 스폰서 contract, or 지역 후원 before that slot opens in its country (→T050), keeping amounts and end. Wages, terms and delegation stay as they were.
- [v] Intervention levels (ProgressControls.tsx, →WEB-45): `contracts` is delegated at levels 2–4, `manager` at level 4; new clubs delegate contracts (→STAFF-13).
- [v] schema.contract.test.ts: a 1.15 world with a sponsor loads with that sponsor as its slot's contract; the new fields round-trip; unknown keys are still refused.
- [v] `pnpm run typecheck && pnpm run test:unit && pnpm run test:contract` pass. Only the contract test's new cases are required; goldens are left to T062.

## result
- outcome: the contracts the commercial, players and staff lanes build on now exist. In types.ts and schema.ts every new field is optional, every new nested object is strict, years are bounded to 1901–4000, season days to 0–365, and amounts are non-negative decimal strings. `World.sponsors: SponsorContract[]` holds at most 5 contracts, one per slot, each `{ slot, name, kind, annual, bonus, from, until, lastPaid, index }` with `from ≤ until`; `SponsorSlot` is local/kit/shirt/stadium/sleeve and `SponsorKind` is stable/performance/exclusive/indexed. Players gain `talk: ContractTalk { kind: renew|raise, demand, floor, year, day }`, `raiseAsked` (the season of his last raise talk, for the once-a-season rule), `unhappy { reason: renewal-declined|raise-declined, year, day }` and `transferRequest { year, day }`. Manager and Staff gain `term?: ContractTerm` (1–5). `DelegationKey` gains `contracts` and `manager`; `World.managerRule` is school/best/strategy (absent means school). `InboxKind` gains contract-talk, unhappy, transfer-request and sponsor-ending. `LedgerKind` lists league-share, league-merit, parachute, `sponsor:<slot>` and free-transfer-compensation, while `Event.kind` stays a string. types.ts and protocol.ts add the commands `contract-offer {player, wage, term}`, `contract-decline {player}`, `sign-sponsor {slot, offer}` (the offer's index in that slot's list) and `manager-rule {rule}`, plus an optional `term` on `renew-manager`, `renew-staff`, `hire-staff` and `hire`; the manager hire stays `hire` (STAFF-17), so no separate `hire-manager` command was added. `delegate` accepts the new keys and `advance-days` the new stop kinds. `operate()` refuses the four new commands, and any chosen term, with an error naming them, and leaves the world unchanged. `validateWorld(input, catalog?)` moves a 1.15 `sponsor` into `sponsors`: to the shirt slot, or to local while that slot is not yet open in the club's country. It keeps name, kind, amounts, end, lastPaid and index, and sets `from` to the end minus the 2 or 4 seasons its offer ran. The slot year comes from an injected `LoadCatalog` because contracts never import catalogs (→ARCH-4); catalogs export `LOAD_CATALOG` and `persistence.decode` passes it. Without a catalog, a world that has a sponsor is refused rather than silently losing it; a corrupt 1.15 sponsor, or two contracts in one slot, fails as 후원 계약 손상. Contract talks are delegated at intervention levels 2–4 and the manager post at level 4; levels 2–4 stop for sponsor-ending along with cash. `createWorld` delegates contract talks, so a new club reads as level 2. Stop labels and defaults, inbox kind labels and outcome titles cover the new kinds.
