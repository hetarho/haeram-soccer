## goal
Catalog a football index per country and year and the year each sponsor slot opens per country, as catalog 2026-demo-2, with 2026-demo-1 worlds still loading.

## acceptance
- [ ] `packages/catalogs/data/football-index.json`: for each of the eight countries, a yearly index from 1901 with source and status (observed / estimated / projected) per span; after its coverage it projects like prices (→ECON-2).
- [ ] The index tracks the price index until commercialisation, then grows faster in real terms. ENG real multiple (index / price index, 1950 = 1): about 1 to 1979, 2 by 1992, 6 by 2005, 10 by 2020. ESP, GER and ITA reach about 70% of ENG's real growth, FRA 50%, POR, NED and BEL 25–35%. Each figure is labelled estimated, with its sources (e.g. Deloitte Annual Review of Football Finance, league reports) in the catalog notes.
- [ ] Sponsor-slot opening years per country (local partners always, kit supplier, shirt front, stadium naming, sleeve; ENG 1970 / 1979 / 1997 / 2017), with sources, marked estimated where they are.
- [ ] `catalogs/src/index.ts` exposes `footballIndex(code, year)` → `{ value, status }` and `sponsorSlotOpens(code, slot)`, cached like `priceIndex`.
- [ ] `scripts/validate-catalogs.ts` checks coverage, ordering and provenance of both tables; `pnpm run validate:catalogs` passes.
- [ ] CATALOG_VERSION is 2026-demo-2 with a regenerated CATALOG_HASH (`scripts/fingerprint-catalog.ts`). Decode accepts 2026-demo-1 envelopes by their recorded hash; writer activation moves the world's catalog pin to 2026-demo-2 (→SAVE-10). persistence.test.ts covers a demo-1 save loading, upgrading and round-tripping.
- [ ] `pnpm run typecheck && pnpm run test:unit` pass.
