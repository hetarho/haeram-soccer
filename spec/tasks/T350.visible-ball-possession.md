## goal
Let viewers identify the controlling team and ball carrier at the presented instant without opening details or player inspection.

## acceptance
- [ ] Show a readable possession-team cue beside the scoreboard and a distinct recorded-carrier marker on the pitch in ordinary observation. Team emphasis uses label/shape as well as colour, keeps shirt numbers and the ball readable, and works for either side and goalkeepers.
- [ ] Resolve the side from the recorded carrier or an authoritative currently presented action. In-flight passes/shots have no carrier marker; preserve a team cue only when the recorded action establishes it. Use neutral phase/contested/unknown states when possession is unavailable, without guessing from distance, a future receiver or frame.side's minute-leading action.
- [ ] Seeks, turnovers, T349's goal hold, hidden/resumed playback and match replacement keep the two cues synchronized with the displayed instant. Sparse historical playback never invents ownership or retains a stale marker.
- [ ] Add small `possessionCue.test.ts` cases for `home carrier`, `away carrier`, `keeper ownership`, `recorded flight side without a carrier`, `contested ball clears ownership`, `unknown history stays neutral`, `turnover changes both cues`, `seek and goal hold use presented facts`, and `minute leader is not current possession`.
- [ ] Extend `mobile-match-presentation.spec.ts` for normal-view ownership at 360/390 CSS px and desktop, both themes and reduced motion, with no details/inspection open. Run affected units, ARCH-30 fast checks, presentation-resource validation and build; leave browser execution to the batch gate.

## impl notes
- Baseline: `docs/benchmarks/set-piece-motion-261022.md`. Every nonempty sampled ownerId mapped to a recorded player (508 default, 2,954 candidate); current carrier text is gated by `details && inspect` in Pitch. Owner absence during flight is common and must not mean an automatic turnover.
- Add one pure `resolvePossessionCue` in `ui/possessionCue.ts`, consumed by both scoreboard and pitch painting. Reuse current presentation samples, recorded squads and timed action facts; ownership has one source, not separate nearest-player logic per visual.
- T349 is a dependency because it changes the same Pitch/clock surfaces and introduces the goal hold. Reuse its presented instant and transient lifecycle; no second playback clock, game command, saved preference or change to match outcomes.
- Use locale resources and semantic theme tokens. If existing action timing cannot establish an in-flight side reliably, show neutral flight status rather than extend saved history or infer a result.
