## goal
Each own match keeps its shot map, heat maps, pass network and tactical-report numbers for the current season; at the season close they become one season heat map per own player.

## acceptance
- [v] The grid engine fills `record.material` (design §9.4) for every match with an own side (`setup.own !== undefined`), observed or not: shots of both sides, own players' 40-cell touch counts plus both side totals, own pass network, both sides' report numbers.
- [v] `recordMatch` keeps `material` on the saved own record; `closeSeason` writes `SeasonArchive.heat[playerId]` (40 ints, the season sum for every own player with touches) and deletes `material` from that season's own matches, after archiving.
- [v] `packages/engine/src/material.ts` exports read helpers for the UI: `shotMap(record)`, `heatMap(record, playerId | 'own' | 'opponent')`, `passNetwork(record)`, `tacticalReport(record, side)` returning typed objects (decoded from the int arrays), and `seasonHeat(archive, playerId)`.
- [v] `material.test.ts` cases: `fills material for own matches only`, `counts touches per cell`, `keeps the pass network pairs that happened at least twice`, `reduces material to season heat maps at the close`, `decodes what it encodes`.
- [v] `pnpm exec tsx scripts/update-golden.ts --write`; typecheck, lint, unit, contract and the web packing round-trip test (`apps/web/src/adapters/packing.test.ts`) pass.

## impl notes
- Encoding is exactly design §9.4; coordinates in the shooter's attack frame × 10, rounded.
- Touch: an action start or a completed reception by that player (count once per event).
- Network: average position over his touches (attack frame, ×10), touches; pairs `[fromLine, toLine, completed]` for completed passes between own lines, only pairs with ≥ 2.
- Report per side (30 ints, order as design §9.4): lane of the entry point for final-third entries (x crosses 70) and box entries; regains and losses by third of the point (x < 35, < 70, else) in that side's frame; set-piece shots/goals = shots within 10 s of a corner or free-kick restart; aerials (won/total), take-ons (won/total), tackles (won/total), counter-attacks (possessions with phase `counter` at start), counter shots, high regains (x ≥ 70), crosses into the box, cut-backs, through balls.
- HIST-8: this reduction is the defined lifetime of material (HIST-16), not pruning of retained facts.

## result
- outcome: `grid/record.ts` adds `MaterialTally`, typed arrays sized at kick-off (own touches per cell and per roster index, summed touch positions, a passer × receiver matrix, 60 report counters, shots as plain ints), created by `Tally` when `setup.own` is set with recorded players and fed from its existing counters (passes, entries, regains, take-ons, dribbles, tackles) plus five one-line hooks in `engine.ts` (action-start touch in open play with the carrier's phase, restart-taker touch, completed reception with its passer, shot with its diagnostic point and set-piece flag, aerial winner); `finish` maps roster indexes to lines and writes `record.material` in the §9.4 layout for every own match, observed or not (equal either way); `resolveCross` marks the outcomes an aerial duel decided (`aerial`: the winning side); `material.ts` holds the layout (`MATERIAL_LAYOUT`, `REPORT_SLOTS`, `SHOT_OUTCOMES`), the decoders `shotMap`, `heatMap(record, id | 'own' | 'opponent', own?)`, `passNetwork`, `tacticalReport(record, side)`, `seasonHeat`, `ownSideOf`, and `reduceSeasonMaterial(w, archive)`, which `closeSeason` calls right after archiving: each own player's season cell sums go to `SeasonArchive.heat` (players with touches only) and `material` is deleted from that season's own matches; `recordMatch` already copies the record with `material` (unchanged); contracts, schema and web packing already carried `material` and `heat`.

- limits: a shot's line is its index among the shooting side's lines (`lineCounts` offset), because a saved own record keeps only the own lines; opponent shots on a saved record keep their line but no player id; a record straight from the engine keeps both sides' lines, so finding an own player's heat or network names needs the own side (`ownSideOf(record, ownClub)`); a touch is an action start or a completed pass reception (a header shot counts as the header taker's touch; a first-time shot after a cut-back is its reception only); final-third and box entries are completed passes and carries only, by the lane they end in; crosses, cut-backs and through balls are attempts; a tackle attempt is a tackle won or a take-on lost to; shots past 80 and network pairs past 300 are not kept (schema caps); outside the touches: `packages/engine/src/grid/resolve.ts` (the `aerial` mark on cross outcomes; the outcome does not otherwise show whether a duel happened) and the one import line of `season.ts`.
