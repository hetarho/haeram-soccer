## goal
Each own match keeps its shot map, heat maps, pass network and tactical-report numbers for the current season; at the season close they become one season heat map per own player.

## acceptance
- [ ] The grid engine fills `record.material` (design §9.4) for every match with an own side (`setup.own !== undefined`), observed or not: shots of both sides, own players' 40-cell touch counts plus both side totals, own pass network, both sides' report numbers.
- [ ] `recordMatch` keeps `material` on the saved own record; `closeSeason` writes `SeasonArchive.heat[playerId]` (40 ints, the season sum for every own player with touches) and deletes `material` from that season's own matches, after archiving.
- [ ] `packages/engine/src/material.ts` exports read helpers for the UI: `shotMap(record)`, `heatMap(record, playerId | 'own' | 'opponent')`, `passNetwork(record)`, `tacticalReport(record, side)` returning typed objects (decoded from the int arrays), and `seasonHeat(archive, playerId)`.
- [ ] `material.test.ts` cases: `fills material for own matches only`, `counts touches per cell`, `keeps the pass network pairs that happened at least twice`, `reduces material to season heat maps at the close`, `decodes what it encodes`.
- [ ] `pnpm exec tsx scripts/update-golden.ts --write`; typecheck, lint, unit, contract and the web packing round-trip test (`apps/web/src/adapters/packing.test.ts`) pass.

## impl notes
- Encoding is exactly design §9.4; coordinates in the shooter's attack frame × 10, rounded.
- Touch: an action start or a completed reception by that player (count once per event).
- Network: average position over his touches (attack frame, ×10), touches; pairs `[fromLine, toLine, completed]` for completed passes between own lines, only pairs with ≥ 2.
- Report per side (30 ints, order as design §9.4): lane of the entry point for final-third entries (x crosses 70) and box entries; regains and losses by third of the point (x < 35, < 70, else) in that side's frame; set-piece shots/goals = shots within 10 s of a corner or free-kick restart; aerials (won/total), take-ons (won/total), tackles (won/total), counter-attacks (possessions with phase `counter` at start), counter shots, high regains (x ≥ 70), crosses into the box, cut-backs, through balls.
- HIST-8: this reduction is the defined lifetime of material (HIST-16), not pruning of retained facts.
