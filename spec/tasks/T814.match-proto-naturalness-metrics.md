- Shared definitions (add them to `Context`, worked out once):
  - open frames: frame indexes `f >= 1` whose frame is `live`, whose `phase` is not `setPiece` and whose beat (`beatOf.get(frame.beat)`) exists and is not one of `RESTART_KINDS`; kept as an ascending list and as a set. Open minutes = open frames × `tick` / 60.
  - a player's speed at frame `f` = `dist(pos(frames[f], i), pos(frames[f - 1], i)) / tick`; his x-velocity = the x difference / `tick`.
  - outfield = both teams' players without slot 0 (20 players).
- The metrics:
  - `events.shortKick`: over `kick` events whose beat has `kind === 'pass'` and whose `other` is defined; a hit is a kick with `dist(pos(frame, actor), pos(frame, other)) < L.shortKick.distance` in the event's frame. `rate(...)` against `L.shortKick.share` (empty → 0, passes).
  - `shape.huddle` and `shape.huddleLongest`: a pair is close in an open frame when two outfield teammates stand nearer than `L.huddle.distance` and are not both inside the same penalty area (both `inEitherBox` and both on the same side of `HALFWAY`). An episode is a maximal run of consecutive frame indexes, all open, in which the same pair is close; its length is its frame count × `tick`. `shape.huddle` = episodes longer than `L.huddle.seconds` per open minute (0 with no open frame), `atMost` `L.huddle.perMinute`, detail `n=<long>/<episodes> minutes=<open minutes>`. `shape.huddleLongest` = the longest episode's seconds (0 with none), `atMost` `L.huddle.longest`, detail `pair=<label>-<label> t=<start seconds>`.
  - `motion.restarts`: for each outfield player walk the open frames in ascending order with a flag `resting` (false at first, never reset between runs of open frames): a frame with speed below `L.motion.rest` sets it; a frame with speed above `L.motion.go` while it is set is a start and clears it. Value = starts / (20 × open minutes) (0 with no open frame), `atMost` `L.motion.restarts`, detail `starts=<n>`.
  - `motion.startSync`: with `w = Math.round(L.motion.window / tick)`, a start at frame `f` is in step when at least `L.motion.together` starts of any players (itself included) lie at frames `f - w … f + w`. `rate(inStep, starts, L.motion.startSync)`.
  - `motion.speedSync`: the Pearson correlation of the speed series over the open frames, averaged over all 190 pairs of outfield players; a pair with a series of zero variance counts 0; 0 with fewer than 2 open frames. `atMost` `L.motion.speedSync`. Detail `bands=<still>/<walk>/<jog>/<run>/<sprint>`: the shares (3 decimals) of all outfield speed samples below `L.motion.rest`, below `(motion.walk + motion.jog) / 2`, below `(motion.jog + motion.run) / 2`, up to `motion.run + 0.3` and above it.
  - turnovers: an open frame `f` whose `team` differs from frame `f - 1`'s, with frame `f - 1` `live`, `f - Math.round(L.turn.before / tick) >= 1` and `f + Math.round(Math.max(L.turn.within, L.turn.runAt) / tick)` inside the playback. It is a counter when `frames[f].phase === 'counter'`, else settled.
  - `turn.reverse`: over settled turnovers and outfield players. A mover's mean x-velocity over the `Math.round(L.turn.before / tick)` frames before `f` has an absolute value of at least `L.turn.moving`; he has turned when some frame `f … f + Math.round(L.turn.within / tick)` has an x-velocity of the opposite sign and at least `L.turn.moving` in size. `rate(turned, movers, L.turn.reverseShare)`, detail extended with `turnovers=<n>`.
  - `turn.run`: for each settled turnover the share of outfield players faster than `L.turn.runSpeed` at frame `f + Math.round(L.turn.runAt / tick)`; the value is the `median` over the turnovers (0 with none), `atMost` `L.turn.runShare`, detail `turnovers=<n>`.
  - `turn.counterRun`: the same over counters, `atLeast` `L.turn.counterRunShare`; with no counter the value is 1 and it passes with detail `turnovers=0 empty`.
  - `mark.near`: over the open frames whose index is a multiple of 5. A sample is an outfield player of the frame's `team` who is not its `owner` and whose attack x (`toAttack(team, pos)[0]`) is at least `L.mark.fromX`; a hit has an outfield opponent within `L.mark.distance`. `share(..., L.mark.share, 'min')` (empty → 1, passes).
