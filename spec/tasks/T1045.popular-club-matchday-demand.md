## impl notes
- Read `gateProjection` and `recentForm` in finance.ts, `localSupporters` and `fanMoodFollowing` in fans.ts, and the `clubView` and gate callers in books.ts. Use the existing owner identity condition `w.owner?.takeover.clubId === w.playerClub` for the new path. The ownerless and other-club views retain the current catchment, ticket exponent and range order. Preserve the NPC book code, read shape and amounts.
- Use the existing stored following and local supporter count. Expose the expected travelling part separately on the shared projection as `travellingSupporters` with zero on the retained path. Keep `supporters` for the local count and use their sum for the matchday pool. International recognition already feeds the following via ECON-23/34.
- Define tunable `FINANCE_CONFIG.travellingShare` and `FINANCE_CONFIG.premiumEasing`. Start at 0.45 of followers outside the catchment and a gain of 8. The latter scales the amount by which default-price demand divided by capacity exceeds 1. Divide only the premium above `DEFAULT_TICKET` by 1 plus that scaled excess, then use the resulting effective price in the existing portable exponential. Below default and up to pressure 1 the response is the existing one. This keeps demand increasing with following even at the cheapest preset.
- Keep the context multipliers and cap wanted demand by the pool before applying the existing 80–100% spread. Clamp each bound to usable capacity after the spread on the owned path, so `gate` in settlement.ts can record a real sellout with its existing seeded draw. Matchday spend, `leagueMatchFactor`, match costs and the paid preset continue to use the real match inputs.
- Keep ticket drafts, cash projections and settlement on the existing `gateProjection` path. The expanded-stadium task checks the same behavior at 105,000. The ECON-29 probe measures and confirms the starting coefficients against both ownership fixtures.
