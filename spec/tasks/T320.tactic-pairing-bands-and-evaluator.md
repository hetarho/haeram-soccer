## goal
Give both engines' measurement reports one versioned definition and verdict of MATCH-29's tactic-pairing bands.

## acceptance
- [ ] `scripts/lib/match-parity.ts` defines `TACTIC_PAIRING_TARGET` holding every MATCH-29 row (pairing draw band, win-share gap, counter against press, points within the four tactics' mean, possession, xG-conceded, high-regain and end-of-match freshness orderings); no other file repeats its numbers.
- [ ] `tacticPairingVerdicts(samples)` pools each pairing's two venues from `MatchSample`s, reuses the library's 95% interval helpers and returns each row as inside, outside or insufficient, with an ordering holding only when every difference exceeds its interval. Any measure `MatchSample` lacks (possession share, xG conceded, high regains, end-of-match freshness) is added from the complete record both paths already produce, not estimated.
- [ ] `scripts/grid-bands.ts --report` prints and writes the MATCH-29 verdicts for its tactics family in the detailed-model artifact; `scripts/match-parity.ts` exposes the same verdicts for the aggregate adapter. Both carry the target's version in their output.
- [ ] `scripts/lib/match-parity.test.ts` covers `pools both venues of a pairing`, `flags a pairing outside the draw band`, `requires counter at least press head to head`, `holds an ordering only beyond its interval` and `reports insufficient samples rather than a pass` with small synthetic samples.
- [ ] No engine behaviour or registered MATCH-23/MATCH-28 value changes; task checks follow ARCH-30.

## impl notes
- This is the shared foundation T321 (detailed tuning) and T284 (aggregate tuning) both consume, so it lands first and stays small: data, evaluator, report wiring and tests only. Corpus runs are not part of this task.
- Follow the library's existing `OUTCOME_TARGET` and `METRIC_REGISTRY` shapes and version naming; bump `MATCH_PARITY_VERSION` if a report field changes.
- Tactics come from the sample's per-side tactic (grid-bands already labels its tactics family `[home, away]`); a pairing is unordered, its venues are the two orders.
