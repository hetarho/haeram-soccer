## goal
A declined player turns unhappy unless the manager talks him round, then plays worse, drags morale and asks to leave; an unrenewed contract ends at the close with Bosman rules; the squad shows it all.

## acceptance
- [v] On decline: the talk-round chance is 15% + 45% for a motivator + (trust − 50)/200 + (flexibility − 50)/400, within 5–85%, from the player's stream for that talk. A failed talk sets `unhappy`. Both outcomes are inbox items.
- [v] Unhappy players:
  - play 3 below their ability in match strength and duels;
  - cost squad morale 1 each settled round they start;
  - refuse renewal talks;
  - after 6 weeks raise `transferRequest`: the stance becomes 이적 명단 and 이적 불가 is refused for him (→CLUB-22).
- [v] Meeting his floor later (`contract-offer`) or selling him ends it.
- [v] Expiry at the close (→CLUB-28): an unrenewed player leaves. From 1996 he goes on a free; before 1996 his new club pays 50% of his value as `free-transfer-compensation`. A retirement announcement needs no renewal.
- [v] Squad cards show wage against market wage, contract end and an 언해피 badge with its reason. The contract-talk sheet shows the demand, the floor, a term of 1–5 seasons, the yearly cost, the runway after it and the talk-round chance (→WEB-68). Event cards cover talks, unhappy players and transfer requests (→WEB-42).
- [v] contracts.test.ts cases:
  - a motivator talks a player round more often over 2,000 seeded talks;
  - an unhappy starter plays 3 lower and costs morale;
  - the transfer request comes after 6 weeks;
  - an expiring 1990 contract brings compensation and a 2000 one does not.
- [v] A Chromium e2e journey covers a contract talk: decline, the badge, then the transfer request.
- [v] `pnpm run typecheck && pnpm run test:unit` pass.

## impl notes
- The ability penalty applies where strength reads player skills (world.ts rating/selection), never to stored attributes.

## result
- outcome: A declined player now turns unhappy unless the manager talks him round (→CLUB-27). The owner's `contract-decline` and a delegated staff decline both go through `declineTalk` in contracts.ts. `talkRoundChance` = 15% + 45% for a 동기부여형 manager + (trust − 50)/200 + (flexibility − 50)/400, clamped to 5–85%. `talksRound` draws from `seed:talk-round:<player>:<talk year>:<talk day>`. The decline event states the chance and the result. A failed talk sets `unhappy {reason, year, day}` and posts an attention `unhappy` inbox item. When an owner's decline is talked round, the result is a non-attention `contract-talk` item; for a staff decline it goes into the staff report. Unhappy players play 3 below their ability (`UNHAPPY_PENALTY`, `playingAbility` in world.ts): this applies to `rating`, to automatic and replacement selection, and to every duel skill in match.ts `matchSkills`. Stored attributes stay. `settleMorale` takes 1 per unhappy starter each settled round; an older save without morale starts from 60 the first time this applies. An unhappy player opens no talks. `transferRequests` runs each club day: after 42 days, counted across the summer, it sets `transferRequest` and stance 이적 명단 and posts an attention `transfer-request` item. `setTransferStance` refuses 이적 불가 for him. `contract-offer` at or above the player's current floor (`talkFor`: the open talk, or an unhappy player's floor as it stands today) signs and ends the unhappiness and the request. When the request had listed him, the listing goes too. A sale (`completeSale`) clears the talk, the unhappiness and the request, and settles their items. At the close, `expireContracts` runs after the year turns: every non-loan player with `until <= year` leaves (status sold). A club is drawn for him with `suitorFor` from `seed:expiry:<player>:<year>`. From 1996 the event is `contract-expiry` with no money. Before 1996 the club is credited 50% of his market value at the division he played in, as `free-transfer-compensation`, with the ledger line 계약 만료 보상금. An announced retirement closes his open talk on the day of the announcement (`contractTalks` runs after `announceRetirements`), and he retires as before. Roster cards show 연봉 / 시장 (market wage), the contract end and flags `언해피 · <reason>` and `이적 요청`. A 협상 badge opens `ContractTalkSheet` (Rich.tsx). The sheet shows demand, floor, a 1–5 season term dial, the yearly cost against the current wage, the contract end, the runway after it, and (for an open talk) the manager's chance with what a failure costs. Its actions are 거절 and 하한 · N시즌 제시. Event cards for `contract-talk`, `unhappy` and `transfer-request` open that sheet (협상하기 / 하한 제시하기) through `openContractTalk` in state.ts. The market's offer board marks an offer for a player who asked to leave.
