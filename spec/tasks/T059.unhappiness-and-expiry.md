## goal
A declined player turns unhappy unless the manager talks him round, then plays worse, drags morale and asks to leave; an unrenewed contract ends at the close with Bosman rules; the squad shows it all.

## acceptance
- [ ] On decline: the talk-round chance is 15% + 45% for a motivator + (trust − 50)/200 + (flexibility − 50)/400, within 5–85%, from the player's stream for that talk. A failed talk sets `unhappy`. Both outcomes are inbox items.
- [ ] Unhappy players:
  - play 3 below their ability in match strength and duels;
  - cost squad morale 1 each settled round they start;
  - refuse renewal talks;
  - after 6 weeks raise `transferRequest`: the stance becomes 이적 명단 and 이적 불가 is refused for him (→CLUB-22).
- [ ] Meeting his floor later (`contract-offer`) or selling him ends it.
- [ ] Expiry at the close (→CLUB-28): an unrenewed player leaves. From 1996 he goes on a free; before 1996 his new club pays 50% of his value as `free-transfer-compensation`. A retirement announcement needs no renewal.
- [ ] Squad cards show wage against market wage, contract end and an 언해피 badge with its reason. The contract-talk sheet shows the demand, the floor, a term of 1–5 seasons, the yearly cost, the runway after it and the talk-round chance (→WEB-68). Event cards cover talks, unhappy players and transfer requests (→WEB-42).
- [ ] contracts.test.ts cases:
  - a motivator talks a player round more often over 2,000 seeded talks;
  - an unhappy starter plays 3 lower and costs morale;
  - the transfer request comes after 6 weeks;
  - an expiring 1990 contract brings compensation and a 2000 one does not.
- [ ] A Chromium e2e journey covers a contract talk: decline, the badge, then the transfer request.
- [ ] `pnpm run typecheck && pnpm run test:unit` pass.

## impl notes
- The ability penalty applies where strength reads player skills (world.ts rating/selection), never to stored attributes.
