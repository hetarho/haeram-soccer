## goal
Cards turn into bans by the English-style rule, bans and injuries keep players out of every club's XI and bench, and own players' bans reach the inbox.

## acceptance
- [ ] `packages/engine/src/discipline.ts` exports `competitionOf(f)`, `applyDiscipline(w, record)`, `serveBans(w, f)`, `isUnavailable(w, p | id, clubId, f)`, `recordNpcInjury(w, ...)`, `yellowWarning(w, p)`, `closeDisciplineSeason(w)` per design §13.3; `World.discipline` is created lazily.
- [ ] Thresholds exactly design §13.3 (league 5/10/15 by round share, cups and Europe every 3rd yellow, red reasons 1/1/2/3 matches, domestic vs Europe scope).
- [ ] Selection: `ownStarters(w, f?)`, `lineupPreset` and `buildGridSetup`'s bench skip unavailable players (injured or banned for the fixture's competition); `startingSquad(w, c, f?)` for clubs nobody owns replaces an unavailable starter with the best available squad place 11–17 of the same line (by `slotOvr` at the slot), else any available place; `prepareMatch(w, f)` passes `f` down. `lineupPreset` also stops picking injured players (existing bug).
- [ ] An own player's new ban is an inbox item (`staff-report` kind, like injuries) and an event naming matches and scope.
- [ ] Season close: `closeDisciplineSeason` resets `yellows`, keeps `bans`; the NPC `out` list is cleared where NPC squads are regenerated for the new season (`prepareSeason`).
- [ ] `discipline.test.ts` cases: `bans for five league yellows before mid-season but not after`, `bans two matches for ten yellows before 84% of the rounds and three for fifteen`, `bans one cup match every third cup yellow and keeps league and cup counts apart`, `bans three domestic matches for violent conduct, one for a second yellow or DOGSO, two for dissent`, `serves domestic bans in league and cup matches but not in Europe`, `carries an unserved ban into next season and resets yellows`, `leaves a banned own player out of the XI and the bench`, `replaces an unavailable NPC starter from the bench places`, `warns a player one yellow short of a league ban`.
- [ ] `pnpm run typecheck`, `pnpm run lint`, `pnpm run test:unit` pass (callers changed but no fixture produces cards yet unless grid matches do — they do for own matches; regenerate the golden if it changes: `pnpm exec tsx scripts/update-golden.ts --write`, then `pnpm run test:contract`).

## impl notes
- Types are already in contracts (T202): `DisciplineState`, `Ban`, `Unavailable`, `CompetitionKey`.
- `competitionOf(f)`: `league` for kinds `league`, `lower`, `playoff`; `` `cup:${f.groupKey}` `` for `cup`; `europe` for `europe`. Domestic scope covers `league` and every `cup:*`.
- League rounds of a club: the number of rounds of its group's league fixtures this season (count `w.fixtures` with that `groupKey` and the club as home or away, or the round maximum); use `f.round` of the match that produced the yellow.
- `applyDiscipline(w, record)` reads `record.incidents` (cards only): yellow → `yellows[player][comp]++` then threshold check; `secondYellow` → also counts that yellow (MATCH-19 counts it) and a 1-match ban; `red` → ban by reason. Ban `club` = the player's club in that fixture (home/away by incident side). Do not double-apply: skip a record already applied (`w.ownMatches` dedupe in `recordMatch` covers own; for NPC fixtures `applyDiscipline` is only called once from `recordMatch` per fixture).
- `serveBans(w, f)`: for each of `f.home`, `f.away`, every ban of that club whose scope covers `competitionOf(f)` and that existed before this fixture (do not serve a ban created by this same match) loses one match; remove at 0. Implementation: call `serveBans` before `applyDiscipline` for the same fixture.
- `isUnavailable`: own players → `isInjured(w, p)` or a ban covering the competition; NPC → `out` entry with `until` in the future (`dayCountedFrom`) or a ban.
- Inbox/event: follow `injure()` in `careers.ts` for `pushInbox`/`addEvent` call shapes; text e.g. `${name} 출장 정지 ${n}경기 (${scopeLabel}) — ${reasonLabel}`.
- `yellowWarning(w, p)`: true when the player's league yellows are 4, 9 or 14 and the next threshold still applies at the current round.
- `recordNpcInjury(w, playerId, clubId, days)` pushes `{ player, club, until }`; T220 calls it.
