## goal
On the candidate model every player is drawn moving at his recorded pace to his place after the ball goes dead and standing there until the kick, and each frame names its stoppages (dead, settled, kick) for the presentation clock — presentation only, no recorded fact changes.

## acceptance
- [ ] During `prepareRestart` (`grid/movement.ts`) the observed trace records the players' positions every `PLAYBACK_CONFIG.sampleSpacing` match seconds from the ball going dead until every player has settled, plus the settling instant; an unobserved match records nothing extra and its facts are unchanged.
- [ ] `buildFrames` (`grid/playback.ts`) draws those samples as breakpoints, so nobody glides across the whole dead interval on one straight line: on the 16 probe fixtures (4 seeds × 4 tactic pairings, as `spec/ideation/done/dead-balls-and-finishing.md` measured) the median drawn speed of players moving over 3 m in a dead interval is at least 2 m per match second for throw-ins, goal kicks, corners, free kicks, kick-offs after a goal, penalties and drop balls (was 0.10–0.78), and a player who has reached his kick position moves less than 0.2 m between his arrival sample and the kick.
- [ ] Every candidate frame lists its stoppages: `MatchFrame.stoppages` holds one `{ kind, dead, settled, kick }` per restart whose kick lies in that frame, with `dead ≤ settled ≤ kick`; kinds cover throw-in, goal kick, corner, free kick (offside's included), penalty, kick-off after a goal and drop ball. A period's opening kick-off is a period transition and is not listed.
- [ ] Tests: `playback.test.ts` "dead time draws players hurrying then standing" (drawn speed and standing checks above on at least two observed fixtures) and "frames list each restart's stoppage in order" (one per restart entry, ordered, bounds hold); `movement.test.ts` "prepareRestart samples until everyone settles" (a two-player scene: samples stop after the last arrival, the settle instant recorded).
- [ ] The facts digests of `grid-throughput.test.ts` and `full-model-throughput.test.ts` are unchanged; the positions digests that move are re-recorded with the old values in their history comments and a one-line reason (dead-time samples, presentation only).
- [ ] ARCH-30 task tier: typecheck, lint, format check and the changed unit tests pass; integration and e2e are left to the final batch.

## impl notes
- Sampling hook: give `advance(s, dt, plan, onStep?)` an optional callback called after each whole step with the elapsed seconds since its start; `prepareRestart` passes one only when the restart preparer was given a sampler. Extend `prepareOnClock(rest, out, kick?, walk?)` with `walk?: (s: GridState) => void`, called at the dead moment, then at every `PLAYBACK_CONFIG.sampleSpacing` boundary while any player moved more than `PLAYBACK_CONFIG.settle` metres since the previous sample, and once at the step where the last player stopped (the settle instant). Define `PLAYBACK_CONFIG.settle = 0.2` (metres). Targets are read once per `advance`, so players arrive and stop within one preparation.
- Trace: add `TraceEntry.walk?: TraceKick[]` (same layout as `kick`), filled by `Tracer.walk(s)` like `Tracer.kick` and attached to the restart's entry; the engine passes `tracer.walk` where it passes `tracer.kick` (`engine.ts`, the candidate restart path). Absent on the pre-feature path and on older traces.
- Playback: in `buildFrames`, add each walk sample's `elapsed` to the candidate breakpoints and draw it like a kick snapshot (always drawn, however close), reading positions from the walk sample whose time it is; the taker's route (`P.paths`) keeps precedence for the taker.
- Contract: `MatchFrame.stoppages?: { kind: 'throwIn' | 'goalKick' | 'corner' | 'freeKick' | 'penalty' | 'kickoff' | 'dropBall'; dead: number; settled: number; kick: number }[]` in `packages/contracts/src/types.ts`, times in seconds relative to the frame's start (`dead` and `settled` may be negative when the ball went dead in an earlier minute of the period). `dead` is the elapsed of the last non-incident entry before the restart (cards, injuries and substitutions in the stoppage lie inside it); `settled` the last walk sample, else `dead`; `kick` the kick snapshot's elapsed, else the entry's. `indirectFreeKick` maps to `freeKick`. Frames are not part of any checkpoint schema (`matchAppearanceSchema` rejects them), so no save change.
- No match-model revision: nothing the engine resolves or records changes; only observed traces and frames grow.
