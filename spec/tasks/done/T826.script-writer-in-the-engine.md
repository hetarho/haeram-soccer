# T826 the script writer in the engine: a detail written as an order of play, exact by construction

## goal
The engine writes a script from a match's detail: every recorded row played exactly once by its recorded player in its recorded cell, shots and booked fouls in their minute, every other row where the order of play reaches it while its player is on the pitch — and a count of the script that gives the record back, on arithmetic that rounds the same on every platform.

## impl notes
- Source: `scripts/match-proto/fit/` (`touches.ts`, `write.ts`, `validate.ts`, `count.ts`, `cells.ts`) and the `fit` and `grammar` values they read in `core/config.ts`, as they stand on the task's base. Port the rules T822 arrived at — only shots, their sources and booked fouls keep a minute (`dealFixed`), every other row floats, the next touch is the cheapest of the whole match (`touchCost`), a minute plays its even share, a side spending its possession-ending rows faster loses the ball by a heavy touch — and keep their unit tests.
- `packages/contracts/src/match-script.ts` (the script crosses the worker boundary in T834): `ScriptBeat` is the prototype's `Beat` (`core/types.ts`) with `team` as `side`, `actor`/`receiver`/`opponent` as record line indexes of their side, points in pitch metres, `event`/`shot`/`foul` ids into the detail, `clock` in match seconds, `period`; `MatchScript { version: 1; seed: string; beats: ScriptBeat[]; bound: true }`. No authored prose in either.
- `write.ts`: `export function writeScript(detail: MatchDetail, seed: string): { script: MatchScript } | { problems: string[] }`. The seed names the script's one random stream through `primitives.ts` `random` (`${seed}:script`); the caller passes the fixture's seed (T833).
- Platform-stable arithmetic (→ARCH-9): cell-to-cell metres come from one table of integer centimetres built once from the cell centres (`Math.round(Math.sqrt(dx*dx + dy*dy) * 100)`), costs are integers, ties break by row id; a point inside a cell is drawn with the stream and `+ − × ÷` only. The prototype's `Math.hypot` and float comparisons go; that the result differs from the prototype's in places is expected and is why the cost line above has a 2% margin.
- New against the prototype — windows: a floating row is eligible only in a minute of its `period` in which its actor and, when it names one, its winner are on the pitch (`detail.stints`); regulation rows are dealt over both halves by the order of play as today; a minute's fixed touches wait for nobody. When the order of play reaches a period's end with eligible rows unplayed, the writer plays them before the whistle through the same cost rule (the prototype's rule for a restart owed at the whistle shows the pattern); if a row is still unplaceable the result is `problems`.
- `validate.ts` `validateScript(detail, script): string[]` — the prototype's checks plus the windows. `count.ts`: `countScript` returns rows in the layout `recordOf(detail)` uses (T825) so `compareRecord` compares every `M`, `TD` and `PD` value, not the prototype's short rows; `scriptCost` is the prototype's `fitCost`.
- `SCRIPT_CONFIG` holds every value the writer reads (`hold`, `dead`, `carryWindow`, `cellMargin`, `fixedPull`, `fixedPush`, `paceBias`, `paceSlack`, `endsSlack`, `carryJoin`, `minPass`, …) under the prototype's names and values.
- Unit tier: one script is about a quarter of a second; keep the ten-fixture cases in one file and add nothing to the integration list for it.
- Not in this task: incidents and special scenes (T827), staging (T828), speed (T837).
