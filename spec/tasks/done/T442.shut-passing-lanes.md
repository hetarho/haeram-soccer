## goal
On the candidate model, the presser closes the carrier on a curve that keeps his most dangerous nearby option in his cover shadow, and a free midfielder or defender screens the lane into a receiver between the lines or in the half-space (MATCH-32 r28).

## acceptance
- [v] `lanes.ts` exports `SHADOW_CONFIG` and `dangerousOption(s, side, carrier)`: among the carrier's teammates within `SHADOW_CONFIG.near` (25 m), the one with the highest xT gain whose path `reachOnPath` (T441) finds unreached, or none.
- [v] Press with a cover shadow: the presser's tracked target moves from 1.2 m goal-side of the carrier toward the carrier–option line by up to `SHADOW_CONFIG.curve` (1.5 m), so that he approaches between them; with no dangerous option, or when the carrier is within `danger` of goal (the urgent press), the target is unchanged.
- [v] Screening: one free defending player per carrier, the nearest midfielder or defender whose shape place is within `SHADOW_CONFIG.screen.reach` (10 m) of the lane to a receiver between the lines or in a half-space (MATCH-15's tags), takes a tactical `screen` job: he stands on that lane at most `SHADOW_CONFIG.screen.step` (6 m) from his shape place, inside his line's bounds, never the rest defence's last man nor a marker of a runner; it ends `cleared` when the carrier plays the ball or the receiver leaves the lane. It is a new `INTENTION_KINDS` entry `screen` (observation only, never archived) with its reason in `INTENTION_REASONS`, its trace mapping and its inspector label 길목 차단 in `Pitch.tsx`, one job per player (§4.6).
- [v] Each tactic shuts lanes from its own block: the screen and the shadow apply only where its press reach applies (`SHAPE_CONFIG` press and counter-press reach by tactic, MATCH-8).
- [v] Tests: `lanes.test.ts` "the dangerous option is the unreached teammate with the highest threat gain"; `coordination.test.ts` "the presser approaches between the carrier and his dangerous option", "one free midfielder screens the lane to a receiver between the lines", "no screen leaves the last defender or a marker's runner", "an urgent press keeps its goal-side target"; `intentions.test.ts` "a screen ends cleared when the ball is played"; every new record passes `validateFullMatchRecord`; candidate digests re-recorded where they change, with the measured change in the test comment; the pre-feature path is unchanged (its digests pass untouched).
- [v] The result reports, on one calibration round against the pre-change source (the next unused round in docs/CALIBRATION.md), per team a match: passes and completion, passes into the space between the lines completed, interceptions, tackles + interceptions, PPDA and high turnovers, final-third passes, front-of-box and box entries, shots, xG, goals and MATCH-29's tactic rows (reported, not gated: MATCH-28 r23).
- [v] Task checks (ARCH-30): typecheck, lint, format:check, `vitest run --changed`; the release-on unit files this touches pass under `vitest.release-on.config.ts` as well.

## impl notes
- The jobs go through `planCoordination`/`applyCoordination` like every other job (one snapshot, ranked by cost, ties by player ID, draws from named streams), so a plan changes nothing until applied.
- Shutting a lane is what makes T441's reach matter in play: the screen puts a reaching opponent on the path, it does not change the duel. No interception share or completion number changes here.
- T432 later moves free players from their slots on revision 2; the screen's step stays bounded by the shape place so the two do not fight.
- If bands.test's coarse guards fail, widen them only to the measured level with this task's evidence in the comment; T437 restores them before T286. Do not request a decision for that.

  - `grid/lanes.ts` exports `SHADOW_CONFIG` (`near` 25, `curve` 1.5, `screen.reach` 10, `screen.step` 6) and `dangerousOption(s, side, carrier)`, `side` being the carrier's. It takes his teammates within 25 m whose pass gains threat (`threatGain`, the cells' xT difference, more than none: a backward or flat pass is the one the defence leaves open), highest gain first and equal gains by player ID. It returns the first whose path nobody reaches, or none. The reach test is `pathReached`, which shares `reachOnPath`'s path and runner set-up (`aim`, `runAt`) and stops at the first opponent within stretch. That is exactly `reachOnPath(...).first !== undefined`, and every T441 digest still passes.
  - Cover shadow: a press intention carries `shadow` (the option's roster index). `pressPoint` in `shape.ts` bends the 1.2 m goal-side point toward the point of the carrier–option line as far from the carrier (never past the option), by at most `curve` (`coverShadow`). A defender's line clamp is applied after the bend. `intentionTarget` passes the option only for a non-urgent press whose option is on the pitch. The plan gives a new tactical press its option and keeps or changes an existing presser's (`CoordinationPlan.shadows`, applied in `applyCoordination`).
    - A press holds its option while it stays a nearby option that gains threat (MATCH-17's retention). The presser's own shadow is what makes its path reached, so re-picking each action would flip him between two lanes.
    - No shadow in a danger (the urgent press, the carrier within 30 m or a runner in behind) and none for a presser beyond the press reach (by tactic, counter-press reach after a loss). An escalated press drops its shadow.
  - Screen: a new `screen` intention kind (`INTENTION_KINDS`, observation only), tactical, 6 s at most (`INTENTION_CONFIG.seconds.screen`), with `opponent` = the carrier and `shadow` = the receiver.
    - It is planned only while some presser is within the press reach of the carrier (MATCH-8's block), and only if no teammate already screens him. The receivers are outfield teammates whose pass gains threat and who stand in a half-space or between the opponents' lines in the central lane (`screenable`, as `computeTags` reads between the lines), minus any option a presser shadows. They are taken by gain, then ID.
    - Eligible screeners are free midfielders and defenders (no intention, not claimed in this plan, so no marker), never the side's deepest outfielder by position. A man is eligible when his shape place lies between the lane's ends within 10 m of it. The first receiver with an eligible man gets the one whose position is nearest his screen point.
    - The screen point (`screenPoint`) is the foot of the lane from his shape place, at most 6 m from it. A defender keeps the back line's bounds (`lineFloor`); a midfielder is never behind his back line.
    - It ends `cleared` (achieved) when the carrier no longer has the ball or the lane stops being screenable for him, and `departed` with its receiver. Its target follows the lane once an action. It is traced as `mark`, and the inspector names it 길목 차단 (`Pitch.tsx`, `motionLabel`). `INTENTION_REASONS.cleared` documents the screen's endings.
