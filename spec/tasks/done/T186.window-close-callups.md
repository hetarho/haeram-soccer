## goal
On the day after a window's last day, each unmet replacement need and each player missing below 20 is called up from the academy.

## acceptance
- [v] On summerClose + 1 and winterClose + 1, after judgeRequests: for each open need, oldest first, callUpFor(w, need.line, 'replacement'); then while activePlayers < 20, callUpFor(w, the line the formation lacks most, 'short'); stop at the first undefined (no room).
- [v] With academy delegated (`isDelegated(w, 'academy')`) the call-ups happen and are reported; with academy direct one attention staff-report lists the prospects the youth director recommends in the same order, and nobody is promoted or generated.
- [v] All needs of the closed window are cleared after the pass, whatever happened.
- [v] packages/engine/src/window-callups.test.ts: an unmet winter need promotes a same-line prospect on day winterClose + 1 although he is not ready and cash is below the promotion floor; no same-line prospect → the ablest of another line; an empty academy → a generated 18-year-old marked graduate; 17 players without needs → 3 call-ups; academy direct → recommendation only; a full registration stops the pass.
