## goal
Open match-model revision 6 and, on it, have the carrier take a clear chance (shoot in or near the box, carry straight at goal further out), go on after beating his man, find a free teammate ahead before a safe square ball, and clear only when pressed; keep a misplaced pass in play unless it runs over a line near its aim; and spread the supporting players by their 판단력 and 위치선정. Revisions 1–5 keep their football draw for draw.
