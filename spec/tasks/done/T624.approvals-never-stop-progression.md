## goal
Owner-held approvals never stop or refuse progression in either mode. The automatic clock plays on, 다음 이벤트 and day steps advance through them, and match-eve play-through and preparation are not blocked. Only observations (match eve, window opening) and mandatory blockers stop. Approvals stay in the office trays with their counts.

## acceptance
- [v] `stoppingReasons(w)` holds observations only, and approvals come from `pendingDecisions(w)`. `assertNextEvent`, host `advance-days`/`next-match` and `next-event` neither throw nor break for pending decisions (engine and runtime unit tests, including manual next-event passing a pending contract talk on its way to the match eve)
- [v] the automatic clock starts, keeps running and resumes with approvals waiting, and a newly arriving approval does not stop it; `playThrough`, `nextEvent` and 다음 이벤트 are enabled with approvals waiting (progression unit tests)
- [v] office counts (navigation entry, office scene, trays, approval-request liveness) read the approvals list, with the same numbers as before (unit tests)
- [v] EventCenter shows only observation events, and its event cards no longer disable on pending decisions
- [v] e2e journeys that waited on a decision stop are updated; result limits list the specs touched (the run is left to the coordinator's final gate)
- [v] web unit tier, typecheck, lint, format pass
