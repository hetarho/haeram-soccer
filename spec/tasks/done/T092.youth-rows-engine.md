## impl notes
- academy.ts: `readinessOf` and `readyGapOf` take their margins from a `debutRule(w)` helper in direction.ts (`{ weakestMargin, startersMargin, fromAge }`) and release from `releaseRule(w)` (`{ age, against: 'weakest' | 'starters' | 'never' }`); `youthAdvice` reads FIRST_TEAM_GAP 10 under early and filters to U-21 under late.
- build.ts: the `pathway` card id goes; add `elite` with `{ intake: -2, intakePotential: 8 }`; the youth-growth and morale effects of 빠른 데뷔 join `buildEffects`-style composition through a `directionEffects(w)` term multiplied in where `buildEffects(w).youthGrowth` and the morale baseline are read (academy growth, first-team growth ≤ 21, morale), so the growth breakdown in 훈련 can name 데뷔 시점.
- schema.ts: a `legacyPathway` load transform (build.youth 'pathway' → delete, direction.debut 'early' unless set) before validation; LEGACY_VISION_BUILDS entries that used pathway map the same way.
- stances: in the window-opening path (where `window-open` inbox items are posted) when `delegated(w, 'transfers')`; starters from `ownStarters(w)`; best by overall.
