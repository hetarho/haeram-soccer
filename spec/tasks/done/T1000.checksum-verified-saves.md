## goal
An autosave encodes once and checks its own bytes (SHA-256 after a gunzip) instead of decoding, unpacking and validating the whole world; the 6 MiB/8 MiB/32 MiB budgets become an export file limit of 256 MiB and a decompressed limit of 1 GiB.

## acceptance
- [v] `checkpoint(w, generation, parentGeneration)` returns `{ raw, envelope }` after re-reading only the payload: gunzip it, compare its SHA-256 and byte length with what was encoded; it never calls `JSON.parse` on the payload, `unpack`, `validateWorld` or `decode`. A corrupted payload makes it throw (unit test).
- [v] The checksum is the SHA-256 of the exact uncompressed bytes written; those bytes come from `JSON.stringify(...)` of what is stored today (`pack(w)`), not `canonical(...)`. `decode` still verifies the checksum and validates the world fully at load/import (unchanged behaviour, existing tests).
- [v] `repository.ts` exports `PORTABLE_FILE_LIMIT = 256 * 1024 * 1024` and `DECOMPRESSED_LIMIT = 1024 * 1024 * 1024`; `INDEXED_CHECKPOINT_LIMIT` and every UTF-16 budget check are gone (`checkpointWriter.ts`, `persistence.ts`); the gzip stream in `compression.ts` stops at `DECOMPRESSED_LIMIT`.
- [v] Every import-size check reads `PORTABLE_FILE_LIMIT`: `App.tsx:957` (already), `GameSlotScreen.tsx:103` (hard-coded 8 MiB today), `protocol.ts:249` inspect `raw` max.
- [v] Task stage, owner implementer: `vibe check --tier task` passes.
