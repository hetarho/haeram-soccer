## goal
For any of the ten settled matches the prototype writes a ninety-minute script that uses every recorded attempt, shot and foul exactly once, at its recorded minute where the record gives one, joined into possessions a ball could travel — and a checker proves it: counted back, the script gives the record's rows to the last pass.

## impl notes
- from review/match-engine-split-261110 F5
- Follow T791's chain rules. The only randomness is `createRng(seed)` and its forks; the writer is a pure function of `(settled, seed)` (→ARCH-5). Nothing here stages anything: the output is a `Script` the existing validator's vocabulary and T821's staging can read.
- The principle, which later tasks must not bend: the record is the fact. A counted beat performs exactly one settled event (or shot, or foul) with its actor, cells, kind and result; the writer chooses only the order, the receivers, the points inside the cells, the clock and the connective tissue between events. Connective tissue is limited to things the record does not count: a carry that is not one of the record's carries, a heavy touch that loses the ball (`miscontrol`), and a restart's delivery when no recorded pass is left to make it with. A connective pass in open play does not exist.
- This task writes a plain, valid script — the baseline. It joins events greedily by the nearest cell and counts what the joins cost. Making the order read as football is T822's measured work; do not add ordering heuristics here beyond the rules below.
- `core/types.ts`, added fields (all optional, so every existing script stays valid):
  ```ts
  // Beat
  /** The settled event this beat performs (index into `Settled.events`); absent on a connective beat and on a shot or foul beat. */
  event?: number;
  /** The settled shot or foul this beat performs. */
  shot?: number;
  foul?: number;
  /** Match seconds when the beat starts (0 = kick-off, 2700 = the second half's). */

  /** Written from a settled result: staging may not replace a beat's opponent (T821). */

  ```
  `BeatOutcome` gains `'loose'` (a heavy touch: the ball runs to the opponent named in `opponent`); `move` carries the connective's name (`link`, `miscontrol`, `restart`) or the event's kind.
- Config, `PROTO_CONFIG.fit` (describe in the header comment):
  ```ts
  fit: {
    /** Seconds a touch takes at least and at most; a minute's time is shared out between its beats within these. */
    hold: [0.4, 5],
    /** Nominal seconds a restart takes off the minute, by kind. */
    dead: { kickOff: 40, throwIn: 12, goalKick: 20, corner: 25, freeKick: 20 },
    /** A carry is moved at most this many minutes to find a release of its player. */
    carryWindow: 3,
    /** A point inside a cell stays this far from the cell's edges. */
    cellMargin: 1.5,
  },
  ```
- `fit/touches.ts`:
  ```ts
  /** One player's spell on the ball: his carries and take-ons in order, then at most one release. */
  export interface Touch {
    side: TeamId; actor: number; minute: number;
    /** Event ids of his carries and take-ons, in the order they are played. */
    carries: number[];
    /** The pass event, shot or foul that ends the spell; absent when a failed carry ends it or nothing does. */
    release?: { event: number } | { shot: number } | { foul: number };
    /** The touch that must come next for the same side (a shot's touch after its source pass). */
    next?: number;
  }
  export function dealMinutes(settled: Settled, seed: string): { events: number[]; fouls: number[] };
  export function buildTouches(settled: Settled, minutes: { events: number[]; fouls: number[] }): Touch[];
  ```
  - Minutes are indexes 0–89 (0–44 first half); the record's minute `m` (1–90) is index `m - 1`; added time belongs to indexes 44 and 89.
  - `dealMinutes`: a shot keeps its minute; a shot's source event takes the shot's minute; a booked foul takes its card's minute; every other event of a side is dealt evenly: the side's remaining event ids in ascending order, shuffled with `createRng(seed).fork('deal:' + side)`, item `k` of `n` to index `Math.floor((k * 90) / n)`; unbooked fouls likewise with fork `'fouls:' + side`.
  - `buildTouches`, per side and minute: every pass event is the release of a touch of its actor; every shot is the release of a touch of its shooter — a shot with a passer makes its touch the `next` of the touch its source pass releases; a shot whose source is a carry or take-on shares that event's touch; a shot without a source (a direct free kick) is a touch on its own. A foul is the release of a touch of the fouled player (he is fouled on the ball). A completed carry or take-on joins the touch of the same actor and minute whose first cell (the first carry's `from`, else the release's `from`, or the shot's or foul's cell) equals the carry's `to`; with none, the one whose first cell is nearest (cell-centre distance; ties: lower touch index); with no touch of his in that minute, the nearest minute within `fit.carryWindow` that has one (ties: earlier), the event's minute changed to it; with none at all it forms a touch without a release. Carries of one touch are ordered so each starts nearest where the last ended. A failed carry or take-on (`tackled`) ends its touch: it takes no release, forming a touch of its own when needed.
- `fit/write.ts`: `export function writeFitted(settled: Settled, seed: string): Script` — `fixture` = `fixtureOf(settled)`, `seed` = the settled id and seed joined, `bound: true`.
  - The walk keeps: the minute index, the side in possession, the ball's cell (in that side's frame) and point, the holder, the restart owed. Kick-off: home at index 0, away at index 45, the conceding side after a goal; the ball is dead at half-time whatever was happening.
  - Next touch: among the side's unplayed touches of the current minute — a `next` owed first; otherwise the one whose first cell is nearest the ball's cell (cell-centre distance; ties: lowest touch index), skipping a touch of the player who just passed (a man does not receive his own pass) unless no other is left, in which case a connective `miscontrol` ends the possession first.
  - Joining: a pass's `receiver` is the next touch's actor and its `to` a point in the event's `to` cell; when the next touch starts in another cell the receiver first makes a connective carry (`move: 'link'`, no `event`) to a point in that cell. A touch's own carries are counted beats in order, each from a point in its `from` cell to one in its `to` cell, with a connective carry between them when the cells do not meet. A point in a cell is the acting player's nominal place for the ball (`nominalSpot`) clamped into the cell less `fit.cellMargin`; a pass's point is moved, inside the cell, to at least `grammar.minPass` from the ball when the cell allows.
  - Results: `complete` passes the ball on; `intercepted` and `looseRecovered` give it to `other` (when absent: the opponent whose nominal place is nearest) at the `to` cell, `tackled` to `other` at the `from` cell; `looseKept` leaves it with the side — the next touch's actor collects it at the `to` cell; `outTouch` owes the other side a throw-in at the nearer touchline beside the `to` cell; `outByline` a goal kick; `cornerWon` this side a corner on the ball's side; `offside` the other side a free kick at the `to` cell, the beat marked `through` with `other` as its receiver. Kinds map to beats as the existing grammar writes them: `long`, `cross` lofted, `through` as `through`, `cutBack` with `reason: 'cutBack'`, a take-on as `takeOn` with the opponent nearest by nominal place (its tackler when it fails).
  - Shots: a beat of kind `shot` (`freeKick` for a direct one) with the outcome mapped (`save` → `saved` with the keeper as opponent, `block` → `blocked` with the opponent nearest by nominal place, `miss` → `wide`), `header` kept, from a point in the shot's cell. After it: a goal owes a kick-off; a shot with `corner` owes the shooting side a corner; a save without one leaves the keeper holding the ball in his goal area; a miss owes a goal kick; a block without a corner leaves the ball with the blocker.
  - Fouls: the fouled player, on the ball in the foul's cell (a connective carry takes him there when needed), is fouled by `fouler` — a `carry` beat with outcome `foul`; a free kick to his side at that spot is owed. A direct free-kick shot is played as the delivery of a foul of its side in that minute (the unplayed one nearest the shot's cell; with none in the minute, the side's nearest unplayed foul moved to it; with none left the shot is played from open play and counted in `cost.orphanFreeKickShots`).
  - Restarts: an owed restart is delivered by the next touch's release when that is a pass event starting in the restart's cell or beside it — the beat takes the restart's kind and keeps its `event`; a recorded `setPiece` event is used for its own kind first (a corner delivery for a corner, a free-kick delivery for a free kick). With no such pass in the minute the delivery is connective (`move: 'restart'`, the restart's kind, no `event`, to the next touch's actor). A recorded corner or free-kick delivery left without its restart at the end of its minute is played from open play as the cross it is (`cost.orphanSetPieces`).
  - Losing the ball without an event: when the side in possession has no unplayed touch in the minute and the other side has, the holder's connective `miscontrol` (kind `carry`, outcome `loose`, `opponent` = the first actor of the other side's next touch) hands it over. When neither has, the minute ends and possession carries into the next.
  - Clock: beat `clock` = minute index × 60 + its offset. A minute's 60 seconds less its restarts' `fit.dead` are shared equally between its beats as hold plus travel (`grammar.speeds`), each hold clamped to `fit.hold`; offsets follow from the clamped values and may run past the minute's end (the next minute then starts where this one ended; record the largest overrun in `cost`). `Script.times` are the clocks; `nominalSeconds` the last clock plus its beat.
- `fit/validate.ts`: `export function validateFitted(settled: Settled, script: Script): string[]` — problems as short identifier strings; empty = valid. It checks: every event, shot and foul id appears on exactly one beat, and that beat's side, actor, kind, cells (the cell of `from` and of `to` by `cellOf` in the side's frame), result and `other` agree with the row; a beat's `from` is where the ball was left (the previous beat's `to` for a completed beat, its cut-off point as the result says otherwise, the restart spot after a dead ball); a completed pass's receiver acts next for his side; a shot's minute and a booked foul's minute are the record's; a goal is followed by a kick-off of the other side; connective beats are only of the three allowed forms; clocks never decrease.
- `fit/count.ts`:
  ```ts
  export interface Counted { team: [number[], number[]]; players: number[][] }
  /** The record's rows (`TEAM_ROW`, `PLAYER_ROW`) counted from a script's beats alone, by the definitions T819 fixed. */
  export function countScript(script: Script, settled: Settled): Counted;
  /** Every cell that differs, as `player:<index>:<row>=<counted>/<recorded>` or `team:<side>:<row>=…`. */
  export function compareRecord(settled: Settled, counted: Counted): string[];
  export interface FitCost { beats: number; counted: number; links: number; linkMetres: number; miscontrols: number; connectiveRestarts: number; countedRestarts: number; orphanSetPieces: number; orphanFreeKickShots: number; movedCarries: number; maxOverrun: number }
  export function fitCost(script: Script, settled: Settled): FitCost;
  ```
  `countScript` reads beats, not event ids: a pass is a beat of a pass-like kind with an `event`, its completion from the beat's outcome, a tackle or interception from the beat's `opponent` and outcome, restarts from beats of restart kinds, and so on — `settled` is used only for the fixture and for which restart beats are counted passes. That independence is what makes `compareRecord` a proof rather than an echo; where a row cannot be counted from beats alone, say so in the result. The cost line `print.ts` prints is `fitCost` as `key=value` pairs.
- `README.md`: a `## fitted scripts` section — the principle above in three sentences, the commands, what the cost numbers mean.
- Not in this task: staging a fitted script (T821); better orderings (T822); substitutions, dismissals, penalties, extra time (the ten matches have none; production planning takes them from T822's findings).

- outcome: `writeFitted` writes a ninety-minute script from any of the ten settled matches; on all ten `compareRecord(settled, countScript(script, settled))` and `validateFitted` are empty (`mismatches=0 problems=0`). `fit/cells.ts` (cell geometry, dead-ball spots) and `fit/fit.testkit.ts` (a hand-made match of 30 attempts, 5 shots, 3 fouls) were added beside the four named modules; `core/types.ts` gains `event`, `shot`, `foul`, `clock`, `bound` and the outcome `'loose'`; `PROTO_CONFIG.fit` is as the impl notes give it.
- choices the impl notes left open (STATE inbox): a minute whose beats need more than sixty seconds is pressed into them, every part alike, so each beat keeps its record minute and the second half starts at 2700 (the greedy baseline's links cross the pitch: minutes needed up to 108–163 s, cascading would have put the half-time kick-off near 88 minutes); a holder with no touch of his own in the minute loses the ball by `miscontrol` (no connective pass exists), and a touch whose actor holds the ball is played before another man's; a touch that must follow another (`next`) waits until that one is played; an unassisted goal is never scored straight off a recorded pass to the scorer; a shot off its own carry with a passer takes his last completed pass of that group as its feed; only a free-kick shot without a source is direct (a free kick's header has its delivery as source) and no carry joins its touch; a half's kick-off is taken on its minute even when the minute holds no touch; an owed restart is delivered before a half ends (a kick-off owed by a goal at the end of a half is the next half's).
- not counted from beats alone: yellow cards (no beat carries a booking; `countScript` takes them from the card rows). Assists are read from beats: the completed recorded pass the scorer received, his own carries between.
