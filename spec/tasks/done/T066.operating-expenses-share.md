# T066 Operating expenses as a share of last season's turnover
> st:done@261013 | ssot:ECON-30 ECON-26 ECON-12 ECON-18 ECON-10 | base:ECON@16 | dep:T065 | touches:packages/engine/src/finance.ts packages/engine/src/finance.test.ts packages/engine/src/settlement.ts packages/engine/src/projection.ts packages/engine/src/projection.test.ts packages/engine/src/world.ts packages/contracts/src/types.ts packages/contracts/src/versions.ts | lane:-

## goal
Each season's operating expenses are a share of the club's turnover over its last completed season, never below a floor per country and division on the football index, set when the season starts.

## acceptance
- [v] At every season start (and at founding) the world records this season's operating expenses: share × last completed season's turnover (its income from the season ledger without player sales or owner capital), never below the floor for the division the club plays in this season; a club's first season pays the floor.
- [v] The recorded amount is paid in league-round installments on the 운영 경비 ledger line and counts in fixed costs, runway, the cash projection, the cash warning and the bankruptcy threshold, exactly where T062's per-division amount did.
- [v] A promotion or relegation at the close sets the new season's floor from the new division before the share is compared.
- [v] Loading a 1.15 save (and a 1.16 world without the field) fills this season's amount with the floor of the division played now, so nothing else changes on load (→SAVE-10).
- [v] finance.test.ts: first season pays the floor; a season after a high-turnover season pays the share, not the floor; player sales and owner capital do not raise it; a promotion raises the floor from the first round; the amateur floor is the lowest.
- [v] projection.test.ts: the projection and runway read the recorded amount.
- [v] Affected unit tests pass; `pnpm run typecheck` and `pnpm run lint` pass.

## impl notes
- Config: `EXPENSE_CONFIG` becomes `{ share: number, floor: Record<code, number[]> }`; the floor tables keep the current per-division shape and units (1901 price units before the football index, amateur last) and stay quoted with `footballQuote`. Start with share 0.12 and today's tables as floors; T062 calibrates both.
- Store the season's annual amount on the world as `operatingExpenses: string` (minor units, this season's money), written when a season starts (the close's rollover) and at founding. A stored figure keeps runway, projection and settlement on one number and needs no archive reread; it is part of rules 1.16.0 (still unreleased), so it is added to the 1.16.0 world shape and its activation from 1.15, with the codec/schema and contract fixtures updated accordingly.
- Turnover: sum the last completed season's income lines from the season ledger the UI shows (→ECON-10), excluding transfer income (player sales, compensation for players who left) and owner capital; if that season's lines are not kept, use the floor. Currency changes between seasons convert like every live amount (→ECON-6).
- `operatingExpensesAt(code, year, tier)` remains the floor; a new `seasonOperatingExpenses(w)` returns the stored amount; the settlement and `operatingCosts` read it.

## result
- outcome: `EXPENSE_CONFIG` is `{ share: 0.12, floor }` (the former per-division tables as floors, quoted with `footballQuote`). `assessOperatingExpenses(w)` = max(floor of the division played this season, 12% × `lastSeasonTurnover(w)`), or the floor when no completed season is kept. Turnover = the last completed season's ledger income (its archived total, so older-rule lines count) less `transfer-out`, `free-transfer-compensation` and `support`, converted to this season's money if the currency changed at the close. It is recorded as `World.operatingExpenses` (optional, schema `amount`) at founding (createBase), at the close right after `advanceEconomy` (after promotion/relegation, before the rollover's renewals weigh fixed costs) and by the `professionalStart` test helper. `seasonOperatingExpenses(w)` returns it, or for a world saved without it the floor of the division played now; `operatingCosts` reads it, so settlement, fixed costs, runway, the cash projection, the cash warning and the bankruptcy threshold use one number. The 운영 경비 ledger detail says `<division> 최저 수준` or `직전 시즌 매출의 12%`.
