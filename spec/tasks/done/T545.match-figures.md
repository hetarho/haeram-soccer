## goal
Live observation and archived replay draw every player as a posed figure in the recorded kit, with number tags and the carrier cue, at the same positions, clock and outcomes as before.

Positions, the ball path, the clock mapping, seeking, the dead-ball beat and goal holds are unchanged (MATCH-12, WEB-32/94).

## impl notes
- Replace only the player-drawing block of the draw loop (`Pitch.tsx` around the `painter.draw(ctx, isKeeper ? keeperShirt : shirt, …)` call); keep its projection `x(v)`/`y(v)`, the skin tokens and the ball painting.
  - The kit sprite warm-up for markers goes; warm the figure painter's cache for the four worn kits instead.
  - `kitPainting.ts` stays for diagrams and swatches.
- Facing for the fixed-shape fallback: home faces +x, away faces −x in pitch units before the screen conversion.
- Canvas mapping for screen vectors: `fw = width − 2·pad`, `fh = height − 2·pad`, so a pitch-unit vector `[ux, uy]` is `[ux·fw/100, uy·fh/100]` px. Speed in m/s is `hypot(vx·1.05, vy·0.68)` from `PlayerMotion.velocity`, which is in units per second.
- Event times: frame index `i`, event `t` → `i·60 + t` on the presentation clock (T543).
- Keepers come from the existing `keepersBySample`.
- Number tags avoid `[ball x, y, ball radius]` and the carrier cue ring.
- Performance: keep the per-frame path free of allocation-heavy work. Action windows and gait distances are built once per playback. ARCH-28 targets ≥ 30 FPS on the reference mobile viewport, which the batch benchmark checks, not this task.
- The batch runs the e2e specs; record their names in `- limits:`.

## result
- outcome: `pitchFigures.ts`: `figureInputs(sample, next, mix, frame)` returns one `PitchFigure` per player of the presented sample: side, recorded motion, number and centre in CSS px, and T543's `FigureInput`. Position and velocity interpolate between his two samples; a player the next sample leaves out holds his position, velocity and distance. Velocities are scaled to screen px by the canvas mapping (`fw/100`, `fh/100`) and speed is `hypot(vx·1.05, vy·0.68)` m/s. `velocityBefore` is his interpolated velocity at `time − hipLag` on the same clock (his current one where no earlier sample has him). `distance` interpolates his `gaitDistances` row; `toBall` runs to the drawn ball on screen; only the possession cue's `carrierId` holds the ball; keepers come from `keepersBySample`. A sample without players stands both sides in `FIXED_SHAPE` (moved here from `Pitch.tsx`), home facing +x and away −x. `Pitch.tsx` builds `figureActionWindows`, `gaitDistances` and each player's `playerLook` once per playback (`useMemo`). Its frame loop poses every figure with `poseAt(input, windows, { reducedMotion: reducedMotion(), unit })` and paints it with `createFigurePainter().draw` at `markerRadius(width)` in `look.sides[side].kit` or `keeperKit` (the unrecorded stand-ins and the "유니폼 미기록" notice unchanged). The figure holding the ball most firmly is drawn last, with the ball eased from its track into his socket (`track + hold·(socket − track)`) through the painter's `ball` callback; otherwise the ball is drawn after the figures as before. The inspection line, target and ring and the carrier's ring now lie on the grass under the figures. The carrier's pointer is drawn over them, and `placeNumberTags`/`drawNumberTag` tags go last, avoiding the drawn ball, the ring and the pointer. The warm-up effect builds the four worn kits' torso sprites (still pose) instead of marker sprites. Positions, ball track, clock, seeking, dead-ball beat and goal holds are untouched. `figurePose.ts` (T543's file, changed here as the brief asked): judged in the paused real match view at 5× zoom, standing figures showed thighs and shins folded out ahead of the chest, which reads as sitting. Legs now bend by `body.kneeBend` 0.12 of their slack (arms keep `bend` 0.35), so a standing knee stays under the chest with only the boot toes ahead, and running strides keep their reach; `figurePose.test.ts` gains "a standing knee stays under the chest", which fails at the old 0.35. e2e: `club-identity-observation.spec.ts` replaces the marker discs with `wornFigures`, which fits every torso around each shirt-coloured region with grids from `FIGURE_TORSO`/`figureShirtColourAt`/`insideFigureTorso`. A two-colour shirt counts only where its own pattern fits better than the same colours in every other pattern. Keeper kits use an area floor of 60 device px (old 150 was a disc). The legacy test judges today's whole two-colour home shirt, because its base colour #bf7956 is within 9 of skin tone #bb8159 and a plain-colour torso matched heads. `club-identity-continuity.spec.ts`: `WORN_PIXELS` 20 → 40 with the figure arithmetic in its comment.
