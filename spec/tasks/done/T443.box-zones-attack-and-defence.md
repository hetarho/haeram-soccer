## goal
On the candidate model, when the ball is wide in the final third or in a cut-back zone, the attackers take the near post, the far post, the penalty spot and the edge of the box with runs timed to the delivery, and the defending side drops goal-side to protect the same zones (MATCH-33 and MATCH-34 r28, open play).

## acceptance
- [v] New `grid/box.ts` exports `BOX_CONFIG` and `boxScene(s, side)`: the scene is on while the ball (the side's attack frame) is in a wide lane from `BOX_CONFIG.wideFrom` (70 m) up or in a cut-back zone (MATCH-14), and names the zones by the ball's side: `nearPost` (3.5 m out, 3 m inside the near post), `farPost` (3 m out, 1 m beyond the far post), `spot` (the penalty spot, 11 m out) and `edge` (19 m out, central), each a point in the attack frame.
- [v] Attack: `boxScene` assigns at most one attacker per zone, nearest suitable first with ties by player ID: forwards to the near and the far post, a midfielder whose role makes box runs (the existing box-run roles plus 섀도 스트라이커 and 공격형 미드필더) or the far-side winger to the spot or the free post, another midfielder to the edge; the rest defence (`COORDINATION_CONFIG.rest`) never goes. Each takes a committed box run toward a load point `BOX_CONFIG.load` (4 m) short of his zone and attacks the zone itself once the carrier's decided action is a cross or cut-back (read at the decision, as the defenders' read in T426 is), so he arrives as the ball does; on the candidate model these runs replace the `box` run's `[boxX, 30 + 8u]` target while the scene is on.
- [v] Defence: while the opponent's scene is on, the defending back line drops between `BOX_CONFIG.defend.line` (5.5–11 m from its goal) and narrows: the ball-side centre-back guards the near post, the other centre-back the middle, the far full-back or wing-back tucks in to the far post, and one holding or central midfielder drops to the edge or the spot (whichever the attack has filled). Markers of runners keep goal-side, between the runner and his zone. The shape releases when the ball leaves the scene's area or is cleared, the line stepping up together; set pieces keep §4.7's defence.

## impl notes
- `grid/box.ts` is the shared module for T443–T445 (`BOX_CONFIG.delivery` comes in T444, `BOX_CONFIG.shot` in T445); zones are points, so T444 can aim deliveries at them and T445 can drop second balls near them.
- The jobs are intentions given out by `planCoordination`/`applyCoordination` (one snapshot, cost ranking, ties by player ID, named streams). Attackers keep the box run's `committed` priority; defenders take `danger` priority only where §4.5's danger rule already gives it, otherwise tactical.

    - The zones are attack-frame points named by the ball's side: near post (101.5, 34 ∓ 0.66), far post (102, 34 ± 4.66), spot (94, 34), edge (86, 34).
  - Attack: one attacker per zone, nearest suitable pair first, ties by player ID; a runner already on a zone keeps it.
    - Forwards take the posts first. Then a midfielder with a box-run role (`BOX_ROLES` boxToBox, mezzala, shadowStriker, plus `boxRun.extra` and `roles` shadowStriker, attackingMidfielder) or the far-side winger (LW/LM or RW/RM) takes the spot or a free post. Then another midfielder takes the edge.
    - Never the carrier, the rest defence (CB, DM), a man holding anything but a tactical run, or one further than `reach` (30 m) from the zone.
    - Each takes a committed `run: 'box'` intention, its zone in `Intention.box` (zone and ball side), for 8 s. It goes to a load point 4 m short of the zone toward halfway.
    - `attackZones` (intentions.ts) sends every runner into his zone itself. `playGrid` calls it right after `decide` when the action is a cross or cut-back, before the in-stride carry, so the defenders' read and the carry already see it.
    - The next update with the scene on sends him back to his load point. A run ends `cleared` when the ball leaves the final third, the scene turns to the other side of goal, or a turnover happens.
    - While the scene is on, `runPlan`'s box runs (the `[boxX, 30 + 8u]` spot) are dropped. Their draws are unchanged.
  - Defence: while the opponent's scene is on, or a guard still holds (`defendingBox`), `shapePlace` drops every back-line player without a job to `boxLineDepth`. The depth is linear from 5.5 m with the ball on the byline to 11 m with it 35 m out. The line also narrows to 0.6 of its distance from the centre line.
    - `boxGuards` plans tactical `cover` jobs with a `box` job, after the press and before the cover behind it. The ball-side CB (by his slot's spot) takes the near post. The most central other CB takes the middle. The far LB/LWB or RB/RWB takes the far post. These three stand on the line's depth at their zone's line.
    - The nearest free DM/CM drops to the edge, or to the spot when the spot is filled and the edge is not.
    - A guard whose zone has a runner stands between that runner and the zone, 1.5 m from him (`boxGuardTarget`), and the mark pairing skips that runner. Any other box runner's marker stands between him and his zone (`boxMarkPoint` in `intentionTarget`). A zone without a runner is held by position. No guard is taken for a mark.
    - A guard ends `cleared` when the ball leaves the final third, crosses to the other side of goal or is cleared, and the line steps back up into the shape. Guards never take danger priority. §4.5's urgent press may still take one.
