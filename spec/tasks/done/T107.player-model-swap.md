- world.ts:
  - `overall(p)` = mainOvr (T106); `playingAbility` = overall − 3 when unhappy (unchanged rule).
  - `makePlayer(code, seed, id, year, rating, position, age?, division?, role?)` calls `generateProfile(random(\`${seed}:player:${id}\`), position, rating, role)`, sets adaptation 0.80 and the rest as today.
  - `createBase`: the founding manager's tactic's formation; one player per slot made at (slot position, slot role) and seven more: a second GK, then one per line in DF, MF, FW, MF, DF, FW order at the line's first formation position. The iteration that matches the XI to the peers' average aims at `average + LEVEL_OFFSET[level]` (`{ easy: 0, normal: −2, hard: −4, extreme: −6 }`); cash = baseline × `LEVEL_CASH[level]` (`{ easy: 2, normal: 1, hard: 0.5, extreme: 0.25 }`).
  - `npcSquad`: the club style's tactic (today's `clubStyle`) and its formation; indices 0–10 one player per slot at (position, role), 11–17 the bench: GK, then DF, MF, FW, DF, MF, FW at each line's first formation position; ids and ages as today.
  - `lineup(players, tactic, selection)`: slots in order, GK slot first; each takes the unused available player with the highest slot value = slotOvr − fatigue load/6 with the selection trait terms of `selectionValue`; `slotOvr(p, slot, multiplier)` = ovrAt(p.attrs, slot.position, slot.role) × levelFactor(p.positions, slot.position) × multiplier − 3 when unhappy.
  - `selectedLineup(fit, ids)`: keep the saved slots; an unavailable player's slot takes the best available by slot value (CLUB-9); `LINEUP_ROLES` is deleted — callers use `FORMATIONS[tactic].slots`.
  - `rating(xi, …)`: Σ (slotOvr − fatigueLoad/6) / 11 at each player's slot; the multiplier is `adaptationMultiplier(p.adaptation)` for own players and `adaptationMultiplier(club.cohesion ?? 0.8)` for every other club's.
  - refill at the close (season.ts): while fewer than 18 active or no GK — GK if fewer than 2 GK, else the line with the fewest players against its formation count + 1 bench (DF, MF, FW order on ties), at that line's first formation position; adaptation by PLAYER-11 'domestic'.
- joining (PLAYER-11): a helper `joinClub(w, p, joined)` (adaptation = adaptationStart(...), clubRounds = 0, no positionStarts) called at the founding squad ('founding'), academy promotion ('academy'), refill, free-agent signings, completed transfers and loans ('domestic', or 'abroad' when the candidate came through 해외 스카우트망 or a scouting card that adds foreign candidates — mark such market candidates with a `foreign` flag where transferOffers builds them).
