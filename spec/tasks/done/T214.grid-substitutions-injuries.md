# T214 Substitutions by the manager and in-match injuries

## goal
Players get hurt during play, managers substitute by era and character, minutes are real, and a side without substitutes plays short.

## acceptance
- [v] `grid/changes.ts` exports `subPolicyFor(manager)`, `injuryHazard(s, gp, dt)`, `planChanges(s, side, r)`; `buildGridSetup` sets `managers[own]` from `subPolicyFor(w.manager)` (NPC sides `DEFAULT_SUB_POLICY`).
- [v] Injuries (design §8.1): each action, every player on the pitch is hurt with `injuryChance(p, pressing) × dt/5400 × (0.7 + 0.6·minute/90)` (import `injuryChance` from `careers.ts`); the engine stops play (a drop ball restart to the side in possession, 75 s dead, 90 s added credit), pushes an `injury` incident, and the player is replaced (or leaves if no substitute may enter).
- [v] Substitutions at every dead ball and at half-time in the order of design §8.1 (injuries, red-card rebalance, policy), respecting `era.maxSubs`, `era.injuryOnly`, `era.windows` (half-time does not use a window); the substitute takes the leaver's slot (`enterSlot`), `onAt` = now; a `sub` incident (`player` = leaver, `other` = entrant); 30 s dead time and 30 s added credit.
- [v] Minutes: a player's `metrics[10]` = regulation minutes on the pitch (design §2.3); record `players` lists starters then used substitutes per side; `lineCounts` reflects that.
- [v] `changes.test.ts` cases: `injures about 1.8% of players a match and more late and under press`, `uses no substitutes before 1965 and plays short after an injury`, `allows only injury substitutes in 1965`, `uses at most three windows from 2022`, `lets a gegenpress manager change tired pressers from the 55th minute`, `protects a lead late under a counter or organiser manager`, `chases a deficit under a firefighter`, `gives minutes to a young player under a developer`, `delays changes by ten minutes with the stable trait`, `rebalances after a red card`, `credits substitutes with their minutes`.
- [v] `pnpm run typecheck`, `pnpm run lint`, the test files pass.

## impl notes
- `subPolicyFor(manager: Manager): SubPolicy` maps STAFF-45 (read the style ids in `styles.ts` / `ManagerStyle` in contracts and match them by their Korean labels):
  | manager | policy fields |
  |---|---|
  | none, interim, founding | `DEFAULT_SUB_POLICY` |
  | 게겐프레싱 style or `rotation` trait | `earlyPress: true` (fatigueFrom 55, half-time changes allowed for tired players) |
  | 실리 역습, 수비 조직가 | `protectLead: true` |
  | 소방수, 동기부여형 | `chaseWhenBehind: true` |
  | 육성형 or `youth` trait | `youth: true` |
  | 데이터 헤드코치 | `data: true` (changes only at minutes 60, 70, 80 by fatigue) |
  | `stable` trait | `delay: 10` (added to fatigueFrom and laterFrom) |
- Policy decision at a stoppage (`planChanges`): candidates = starters still on the pitch; "tired" = `matchFatigue − 20 > 15` with `matchFatigue = p.fatigue + (100 − stamina) × minutesOn/90 × 0.35 × (pressing ? 1.3 : 1) × drain`; the default replaces the most tired from `fatigueFrom`, then any remaining changes after `laterFrom` for the most tired; `protectLead` (ahead after 75′): a DF/MF bench player for the most advanced FW; `chaseWhenBehind` (behind at half-time or after 60′): a FW bench player for a DF or MF; `youth`: among equal options prefer age ≤ 21; level or ahead after 80′ bring one on.
- Bench choice for a slot: the bench player with the best `slotOvr` there (`world.ts`), ties by bench order.
- Drop ball: add `'dropBall'` to `RestartKind` in `restarts.ts`; the side in possession restarts with a short pass at the spot.
- Regulation minutes: `regMinute = periodOffset + min(periodLength, t/60)` with offsets 0/45/90/105 and lengths 45/45/15/15.

## result
- outcome: `grid/changes.ts` maps the manager to a `SubPolicy` (`subPolicyFor`, STAFF-45; the own side in `buildGridSetup`, NPC sides the default), draws in-play injuries with one match-stream draw per action against the summed `injuryHazard` (CLUB-23 chance × dt/5400 × (0.7 + 0.6·minute/90)) and plans changes at every dead ball and at half-time (`planChanges`: injuries, red-card rebalance, policy; era `maxSubs`, `injuryOnly`, `windows`); the engine applies them (`applyChanges`) with `sub`/`injury` incidents, a `dropBall` restart (75 s dead, 90 s credit) and 30 s dead/credit per substitution; `GridState.changes` keeps each side's bookkeeping and `regMinute`; the record lists starters then substitutes in entry order with regulation minutes.

Measured: about 0.44 injuries and 3 substitutes a side a match in 2010; about 95 ms a match against 90 ms before.
- limits: A red-card rebalance puts the substitute into the sent-off player's empty slot and takes the forward off (design §8.1 says the leaver's slot; see inbox). Anyone who played is credited at least 1 minute, so a substitute in added time still counts as an appearance. A goalkeeper sent off is still covered by an outfield player; a substitute keeper for him is not modelled. Substitution timing (all remaining changes soon after 70′) is uncalibrated, for T215.
