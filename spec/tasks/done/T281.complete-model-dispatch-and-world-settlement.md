## goal
Route candidate own matches to detailed football and every other fixture to full aggregate generation with one shared settlement ledger.

## acceptance
- [v] Under the recognized candidate model, every own-club fixture uses the detailed engine and every non-own fixture uses `simulateAggregate`, including the own league, cups, playoffs and Europe. Viewing or timing measurements cannot change the choice.
- [v] Both candidate paths pass `validateFullMatchRecord` before ordinary settlement. Extend the detailed tally/setup as needed to supply all named participants' PD/metrics, not only the own players, while preserving frozen actual appearances and existing own-match material lifetimes.
- [v] `npc-stats.ts` owns a shared exactly-once reducer for complete competition/club/player aggregates and tracking boundaries. Preserve departed contributors and actual per-club transfer records; no full Player object graphs or match traces are persisted there.
- [v] Existing fixture, standings, scorer, Elo, injury, suspension, personnel and own history effects consume the same settled facts once. Duplicate commands/fixtures, observation and stat reads cannot alter the ledger or future random draws.
- [v] Focused tests cover `dispatches every non-own competition to aggregate`, `settles a fixture once`, `conserves transferred-player contributions`, `counts substitute appearances`, `carries incidents into the next lineup`, and `keeps day round and season advancement equivalent` using shared fixture worlds.
- [v] Production activation remains disabled; supported unactivated worlds retain their prior settled model. Task checks follow ARCH-30.

## result
- outcome: Under `activeMatchModel(w)`, match.ts `simulateMatch` hands every fixture to `candidateMatch`: an own fixture plays the grid (`buildGridSetup` on the world's model, every named line of both sides) and must pass `assertFullMatchRecord`; every other fixture (its league, the amateur league, cups through `playFixture`, promotion playoffs through `resolveTie`, Europe) takes `simulateAggregate(w, f, decide)` with the competition's own tie rule. Observing adds the own match's frames only, and `keepPlayers` and `setOwnLeagueEngine` never reach the choice. A world without a model keeps the pre-feature paths unchanged (the portable golden facts pass). season.ts `recordMatch` asserts any record carrying `model` before settling it, keeps every existing effect (fixture, Elo, table, scorers, discipline, injuries, own record and personnel) and then calls `settleNpcSeason`. `prepareSeason` (lifecycle) opens an empty ledger for the new season under an active model. The new npc-stats.ts holds the season's complete aggregates per competition (`kind`, `groupKey`). Each club row has its matches, team metrics, TD, and xG and xGA in hundredths. Each participant row has his id, name and position, with one contribution per club: appearances (lines with minutes > 0, substitutes who came on included), metrics and PD. Every club is counted, the own club included. The module also provides `trackingBoundary` (the first game day and league round not yet lived, so `{ day: 1, round: 1 }` means the season is tracked whole), `openNpcSeason`, and the pure reads `npcCompetition` and `playerTotals`. Its ID indexes live in WeakMaps beside the stats. Exactly-once rests on the existing settled-fixture identity: a scheduled fixture plays only while unscored, and an own result already saved is a no-op. The worker serializes commands. No outcome list is kept and nothing is drawn. Pre-feature records, other seasons' records and a missing ledger are not counted. Shared code (T280 hand-offs 4 and 6): match.ts `slotSkill` and `drainPerMinute` are the one effective-skill formula, used by the summary `matchSkills` (same arithmetic, so the results are bit-identical) and by `aggregateSkillAccess`. grid/setup.ts `fixtureRosters` is the one matchday availability rule. The candidate grid and the aggregate `matchdayRosters` replace a starter the XI had to keep while unavailable with the best available substitute. The pre-feature grid keeps him (`replace` false). `playAggregate` takes `limits` (`AggregateRebalanceLimits`, default config). On the last retry, `settleParticipation(..., { settle: true })` returns the searched stretch with the fewest disagreeing red-card answers: the earliest among equals, with at least one plan searched. That gives a deterministic, valid record, the answers listed in `unreconciled`, and no throw. The pre-plan accessor (`UNPLANNED`) now reads match.ts lazily, because match.ts and aggregate/engine.ts now import each other. The index exports `simulateAggregate` and npc-stats. tests/fixtures/worlds.ts `withCandidateModel(w, coverage)` records the candidate model at the world's boundary and opens the ledger, for tests and measurements only.
