## goal
Everything a record can hold is staged as a scene: a substitute comes on and the man he replaces walks off, a sent-off or injured player leaves and his side plays on a man short, a penalty and a shoot-out are taken from the spot, corners keep their numbers back, the sides change ends at every period, and extra time is played — so no match the game can settle lacks a picture.

## impl notes
- Scenes are dead-ball scenes like the prototype's restarts (`stage/scenes.ts`): players walk at their bounded pace to their places and stand (→MATCH-12); the presentation shortens them (→WEB-32), the staging does not. Their lengths are named values in `STAGING_CONFIG.stage.scene` (start: substitution 30 s, card 15 s, injury 45 s, penalty 40 s before the kick, shoot-out 25 s a kick).
- Who is on the pitch: `Staging` keeps each side's present lines; a `StageFrame` lists them. A beat's `incidents` (T827) drive it: `sub` — the man off walks to the halfway touchline on the benches' side (the home side's left touchline, `y = 0`), the man on enters there and takes the slot; `red`, `secondYellow` — the man walks off, `present[slot]` is cleared for T823's shape; `injury` — he stays down for `scene.injuryDown` seconds, then leaves, with a `sub` when the same beat carries one. A yellow card is a stoppage in which nobody leaves. Events: the new `StageEventKind`s `card`, `sub`, `injury` with `actor`, `other`.
- A man short: `nominalSpot` for the remaining slots is T823's with `present`; marking, pressing and the back-line group simply skip the absent slot. The script names who keeps goal after a keeper leaves (T827); nobody else changes slot.
- Penalty: the taker stands at `PENALTY_X`, the keeper on his goal line, every other player outside the box and the arc and behind the ball; the kick is a shot from the spot with the scripted outcome; a save or miss plays on as the next beat says. Shoot-out: all 22 at the halfway line except the taker and the two keepers, every kick at the goal of period 4's home attack end; kicks are `shot` events flagged `shootout`.
- Change of ends: a side attacks toward `x = 105` in periods 1 and 3 and toward 0 in periods 2 and 4 when it is the home side, the away side the opposite; all of the stage's attack-frame helpers take the period. The interval itself is a transition, not staged play: each period starts from its kick-off places.
- Corner numbers (→MATCH-18): the attacking side keeps `scene.corner.back` (drawn 2–3 from the motion stream) outfielders near halfway, the defending side `scene.corner.up` (1–2) forward; the rest take the box as the prototype's corner scene places them.
- Before 1970 a card is still a scene; whether it is drawn as a card is the web's (→MATCH-19).
