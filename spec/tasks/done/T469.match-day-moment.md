## goal
Replace T465's late-step indicator and the visible late-step catch-up with WEB r155's match-day moment: while the page is shown, an own fixture day of automatic play holds an animation-only moment for at least 2 s at every pace (in home's NEXT MATCH card, small in the bottom controls elsewhere), every visible step is one day, and an optional 경기 결과 알림 notice follows the moment.

## acceptance
- [v] Visible steps are one day: `owed()` returns `{ days: 1, catchUp: false }` whenever the page is shown and not returning from away time; the `owing` flag and `MAX_STEP_DAYS` are removed. Hidden-tab and away catch-up (`this.hidden`, `AWAY_GAP_MS`, `CATCH_UP_STEP_DAYS`) are unchanged.
- [v] The match-day moment: a visible, non-catch-up automatic step that settles the day of an own fixture sets `ProgressionState.matchMoment` when it is sent and holds the next step until both the step has landed and `MATCH_DAY_MOMENT_MS` has passed since it was sent; then `matchMoment` clears and the next tick plays the next day at the pace. Days with only other clubs' fixtures, hidden or away steps, watched matches and manual mode never set it; a fixture played through from its match eve gets it on its day.
- [v] Stopping the clock (owner stop, event stop, watching, mode switch, world change, disposal) or hiding the tab during the moment clears it at once without a notice; the settled day stays settled.
- [v] T465's `lateStep`, its timer, caption and CSS are removed; a late ordinary visible day shows nothing and is simply waited for.
- [v] `NextMatchCard` shows the moment's looping animation in place of its content while `matchMoment` is set (no clubs, score, minute or other result; decorative, `aria-hidden`; the card keeps its size so the home layout does not move); `ProgressControls` shows the same animation small in its reserved slot only when the home's NEXT MATCH card is not on screen.
- [v] 경기 결과 알림: a per-browser preference (off by default) shown as a checkbox in the owner office's automatic section; when on, the end of a moment whose step added an own match record shows a notice in the bottom control area for `RESULT_NOTICE_MS` with `<home club> <home goals>–<away goals> <away club>`; tapping it opens that record's `MatchReport` in a `Dialog`. Off, no notice appears.
- [v] Results are unchanged (→MATCH-7): the moment only delays when the next step is sent; the step, its command and its results are identical.
- [v] Unit tests in `progression.test.ts` with fake timers and a deferred client reply: "a visible late step carries no owed days", "an own fixture day holds the next step for MATCH_DAY_MOMENT_MS when it lands early", "a match day longer than MATCH_DAY_MOMENT_MS ends its moment on landing", "a day with only other clubs' fixtures has no moment", "stop and hide end the moment without a notice", "a hidden tab still catches up owed days", "the result notice follows the moment only with the preference on". `ProgressControls.test.ts` and `ClubHub.test.ts` render the animation from the store state; the old late-step cases are removed.
- [v] tests/e2e/progression-settings.spec.ts: the T465 journey becomes "an own match day shows the match-day moment in NEXT MATCH for at least 2 s without moving the controls" at 360, 390 and 1280; it is written and left to the host batch (ARCH-30).

## impl notes
- Constants in `progression.ts`: `MATCH_DAY_MOMENT_MS = 2000` (WEB-18's floor), `RESULT_NOTICE_MS = 4000`. The moment is a presentation hold only: keep a private `momentUntil` timestamp and a `momentStep` flag (step still in flight); `tick()` returns early while either holds, without touching `stepAt`, so the interval simply resumes. One `setTimeout(MATCH_DAY_MOMENT_MS)` plus the step's `finally` decide which of the two ends the moment last; clear both in `stop()`, `setHidden(true)` and `dispose()` (where T465 cleared `lateStep`).
- Own fixture day: reuse T465's test, `daysUntilNextMatch(w)` within the step's days on the world the step starts from (`w` before `command`), with the one-day visible step.
- Result notice: compare `ownMatches.at(-1)?.id` before and after the moment's step; the notice reads that record from the view (`ownMatches` holds the latest 30). Store it as `ProgressionState.resultNotice?: { matchId: string }` and clear it after `RESULT_NOTICE_MS` or on stop. The preference extends `BackgroundSettings` with `resultNotice: boolean` in the existing `haeram-soccor:background` key (default false when absent), set through `setBackground`; the office shows it as its own checkbox labelled 경기 결과 알림 above the 자리를 비운 동안 section, hidden in manual mode with the other automatic preferences.
- `ClubHub` receives the progression controller from `App.tsx` (the `<ClubHub …/>` call at the home route) and passes `useProgression(controller, (s) => s.matchMoment)` to `NextMatchCard`. `ProgressControls` learns whether the home card is shown from the current route `App.tsx` already knows (pass a `homeVisible` prop) rather than querying the DOM.
- The animation is CSS only (a looping keyframe, `prefers-reduced-motion` keeps a static mark), styled from theme tokens in each module's CSS.
