# T426 The player on the ball takes it in stride instead of stopping

## goal
On the candidate model, the player on the ball carries it in stride between receiving it and playing it, instead of standing on it for one to three seconds before every pass or shot (MATCH-12 r25).

## acceptance
- [v] `movement.ts` exports `IN_STRIDE_CONFIG` with `speed` 3.5 (m/s for a fresh player of average pace, scaled by the same pace and fatigue rule as his other movement) and `pressureRadius` 3 (m). In `engine.ts`, the candidate actor's wait before his kick (today `holdUntil: dt − flight` in `motionOf`) becomes an in-stride carry: he moves with the ball along his current heading blended toward his action's target for the hold time, bounded by the movement rules and the pitch.
- [v] The action is resolved from where the ball is kicked: the hold time is fixed before resolution from `ACTION_SECONDS` and the pre-carry flight estimate, the match clock runs through the carry before the resolution with every player moving on his intention (pressers and markers tracking the carrier), then the pass, shot or other action resolves from the new spot (interception lines, xG, `shotAt`, tally `from`), and `dt` is `max(least, carry + actual flight, reach)`. The engine records the carry as `carry` knots from the reception spot to the kick spot.
- [v] With an opponent within `IN_STRIDE_CONFIG.pressureRadius` he shields on the move: he takes the ball in stride away from the nearest opponent at the same bounded pace (MATCH-12 r27). He stands on the ball only for a hold-up or lay-off, a keeper's distribution and a restart's taker; a first-time action with no hold is unchanged. The pre-feature path is unchanged (its digests pass untouched).
- [v] `playback.ts` draws the candidate actor along the recorded carry with the ball at his feet up to the kick, instead of pinning him to his reception spot (`route.push([t, here])` for a candidate kick).
- [v] Tests: `engine.test.ts` "a free receiver carries in stride before his pass" (kick knot at least 1 m from the reception spot, tally `from` equals the kick spot) and "a pressed receiver shields" (kick knot at the reception spot); `playback.test.ts` "the candidate's carrier moves with the ball between reception and kick": on three observed candidate fixtures (possession v counter), on the test's drawn-hold measure and leaving out hold-ups, lay-offs, keepers and restart takers, the share of open-play holds that stay still (under 0.3 m/s) for most of their length is at most 0.05 (0.42–0.45 before on that measure, 261025), every new record passes `validateFullMatchRecord`; candidate digests built with an explicit enabled release are re-recorded where they change, with the measured change in the test comment.
- [v] The result reports, on one calibration round against the pre-change source, the per-match passes, carries, progressive carries, box entries, shots and xG on the candidate (reported, not gated: MATCH-28 r23; T286 and T410 read the released model afresh).
- [v] Task checks (ARCH-30): typecheck, lint, format:check, `vitest run --changed`; the release-on unit files this touches pass under `vitest.release-on.config.ts` as well.

## impl notes
- Cause (261024): `motionOf` keeps the actor on the ball until the kick so the ball lands as the action ends (`least` 2–3.6 s, flights about 1 s), and playback pins him to his reception spot until the kick; three observed candidate fixtures held still for most of 11.3% of holds (15% with at least 0.5 s still), the pre-feature path 24%.
- Keep the action time per action as it is (the match's tempo and action counts); only what the carrier does inside it changes. The decision (action and target) is still made at reception from his cell; only its resolution moves to the kick spot.
- `IN_STRIDE_CONFIG` is the only place for the two numbers; other tasks read it by name.
- No change to the pre-feature path: the live game gets this at T286's activation (ideation/match-viewing-pace-and-freedom: no second implementation on the pre-feature path).
- If a carry would cross into a cell whose decision table differs enough to change the chosen action's sense (for example out of the box before a shot), cap the carry at the cell boundary rather than re-deciding.

## result
- outcome: done. MATCH r27 is implemented, with the defenders' read of the decided pass restored (planning fd16927a).
  - The carry: `inStride` in `engine.ts` fixes the hold before the resolution and runs the match clock through it. `carryInStride` (`movement.ts`) moves the carrier at `strideSpeed`. A free carrier blends his heading with his aim; a pressed one (`presserOf`) shields on the move, straight away from the presser. Shots, crosses, cut-backs and clearances stay in the reception cell. Everyone else moves on his intention, and press, mark and cover targets are re-read every step.
  - The defenders' read (`readsOf`): for an action with a receiver, each defender heads from `readAt` on toward the target the pass would give him, with the carry as the kick's wind-up, as before the carry existed. That target is his reaction to the receiver on the ball at the aim, worked out in a provisional `updateIntentions` and then undone. Coordination draws from named streams, so the provisional reaction draws nothing from the match.
  - The kick: the action resolves from the kick spot and the knots run `carry, carry, kick`. After a carry he kicks at once and the flight lasts until the action ends. Only a keeper's distribution, a hold-up or lay-off, a restart and a first-time ball stand on the ball.
  - Playback draws the carry and keeps each carried kick as a drawn sample. A carrier whose ball has left him shows his live intention's target as his intent.
  - Still holds on the test's drawn-hold measure (no hold-ups, keepers or restarts): 0.032 / 0.033 / 0.033 on the three fixtures.
