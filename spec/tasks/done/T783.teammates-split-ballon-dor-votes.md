## goal
A club's season no longer hands all three podium places to its own players: its honours and league place count in full only for its best player, as votes split between teammates.

## acceptance
- [v] Each club's eligible players are ordered by individual points (bounded production plus class, then OVR, then ID); its honours and league-place points count at `BALLON_DOR_CONFIG.shares` 1 / 0.55 / 0.35 by that order, each honour once per player at his better share.
- [v] A unit case shows a champion's second player behind his captain by the split while a rival club's star reaches the podium; every existing Ballon d'Or case (eligibility, trophy participation, bounded scorer, top-six finish, transferred player's best finish, untracked seasons, premium, bonus) still passes.
- [v] `scripts/ballon-dor-podiums.ts` reads the same closes under the released rule and the split, on 4 career worlds from 1955/56: one club's three below 10% and two from one club within 10–30% of podiums, the winner unchanged.
- [v] Archived podiums are untouched; the split applies to future closes only.

## impl notes
