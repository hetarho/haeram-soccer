## impl notes
- Read the new contract and ARCH-31. Existing historical seconds from another machine cannot prove this task's improvement.
- Broad touches allow a measured hotspot to be fixed in its owning module; preserve the shared aggregate/input/settlement contracts rather than inventing parallel fast paths.
- A change that alters football outcomes or probability settings belongs to refreshed T284 calibration evidence, not an unreported performance tweak. Keep observation rendering outside the season engine timing.
- T281 measured about 2.5 ms per aggregate fixture, 146 ms per candidate own match, 0.53 s a round (~25 s a season) and a 481k-character checkpoint at round 46. Settling the ledger during the season close is discarded work (`prepareSeason` replaces it in the same call); skipping it there is an allowed optimisation.
