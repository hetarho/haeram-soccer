## impl notes
- the origin lives in task memory, not in the address: no address format or e2e URL regex changes, and it is scoped to one career by `useTaskMemory.activate`. A reload or a pasted address has no origin and falls back to the office, as before.
- `state.ts`: export `HOME_AREA_ORIGIN = 'investment-home-area'` (a task-memory `values` key). `openInvestment(area?: InvestmentArea, from?: 'home')` first sets `values[HOME_AREA_ORIGIN]` to `area` when `from === 'home'`, else to `undefined`, then visits as now. Every other opener (`openOfficeLobby`, `openControl`, `openFacility`, `officeLedger`) passes no origin and so clears it; do not touch their call sites.
- `clubSections.ts`: pure `officeBack(page: Page, search: string, homeArea?: unknown): 'home' | 'office' | undefined` — `undefined` when `clubSection(page)` is undefined; `office` when `page === 'operations'` and the existing detail test holds (`operationsView(view) !== 'investment'` or `area` is one of `INVESTMENT_AREAS`) and not (`operationsView(view) === 'investment'` and `area === homeArea`); else `home`. Move the detail test out of `ClubNavigation.tsx` into this helper.
