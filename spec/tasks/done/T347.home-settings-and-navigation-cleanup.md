## goal
Reduce visual crowding and make home, preferences and navigation coherent while preserving game behavior and access to every feature.

## acceptance
- [v] Home has compact clearly ranked match actions and quieter progression controls with full touch targets; unfinished playback resumes from home, and the menu contains no watch/return action.
- [v] Theme and language are editable directly at the top of the menu. Gameplay, help and save utilities are grouped, with existing retry/session-only/error behavior and safe dialog handoffs retained.
- [v] Bottom navigation is visibly distinct from scrolling content in standard and pixel, retaining all destinations, active indication, safe areas and touch targets.
- [v] Responsibility settings have clear sections and focused defaults with explanations available on demand; draft/apply/cancel and ownership behavior are preserved.
- [v] Inspect all seven management destinations at mobile/desktop sizes, plus 360px home/settings and theme switching, and correct shared spacing/control issues found.
- [v] Relevant unit cases and fast static/resource/build/spec checks pass. Update affected browser journeys and run the focused combined UI gate once after implementation.

## impl notes
- Follow progressive disclosure, intentional visual hierarchy and purpose-based toolbar grouping from NN/G, Apple HIG and Google Material research. Favor measurable readability and task access over decorative effects.
- Reuse PresentationSettings controls in an inline menu section and the existing standalone host. Keep registries and resource installation unchanged.
- Keep seven primary destinations. Use semantic surface/shape tokens for bottom navigation rather than theme-ID branches; retain angular pixel resources.
- Move the existing MatchAction to the home fixture with actual watch/resume callbacks; preserve the single queued observation and pending-command safeguards. Update watch helpers and resume expectations together.
- Preserve responsibility-setting costs, ownership, pending-work previews and confirmation semantics. Group secondary explanations in labelled disclosures without hiding essential consequence information.
- CSS Modules remain the styling mechanism. Consolidate obsolete conflicting clock/menu rules in the touched sections rather than adding another broad override layer.
