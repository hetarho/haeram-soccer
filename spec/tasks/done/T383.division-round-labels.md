## goal
The information row, league views and club notices number rounds by the division's own rounds (e.g. 19 / 38 in a 20-club 1부), never by the country calendar's shared week (1부 showed ROUND 41 of 46).

## acceptance
- [x] information row: `game-round` shows the rounds our division's fixtures have played this season and `game-round-total` its season total; a week the division sits out leaves it unchanged; the amateur league counts its own rounds
- [x] league views: round selector options, the last-result line, the standings round tag and the rank chart's first/last labels and aria text use division rounds; the rank chart drops snapshots of weeks the league sat out
- [x] club notices (home noticeboard) and manager-history fallbacks are stamped with the date instead of `ROUND <calendar week>`
- [x] `apps/web/src/ui/league.test.ts` covers `leagueRounds`/`divisionRound`/`ownDivisionRound` on a 20-club league inside a 46-week calendar (idle weeks, before the first round, season end) and an odd-sized league; `managerHistoryView.test.ts` expects the date stamp
- [x] e2e specs that read `game-round` (management, staff, recovery) expect division rounds; batch-run at the push gate

## impl notes
- shared helpers in `apps/web/src/ui/league.ts`: `leagueRounds(fixtures)` sorted distinct calendar rounds of the given league fixtures; `divisionRound(rounds, calendarRound)` = how many of them are ≤ `calendarRound` (a fixture's own round gives its index + 1; `w.round`, the last settled week, gives rounds played); `ownDivisionRound(w)` = `{ played, total }` from `leagueFixtures(w, ownLeagueIds(w))`. UI-only: the engine's calendar round, fixtures and rank history stay as they are (saves unchanged).
- rank timeline: drop an entry whose rows equal the previous entry's (a week the league sat out changes nothing), then label entries by position (round 0 snapshot = 0) — works for archived seasons that keep no fixtures.
- notice and manager-history stamps: `seasonDayLabel(year, round * ROUND_INTERVAL_DAYS)` from `packages/engine/src/calendar.ts`, the day `currentDay` gives the same week.
- e2e: specs that compared `game-round` with `world.round` read the expected division round instead; early-1950 amateur weeks need not be league rounds.
