## goal
Own players settle by starting matches and lose some of it when a new manager arrives, an unsettled one is reported once, starts retrain positions, and every other club's 조직력 rises and falls by the same measure.

## acceptance
- [v] each own match: every starter gains MATCH_GAIN.start × his 적응력 rate × gainLevelFactor at the slot he started in × the manager's school factor (PLAYER-12, STAFF-21), halved above 0.80; nobody who did not play gains or loses
- [v] a new manager (hired, appointed by the staff, or an interim) applies `managerChangeLoss` to every own player: 10% of the distance to 0.50 when the predecessor had the same school, 30% otherwise (an interim or the founding manager has no school)
- [v] `clubRounds` counts settled league rounds at the club; at 23 or more with adaptation below 0.50 and no flag for this stay, an `unsettled` event and a news item name him once (PLAYER-14)
- [v] starts at a position he is not 최상 at raise its level after 20 / 40 / 60 starts there (미숙→어색→준수→최상), counted from its last change (PLAYER-4)
- [v] clubs nobody owns: cohesion starts 0.80, rises 0.010 a settled league round below 0.80 and 0.005 above (≤ 1.0); a new manager costs 30%/10% of the distance to 0.50; between seasons a takeover or project −0.15, promotion or relegation −0.10, floor 0.30 (LEAGUE-12)

## impl notes
- adaptation.ts holds the World-level rules over T106's pure functions: `settleStarts(w, xi)`, `onManagerChange(w, before, after)`, `settleClubRounds(w)`, `settleCohesion(w)`, `cohesionAfterManager(club, sameSchool)`, `cohesionAfterSummer(club, change)`.
- season.ts own-match loop: take `const xi = ownStarters(w)` before the loop that adds metrics and fatigue (fatigue changes selection), then call `settleStarts(w, xi)` with each starter's slot (index in the XI = formation slot); there are no substitutes in the engine, so MATCH_GAIN.sub is unused until there are.
- season.ts round settlement (where a league round is settled, beside settleTraining): `settleClubRounds(w)` then `settleCohesion(w)` for every club nobody owns.
- ops-staff.ts: call `onManagerChange` where w.manager takes another person (the hire at the `w.manager = withTerm({ ...manager …` assignment, the interim at `w.manager = interim`) and in the delegated appointment path (STAFF-18; find every assignment of a different id to w.manager). A renewal is not a change.
- managerMarket.ts: where a club nobody owns names its next manager, compare schools and apply the loss to its cohesion.
- dynamics.ts `evolveClubs`: apply −0.15 on a takeover or a new project, −0.10 on promotion or relegation, after the level change.
- retraining: `positionStarts[pos]` counts starts at a slot whose position is not 3 for him; reaching the threshold raises the level one step and resets that count; no event.
- news.ts: the `unsettled` event becomes a news item ("<name> 적응 실패 · <n>라운드째 적응도 <a>") in the style of the other player news.
