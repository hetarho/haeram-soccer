## goal
Every stoppage plays as a rush (players at ≥ 2× the running rate, at most 1.5 s at 1x), a still beat per kind and the restart at the selected speed, both scaled with speed, with a short caption for cards, injuries and substitutions.

## acceptance
- [v] `presentationClock.ts` replaces the corner/free-kick/goal-kick preparation (`SET_PIECE_PREPARATION_MS`, `PREPARED_RESTARTS`, `setPiecePreparations`) with dead-ball beats built from T477's `MatchFrame.stoppages`: the rush `[dead, settled]` runs at `max(rushFactor × runningRate(speed), span / (rushMaxMs / speed))`, the beat `[settled, kick]` at `max(runningRate(speed), span / (beatMs[kind] / speed))`, so neither is ever slower than running speed; frames without `stoppages` keep running speed.
- [v] A stoppage holding a card, injury or substitution event (frame events in `(dead, kick]`) takes the incident beat and shows a caption for the beat: `경고 · 이름`, `퇴장 · 이름`, `부상 · 이름`, `교체 · 나간 선수 → 들어온 선수`; several incidents show in order within the same beat. The caption stays readable in every theme and at 360 px, does not cover the match controls and is static under reduced motion (→WEB-46).
- [v] Speed changes, seeking, hidden-tab return and the goal hold (→WEB-94) keep the current match-time position; a kick-off after a goal starts its rush after the goal hold.
- [v] Tests: `presentationClock.test.ts` cases "a throw-in rushes at twice running speed then holds its beat", "the rush is capped at 1.5 s at 1x and halves at 2x", "beats by kind at 1x and 2x" (every `beatMs` entry), "a stoppage shorter than its beat plays at running speed", "an injury in a stoppage takes the incident beat and its caption", "frames without stoppages run at running speed"; existing pacing cases updated to the new model.
- [v] E2E specs that assumed the 1.5 s set-piece preparation (`tests/e2e/preparation.ts`, `set-piece-defence.spec.ts`, `stable-controls-and-pacing.spec.ts`, `mobile-match-presentation.spec.ts`) are updated to the dead-ball beat; they run only in the final batch (listed in result limits).
- [v] ARCH-30 task tier: typecheck, lint, format check and the changed unit tests pass.

## impl notes
- Config in `presentationClock.ts`, the only place these numbers live: `DEAD_BALL_CONFIG = { rushFactor: 2, rushMaxMs: 1500, beatMs: { throwIn: 400, goalKick: 600, kickoff: 800, corner: 1000, freeKick: 1000, dropBall: 1000, penalty: 1500, incident: 2000 } }` — real ms at 1x, divided by the speed. The owner reviews them in play (WEB-32).
- Keep the clock's state in running match seconds with per-segment rates (the T422 design): a stoppage becomes two segments (rush, beat) in place of today's single preparation; `presentationAdvance`, `presentationMs` and `runningTime` walk the segment list as they walk preparations now. Pitch.tsx swaps `setPiecePreparations` for the new builder (e.g. `deadBallBeats(frames)`), and the caption reads the beat currently under way.
- Absolute running seconds of a stoppage time are `index × 60 + t` for the frame `index` that lists it, as `minuteTime` computes today.
- Names come from the playback's squads by player ID, as the event line already resolves them.

## result
- outcome: `deadBallBeats(frames)` turns T477's `MatchFrame.stoppages` into a rush segment (≥ 2× running, ≤ 1.5 s at 1x) and a still beat per kind (`DEAD_BALL_CONFIG.beatMs`, 0.4–2.0 s at 1x, divided by the speed, never slower than running); the clock walks them as it walked the set-piece preparations, and goal feedback, seeking and speed changes are unchanged. A beat holding a card, injury or substitution takes the incident beat and Pitch shows its caption (`beatAt`, `beatCaptions`, locale keys `match.stoppage.yellow|red|injury|sub`) along the top of the pitch, static under reduced motion. `SET_PIECE_PREPARATION_MS`, `PREPARED_RESTARTS` and `setPiecePreparations` are gone.
