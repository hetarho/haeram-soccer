## impl notes
- `packages/contracts/src/match-model.ts`: append the revision id (its number is its index + 1 = 11); `export const isScriptedModel = (revision: MatchModelRevision | undefined) => revision !== undefined && matchModelNumber(revision) >= 11;`. `packages/engine/src/match-model-release.ts`: `export const SCRIPTED_RELEASE: MatchModelRelease = { revision: 'scripted-aggregate-11', enabled: true };` — used by tests and tools only until T838; `MATCH_MODEL_RELEASE` is not touched. A world created with it (`careerWorld(input, SCRIPTED_RELEASE)`) settles other clubs' fixtures on the aggregate path with the rule below; its own fixtures still play the grid until T833.
- The aggregate path does not branch on revisions today (its streams are named after revision 1 and stay so). Add one input: `AggregatePlan.placed: boolean`, set by `aggregatePlan` from `isScriptedModel(activeMatchModel(w))`, with each side's `ShapeSide` (tactic, roles by slot) beside it; `scripts/match-parity.ts`' direct callers get `false` by default.
- The place term, in `aggregate/players.ts` beside `share`: `placeTerm = max(AC.place.floor, exp(-(d / AC.place.reach) ** 2 / 2))`, `d` the metres between the cell's centre and `placeFor(sides, side, slot, cell, inPossession)` (T823). The cell: a pass, carry or take-on group's `from` (the actor's side in possession); a tackle or interception: the group's `to` seen from the winner's side, out of possession (mirror the cell into his frame as `mirrorBand` does for bands, the lane mirrored too); a foul: its cell, the fouler out of possession and the fouled in possession; an offside: the group's `to`, in possession. Set-piece groups keep their taker; keepers are weighed like any slot. Shots, key passers, assists, saves, cards, injuries and substitutions are not placed.
- New values in `AGGREGATE_ATTRIBUTION_CONFIG` (`AC`): `place: { reach: 12, floor: 0.02, iterations: 8, target: { median: 13, far: 0.2, winnersFar: 0.25 } }`. `reach` may be lowered (never below 8) if the targets are missed; say so in the result.
- Expected totals: for one side and period, let `w[p][g]` be today's weight of line `p` for group `g` and `n[g]` the group's count to split; the unplaced expectation of `p` in class `c` (short and long passes, crosses and cut-backs, through balls, carries, take-ons, tackles, interceptions) is `E[p][c] = Σ_g∈c n[g]·w[p][g] / Σ_q w[q][g]`. The placed weight is `w[p][g]·placeTerm[p][g]·s[p][c]`, the scales `s` found by `iterations` rounds of proportional fitting so each `(p, c)` expectation returns to `E[p][c]` (a line with `E = 0` keeps weight 0). The draws then run as today on the placed weights. Fouls and offsides are placed without the scale (their per-player counts are small and already weighed by attribute).
- Offsides under revision 11: draw each offside attempt's caught receiver among the lines other than that attempt's actor (the trace pairs them), so no later step has to move an offside off the record's player; revision 10 keeps today's draw.
- Random streams: revision 11 need not consume draws as revision 10 does; nothing compares the two fixture by fixture.
- The trace (`AggregateAttributionTrace`, T819) is the measurement's source; extend it only with what the tests above need (the offside pairs).
- Not in this task: the own club on the aggregate path (T833), the script, any change to shots.
