## goal
The world stores one intervention level (1–4) that alone decides which decisions the staff take and where the clock stops; homes lose their staff switches, the dialog its per-event stops, the popover its decision list and 세부 설정 link, and every criterion stays editable but applies only while delegated.

## impl notes
- engine module packages/engine/src/intervention.ts owns the delegation half of the table: `INTERVENTION_DELEGATES: Record<InterventionLevel, readonly DelegationKey[]>` (1 none; 2 and 3 training, academy, contracts; 4 every key), `interventionOf(w)` (absent → 1), `delegated(w, key)`, `setIntervention(w, level)`; every `w.delegation?.[key]` read becomes `delegated(w, key)` (`coachDelegated` keeps its signature over it). `InterventionLevel = 1 | 2 | 3 | 4` in contracts types; `DelegationKey` stays as the name of a delegable decision.
- the clock stops stay a web concern (WEB-18): apps/web/src/ui/intervention.ts keeps names, summaries and stop lists per level; `stopsOf(w)` replaces the controller's `stopOn` state; drop STOP_KEY reading and `setStopOn`/`setStopOnAll` (remove the stored key once).
- schema: `intervention: z.number().int().min(1).max(4).optional()`; `delegation` accepted only on input and mapped in the legacy preprocess (extend `legacyLevers`), never written. activation.ts drops its contract-talks step (the load mapping covers it) and keeps the reserved-strategy step.
- one shared web component (e.g. `DecisionOwner` in a new apps/web/src/ui/DecisionOwner.tsx) renders "자동 결정 · 개입 N단계 이름" or "직접 결정 · 개입 N단계 이름" with a short note on what the criteria do now, and opens `useInterventionDialog`; it replaces `StaffSwitch`, `DecisionSwitch` and the sponsor checkbox.
- the match-eve card's 멈춤 설정 opens the same dialog, labelled 개입 수준; scripts/calibrate-economy.ts sets level 4.
- release-staff no longer flips the department; a delegated department refills the post by the daily look (STAFF-15), so no extra fill call there.
