# T055 League distribution, local-supporter gate, no supporter income
> st:done@261012 | ssot:ECON-27 ECON-28 ECON-9 ECON-19 ECON-23 ECON-26 | base:ECON@14 | dep:T052 T054 | touches:packages/engine/src/finance.ts packages/engine/src/fans.ts packages/engine/src/settlement.ts packages/engine/src/finance.test.ts packages/engine/src/fans.test.ts packages/engine/src/business.test.ts apps/web/src/ui/FinanceOutlook.tsx apps/web/src/ui/FinancialBreakdown.tsx | lane:commercial

## goal
Competition money is a league distribution (equal share and merit, with broadcasting) and the gate comes from local supporters bounded by the catchment; no income grows with supporters alone.

## acceptance
- [v] Equal share per division, paid in league-round installments through `roundShare`; merit by final position at the close in place of `seasonPrize`; champion total 1.6× the bottom club's in the top flight and 1.3× below; a relegated club keeps 50% of the share it lost for one season; amounts × the football index (→ECON-26), per country and division, in one config table.
- [v] Per-match result bonuses (`matchBonus` income) end for league games; sponsor bonuses stay (→ECON-14).
- [v] Global fan income (`fanIncome`, `fan-income` ledger line) ends; the following no longer pays.
- [v] Gate: attendance from local supporters bounded by a catchment of city, division and stature (→LEAGUE-10), form, opponent and price, capped by capacity; ticket presets × the football index; `gateProjection`, previews and the cash projection use the same model (→ECON-13, →ECON-18).
- [v] Ledger lines `league-share`, `league-merit` and `parachute` are named in financialBreakdown; FinanceOutlook projects them.
- [v] Tests: the distribution sums and the merit order (finance.test.ts); parachute for one season only; a club with 2M followers in the third tier draws no more than its catchment (fans.test.ts); no `fan-income` entries after this rule (business.test.ts).
- [v] `pnpm run typecheck && pnpm run test:unit` pass.

## impl notes
- Constants are first estimates. T062 calibrates them against ECON-29, ECON-9 and CLUB-25 on 50-season runs; keep them in one exported config object so T062 tunes them in one place.
- Cup and continental prizes keep their rules (→LEAGUE-5, →EURO-7) and are only scaled by the football index.

## result
- outcome: A club's league money is now a league distribution. `DISTRIBUTION_CONFIG` in finance.ts holds each country's equal share by division: one entry per professional division plus the amateur league, in 1901 units. Each `settleRound` pays `roundShare(leagueShare(w), round)` into one `league-share` ledger entry per season. The close pays `league-merit` (`leagueMerit` replaces `seasonPrize`, `settleLeagueMerit` replaces `settleSeasonPrize`). The merit is 0 for the bottom club and rises evenly to the champion, whose share plus merit is 1.6× the bottom club's in the top flight and 1.3× below. In the season after a relegation (the latest history entry is last year and one division higher), `parachutePayment` pays half the lost equal share as `parachute` installments. `footballQuote` (price units × the football index's real multiple) prices the shares, merit, ticket receipts and the remaining cup/continental match bonuses, and it reprices the cup prizes (cups.ts) and continental prizes (europe.ts). League games pay no `matchBonus`; the performance sponsor's league bonus stays. `fanIncome`, `fanIncomeUnits` and the `fan-income` line are gone. The gate is built from `localSupporters` = min(following, `catchment`). `CATCHMENT_CONFIG` in fans.ts sizes the catchment by division (60k/28k/14k/8k, 2.5k amateur), a per-country town factor and stature (+4% per point). Attendance follows price, form, the opponent (±12% by reputation gap, the actual opponent in settlement, an even one in projections), marketing and the build. It is capped by local supporters and by capacity. The old reputation and facility-comfort factors are dropped. financialBreakdown names 리그 분배금, 리그 순위 배분금 and 강등 보전금 and returns the catchment. financeProjection lines are 급여·운영비, 홈 경기 수입, 리그 분배금, 강등 보전금 (only while one pays), 후원 계약, 후원 성과 보너스 and 리그 순위 배분금. FinanceOutlook renders them with a note. FinancialBreakdown shows local supporters against the catchment.
