## goal
Every club has a price (ECON-37) and the start can list every club of the playable countries with what WEB-15 shows, plus each tier's anchor and fortune (OWNER-3).

## acceptance
- [v] `clubValue(w, club)` = players' transfer values + stadium/facility stage costs + following × `CLUB_VALUE.following` + division distribution × `CLUB_VALUE.placeSeasons` + cash − debt, floored at `CLUB_VALUE.floor`; reads `club.books` when present, zero cash/debt otherwise — tests with a small fixture world (indebted big club cheaper than a healthy one of equal squad)
- [v] `clubListings(w): ClubListing[]` for every club of the playable countries, valued in its own currency and in pounds (ECON-35)
- [v] `tierAnchor(w, tier)` = median pound price of the clubs in the tier's reach band (top-flight upper/mid/lower+second-top/second–third/lower pro/lowest tier) and `tierFortune(w, tier)` = anchor × `OWNER_TIERS` multiple; test that each tier can afford a club in its reach and 석유 재벌 can afford every club
- [v] facility stage of a club nobody owns comes from `CLUB_TAKEOVER.facilities` (division and stature) for valuation
