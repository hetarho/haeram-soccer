## goal
When an automatic step is still in flight one pace interval after it began (x10's own match day takes about 0.9 s against 0.1 s), the bottom transport shows a looping progress animation captioned 경기 진행 중… (own fixture day) or 진행 중… until the step lands, instead of a calendar that looks frozen; the step, its results and the owed-day catch-up stay exactly as today.

## acceptance
- [v] The progression store publishes the indicator state: it turns on only when a running automatic step (`tick`) has not landed `PACE_MS[pace]` after it began, at every pace, and turns off when the step lands, is cancelled, or the clock stops (owner stop, event stop, watching, mode switch, disposal); a step that lands within its interval never turns it on. Manual 다음 이벤트 keeps its existing processing presentation and never shows it.
- [v] Its caption is 경기 진행 중… when the days the step settles include the day of an own fixture (from the world the step started on, e.g. `nextOwnFixture`), else 진행 중….
- [v] `ProgressControls` renders the indicator in automatic mode inside the transport in reserved space: play/pause, the pace dropdown, the mode text and the season progress rule do not move or resize when it appears or disappears, at 360x740, 390x844 and desktop widths (→WEB-17). It shows no score, result or other settled fact. The animation is decorative (hidden from assistive tech) and the caption is readable text that does not repeat an announcement on every step.
- [v] Results are unchanged (→MATCH-7): the indicator neither delays, pauses nor shortens a step, and owed-day handling (`owed`, `MAX_STEP_DAYS`, background catch-up under →WEB-64) is untouched; existing progression unit tests pass unchanged.
- [v] Unit tests with fake timers and a deferred client reply: off for a step landing inside its interval; on after one interval for a slow step with the own-fixture caption on a match day and 진행 중… otherwise; off on landing, on stop while in flight and on cancel; the x10 0.1 s step test still passes. A ProgressControls test renders the indicator text from the store state and shows none in manual mode.
- [v] A browser check (new or extended e2e in tests/e2e, e.g. the pacing journey) observes the caption on an own match day at x10 and its disappearance once the day lands; execution is left to the host batch and recorded in result limits.
- [v] typecheck, lint and format pass.

## impl notes
- Decision source: ideation/save-storage-and-fast-pace (converted); x10 throughput measured 261026 at about 7.8 days a second, the halt being the own match on the detailed engine (~0.4–0.5 s) plus the day's aggregate fixtures (~0.4 s).
- `tick()` in `apps/web/src/runtime/progression.ts` already sets `inFlight` and `stepAt`; arm a timeout of `PACE_MS[pace]` at the step's start that sets the store flag, clear it in the `finally` and in `stop()`/`dispose()`. Keep the flag in `ProgressionState` beside `running`, not in the game store, so management locks and `processing` keep their meaning.
- No new progression path, engine call or worker message; no change to `command()` or the client.
- e2e journeys that wait on automatic progression must not depend on the indicator's text to advance.

## result
- outcome: `ProgressionState.lateStep` ('match' | 'day') is set by a `PACE_MS[pace]` timeout armed when `tick()` sends its step and cleared in its `finally`, `stop()` (owner/event/watch/mode/world stops) and `dispose()`; the caption kind is read per command from the world it starts on (`daysUntilNextMatch` within the command's days). `ProgressControls` reserves a one-line slot after the AUTO text in automatic mode with an aria-hidden spinner and the plain caption 경기 진행 중… / 진행 중… (no live region). Steps, `owed`, `MAX_STEP_DAYS` and catch-up are untouched; `command()` only had its return type narrowed to the advance-days command.
