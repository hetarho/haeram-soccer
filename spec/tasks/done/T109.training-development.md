## goal
A player can train on his own focus by the assistant's rule or the owner's suggestion or order; 하드 costs injuries and morale; training settles adaptation up to 0.80; growth runs at each player's 프로의식, past potential only when settled, and attributes age by group.

## acceptance
- [v] per-player focus: `w.trainingOrders.players[id]` from an accepted suggestion or an order holds 4 settled rounds for him; else the assistant's player rule gives 회복 at fatigue 40+; else the squad's focus (CLUB-11, CLUB-13)
- [v] the `coach-training` command takes an optional `player`: one suggestion per target (the squad, each player, the academy) every 2 settled rounds; the acceptance chance uses the player rule for a player; every order raises the coach's strain (STAFF-19)
- [v] each settled round under 하드 (squad or his own) a player has 0.5% × (1 + excess fatigue/25) of a training injury from that round's stream, drawn from CLUB-23's table (CLUB-23)
- [v] morale: −1 more each settled round from the squad's third 하드 round in a row; the baseline +1 per 리더 in the last starting XI, at most +3 (CLUB-19)
- [v] training adds TRAINING_GAIN[focus] × 적응력 × gainLevelFactor at his main position × the school factor to adaptation, never past 0.80 by training alone (PLAYER-12)
- [v] growth × PROFESSIONALISM_RATE (PLAYER-15); at or past potential growth continues only at adaptation 0.90+, × overshootFactor(main OVR − potential), never above 99 (PLAYER-16); `developAnnually` ages each attribute by AGEING (grow until, hold, decline from, per season) with ±0.5 noise (PLAYER-17)

## impl notes
- orders.ts: `countDownOrder` also counts down every `trainingOrders.players[id]` and drops expired ones; a sold, retired or loaned-out player's order is dropped.
- coach-training.ts / ops-staff.ts handler: target = squad (no player), a player id (must be an active own player, first team only; the youth director has no player target), or the academy; a player's last suggestion is `trainingOrders.asked[id]` (the coach's own `asked` stays for the squad and the academy); the strain rule and resignation stay as they are.
- staff.ts: `autoPlayerFocus(w, p)` = 'recovery' at fatigue ≥ 40, else undefined; `activeTrainingFocus` becomes `focusFor(w, p)` (player order → player rule → squad order → squad rule); the training summary names who set each.
- training.ts: `settleTraining` loops players with `focusFor`; recovery and growth use that player's focus; the training injury draw uses `random(\`${w.seed}:training-injury:${w.year}:${w.round}:${p.id}\`)` and the existing injury creation in careers.ts (export a helper there); `trainingOrders.hardStreak` counts the squad's settled rounds in a row under 하드 (reset by any other squad focus).
- morale.ts: the 리더 term reads the last own match's starters; the hard-streak term applies in `settleMorale`.
- development: `roundGain` × PROFESSIONALISM_RATE[grade − 1]; when main OVR ≥ potential the gain is 0 unless adaptation ≥ 0.90, then × `overshootFactor(overall − potential)`; the per-attribute 99 cap stays.
- `developAnnually`: for each attribute, age ≤ growUntil → today's yearly growth term (under 27 only, as now; 27–30 +0.3) split by position weights; growUntil < age < declineFrom → 0 (+ noise); age ≥ declineFrom → −perSeason (+ noise); noise = U(0,1) − 0.5.
- apps/web/src/runtime/protocol.ts: the `coach-training` body gains `player?: string` and focus enum 'balanced' | 'recovery' | 'hard' | 'fundamentals' | 'firstTeam'.
