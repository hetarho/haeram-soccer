# T116 Pace chips set the pace only
> st:done@261015 | ssot:WEB-18 | base:WEB@67 | dep:- | touches:apps/web/src/ui tests/e2e | lane:- | explore:-

## goal
Tapping x1, x2, x5 or x10 chooses the clock's pace without starting it; only play starts a stopped clock (owner's request 261014, first built on work/direction as its T106 and carried onto main here).

## acceptance
- [v] a pace chip on a stopped clock sets the pace and leaves it stopped; on a running clock the clock runs on at the new pace; play then runs at the chosen pace
- [v] the chips' accessible names say they choose the pace (e.g. "0.1초에 하루 속도 선택"), with the chosen one pressed
- [v] tests: the e2e specs that started the clock with a chip press play after choosing the pace; one check that a chip alone leaves the date where it was and play then moves it

## impl notes
- ProgressControls.tsx: drop `if (!running) controller.start()` from the chip's click; the chips stay enabled while stopped.
- staff.spec: play ignores a tap in the first half second after the clock stops itself, so the renewal loop retries play until the clock runs or a card shows.
