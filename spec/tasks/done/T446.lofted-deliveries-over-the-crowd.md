## goal
On the candidate model, a lofted cross passes over the players between the crosser and its zone, and the carrier's choice of delivery reads the same reach as a pass, so post deliveries arrive as often as real ones instead of 1.5% (near post) and 0.03% (far post) after T444 (MATCH-31 and MATCH-33 r30).

## acceptance
- [v] `reachOnPath` takes a lofted mode: for a `far` delivery (lofted) only the first `BOX_CONFIG.delivery.loft.foot` (3) m off the crosser's foot and the last `BOX_CONFIG.delivery.loft.drop` (2) m before its zone are reachable; a defender reaching the zone first goes to the existing aerial duel there (MATCH-11). Ground deliveries (`near`, `back`, `square`) and every pass keep T441's reach unchanged.
- [v] `chooseDelivery` subtracts `BOX_CONFIG.delivery.risk` × the delivery path's `reachOnPath` weight (lofted mode for `far`) from each option's score, as `passOptions` does with `LANE_CONFIG.risk`; when every option is cut out cleanly the carrier delivers nothing (the no-blind rule extended) and plays by the rest of his table.
- [v] Tests: `lanes.test.ts` "a lofted ball passes over a defender midway", "a defender at the crosser's foot still blocks it", "a ground delivery keeps the pass's reach"; `decide.test.ts` "a delivery whose path is cleanly reached is chosen less often", "no delivery when every option is cleanly reached"; every new record passes `validateFullMatchRecord`; candidate digests re-recorded where they change, with the measured change in the test comment; the pre-feature and aggregate digests pass untouched.
