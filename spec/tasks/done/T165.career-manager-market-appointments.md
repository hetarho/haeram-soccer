## goal
Merge qualified career candidates with the existing manager market and preserve identity/costs on appointment.

## acceptance
- [v] Qualified unemployed coaches appear as free candidates, own employed coaches as internal-promotion candidates and other-club employed coaches with their actual club/employment status.
- [v] Retained candidates are additional to the ordinary 4–8 free-agent pool and eight other-club manager places; normal generated candidates remain usable.
- [v] Recruiting an employed foreign-club coach charges 1.5× current annual coaching wage compensation plus ordinary manager hiring costs. Internal promotion has no other-club compensation; all ordinary disclosed contract costs remain.
- [v] Appointment ends the coaching post/pay and starts the manager role as the same person, without resetting age/coaching history. Failure/stale availability changes no employment or cash; duplicate requests do not charge twice.
- [v] Direct/delegated manager authority, preferred school, affordability and poaching permission remain active. A nonpoaching delegated policy cannot recruit a compensated other-club coach.
- [v] career-manager-market.test.ts covers all three employment states, rational 3/2 wage rounding, insufficient funds, internal vacancy/refill, hired-candidate exclusion and repeated appointment.
