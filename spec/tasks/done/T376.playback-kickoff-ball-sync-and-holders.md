## goal
Watching an own match (the pre-feature path while `MATCH_MODEL_RELEASE` is off), the first and second half start from the kick-off formation, the ball reaches the player the engine recorded instead of turning in open space, and the recorded carrier is marked whenever a player holds the ball.

## acceptance
- [v] Every pre-feature minute carries explicit times (`startSeconds`, sample `elapsedSeconds`, `ballTimes`) that pass `frameTimeIssues`; the period's last minute maps its longer span onto 60 drawn seconds, as its events already do. Each period's opening is drawn: ball on the centre spot and both sides in their own halves.
- [v] Pre-feature players are sampled at every snapshot (always drawn) and wherever the ball changes segment (at least `preFeatureSpacing` apart), so a completed pass's receiver stands within a metre of the ball where it lands. The ball track adds the segment starts to its 80 regular points.
- [v] Pre-feature flights take `preFeatureFlight` (0.25) of their motion-second duration, so passes fly about as long as the candidate's recorded flights and receivers are seen on the ball. Facts, draws and the candidate's samples and track are unchanged.
- [v] Each observed frame records the ball's holders (`MatchFrame.holders`: seconds into the minute as events' `t`, holder ID or '' while nobody holds it). `resolvePossessionCue` marks the recorded holder first; without one the action under way gives the flight side, a carry that lost the ball is contested, and a sample's owner is read only on frames without holders.
- [v] Unit cases for the kick-off opening, receiver on the ball, holders and the three new cue cases; candidate positions digests re-recorded (facts unchanged); unit suite, typecheck, lint, format, build.

## impl notes
- Probe 261023 (seed dump-1, own league match, pre-feature): the first sample was 7.5 s after kick-off (`j === 0 && !drawFirst` skipped it) so the kick-off was never drawn; the second half held the first half's last scene for 7.5 s, then players jumped ~45 m. Ball to receiver at arrival p50 2.6 m, p90 6 m, max 27–45 m (players 8 samples a minute, ball 80 points). Ball held only 14% of the time (flights squeezed back to back by the launch bound in motion seconds; candidate 39%); carrier ring shown 14%, and a receiver holding the ball before his pass read as a flight because events carry only their arrival.
- No SSOT change: MATCH-12 already requires samples that resolve departures and arrivals and flights that end at the receiver; WEB-95 already reads ownership from recorded facts at the presented instant.
- Frames are never stored (in-memory playback only), so the optional `holders` field needs no save migration; frames without it keep T350's reading.
