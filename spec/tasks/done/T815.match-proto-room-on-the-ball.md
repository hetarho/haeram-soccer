## goal
A short pass is played over a few metres and the two players part again: the script no longer aims a pass at a teammate standing at the ball, the passer takes one touch instead of walking into his receiver, and free teammates leave room around the man on the ball.

## impl notes
- from review/match-engine-split-261110 F2
- Follow T791's chain rules (`scripts/match-proto/README.md`). Staging still never changes a beat's outcome, actor or receiver; it may move the point a pass is received at, as `reachablePoint` already does.
- What was measured on `artifacts/match-proto-261110/playback.json` (seed `demo#17`), so the cause is not re-derived:
  - 15% of completed script passes travel under 6 m and one 0.2 m (`recycle` at 08:06): `feetPoint` aims at the receiver's nominal spot moved `grammar.showFor` toward the ball, and receivers are cast by `nearBall`, so the man whose spot is nearest (the inverted full-back beside the pivot, 9 m apart by the shape) is asked to receive 2–6 m away.
  - while holding, the passer's order is a point `stage.holdDrift` ahead of himself, renewed every tick: he walks toward his pass's aim at 1.8 m/s for the whole hold (6 m in 3.5 s) and ends on his receiver; the passer–receiver gap at the kick is under 1 m in the worst tenth of passes and under 5 m in 22%.
  - `canReceive` adds `motion.reactTime` even for a receiver already standing on the point, so a pass shorter than about 4.2 m at the kick is never ready and every such pass waits the full `stage.maxExtraHold`, the passer still walking in.
  - together: 24 episodes in ten minutes of two teammates within 3 m for more than 2 s, the longest 7 s (`intoPivot` → `circulate` → `circulate` between `H3` and `H5` from 02:00).
- Config (`core/config.ts`; describe each in the header comment):
  - `grammar.minPass: 7` — an open-play pass to feet or into a stride is aimed at least this far from the ball.
  - `stage.kickGap: 5.5` — the least distance between the ball and the point an ordinary pass is received at, and how near to that point the passer's touch may take him.
  - `stage.ownerRoom: 4` — how far a free outfield player of the side in possession keeps his target from the man on the ball and from teammates under an order.
  - `stage.holdDrift` keeps its value (1.5); its comment becomes "the length of the passer's one touch while holding, at most".
- Script, `script/cast.ts`: a private helper `spaced(state, index, p)` returns `p` when `dist(state.ball, p) >= grammar.minPass`; otherwise the point `grammar.minPass` from the ball along the direction ball → `p` (when `p` is within 1e-6 of the ball: ball → `spot(state, index)`; when that is as near too: toward the pitch `CENTRE`), then `clampToPitch(…, PROTO_CONFIG.margin)`. It draws nothing. `feetPoint` applies it to the shown-and-jittered point after its two draws (in place of its final clamp's argument); `stridePoint` applies it to its result. No move, weight or cast rule changes; receivers are cast as before and move away from the ball to receive.
- Stage, `stage/pass.ts`:
  ```ts
  /** The passer's order while he holds: one touch of at most `stage.holdDrift` toward `to`, never nearer to `to` than `stage.kickGap`; the point is fixed when the hold starts. */
  export function holdTouch(state: StageState, actor: number, to: Vec): Order;
  /** `to` moved straight away from the ball until it is `stage.kickGap` from it; unchanged when it is already that far. */
  export function roomyAim(state: StageState, receiver: number, to: Vec): Vec;
  ```
  - `holdTouch`: with `start` = the actor's position now and `d = dist(start, to)`, the target is the fixed point `start + norm(to - start) * Math.min(stage.holdDrift, Math.max(0, d - stage.kickGap))`, pace `walk`. `holdUntil` sets it in place of its moving target; `stage/through.ts` sets it in place of its own moving target (the lines that add `STAGE.holdDrift` to the passer's position every tick).
  - `roomyAim`: with `ball = state.ball.pos`, when `dist(ball, to) >= stage.kickGap` return `to`; else the point `stage.kickGap` from the ball along ball → `to` (when `dist(ball, to) < 0.5`: ball → the receiver's position; when that is also under 0.5: ball → `CENTRE`), then `clampToPitch(…, 0.5)`. `performPass` and `performPassIntercepted` apply it once, right after `ensureOwner`, to their aim (`to`, `R`) when `beat.kind === 'pass'` and the beat is not a through ball; restart kinds, clearances, through balls and `performPassOut` are left as they are.
  - `canReceive`: before the time comparison, `if (dist(state.bodies[receiver].pos, to) <= STAGE.controlRange) return true;`.
- Stage, `stage/world.ts` `separated`: the distance kept from a teammate is `stage.ownerRoom` when the moved player's team is `state.team` and that teammate is the ball's owner or has an entry in `state.orders` or `state.cues`; otherwise `stage.separation` as today. The early returns (keeper, the owner himself, a dead ball, `separation <= 0`) stay.
- A test that compares a reception with `beat.to` (for example `the receiver is at the reception point when the ball arrives`) reads the aim `roomyAim` gives for that beat when the scripted `to` lies inside `stage.kickGap`; prefer leaving the script as it is and computing the expected point.
- Not in this task: no rule against returning the ball to the man who just passed it (10 of 89 completed passes on `demo#17`, a share real football also shows), no change to `grammar.showFor`, weights or probabilities, no change to marking, pressing or free players' pace (T816, T817). `shape.spacing` is expected to fall but is not gated here; T818 reads it.
