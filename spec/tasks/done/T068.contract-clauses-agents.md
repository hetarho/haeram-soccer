## goal
Every contract carries its own promotion and relegation clauses drawn at signing and tilted by the player's agent, applied when the club moves; every player has an agent rated 1–5 that raises his demand.

## acceptance
- [v] Every player has `agent: 1–5`, drawn when he is created (generated squads, market candidates, academy prospects, free agents) from his own stream, stronger more often for better players; it never changes.
- [v] Every contract signed (renewal, improved contract, transfer in, loan excluded, free agent, academy promotion) settles `clauses: { promotion?: number; relegation?: number }` (whole percent) from the signing's stream: a promotion rise in 30% (agent 1) to 80% (agent 5) of contracts, +10% to +60%, larger with a stronger agent; a relegation cut in 80% (agent 1) to 40% (agent 5), −25% to −50%, smaller with a stronger agent; values come from a config object T062 may tune within these ranges.
- [v] At the close, a club that goes up applies each running contract's promotion rise, one that goes down its relegation cut, to the wage from the new season's first round; nothing else changes; the inbox reports the squad's total wage change.
- [v] The demand (→CLUB-26) is multiplied by 1 + 0.03 × (agent − 1).
- [v] Activation from rules 1.15 (→SAVE-10) gives every existing player an agent drawn deterministically from his id and leaves existing contracts without clauses until their next signing.
- [v] The contract talk shows the agent rating and the clauses the new contract would carry (settled before the owner answers, so the answer cannot reroll them); the player sheet shows the agent and the running contract's clauses.
- [v] contracts.test.ts: clause shares and sizes stay within the ranges and lean with the agent over many draws; a promotion applies rises only to contracts that carry one; a relegation applies cuts; the agent premium; activation assigns agents and no clauses. `pnpm run test:unit`, `pnpm run typecheck`, `pnpm run lint` pass.

## impl notes
- Streams: agent from the player's creation stream (`${w.seed}:agent:${player.id}` for activation); clauses from `${w.seed}:clauses:${player.id}:${year}:${day}` at signing, so a talk's clauses are known when it opens and an offer does not reroll them (store them on the talk).
- Agent weighting: P(agent = k) shifts toward 5 with ability: draw u in [0,1), add (ability − 60)/100 clamped to ±0.3, map to 1–5 evenly.
- Clause odds and sizes interpolate linearly by agent level between the endpoints in the acceptance; sizes draw uniformly within an agent-dependent sub-range (e.g. promotion size from 10 + 5·(agent−1) to 35 + 6.25·(agent−1)).
- These are rules 1.16.0 additions (unreleased): add the fields to the 1.16.0 shapes and the activation from 1.15; portable goldens are regenerated by T062.
- Loans keep the parent club's contract; no clauses on loan deals.

## result
- outcome: Every player made by `makePlayer` (generated and youth squads, other clubs' squads, market candidates and free agents, academy prospects) carries `agent` 1–5, the last draw of his own creation stream so his other attributes stay as they were: one uniform draw plus (ability − 60)/100 within ±0.3, cut into five even bands (`AGENT_CONFIG`). The demand is ×(1 + 0.03 × (agent − 1)). Every renewal, improved contract, transfer in, free-agent or market signing and academy promotion stores `clauses` from `${seed}:clauses:${id}:${year}:${day}` (four draws: in or not and size, for each clause); loans get none. `CLAUSE_CONFIG`: promotion in 30%→80% of contracts with size drawn from 10–35% (1-rated agent) to 30–60% (5-rated); relegation in 80%→40% with a cut from 35–50% to 25–35%, stored negative; all linear in the agent's level. A talk stores its clauses when it opens (an unhappy player's floor settles them with that day's stream) and the agreement signs those. The close that moves the club applies each running contract's rise or cut once to the wage after the expiries, before the summer-opening talks, and posts a non-stopping `finance` inbox item with the squad's total wage change. Writer activation (`host.ts`) runs `activateAgents` after `upgradeWorldRules`: every player without an agent (squad and history, academy, pending bids' players) gets `drawAgent(${seed}:agent:${id}, ability)`, and running contracts keep no clauses; `agentOf` gives the same value for a world not yet activated. The talk sheet shows the agent rating, its demand premium and the new contract's clauses; the player sheet shows the agent and the running contract's clauses.
