# T817 match-proto turnovers and marking: players read their man and the kind of turnover, not only the ball
> st:done@261111 | ssot:ARCH-5 ARCH-7 ARCH-21 ARCH-30 | base:ARCH@38 | dep:T816 | touches:scripts/match-proto/stage scripts/match-proto/core/config.ts | lane:- | explore:-

## goal
When the ball changes sides in settled play the two teams no longer wheel round together: near players react first, far players later, and the shapes re-form over seconds at a walk or a jog, while a counter still turns everyone at once and at speed; a defender picks up the man in his zone and moves with him, and a free attacker steps off his marker.

## impl notes
- from review/match-engine-split-261110 F3
- Follow T791's chain rules; staging stays a pure function of the script (→ARCH-5) and never changes a beat's outcome, actor or receiver.
- What was measured on `artifacts/match-proto-261110` (planner's scratch read, `demo`–`demo5`), so the cause is not re-derived: `setPossession` flips `state.team`, every player's nominal place jumps between the in-possession and out-of-possession tables in that tick, and `motion.transitionSeconds` makes all twenty run for three seconds. After a settled turnover (the new side's phase is not `counter`) 44–59% of the players moving along the pitch have reversed within one second and 75–85% of the outfield players are faster than 4.5 m/s after 1.5 s — the same picture as after a counter (80–90%). Marking is a lean of `shape.mark.blend` 0.2 toward the nearest opponent within 7 m of a defender's place (a back-line player across the pitch only): the median distance from a defender to the nearest attacker is 7.4 m and only 28–31% of attackers in the last 40 m have a defender within 4 m, so a defender's movement follows the ball's and nothing else.
- T816 already delays what a free player sees and lags his place. This task adds what kind of turnover it was, and the man.
- Config:
  ```ts
  // motion (stage/mind.ts): after the ball changes sides in live play
  turn: {
    /** Seconds a turnover shapes how free players move. */
    window: 6,
    /** A settled turnover is believed this many seconds later per metre from the ball (up to `own.farCap`). */
    delayPerMetre: 0.02,
    /** The place's time constant inside the window: at least `settled` after a settled turnover, at most `counter` after a counter; the back line keeps `own.lag.line`. */
    lag: { settled: 2, counter: 0.5 },
    /** Inside the window after a counter a free player runs to a place farther than `runBeyond` metres and sprints to one farther than `sprintBeyond`. */
    runBeyond: 3,
    sprintBeyond: 12,
  },
  // shape (stage/mark.ts)
  mark: { radius: 10, keep: 3, goalSide: 1.5, blend: { DF: 0.6, MF: 0.6, FW: 0.3 }, lag: 0.3 },
  unmark: { radius: 4, step: 2.5 },
  ```
  `motion.own.memory` becomes 2.2 (the longest delay is now `own.delay.base + own.delay.perIq + (own.delay.perMetre + turn.delayPerMetre) * own.farCap` = 1.9 s). `shape.mark`'s old `blend: 0.2` and `radius: 7` are replaced by the values above; T800's note that a wider separation pulled defenders off their contests concerned `stage.separation`, which is not changed.
- Turnover state, `stage/world.ts`: `StageState.turn?: { at: number; counter: boolean }`. `setPossession` sets it when `beat.team !== state.team` and `state.live` is true: `{ at: state.t, counter: beat.phase === 'counter' }` (it keeps setting `turnedAt`). `stage/mind.ts` exports `turnOf(state): { counter: boolean } | undefined` — the turnover while `state.t - state.turn.at < motion.turn.window`, else undefined.
- `stage/mind.ts`:
  - `seenBy`: `ref` is read `reactionDelay` ago as in T816; `team` and `phase` are read `reactionDelay + extra` ago, `extra = turn.delayPerMetre * Math.min(ballMetres, own.farCap)` while `turnOf(state)` is a settled turnover and the player is not a back-line player, else 0. Both come from `state.seen` entries (the oldest kept when the memory is shorter).
  - `placeLag(spec, ballMetres, turn?)`: back line `own.lag.line` always; otherwise T816's value, raised to at least `turn.lag.settled` inside a settled turnover's window and lowered to at most `turn.lag.counter` inside a counter's; last, a player with a man in `state.marks` (below) uses at most `shape.mark.lag` whatever the window says — once he believes he is defending he stays with his man.
  - `freeSpeed(d, ballMetres, cap, turn?)`: inside a counter's window it returns `cap` when `d > turn.runBeyond`, and the caller passes the sprint (`paceSpeed('sprint', spec)`) as `cap` when `d > turn.sprintBeyond`, else the run; a nearer place is reached as in T816. Outside the window, and after a settled turnover, T816's behaviour. This is the owner's line: in a fast counter everyone may turn and run at once; in settled play they may not.
  - Direct players are untouched: the presser and the cover still go at once (that is the counter-press and the first man back), keepers, the last-shoulder role, ordered players, dead balls.
- Marking, `stage/mark.ts`:
  ```ts
  /** Who marks whom this tick (defender index → attacker index), one to one; also stored in `state.marks`. */
  export function assignMarks(
    state: StageState,
    snapshot: readonly Vec[],
    zoneOf: (index: number) => Vec,
    canMark: (index: number) => boolean,
  ): Map<number, number>;
  /** Where a marker of `team` on `line` stands for a man at `man`, from his zone place. */
  export function markSpot(team: TeamId, line: Line, zone: Vec, man: Vec): Vec;
  ```
  - Defenders that can mark (`canMark`): outfield players of the side truly without the ball, in live play, without an order or cue, not this tick's presser or cover, who believe they are defending (`seenBy(...).team` is the other side). `zoneOf` is the player's nominal place from what he sees (`defaultTarget`'s `base`).
  - Men: outfield players of the side in possession except the ball's owner.
  - Cost of a pair = `dist(zoneOf(defender), snapshot[man])`; a pair is allowed up to `shape.mark.radius`, and up to `shape.mark.radius + shape.mark.keep` when the man was this defender's man in `state.marks` the tick before.
  - Matching, deterministic: first every pair of the tick before that is still allowed is kept, in ascending defender index; then the remaining allowed pairs are taken in ascending cost (ties: lower defender index, then lower man index), skipping a defender or man already matched. `state.marks` is replaced by the result every tick and emptied while the ball is dead.
  - `markSpot`: `m = man + norm(ownGoal(team) - man) * shape.mark.goalSide`; for `MF` and `FW` `lerp(zone, m, shape.mark.blend[line])`; for `DF` `[zone[0], zone[1] + (m[1] - zone[1]) * shape.mark.blend.DF]` in the world frame's axes as today (the back line keeps its depth, so the offside line and the trap of `stage/through.ts` stay as they are).
  - `stage/world.ts`: `step` calls `assignMarks` once per tick after `remember`, before the targets; `defaultTarget` replaces its nearest-opponent lean by `markSpot` for a defender with a man in `state.marks`, and returns his `base` for one without. When `defaultTarget` is called outside `step` (tests) it reads whatever `state.marks` holds.
- Stepping off a marker, in `defaultTarget` for a free outfield player who believes his side has the ball (not the `onShoulder` role, not the owner): with `o` the nearest outfield opponent to his `base` and `d` their distance, when `d < shape.unmark.radius` his target is `base` moved `shape.unmark.step * (1 - d / shape.unmark.radius)` metres in y away from `o` (when their y are equal within 1e-6: toward the pitch's long axis), y clamped to the pitch margin, x unchanged (the onside cap already applied to `base` holds).
- Expected side effects to record, not to fix here: defenders now stand nearer their men, so `shape.swarm` (seven outfield players within 10 m of the ball) and `shape.spacing` may move either way, and the slow re-shaping after a settled turnover leaves staged places farther from the script's nominal ones for some seconds, so `events.recastRate` and `events.stretchRate` may rise. If a hard metric fails, reproduce the frame in a unit test with a short hand-written script and fix the cause; do not turn a mechanism off to hide it.
- `stage/script.testkit.ts`: `BeatSpec` gains an optional `phase` (default `buildUp`, as today) so the three scripts can be written with `beats(...)`.
- Not in this task: no new script rule (the script already says whether a turnover becomes a counter), no change to pressing roles, press heights, formations' anchors or the offside trap; values are tuned in T818.
