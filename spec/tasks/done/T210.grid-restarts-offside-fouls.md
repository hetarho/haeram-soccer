## goal
The ball leaving play, fouls, offside and every set piece become real scenes at their real spot with their real dead time.

## acceptance
- [v] `grid/restarts.ts` exports `RESTART_CONFIG` (dead times of design §7.2), `classifyOut`, `isOffside`, `foulRestart`, `playRestart`, `Restart`.
- [v] `restarts.test.ts` cases:
  - `gives a throw-in at the crossing point to the other side`
  - `gives a goal kick when attackers touched it last over the byline and a corner when defenders did`
  - `turns a saved or blocked shot over the byline into a corner`
  - `places a free kick at the foul and a penalty inside the box`
  - `flags offside only for a target in the opponent half beyond both the ball and the second-last opponent when the pass is played` (whatever the passer's position; never on a throw-in, goal kick or corner, nor for a target in his own half)
  - `lets a high-decisions runner time his run better and a line breaker more so`
  - `shoots direct free kicks only within range, crosses wide free kicks and corners into the box`
  - `throws in short to the nearest free teammate`
  - `kicks off backwards from the centre spot`
- [v] `pnpm run typecheck`, `pnpm run lint`, the test file pass.

## impl notes
- Types:
  ```ts
  export type RestartKind = 'kickoff' | 'throwIn' | 'goalKick' | 'corner' | 'freeKick' | 'indirectFreeKick' | 'penalty';
  export interface Restart { kind: RestartKind; side: 0 | 1; spot: [number, number]; dead: number }  // dead = seconds (RESTART_CONFIG)
  export function classifyOut(out: { at: [number, number]; lastTouch: 0 | 1; over: 'touch' | 'byline'; fromShot?: boolean; blockedOrSaved?: boolean }): Restart;
  export function isOffside(s: GridState, side: 0 | 1, target: number, r: () => number): boolean;
  export function foulRestart(s: GridState, fouledSide: 0 | 1, at: [number, number]): Restart;   // penalty when inBox in the fouled side's frame
  export function playRestart(s: GridState, rs: Restart, r: () => number, ctx: ShotContext): RestartResult;
  ```
  `RestartResult` reuses `Outcome` (T209) plus the taker index: `{ taker: number; kind: Decision['kind'] | 'cross' | 'shot' | 'penalty'; outcome: Outcome }`.
- Geometry in the engine frame; convert to the restarting side's attack frame to place spots (corner spots (105, 0.5)/(105, 67.5), goal kick (5.5, 34) area edge, kick-off (52.5, 34)).
- Byline rule (design §7.2): ball over the byline the attacking side attacks toward → goal kick when the attackers touched it last, corner when the defenders did; a shot that is saved or blocked out → corner.
- Offside (design §7.3): only when the pass is played by the attacking side from any point, the target stands in the opponent half (attack-frame x > 52.5), the target's x > `offsideLine(s, defending)` and > the ball x, all at the moment the pass is played (MATCH-18 r12: the target's position decides; design §7.3's "played in the opponent half" means the target's half). Probability formula exactly §7.3; `linebreaker` is the play trait id.
- Takers: corners and wide free kicks `setup.takers[side].corner`, direct free kicks `directFreeKick`, penalties `penalty`; if the taker is off the pitch, re-pick by the same key among players on the pitch.
- Free kick (§7.4): direct shot when in band 5 central/half lanes and within 30 m of goal, with probability 0.4; otherwise a cross for band ≥ 5, else a pass (`choosePass`). Corners: 15% short (pass to the nearest teammate), else a cross (`resolveCross` from the corner spot).
- Throw-in: `choosePass` restricted to teammates within 20 m, success uses `resolvePass` with `DUEL_CONFIG.pass` and a +0.05 bonus.
- Kick-off: the owner passes to the nearest teammate behind him (`backBias` 10).
- Goal kick: `keeperDistribution` (T208).
- Penalty: `resolvePenalty` (T209).

## result
- outcome: `grid/restarts.ts` classifies the ball leaving play (throw-in, goal kick, corner; a saved or blocked shot out is a corner), turns fouls into free kicks or penalties, decides offside from the target's position and the runner's timing, and plays every restart (kick-off, throw-in, goal kick, corner, direct and indirect free kick, penalty) at its spot with its dead time; `resolvePass` takes an optional bonus for throw-ins.
