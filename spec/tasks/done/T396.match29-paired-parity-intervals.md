## goal
`scripts/match-parity.ts --reference` reports MATCH-29 parity as a MATCH-28 verdict: every tactic-pairing row's aggregate − detailed difference over the same forced fixtures carries a 95% interval from paired samples and reads equivalent, different or insufficient under MATCH-28's margins.

## acceptance
- [v] The runner keeps, for both paths, the compact per-fixture values each MATCH-29 row reads (result, possession share, xG conceded, high regains, end-of-match freshness, by pairing and venue), keyed by the fixture's seed so the two paths pair fixture by fixture.
- [v] Each row's difference has a 95% interval from the paired samples: outcome-rate rows (draws, win shares and their gap) against MATCH-28's 3 percentage points, mean rows (points a match, possession share, xG conceded, high regains, freshness) against the larger of 5% of the reference mean or 0.1 reference standard deviations; ordering rows report each paired pairwise gap with its interval. A verdict is equivalent only when the interval fits the margin; one straddling the margin is insufficient, never a pass.
- [v] `tacticPairingParity` in the parity artifact carries per row: both statuses, the difference, its interval, the margin and the verdict, plus counts by verdict; the console summary prints them.
- [v] Compact tests on small synthetic samples in `scripts/lib/match-parity.test.ts`: `pairs MATCH-29 rows by fixture and gives each difference a 95% interval` and `a MATCH-29 difference whose interval leaves the margin is different and one straddling it insufficient`.
- [v] No engine change; `docs/CALIBRATION.md` names the method in one paragraph under T372's section heading for follow-ups. Task checks follow ARCH-30.
