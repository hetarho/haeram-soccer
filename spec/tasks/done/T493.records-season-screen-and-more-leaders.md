## goal
A season opened from 시즌별 기록 (or 감독 이력) fills the records area under a top navigator bar instead of the records tabs, and 선수 통산 boards grow by ten per 더 보기 up to 100 players.

## acceptance
- [v] With a season open, the records page tabs are not rendered; a navigator bar at the top of the records content shows the back action on the left (`← 시즌별 기록`, or `← 감독 이력` when 감독 이력 opened the season) and the heading `<season> · 구단 시즌 기록` with `· 진행 중` for the current season. The season picker, competition scope, 분석/경기/결정 and the 리그 순위표 / 컵 대회 기록 links follow below it.
- [v] Back restores the list it came from and its scroll position (season list via the stored `record-list` scroll, 감독 이력 via `returnedToManagers`), and browser back/forward keep working as today.
- [v] With no season open, the records tabs and tab panel behave as before; the open-season content is a labelled region (no tabpanel pointing at a hidden tab).
- [v] 선수 통산: each board shows ten rows; `더 보기` adds the next ten (label names the next place range) until 100 rows or the board's end, then disappears; switching boards starts again at ten. Shared places stay correct across pages.
- [v] `apps/web/src/ui/careerRecords.test.ts` covers `clubLeaders` with `LEADER_LIMIT` (101 eligible players → 100), and the paging helper (`nextLeaderCount(shown, total)`: 10→20, 95→100, at 100 or total → no more).
- [v] tests/e2e: `records.spec.ts`, `owner-workflows.spec.ts` and `record-navigation.ts` use the new back names (`← 시즌별 기록`) and expect no 기록실 tablist while a season is open; a records journey checks 더 보기 on a board with more than ten players when the fixture career has them. Left to the batch run.

## impl notes
- `RecordsRoom.tsx`: `const seasonOpen = tab === 'seasons' && year !== undefined`. Render the `recordsNav` TabList only when `!seasonOpen`; when open render `<nav className={s.recordNavigator} aria-label="시즌 기록 이동">` with the back button (managers.opened → `returnToManagers` with label `← 감독 이력`, aria-label kept descriptive; else the existing list return with label `← 시즌별 기록`) and `<h2>` heading; move 리그 순위표 / 컵 대회 기록 into the `recordControls` row under it. The container becomes `<section aria-label="구단 시즌 기록">` when open, keeping `id="records-panel"` only for the tab panel.
- `App.module.css`: `.recordNavigator` sticky at the scroller top (z-index above content, theme surface and bottom hairline like the page-tab bar, 44px back target).
- `careerRecords.ts`: `export const LEADER_PAGE = 10, LEADER_LIMIT = 100`; `clubLeaders` default limit stays 10 for other callers; `nextLeaderCount(shown, total) = Math.min(shown + LEADER_PAGE, total, LEADER_LIMIT)`.
- `CareerRecordBook.tsx` `LeaderTable`: compute `all = clubLeaders(w.players, board, LEADER_LIMIT)` and `boardPlaces(all, board)` once, show `all.slice(0, shown)`; `ClubLeaders` passes `key={board.key}` so switching boards resets to `LEADER_PAGE`. The button uses the shared quiet text-button treatment, label `더 보기 · {shown+1}–{next}위`.
- No engine, contract or save change.
