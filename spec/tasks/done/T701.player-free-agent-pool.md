## goal
The world keeps real free-agent players (CLUB-49): who joins at the close and at a world's start, who leaves at each refresh, the 200 cap, decline without growth, and the close's top-up signing them before any generated youngster.

## acceptance
- [v] `World.freeAgents` holds full `Player` objects plus the season he became free; it is optional, validated by the world schema (bounded by the cap) and round-trips through save/load; `ProjectedWorld` omits it so the daily view never carries the pool.
- [v] At the close: our expiring player whom `suitorFor` finds no club for joins the pool instead of vanishing (event text unchanged otherwise); every club nobody owns lets go its two least able players aged 21+ of the closing season's squad (announced retirements excluded); a free agent unsigned through a whole season retires; retirement chances (CLUB-3) apply; the pool is cut to the cap by retiring the least able first.
- [v] Every 4 league rounds and at the close (the same call sites as `refreshManagerMarket`), each free agent leaves for a club nobody owns with the configured chance from his own stream; nothing else draws from that stream, so match and other outcomes stay put for a given seed except through the pool.
- [v] A new world (founding, and a generated world before its takeover) starts with the let-go players of its first season by the same rule, so `freeAgents` is non-empty from day one; pre-takeover generation runs the same close step.
- [v] Free agents age and decline (PLAYER-17) at the close and never grow.
- [v] The close's top-up (CLUB-3/CLUB-40) signs the ablest free agent of the needed line rated at most the division's average strength, on that division's market wage with signing clauses, before generating an 18-year-old; delegated academy call-ups keep coming first.
- [v] New `packages/engine/src/free-agents.test.ts` covers each rule above on small synthetic worlds; affected suites (season close, contracts expiry, persistence round trip) are updated and pass.
- [ ] calendar.progression, season and full-model-throughput equivalence tests run and pass or are re-recorded with the reason; golden and prepared worlds regenerated as T685 did.
- [v] typecheck, lint, format:check and the changed/affected unit tests pass.

## impl notes
- New module `packages/engine/src/free-agents.ts` owns the pool: `FREE_AGENT_CONFIG = { letGoPerClub: 2, letGoMinAge: 21, joinChance: 0.2, cap: 200 }`, `seedFreeAgents(w)`, `closeFreeAgents(w, closedYear)`, `refreshFreeAgents(w)`, `joinFreeAgents(w, player, since)`, `takeFreeAgent(w, id)`. T702 reads the pool through these names; keep them exported.
- Type: `FreeAgent = Player & { freeSince: number }` in `packages/contracts/src/types.ts`; `World.freeAgents?: FreeAgent[]`; schema in `packages/contracts/src/schema.ts` as an optional array `.max(FREE_AGENT_CONFIG.cap)` (import the cap from a contracts-level constant if schema cannot import engine; put `FREE_AGENT_CAP = 200` in contracts and have the engine config read it).
- Strip per-season metrics to zeros when a player joins (`zeroMetrics()`), drop `stance`, `loanUntil`, contract clauses; keep identity, attributes, positions, personality, qualities, agent, born.
- NPC let-go reads the closing season's squad: call it before the year rolls over, or read `npcSquad(w.seed, closedYear, club)` through `squad`/`continuousSquad` with the closed year; exclude players with a retained person who announced retirement. Record a person move only for players that already have a `people` record (`movePerson`), never create registry entries for every let-go player (storage).
- Streams: `random(\`${w.seed}:free-agent:${p.id}:${w.year}:${w.round}\`)` for leaving, `random(\`${w.seed}:free-agent-retire:${p.id}:${w.year}\`)` for retirement; the retirement chance table is CLUB-3's (reuse its existing function).
- Decline: reuse the age-decline part of the annual development (PLAYER-17) without the growth part; if it is inlined in `developAnnually`, extract a pure helper in its module and call it from both.
- Top-up: in `season.ts`'s close loop before `makePlayer(... youth ...)`, call `takeFreeAgent` for the line of `refillPosition`/GK need with `overall ≤ round(divisionStrength(w))`, ablest first then id; sign like the youth (clauses, `joinClub` domestic or foreign by his nationality vs the club country, `movePerson`), wage = market wage of the division (CLUB-25).
- Measure generation time before/after on one machine for 1950→1970 (ARCH-34 ratio) and the save size with a full pool against SAVE's budgets; report both in result. If generation slows beyond noise, cache the per-year let-go selection.

## result
- outcome: the world holds real free agents (`World.freeAgents`, at most 200, schema-checked for the cap and duplicates, saved whole, left out of the daily view). A new world starts with every club nobody owns's two least able aged 21+ of its first squads. Each close retires those unsigned through the season, admits the clubs' let-go (read before the playoff) and our expiries no club signs (same event text), draws CLUB-3's chance for let-go players without a recorded 1 April decision, stops everyone at 41, ages newcomers by PLAYER-17's decline only and keeps the ablest 200. Every 4 rounds and at the close each joins a club nobody owns with 20% from his own stream. While he is free his old club's generated place is someone else's, the fixture helpers' read copy included. The close's top-up (`topUpAtClose`) signs the ablest free agent of the needed line rated at most the division average, on its market wage with clauses, before any generated 18-year-old; delegated call-ups come first. Measured 1950→1970 (generation-reference, node): before 7.7–11.3 s, after 8.5–11.3 s; five alternating pairs after/before 1.01–1.13 (median 1.09) on a machine shared with other sessions (load 6–16); about 30 ms a close in the profile. World JSON 7.66 → 7.95 MB, gzip 815 → 852 KB, people 4,937 → 5,092. A full pool of 200 is 140,109 B JSON, 15,049 B gzip against SAVE-4's 6 MiB checkpoint and 32 MiB decompressed limits.
