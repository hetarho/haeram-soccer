## goal
Measure the grid engine against every MATCH-23 band between equal top-tier sides and tune the `*_CONFIG` constants until the bands hold or the timebox ends.

## acceptance
- [v] `scripts/grid-bands.ts` (run `pnpm exec tsx scripts/grid-bands.ts [--matches N] [--quick]`) plays N (default 600, quick 150) grid matches between equal-strength top-tier NPC sides of ENG/ESP/GER/ITA (pairs whose `rating` differs ≤ 2, all four tactics mixed) and prints one table: every MATCH-23 row, its band, the measured value and pass/fail, plus added time per match and ball-in-play minutes.
- [v] `GridResult` exposes `diagnostics: { ballInPlay: number; shots: { side: 0|1; x: number; y: number; xg: number; header: boolean }[] }` (attack-frame metres) used by the script (no record change).
- [v] `packages/engine/src/grid/bands.test.ts` case `stays inside coarse real-football guards` (120 matches, < 30 s): goals per match 2.0–3.6, shots per team 9–17, passes per team 350–600, shots from 30 m+ ≤ 15%, shots from the own half ≤ 1%, yellows per match 2–6.
- [v] `docs/CALIBRATION.md` gains a section `격자 엔진 분포 (T215)` with the final table (measured vs band), the constants changed and their final values, and the date/seed/match count.
- [v] exit: when 4 h have passed, record the last full table and the constants in `docs/CALIBRATION.md` and this result, list every band still failing with its closest value; the task is done then. A band that cannot be reached without changing a rule (e.g. the decision model itself) is reported as an update-ssot suggestion in the result, not forced.

## impl notes
- Equal sides: take `createWorld` worlds for seeds `grid-bands-1..4`, clubs with `tier === 0`, compute `rating(w, c)`, pair clubs within 2 points; build fixtures `{ ...template, id: `bands:${i}`, home, away }` and call `playGrid(buildGridSetup(w, f, { observe: false, recordPlayers: true }))`.
- Measures (team per match unless noted, both sides averaged): passes attempted (`metrics[2]`) and completion (`[3]/[2]`); direction shares from a counter you add to diagnostics (forward/lateral/back of every pass attempt, ±45° in the attack frame); possessions (count possession changes / 2) and median passes per possession; share of possessions ending in a shot; shots, on-target share, xG per shot; shot place shares (goal area / rest of box / outside) and mean distance, 30 m+, own half; crosses (TD.crosses) and completion; take-ons (TD.takeOns) and success (`metrics[8]/TD.takeOns`); tackles + interceptions; throw-ins, corners, goal kicks; fouls, offsides; yellows, reds (both sides); goals per match; set-piece goal share (goals within 10 s of a corner/free-kick restart — add a `setPiece` flag on diagnostics shots), penalty goal share, headed goal share; goals by line (FW/MF/DF from the scorer's slot line); ball in play (minutes).
- Tuning order (stop when bands hold): 1) action durations and dead times → ball in play, possessions; 2) `DECISION_CONFIG` mix factors and `SHAPE_CONFIG` pressing → passes, direction, take-ons, crosses; 3) `DUEL_CONFIG.pass` → completion, possessions; 4) `SHOT_CONFIG` and shot restrictions → shots, places, xG/shot; 5) `CARD_CONFIG` and foul probabilities → fouls, cards; 6) corner/free-kick delivery → set-piece share.
- Never tune by special-casing results (no "if goals > 3"); only the documented constants.
- Keep `bands.test.ts` ranges coarse so later tasks do not flake; T216 changes strength sensitivity, not these distributions.

## result
- outcome: all 37 MATCH-23 rows hold over 600 matches (seeds `grid-bands-1..4`, 2024 rules; 7/37 at the start, 26/37 at the h8t4 WIP) and over 1,200 (goals 2.84, headed goals 18.8%). `scripts/grid-bands.ts` prints the table; `GridResult.diagnostics` carries ball in play, shots (place, xG, header, set piece, penalty, goal, line), pass directions and possessions; `bands.test.ts` holds the coarse guards. Beyond the constants, the scenes real football has were added: a set-piece arrangement (best headers up, defenders mark goal-side and hold the six-yard line, the men who went up hold 8 s for the second ball), a back line that drops goal-side of a ball played behind it, 50/50 fouls outside the boxes and careful defenders in their own third, the failed pass's receiver weighing 0.35 in the loose ball, a cross target drawn by aerial strength against his marker with open-play crosses mostly brought down, short carries in the box, a long-shot falloff beyond 28 m and a floor on the shot pressure factor (it made xG negative above pressure 2). Final table, constants and reasons: docs/CALIBRATION.md `격자 엔진 분포 (T215)`.
