## result
- outcome: clubs nobody owns trade by pull (packages/engine/src/npc-market.ts). Each settlement day every club with a need, in stature order, bids for up to `NPC_MARKET.targetsPerNeed` reachable targets by slot gain. Targets come from an index by country, division and line, sorted from the ablest down. Each list is read from the buyer's wage ceiling (the highest OVR whose wage at the club, at the cheapest age of 14 or 41 with no promise, fits its headroom). The read stops once nobody left can beat both the need's threshold and the kept targets at the slot; a slot rating stands at most `searchSlack` 6 above main OVR, and `targetsPerNeed` × `searchSpare` targets are kept. The fixed 24-entry depth is gone. A player whose club cannot sell now, or whose wage and lowest bid the buyer's envelope cannot hold never takes a target's place. This affordability check is pure and fires no wage-decision observer. Reach is the own country's division and below, a higher division only on loan, other top flights on a seeded day. The bid is the asking fee × a seeded share (0.5–1), or the fee itself for a buyer holding 3× it. The seller answers through CLUB-52 (`npcSaleContext`: relegated-starter and above-division rows) and the player through CLUB-53 (`npcTermsContext`, 100% of his market wage at the buyer). The bid, the seller's draw and the player's draw are keyed `<seed>:npc-bid|npc-sale|npc-terms:<year>:<summer|winter>:<buyer>:<player>`, one draw per pair and window. CLUB-52's contract rows count half before 1995 for every seller, our club included (`saleContext` supplies the year). `NPC_MARKET_ERA.anchors` / `npcMarketCaps` cap bids, buys and sales per club and window; `compareSellDown` orders sell-down; the move observer gets a `MoveReason` and nothing new is stored. Decision A (coordinator, 261118, CLUB-52/53 r77 as written): the designated per-pair bid day (`npc-bid-day`, `npcBidDay`) is gone. Every buyer reads every target on every settlement day in stature order, so a need that opens later in the window still reaches its best target and the higher-stature club still wins a contested player. A buyer may ask for the same player again on a later settlement day; it meets the same per-window bid, sale and terms draws, so a re-ask never rerolls, and each re-bid counts against the era's bid cap. A pair is asked at most once a settlement day. No saved state and no contracts type change. Review round 4: the relegated-starter and above-division rows are the seller's own circumstances whoever bids, so our bid to a club nobody owns reads them too. `npcSellerRows` is shared by `saleContext` (the buyer's division is our club's) and `npcSaleContext`. Our club's own selling rows are unchanged, and it reads neither row as a seller.
