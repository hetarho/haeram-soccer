## goal
Give the candidate engine enough match time for every ball route and player relocation, including stoppages.

## acceptance
- [v] Add shared `GRID_MOVEMENT_CONFIG` and `GRID_BALL_CONFIG` in `grid/movement.ts` using the contract defaults. Movement uses pace/fatigue bounds, bounded acceleration/turning and actual elapsed match time.
- [v] Replace candidate direct position resets for restart takers, set-piece participants, receptions and possession changes with scheduled preparation/movement. The ball returns to its restart spot through a recorded retrieval/placement interval; a kick cannot occur before arrival.
- [v] Ball travel determines a feasible action duration instead of being compressed into a fixed deadline. Dead time, in-play time, added time, incidents and participation all use the same elapsed-time accounting; preparation is not double counted. A side's player minutes never exceed eleven times the played length (T273 found 991 > 990 from per-line rounding), so every candidate record passes `validateFullMatchRecord` without loosening it.
- [v] Observation traces expose semantic movement knots and explicit period/entry/exit transitions. Candidate simulation with and without observation yields identical settled records.
- [v] `grid/movement.test.ts` covers `bounds speed acceleration and turning`, `does not teleport the restart taker or set-piece group`, `records retrieval before the kick`, `waits for a long flight to land`, and `keeps continuity at minute boundaries`; engine/restart cases cover substitution, injury, dismissal, goal and extra-time transitions.
- [v] Task checks follow ARCH-30. Record any resulting target-band drift for T278; do not weaken distribution targets in this task.

## impl notes
- Use T273's candidate revision and path contract. Preserve the pre-feature path for unsupported/missing model metadata until prospective activation; do not rewrite previously settled playback.
- Motion logic belongs in the engine and its trace, not in a renderer-only fake route. Restarts and shape consume the shared scheduler rather than each maintaining their own relocation shortcut.
- Retain actual incident/outcome destinations and roster identities, including the frozen match appearance added by the identity feature.

## result
- outcome: Done. When `GridSetup.model` is set, the grid runs the candidate motion model; without it the pre-feature path runs unchanged (`moveAll`, fixed durations, direct placement, old minute rounding). `grid/movement.ts` adds `GRID_MOVEMENT_CONFIG` (acceleration 4 and braking 6 m/s², turn 180°/s, 0.2 s step) and `GRID_BALL_CONFIG` (pass/turnover 20, restart 18, shot 30, max 40 m/s; retrieval 6 m/s plus 1.5 s placement). Top speed stays `speedOf` (pace and fatigue). `stepToward`/`reachSeconds`/`advance` form the shared scheduler, and `prepareRestart` prepares every restart. An action lasts the longest of its drawn duration, the ball's flight along its legs, and the new owner's run to the ball. The kick is timed so the ball lands as the action ends. Receivers, interceptors, tackling players, loose-ball winners and carriers move there instead of being placed (`setOwner` and carries in `engine.ts`). A restart carries the ball from where it rests (an out point, the foul, the net, the byline) to the spot, while the taker and its scene walk there. The scene is `setPieceTargets` for deliveries into the box and every kick-off spot after a goal. Dead time is the nominal value or the time actually needed, whichever is longer, never both. `playRestart` takes this through a `RestartPreparer`; `placeAtOnce` keeps the old behaviour. Only a period's start places players directly, kick-off taker included. A substitute enters at halfway on his touchline. Added-time credits are the dead seconds actually charged plus any wait beyond the nominal dead time. Records carry `model` and always `incidents`. `lineMinutes` in `record.ts` rounds each entry/exit boundary once, in the order they happened (by elapsed seconds), so a side never passes 11× the match length and everyone who came on gets at least a minute. Trace entries gain `knots` (retrieve, place, kick, touch, receive, arrive, carry, tackle) and `transition` (period, entry, exit). Both are documented in docs/MATCH-MODEL-CONTRACT.md "Detailed movement (T274)" and design §1.2/§4.4/§7.2/§8.2/§8.3/§10.1.
