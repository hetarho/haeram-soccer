## goal
On revision 4 a corner leaves 2–3 attackers back near halfway and 1–2 defenders up as outlets, drawn per corner, with more up for a side chasing the game and more back for a side protecting a lead from the 80th minute, and the keeper up in added time one goal behind.

## acceptance
- [v] On revision 4 `setPieceTargets` for a corner draws the attacking side's rest count and the defending side's outlet count from `SHAPE_CONFIG.setPiece.numbers` with the restart's stream: by default rest 2–3 and outlets 1–2; from `lateMinute` (regulation minute, `regMinute`) an attacking side behind keeps `chasingRest`, one ahead `protectingRest`; a defending side behind leaves `chasingOutlets` up, one ahead `protectingOutlets` (everyone in the box).
- [v] The rest players stand near halfway goal-side of the outlets (one per outlet, the others central behind them) and the outlets near halfway in their own half, both as scene places the preparation walks them to; the attacking players not kept back take the box spots and edge places (`box`, `edge`, then `extraBox` when more go up), the least aerial staying back; the defenders not left up take the man, zonal and edge places, extra ones `extraZonal`.
- [v] In added time of the match's last period (period 2, or 4 after extra time) an attacking side exactly one goal behind sends its keeper to `keeperSpot` in the box as one more attacker; he returns to his goal after the second ball like everyone else.
- [v] Revisions 1–3 and the pre-feature path keep today's scene draw for draw (no extra draw); delivered free kicks keep today's numbers on every revision.
- [v] Tests (`grid/shape.test.ts`): "revision 4 corners keep 2–3 back near halfway and 1–2 outlets" (many draws: counts within range, both values seen, positions within the halfway band), "from the 80th minute a side behind sends more up and a side ahead keeps everyone back", "the keeper goes up in added time one goal behind, not two", "revision 3 corners keep today's scene".
- [v] Measured on the 16 probe fixtures (scratch script, not kept): attacking outfielders within 25 m of the goal at the kick, taker included, mostly 7–8 (was 9 in 36%), defending mostly 8–9 (was 9 in 77%), the rest standing a median 45–60 m from that goal line; reported in result.

## impl notes
- Config, defined here only (attack frame of the corner-taking side, metres): `SHAPE_CONFIG.setPiece.numbers = { rest: [2, 3], outlets: [1, 2], lateMinute: 80, chasingRest: [1, 2], protectingRest: [3, 4], chasingOutlets: [2, 2], protectingOutlets: [0, 0], restX: 50, restCoverX: 45, outletX: 55, outletY: [24, 44], keeperSpot: [99, 34], extraBox: [[99, 34], [96, 27], [96, 41]], extraZonal: [[101.5, 40], [101.5, 28]] }`. A count range `[a, b]` draws uniformly among its integers; a fixed count draws nothing.
- `setPieceTargets(s, side, taker, r?)` takes the restart's stream (`playRestart`'s `r`) only on revision 4; the existing `stayUp` stays the revision 1–3 rule. Outlets are the defending side's most advanced outfielders, as today's `stayUp` picks.
- The rest and outlet players get places in the scene (`up`/`marks` keep their meaning; add a `rest` list to `SetPieceScene` for both sides' players who stay away from the box) so `prepareOnClock` walks them there and `defendSetPiece`/`restartTaken` hold them through the second ball (`SHAPE_CONFIG.setPiece.stay`), after which shape takes over.
- A keeper going up is one more `up` entry for the attacking side; nothing else about keepers changes.
