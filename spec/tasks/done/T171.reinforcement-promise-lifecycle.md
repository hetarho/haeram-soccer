## goal
Resolve each commitment from actual recruitment facts and personnel events without extra numerical penalties.

## acceptance
- [v] At creation persist the original line/position/slot target and absolute end of the next transfer window after the current window. Do not recompute a later deadline on each read/season.
- [v] One actual external arrival fitting meetsRequest from promise date through deadline inclusive qualifies: permanent, loan or free agent, including an early qualifying alternative. Existing players, bids and academy promotions do not qualify.
- [v] Judge the day after the deadline, after deadline-day arrivals are recorded. No qualifying arrival gives unfulfilled, including lack of funds/candidates; retain actual cause and a distinct response.
- [v] Requesting-manager departure cancels an outstanding promise; director change alone retains it. Cancellation is not fulfilment/failure, and a successor is not judged for a predecessor's commitment.
- [v] Same manager/target outstanding commitment cannot duplicate or reset/extend its deadline through another mediation, shortlist refresh, settings change, menu or reload.
- [v] Promise choices/outcomes add zero numerical trust/morale change. Existing window request judgement still applies once (−3 only when none was met); a later free agent cannot rewrite that completed evaluation.
- [v] promises.test.ts covers summer→winter/winter→summer deadlines, exact boundary arrivals, all three arrival forms, original target after tactics change, both personnel changes, repeated judgement and no double trust penalty.
