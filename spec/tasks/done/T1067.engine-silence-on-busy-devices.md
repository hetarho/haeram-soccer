# T1067 Keep a busy engine worker alive on slow devices
> st:done@261124 | ssot:ARCH-6 WEB-18 WEB-64 SAVE-8 ARCH-30 SAVE-10 | base:ARCH@54 WEB@232 SAVE@76 | dep:- | touches:apps/web/src/runtime tests/e2e | lane:- | explore:- | run:claude/opus@high

## goal
Main CI 45417944693 (0.8.26) failed `tests/e2e/second-career.spec.ts:292`: choosing AUTO at a real season close answered "진행 방식이 변경되지 않았어요. 다시 시도해 주세요." A local repro on two cores (`taskset -c 0,1`, `CI=1` built preview, two workers, `--repeat-each=4`) failed 2 of 4 twice, and client diagnostics showed why: the client failed the engine worker as hung ("계산 Worker가 종료되었거나 응답하지 않습니다"). First a manual `next-event` across the season close stayed silent past its 30 s watchdog. With that limit raised, the `settings` command right after the close stayed silent past its 15 s one. Engine work is synchronous and reports only between days, so one season-close day can hold a busy device silent that long; a failed worker drops the unsaved days. A player on a slow device needs the same tolerance.

## acceptance
- [v] Commands and reads share one engine silence limit long enough for a season close on a busy device (storage and generation keep theirs). A unit test with a held worker shows a manual event and a settings command surviving more than 60 s of silence, and a worker silent past the limit still being failed; the first two fail on the old limits (deterministic; worker, unit).
- [v] A request queued behind an earlier request allowed at least as long still waits its turn (`waitsBehindLonger`), and once the request ahead is answered it gets its whole limit from then, so a read behind a slow command is not taken for a hang (deterministic; unit).
- [v] The two-core repro of second-career.spec.ts:292 passes 4 of 4 with the fix (host, recorded in result); browser journeys otherwise run in CI (ARCH-30).
- [v] Run the ARCH-30 task tier with `vibe check --tier task` before committing.

## impl notes
- Independent review (Codex, read-only) of the first draft found the equal-limit queue case (in the acceptance above) and that the progression controller (WEB-64) takes a shown page's step or reads silent past `AWAY_GAP_MS` (2 min) for a slept page and plays owed days. A `landedAt` fix for it was withdrawn: the re-review showed it drops the catch-up of a page that slept with a step in flight, which is likelier at x10 than one step silent for 2 min. Before this task such a step failed the worker, so the away catch-up is the milder outcome; it stays a known limit.
- Main push is a release to players: prepare the patch with `pnpm run release:prepare` (SAVE-10).

## result
- cause: the client's request watchdog (apps/web/src/runtime/client.ts) failed the engine worker after 15 s without an answer (30 s for a manual `next-event`). Engine work is synchronous and reports only between days. Under the two-core repro, client diagnostics logged `expire … command:next-event 30000` on the season-closing step (2 of 4 runs), and with that limit raised `expire … command:settings 15000` on the AUTO change right after the close (2 of 4 runs; the dialog then showed "진행 방식이 변경되지 않았어요"), exactly main CI 45417944693's failure. 0.8.25's second-career failure (main 45389461640, MANUAL 30 s after AUTO) fits the same cause.
- fix: commands and reads share `ENGINE_SILENCE_MS` = 120 s (storage and generation keep 600 s); `waitsBehindLonger` (requestTimers.ts) keeps a request waiting behind an earlier one allowed at least as long; when a request is answered, every request sent after it is re-armed from that answer, so a read behind a slow command gets its whole limit once its turn comes. The oldest pending request still fails a hung worker after 120 s of silence.
- tests: client.test.ts — a manual event and a read behind it finish past 61 s of silence; a settings command silent past 30 s lands; a read behind a step answered after 100 s survives to 130 s; a worker silent for two minutes is still failed. The first, second and third fail on the code before each fix (verified by reverting client.ts). requestTimers.test.ts covers the equal-limit queue.
- repro (host): a copy of second-career.spec.ts:292 (`tests/e2e/diag-mode.spec.ts`, not committed) with client diagnostics, `CI=1 PREVIEW_BUILD=1`, Chromium, two workers, `--repeat-each=4`, pinned to two cores with `taskset -c 0,1`: on 30f72ad3d it failed 2 of 4 in two runs (worker failed on `next-event`); with only the next-event limit raised, 2 of 4 (worker failed on `settings`); with the final code (262f9d0f1's client.ts and requestTimers.ts) 4 of 4 passed, with no watchdog expiry logged.
- review: Codex read-only reviews (three rounds). Round 1 found the equal-limit queue and the away gap; round 2 found the re-arm gap and that a `landedAt` away-gap fix drops the catch-up of a page slept with a step in flight, so that fix was withdrawn (known limit in impl notes); round 3 found no remaining correctness issue.
- at: 262f9d0f1
- verified: `vibe check --tier task --diff-base 30f72ad3d` (typecheck, lint, format, i18n, affected units 68 tests); `vitest run` client, requestTimers, progression, progression.runahead and progressMode tests (154 passed).
