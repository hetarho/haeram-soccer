## goal
A device with three or more cores starts two fixture helpers, never one, and on a round day the engine worker computes its own share of the other fixtures once our match is played; worlds stay identical with and without helpers.

## acceptance
- [v] `HELPER_CONFIG` starts two helpers from `hardwareConcurrency` 3 (minCores 4→3, count 2); fewer cores or an unknown count starts none (deterministic; worker, task stage).
- [v] `HelperPool.run` gives the engine worker a share computed after our match with the same round-job code the helpers run, and resolves every result in job order; a failing or late helper still ends helper use and the engine worker computes the rest (helperPool.test.ts) (deterministic; worker, task stage).
- [v] Hand off the season comparison in fixtureHelpers.integration.test.ts — no helpers, two helpers, and two helpers with the engine share — to the shared integration gate (deterministic; host, integration stage).
- [v] Measured round-day wall time on this Linux host pinned with `taskset -c 0,1`, `taskset -c 0-2` and unpinned: no helpers, the current setup and the new one, through scripts/perf/helpers.mjs, kept in artifacts/benchmarks/x10-cost-levers/T997.
