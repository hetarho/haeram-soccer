# T1066 Unlock management after a stop under load

## goal
Under load the game can stay locked after the owner stops automatic play or changes the progression mode. Every office control stays disabled with "자동 진행 중에는 현재 날짜의 정보가 아직 계산되지 않아 이 영역을 볼 수 없습니다.", and `자동 진행 시작` is shown but disabled, for minutes. Main CI 45389461640 failed `tests/e2e/progression-settings.spec.ts:201` (the 위임 설정 `부재 중에도 업무 계속` checkbox stays disabled for 150 s after `자동 진행 정지`) and `tests/e2e/second-career.spec.ts:292` (`progression-mode` stays MANUAL 30 s after choosing AUTO at a season close). Locally with `CI=1`, two workers and other load, these failed 2 of 6 runs on 0.8.25 (22a22cf0f), 3 of 8 on the same code with T1062 reverted, and 0 of 8 on 0211dd2a0 when the host was quieter, so the lock predates 0.8.25 and depends on load. WEB-97 locks management only until the admitted step finishes and the computed days ahead have been shown. Find why the lock outlives that and fix it, so a player never needs a reload after stopping.

## result
- explore: started 2026-11-24T13:36Z, timebox 2 h; ended at about 13:50Z with the cause reproduced and fixed.
- cause: `ProgressionController.catchUp` (apps/web/src/runtime/progression.ts) never ended an owner's stop. A hidden tab, or a return after a slept page (WEB-64), plays its owed days as catch-up steps; when the tab is shown again while such a step is still in flight under load, the shown tick finds the step in flight and schedules nothing, and `pause()` then sees a step in flight and enters `draining` (WEB-97's lock while computed days are shown). When the step lands, `catchUp` shows it and only saved, without `drained()`; no timer was left, so `draining` (and the client's `clockRunning`) stayed on for good: every office control dimmed with the 자동 진행 중 reason and 자동 진행 시작 shown but disabled. This is exactly `progression-settings.spec.ts:201` (hide → show → 자동 진행 정지 → 위임 설정's 부재 중에도 업무 계속 disabled), and its `저장 완료 · r5` snapshot is that early game; it needs a catch-up step slower than the time between showing the tab and the stop, hence the load dependence and its age (catch-up predates 0.8.25).
- fix: when `catchUp` ends with the clock stopped while draining, it calls `drained()` (unlock, stopped reads, one save) instead of only saving. WEB-97's lock still holds while the step is in flight and until its day is shown; nothing else changed.
- reproduction (unit, fake timers and a fake worker whose steps take 3 s under "load", `progression.runahead.test.ts` › "the lock after a stop under load (T1066, WEB-97)"): (1) x1, hide, a 3 s catch-up step in flight, show, stop: before the fix the lock stays on after the step landed and was shown (`expected true to be false`), after it unlocks, saves once and sends no further command; (2) a return's catch-up step in flight at the stop: same failure before, passes after; (3) a mode change published past seven held days (no step in flight, as the engine worker answers in order): the clock stops, management unlocks and the mode stays manual — passes before and after, so a mode change answered with days queued is not a lock cause. A stop with a day in flight and days held is already covered by "a step still in flight at the stop is shown at the pace before the lock lifts" and passes.
- second-career.spec.ts:292 (MANUAL 30 s after choosing AUTO at a season close): no lock path found.
