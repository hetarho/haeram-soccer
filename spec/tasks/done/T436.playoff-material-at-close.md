# T436 The promotion playoff's own material reduced at the season close

## goal
An own club that plays the promotion playoff keeps none of that match's post-match material past the season close, like every other own match of the season (MATCH-5).

## acceptance
- [v] `closeSeason` in `season.ts` reduces the season's own match material only after `promote` has settled the promotion playoff, so the playoff's own matches join the season's heat maps and lose their per-match material with the rest; nothing else in the close changes order.
- [v] `material.test.ts` gains "the promotion playoff's material is reduced at the close": the FOUNDING club on the candidate model (`ENABLED_RELEASE`) reaches the lower promotion playoff, and after the close `1950:lower-promotion:0:0` and `:1:0` keep outcomes, highlights, match xG and counters but no material; the case fails on the unfixed source.
- [v] Existing `material.test.ts` cases pass under `pnpm exec vitest run packages/engine/src/material.test.ts` and under `--config vitest.release-on.config.ts`.
- [v] Task checks (ARCH-30): typecheck, lint, format:check, `vitest run --changed`.

## impl notes
- Found by T420 (limit 1): `reduceSeasonMaterial` (`season.ts`, after the season's history entry) runs before `promote`, which plays the playoff, so the playoff's material survives the close against MATCH-5's bounded retention.
- An approved implementation from before the renumbering is on branch `task/v9n4-release/T427-9420e805` (commit 0969b77c, "Implement T427"): cherry-pick it, rename what names T427 to T436, and re-run the checks.
- The heat map a player keeps for the season includes his playoff touches once the order is fixed; the history entry the reduction reads is already written before `promote`, so read it as today.
