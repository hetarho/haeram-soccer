## goal
Reference careers at every difficulty level are measured against LEAGUE-13's bands, and the rules' global constants are tuned toward them within the timebox.

## acceptance
- [v] `scripts/calibrate-economy.ts` takes `--levels easy,normal,hard,extreme` and reports per level: careers with a top-flight title, seasons to the first (median, fastest), share of top-flight titles after the first, seasons in the top flight, bankruptcies — beside the ECON-29 bands

- [v] tuning, if any, changes a constant for every level alike (adaptation gains or starts, the manager-change loss, cohesion rise or drops, the 하드 numbers, generation offsets) — never a level's own number and never a probability the owner rolls; each change is listed with before and after
