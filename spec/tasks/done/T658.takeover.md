## goal
`takeOver(w, clubId, choice, preset)` turns an ownerless world into a career: the chosen club as it stands becomes `w.playerClub` with its squad, manager, staff posts, facilities, supporters, identity, books and loan, and the owner's state starts.

## acceptance
- [v] the club's generated squad becomes persisted own players, filled to 20 at its level covering the manager's formation with two goalkeepers (CLUB-1); every player's adaptation starts at the club's 조직력 (OWNER-4)
- [v] the club's manager stays with his school, style and level (OWNER-4); other staff posts as `basicStaff` gives a new club; academy empty; settings `defaultSettings()`; direction from the chosen preset via the founding preset installer
- [v] facilities at `CLUB_TAKEOVER.facilities`; supporters from reputation (ECON-23); identity kept (no editor)
- [v] cash above zero stays; debt becomes `TakeoverLoan` (`TAKEOVER_DEBT`); the price leaves the fortune (`tierFortune` − price); refuses a club the owner cannot afford
- [v] `w.owner` starts with tier, traits, fortune, stake 0, dividend 0, fanMood `FAN_MOOD.start`, takeover facts; the first history entry is the takeover (HIST-5)
- [v] test: a world from `generateHistory` (or a fixture world when T655 is not merged — use a small built world) taken over plays one season with the existing loop
