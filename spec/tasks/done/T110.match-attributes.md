## goal
Crosses, headers, key passes, a keeper's distribution and sweeping read their own attributes, and a slot's role and a player's play traits shift what he attempts, while the calibrated team rates hold.

## acceptance
- [v] crosses: a wide carrier (LB, RB, LWB, RWB, LM, RM, LW, RW) in the final third may cross — share by role and traits — crossing vs the defenders' heading and the keeper's handling; a won header is a shot on the header's heading (MATCH-11)
- [v] key and progressive passes read vision; a keeper's passes read distribution; 스위퍼 키퍼 wins some balls in behind on pace and distribution
- [v] roles and play traits shift action shares within the team rates: each role and trait adds a bounded weight to its action (shot, long shot, dribble, cross, key pass, press) for its holder; the side's totals stay anchored by the profile (MATCH-8/9)
- [v] `strategy` tactical fit reads MATCH-8/9's attributes; motion players take their formation spots and roles move them (an inverted winger cuts in, a 펄스 나인 drops)
- [v] `calibration.test` keeps its bands; `scripts/calibrate.ts` re-run, mean absolute error recorded in docs/CALIBRATION.md (within 2.5 points of the Elo target, MATCH-2)
- [v] tests: `duel.test` `crossers cross and headers score`, `vision sets up key passes`, `traits shift shares`, `roles shift shares`; `strategy.test` `fit reads attributes`; `motion.test` `formation spots`; `pnpm run typecheck` and `pnpm run test:unit` pass

## impl notes
- action weights: a table `ROLE_ACTION[role]` and `TRAIT_ACTION[trait]` of multipliers 1.0–1.6 on the chooser's weight for the action they name (e.g. invertedWinger shot ×1.4 cross ×0.7, traditionalWinger cross ×1.5, targetMan header target ×1.6, poacher shot in box ×1.4, longshot long-range share ×1.5, crosser cross ×1.5, technician dribble ×1.4, playmaker key pass ×1.4, workrate press duel ×1.2, speedster run in behind ×1.4); the side-level counts (shots, passes) stay as the profile sets them — weights only pick who does it.
- cross: replaces part of the final-third pass attempts from wide slots (about 12 crosses a side a match before weights); its success → a header duel (heading vs heading of the marker), a won header is a shot with quality of the box band.
- keep the team-level summary path's chance model consistent: it uses the same shooter weights (now including roles and traits) without the pass chain.
- re-run `pnpm exec tsx scripts/calibrate.ts` (about 1¼ min) and `--only own` if needed; if MAE leaves 2.5, adjust only the base constants that T107 also may have moved and record them.
