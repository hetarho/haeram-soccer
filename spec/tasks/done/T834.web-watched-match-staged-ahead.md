## goal
When a playback arrives with a script instead of frames, the page starts showing the match within a moment: a worker of its own stages the script minute by minute ahead of the presentation, the theatre plays what is staged, and a jump to a moment not staged yet waits on a short preparing state while the result itself shows at once — grid playbacks, which arrive with all their frames, behave exactly as before.

## impl notes
- Today one reply carries the whole `MatchPlayback` with every frame (about 10 MB of JSON, measured 261111) and the page plays it on its own clock (`apps/web/src/entities/match/lib/presentationClock.ts`, `features/match-viewer/ui/Pitch.tsx`); nothing steps the engine. With a script the reply is small and the frames are made on the page's side.
- `stagingWorker.ts`: a module worker (Vite `new Worker(new URL(...), { type: 'module' })`, as `runtime/worker.ts` and `helperPool.ts` are created) that receives `{ id, script, staging, record, squads }`, runs `stageFrames` (T831) and posts `{ id, from, frames }` in batches of `STAGING_BATCH_MINUTES` (2) match minutes, then `{ id, done: true }`; it yields to its message queue between batches so a `stop` is honoured. It imports the engine's `staging/` only (a leaf, T823) — no world, no host.
- `runtime/staging.ts`: owned by the client (`runtime/client.ts`); on `reply.playback` with a `script` it stores the playback with `frames: []` and `staged: { done: false }`, starts the worker and appends each batch to `state.playback.frames` through the store so subscribers re-render; a later playback, `endWatch` or a career change stops it. Without Worker support (tests, old browsers) it stages on a timer in the same batches.
- The theatre: every derivation over `frames` (`presentationSamples`, `deadBallBeats`, `goalInstants`, the charts) already takes the array — memoise on its length. The presentation clock's length is the staged running length until `staged.done`; reaching it holds the picture (no catch-up afterwards, as a stall today). `reveal` marks the match finished at once (score, scorers, final analysis and the report need only the record) and seeks to the end when `staged.done`; the moments list (`features/match-viewer/lib/moments.ts`) disables entries beyond the staged frontier with the preparing label.
- The label: source message in `packages/contracts/src/source-messages.ts` and every locale pack (→ARCH-32; follow `apps/web/AGENTS.md`). No other new copy.
- The prepared world lets a browser journey reach revision 11 before it is released: a world on a later revision than the release keeps it on load (`activatesMatchModel`).
- A playback kept across a reload (`client.ts`) keeps its script and is staged again from the start; frames are never saved (→ARCH-15).
- Not in this task: the lab (T836), the other journeys (T839), speed (T837).

## result
- outcome: Scripted watched playbacks reach the client without frames and are staged in their own module worker, two minutes per message. Immutable ordered appends update store subscribers; replacement, leaving the theatre, career change and disposal stop the worker, and stale replies are ignored. A resumed worker recomputes from the script while retaining its delivered prefix; browsers without workers use the same bounded timer program. Errors retain delivered frames and report through the existing notification resource. The theatre recomputes its samples, stoppages, goals, poses and charts as frames arrive, waits at the frontier without catch-up, and reveals the settled score, scorers and analysis immediately. The final seek waits for done; later moments and the initial empty scrubber are disabled. Clips require staged, seen goals and can be left for the result while staging continues. Completion is delivered once. Grid playbacks keep their existing complete-frame path. One preparing message is translated in all 12 packs.
