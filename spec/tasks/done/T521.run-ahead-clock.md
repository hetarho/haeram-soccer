## goal
While the page is shown the worker computes up to N days ahead and the calendar reveals one day per pace interval, so slow match days are absorbed without skipping a shown day.

## acceptance
- [v] `RUN_AHEAD_DAYS` per pace (x1 3, x2 3, x5 5, x10 7) in `progression.ts`; computing continues back to back while fewer than that many days are held, and the calendar shows the next held day one pace interval after the previous one, or as soon as it lands when late.
- [v] No day is computed past one whose reply carries a new stopping event, a pending decision, a critical alert, bankruptcy or an own match eve that stops for observation; such a day stops the clock when it is shown, with the world on the shown date.
- [v] The owner's stop reveals the held days at the pace, then stops; the management lock (→WEB-97) lasts until they are shown; the stop's save is requested after the last one is shown (→SAVE-8). Entering the owner office, and hiding the tab, show the held days at once; nothing is computed ahead while hidden or right after returning.
- [v] A pace change applies to days computed from then on; held days are shown at the faster of the old and new pace.
- [v] `progression.runahead.test.ts` (unit, fake worker with measured step times): x10 for 30 s shows no skipped day and fewer stalls than depth 0; stop drains then unlocks and saves once; a stopping event or match eve lands on its day with nothing computed past it; office entry and hiding reveal at once; x10 → x1 with a full buffer reveals at x10. `progression.test.ts` still passes.
- [v] typecheck, lint/format on touched files and `pnpm exec vitest run --project unit apps/web/src/runtime/progression.test.ts apps/web/src/runtime/progression.runahead.test.ts` pass; `client.test.ts` (integration) listed in result limits.

## impl notes
- Port prototype commit `db28c045` (branch `worktree-agent-aeba6917277c9c905`, in `perf/integrated` as `b9a6956a`, with the client.ts merge against T520 resolved there): the client holds run-ahead views as `head` (requests and saves use it, `reveal()` publishes held views in order); its `RUN_AHEAD_PROTOTYPE` option becomes the fixed `RUN_AHEAD_DAYS`.
- Rolling back to the shown day is not used: a world clone costs 70–230 ms (7.9 MB at season end), beyond x10's 100 ms day. Held views share structure; N = 7 adds under about 2 MB.
- Measured 261027 (1950 ENG season, with T517–T520's speed-ups): x10 54 → about 10 stalls, 4.3 → about 8.9 days a second; x1/x2/x5 practically none. Without them, depth alone leaves about 34 at x10.

## result
- outcome: automatic play computes ahead while the page is shown. The client holds automatic days (`held`; the newest is the head that requests and saves start from) and `reveal()` shows them in order; the controller computes back to back below `RUN_AHEAD_DAYS`, shows a held day one pace interval after the previous one (a late one as soon as it lands, the next a full interval after it), computes nothing past a day that stops the clock (new stopping event or pending decision, critical alert, bankruptcy, observed match eve) and stops when that day is shown. The owner's stop shows the held days at the pace with the lock (`clockRunning`) held, then unlocks and saves once; office entry, leaving play for the slot screen (`pause(true)`), hiding and blocker stops show them at once; hidden and away play compute nothing ahead (catch-up steps are shown as they land); a pace change keeps held days at the faster pace; a notice read during play is read on the shown and every held day. Fake-worker model at x10 for 30 s (25/120/400 ms steps): no stall and 300 days shown, against 44 stalls and 157 days one day at a time.
