A defending shape that trails the play is the usual cause; shorten `motion.own.lag.line`, `motion.own.lag.base` or `motion.turn.lag.settled` before touching `stage.*` contest values, and record the trade when a naturalness metric and a contact metric cannot both pass.
  8. delivery files and README, leaving at least fifteen minutes for them.
