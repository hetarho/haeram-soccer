## goal
The owner takeover plays by OWNER r2, ECON r43 and LEAGUE r13: reach by price rank with every cheaper club buyable, living costs 0.25%, gentler dividend and star-sale drops, books that live within their means and a ten-season takeover loan.

## acceptance
- [v] reach (OWNER-3): each tier's reach is its band of the price ranking of every club of the playable countries in the takeover season, dearest first (`OWNER_TIERS[tier].band`); the anchor stays the reach's median pound price; every club whose price the fortune pays is affordable, a 석유 재벌 paying for every club below its reach. - [v] living costs (OWNER-8): `OWNER_LIVING.rate` 0.0025; the owner-settlement unit test reads the constant.
- [v] 팬 민심 (OWNER-12): dividend −0.5 per 1% of turnover; a star sale −4, at most once a transfer window (the window of the last drop kept on the owner state); `fan-mood.test.ts` covers two star sales in one window (one drop) and one in the next window (a second drop).
- [v] books (ECON-36): in the world's first season every club nobody owns opens with `CLUB_BOOKS.opening` × its season income in cash; at each close cash above `CLUB_BOOKS.cashSeasons` × the season's income leaves the books and debt beyond `CLUB_BOOKS.debtSeasons` × the season's income is written off; `books.test.ts` covers the opening, both bounds and a club inside them untouched; a 1930 takeover starts with that opening cash (takeover unit test).
- [v] takeover loan (ECON-38): `TAKEOVER_DEBT.seasons` 10; the installment test reads the constant.
- [v] typecheck, lint, format:check, `pnpm run build` and `pnpm exec vitest run --project unit --changed "$HAERAM_DIFF_BASE" --passWithNoTests` pass; integration and e2e cases whose numbers move with these rules are updated with the code and listed in `- limits:` for the batch.

Nothing else hard-codes these numbers.
- Reach: replace `REACH_BANDS`/`inReach`/`divisionPlaces` in `club-value.ts` by the price-rank band inside `reachOf(w, tier, priced)` (rank by `pricePounds`, ties by club id; a club at a band edge belongs to the band whose `from` its rank share reaches). `tierAnchor`/`tierFortune` keep their signatures; callers (`listing.ts`, `scripts/owner-balance.ts`, the start flow's tier cards) keep working. The tier cards keep naming their typical clubs (OWNER-3's column).

The removed cash is not recorded anywhere else (it is spent on the club).
