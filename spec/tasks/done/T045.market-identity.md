# T045 Market candidate identity and bids that keep their offer
> st:done@261012 | ssot:CLUB-14 ECON-16 SAVE-10 | base:CLUB@16 ECON@13 SAVE@20 | dep:T042 | touches:packages/engine/src/operations.ts packages/engine/src/transfers.ts packages/engine/src/transfers.test.ts packages/contracts/src/types.ts packages/contracts/src/schema.ts | lane:-

## goal
One market ID names one player whatever the settings, the same settings always show the same list, and an outgoing bid stays on the player and terms it was made for.

## acceptance
- [v] transferOffers: candidate IDs are `market:${year}:${sig}:${i}`, sig = a short stable hash of every input transferOffers reads to shape the list (recruitment offer profile, chief-scout effects, build and synergy market effects). The player's draw stays keyed by its ID; the same settings give the identical list and IDs.
- [v] placeBid stores `offer: { player, fee, loanFee }` on the bid (the Player DTO as in the squad). decideOutgoing, counters and completion use `bid.offer` when present; availability = the player is not already in our squad.
- [v] A pending bid without `offer` (saved before 1.15.0) resolves through the existing lookup (`transferOffers(w, bid.year)` by playerId), as now.
- [v] contracts: `TransferBid.offer` optional in types.ts; the `bid` strictObject in schema.ts accepts it, reusing the player schema.
- [v] transfers.test.ts: changing the recruitment level gives a list sharing no ID with the old one, and switching back gives the identical list; a bid followed by a recruitment-level change signs the bid's player (same id, name and attributes) at the bid's fee; a legacy pending bid without `offer` still resolves.
- [v] A world with a pending bid carrying `offer` survives encode → decode (validateWorld) in a contracts or persistence unit test.
- [v] `pnpm run typecheck && pnpm run test:unit` pass.

## impl notes
- sig: `hash()` from primitives.ts over a canonical string of the numeric inputs, rendered as 6–8 base36 chars; keep IDs at most 100 characters like bidId.
- Signed players keep their market ID; academy, inbox and news refer to IDs only.
- apps/web/src/adapters/packing.ts has no bid-specific stream (checked 261012), so bids pass through whole; the round-trip test proves the new field survives. If it does not, extend the codec in this task.
- A bid snapshot's amounts are converted at a currency change in T047, not here.
- No rules version bump here; T047 bumps to 1.15.0. Goldens are left to T047.

## result
