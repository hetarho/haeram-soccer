## goal
Delegated coaching departments and the manager post hire and renew by the criteria the owner sets (STAFF-15, STAFF-18), replacing the coaching investment dial and the manager rule.

## acceptance
- [v] Coaches (staff.ts):
  - `coachMatches(w, role, replacing?)` returns the season's candidates (`staffCandidates`) that meet the department's criteria and are affordable. Affordable means `affordable(w, fee, wage − replaced wage)`. The criteria checks:
    - ability ≥ `minAbility`;
    - the trait, when one is set;
    - wage ≤ `coachWage(w, maxWageOvr)` when a cap is set.
  - They are ordered by `priority`: ability descending, or wage ascending; ties go to the lower candidate index. `coachWage` is exported.
  - `coachPickPreview(w, role)` returns `{ pick?, reason }` for the UI: whom the staff would hire now, or why nobody (no candidate meets X, or nobody affordable).
  - With `volunteer: true` the department keeps or gets an unpaid ability-50 volunteer.
- [v] Delegated departments:
  - `dailyStaff` fills a vacancy or a volunteer's post (unless `volunteer`) at once with the first match, for the criteria's `term`.
  - With no match, a volunteer holds a vacancy and one `staff-report` is posted per department and season, using `ref` `coach-search:<role>:<year>` to avoid repeats.
  - Turning a department's switch on, or changing its criteria while it is on, acts the same way at once. These are the `delegate` and `hiring` handlers.
  - A contracted coach who no longer meets new criteria stays until his contract ends.
- [v] Season boundary (`seasonStaff`), per delegated department, for an expiring paid coach:
  - If he meets the criteria and is affordable, he is renewed (going wage, 10% fee, the criteria's term) unless the first match beats him clearly: 3+ ability under 능력 높은 순, or a wage 10%+ lower under 연봉 낮은 순.
  - Otherwise the first match replaces him; with no match he is renewed if affordable, else a volunteer takes the post.
  - With `volunteer` set, a volunteer takes the post.
  - One summary report per season, as today. Non-delegated departments keep the 1 April renewal item and the vacancy item.
- [v] Manager (managerMarket.ts, ops-staff.ts):
  - `managerMatches(w)` returns candidates meeting every manager criterion: OVR, schools, flexibility, pride, youth, selection trait (`'none'` matches a manager without a trait), and the wage cap `staffQuote(w, 120 + maxWageOvr)`.
  - The candidates are the free agents, plus other clubs' managers from `managerCandidates` when `poach`.
  - Affordable means `affordable(w, fee + compensation, wage − current wage)`.
  - They are ordered by `priority`: OVR, potential (ability when absent), or wage ascending.
  - `managerPickPreview(w)` returns `{ pick?, reason }`.
- [v] Manager vacancy (`appointDelegatedManager`):
  - The first match is hired for the criteria's term (the firefighter 1, capped by `managerTermLimit`). A poached manager goes through `takeManager`, so his club gets compensation and names a successor.
  - With no match: `whenNone: 'best'` appoints the best affordable free agent in the chosen order; `'wait'` keeps the interim without a stop and posts one attention `staff-report`. `dailyStaff` retries daily.
  - Reports name the criteria lines (`managerCriteriaLines`).
- [v] Manager renewal on 1 April (`managerRenewalReminder`), with the post delegated:
  - A manager who meets the criteria is renewed for the criteria's term unless the first match beats him by `MANAGER_RENEWAL_MARGIN` (3) OVR or potential points, or by a wage 10%+ lower, depending on the order.
  - One who does not meet them is let go at the close when a match exists (the staff appoint then), and is renewed when none does.

## impl notes
- Margin rules use the order's own measure:
  - 능력/OVR: 3 points;
  - potential: 3 points;
  - wage: the match asks at most 90% of the incumbent's going renewal wage.
- `managerCandidates` already lists the eight best of our league and the division above with compensation. Do not widen it.
- Reports, Korean, in the money notation via `moneyLabel`:
  - coach no-match: `<부서명>: 기준에 맞는 코치가 없어요` with the criteria lines and `자원봉사 코치가 자리를 지켜요`;
