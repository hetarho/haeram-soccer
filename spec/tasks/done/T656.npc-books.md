## goal
Each club nobody owns closes every season with a book — income, wages, costs, result, cash and debt — from the owner club's own scales, kept for the current and last season.

## acceptance
- [v] `books.ts` exports `settleNpcBooks(w, club)` and `booksClose(w)` (all clubs nobody owns), using the existing distribution (ECON-28), gate (ECON-9), commercial (ECON-8), wage (CLUB-25) and operating-expense (ECON-30) functions summed over the season; no new income kind
- [v] negative cash is debt; a LEAGUE-10 takeover or project clears it; clubs nobody owns never go bankrupt — tests for each
- [v] only current and last season kept per club (SAVE-27); a 1930→2000 world's books add less than the size T654 measured as acceptable (record bytes per club in result)
- [v] `generateHistory` closes books every season; equivalence tests for founding careers unchanged (books are not settled while a club is the owner's)

## result
- outcome: `packages/engine/src/books.ts`: `keepsBooks(w, club)` (a league club of a playable country, not the owner's), `booksClose(w)` (every such club; it returns at once in a founded career — books settle in a world nobody owns and in a career with an owner, never for the owner's club), `settleNpcBooks(w, club)`, `clearDebt(club)` and `takeoverFacilities(w, club)` (the division's stages from `CLUB_TAKEOVER.facilities`, raised for a giant or a big club and lowered for a small one by stature). A season's book reads the owner's functions through a view of the world with the club in the owner's place and every own setting at its neutral default (`DEFAULT_TICKET`, its takeover facilities, no matches, history, campaigns, sponsors, policy, build or bases): income = `leagueShare` + `leagueMerit` by its final rank in its league + the parachute the season after a relegation (`DISTRIBUTION_CONFIG.parachute` of the share lost, its last division read from the previous season's archive) + every home league gate at the middle of `gateProjection`'s range + each open slot's stable offer from `sponsorOffers`; wages = `marketWageAt` of its season squad (`squad(w, club)`) in its country, year and division; costs = `operatingExpensesAt` for the first season and after a relegation, otherwise the larger of it and `EXPENSE_CONFIG.share` of last season's income (converted when the money changed). Result = income − wages − costs, added to cash − debt; below zero it is debt, never a bankruptcy. The books keep `[this season, last season]`, each in the money it was played in; cash and debt move into the money of the season that follows at the close (`seasonAfter`, so across the war too; ECON-6). `dynamics.ts` `evolveClubs` clears the debt of a club with a takeover or a project (LEAGUE-10). `booksClose` runs from both closes after the archive and before promotion (T655's call sites), so `generateHistory` closes books every season and the war adds none.
