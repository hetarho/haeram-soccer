## goal
In the summer and winter windows (CLUB-17) clubs nobody owns buy, sell and loan among themselves at market value within their books and for need, a club above its wage limit sells down to it, and their bids for our players come from the same need.

## acceptance
- [v] `packages/engine/src/npc-market.ts` runs on `NPC_MARKET.days` fixed days of each window (its first day, a middle day and its last day), never every day: each club ranks its needs (a line below minimum, an XI slot whose best player sits `NPC_MARKET.upgrade` below the club's affordable level) and its surplus (beyond `NPC_SQUAD.size`, blocked behind better players, last contract season); buyers in stature order take the best affordable listed player at CLUB-46 value (fee from cash or within debt rules of ECON-36), the seller's need allowing; young surplus goes on loan.
- [v] Sell-down (→WORLD-17): a club whose wage bill stands above its limit lists its highest-paid players, highest first, until the bill fits, and buys nobody meanwhile; no sale takes a seller below →CLUB-5's minimum, a line minimum, a goalkeeper or 18 players.
- [v] A player two clubs want joins the higher-stature club, then the higher wage (→WORLD-16).
- [v] Each completed move keeps one live record (→WORLD-11): it updates `w.squads`, both books, the player's contract (new wage/until) and appends to `World.playerRecords[id].moves` (`PlayerMove { year, day, from, to, fee, loan? }`, the type defined here); a registered person's move also goes through `movePerson`. Only the two clubs' matchday choices and strengths are refreshed.
- [v] `playerRecords` follows T867's three steps: `worldSchema` entry, a packer with a round-trip test, and exclusion from the view (`projection()`, `ProjectedWorld`) under T867's classification test.
- [v] Loans: a loan ends at the close before contracts are renewed and the player returns to his parent club's squad; the war gap moves a loan's end with the contract's (T861's `crossWar`).
- [v] Short squads (→WORLD-16): on a summer window's last day a club still below →CLUB-5's minimum or without a goalkeeper takes generated youth, in the summer routine.
- [v] Bids for our players (CLUB-22) come only from clubs whose need the player fills and whose books afford the fee and wage; stance and appeal rules for the owner's answer stay.
- [v] A window's market is deterministic for a seed (own random streams) and runs in time proportional to listed players × buyers' needs, not clubs × all players.
- [v] `persisted-world.integration.test.ts` extended: lived 1930 → 1970; a club above its limit at a close is at or under it, or at →CLUB-5's minimum, by the next summer window's last day; every squad stays within its registration limit (→CLUB-44); no id is live twice after any window; every move in `playerRecords` names two clubs that held him in that order.
- [v] Tests: `packages/engine/src/npc-market.test.ts` — a club short of a keeper buys one; a club over its wage limit cannot buy and sells its highest-paid player; no sale under the seller's minimum; stature wins a contested player; a winter move splits his club totals; a loan returns at the close; repeated runs on one seed give the same moves; an incoming bid needs a real need.
