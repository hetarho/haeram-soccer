## goal
Every player has a market wage and value from ability, age, potential, division and the football index, rising steeply with ability.

## acceptance
- [v] `contracts.ts`: `marketWage(w, player, division?)` and the value used for fees follow ability (steeply, e.g. exponential), age and potential, the division and `footballIndex` (→T050). Generated players (makePlayer, market candidates, academy promotions) start at their market wage.
- [v] `playerValue` and asking fees follow the same index (→CLUB-25).
- [v] contracts.test.ts: in a top flight a player rated 80 earns 6–10× one rated 60, and a second-tier squad earns 35–50% of a top-flight squad of the same ability, with the first constants and a note that T062 recalibrates; wages for one ability rise with the index across eras.
- [v] `pnpm run typecheck && pnpm run test:unit` pass.

## impl notes

- Keep the constants in one exported config for T062.

## result
- outcome: `packages/engine/src/contracts.ts` prices every player on one market scale (→CLUB-25), in 1901 units carried by the football index through the new `footballQuote` (world.ts, `quote`'s conversion with `footballIndex` in place of prices). Wage = `wage` × `perPoint`^(points − `pivot`) × the division's share. The points are ability, plus a share of a young player's headroom (the whole share to 18, none from 24), minus a point per year under 21 and over 30. The first constants live in `MARKET_CONFIG`: a top-flight player rated 70 earns 200 units a year (about the 1950 English maximum wage); ×1.0965 a point gives 80/60 = 6.3×; division shares are 1/0.42/0.35/0.3/0.25 by tier from the top, with the amateur league as the tier below the professional ones. A value is 6 years of a wage that counts twice the promise, one year fewer per year after 28, at least 1. `marketWage`/`marketValue(w, player, division = our tier)` and their `…At(code, year, division, player)` forms are exported. `divisionFor(code, ability)` is the fractional division a player's ability suits, and `divisionShare` interpolates between tiers. makePlayer takes an optional division (founders: our tier, NPC squads: their club's tier, season-close youth: our tier, none: the division his ability suits) and starts every generated player at his market wage. Market candidates join at our division's wage. They are priced at the division their ability suits: fee = value × the recruitment, scout and synergy fee multipliers, loan = 10% of value. A free agent has no club, so both his fee and his loan fee are 0. Academy promotions sign at their market wage. `playerValue(w, player, division?)` is `marketValue`, so incoming offers, the sale bar, stances and squad value follow it.
