## goal
Let viewers identify the controlling team and ball carrier at the presented instant without opening details or player inspection.

## acceptance
- [v] Show a readable possession-team cue beside the scoreboard and a distinct recorded-carrier marker on the pitch in ordinary observation. Team emphasis uses label/shape as well as colour, keeps shirt numbers and the ball readable, and works for either side and goalkeepers.
- [v] Resolve the side from the recorded carrier or an authoritative currently presented action. In-flight passes/shots have no carrier marker; preserve a team cue only when the recorded action establishes it. Use neutral phase/contested/unknown states when possession is unavailable, without guessing from distance, a future receiver or frame.side's minute-leading action.
- [v] Seeks, turnovers, T349's goal hold, hidden/resumed playback and match replacement keep the two cues synchronized with the displayed instant. Sparse historical playback never invents ownership or retains a stale marker.
- [v] Add small `possessionCue.test.ts` cases for `home carrier`, `away carrier`, `keeper ownership`, `recorded flight side without a carrier`, `contested ball clears ownership`, `unknown history stays neutral`, `turnover changes both cues`, `seek and goal hold use presented facts`, and `minute leader is not current possession`.
- [v] Extend `mobile-match-presentation.spec.ts` for normal-view ownership at 360/390 CSS px and desktop, both themes and reduced motion, with no details/inspection open. Run affected units, ARCH-30 fast checks, presentation-resource validation and build; leave browser execution to the batch gate.

## impl notes
- Baseline: `docs/benchmarks/set-piece-motion-261022.md`. Every nonempty sampled ownerId mapped to a recorded player (508 default, 2,954 candidate); current carrier text is gated by `details && inspect` in Pitch. Owner absence during flight is common and must not mean an automatic turnover.
- Add one pure `resolvePossessionCue` in `ui/possessionCue.ts`, consumed by both scoreboard and pitch painting. Reuse current presentation samples, recorded squads and timed action facts; ownership has one source, not separate nearest-player logic per visual.
- T349 is a dependency because it changes the same Pitch/clock surfaces and introduces the goal hold. Reuse its presented instant and transient lifecycle; no second playback clock, game command, saved preference or change to match outcomes.
- Use locale resources and semantic theme tokens. If existing action timing cannot establish an in-flight side reliably, show neutral flight status rather than extend saved history or infer a result.

## result
- outcome: `ui/possessionCue.ts` `resolvePossessionCue` reads the presented instant only. A measured probe (seed grid-playback, default and candidate) found event `t` is where the ball arrives (859/864 and 790/794 passes end nearest `to`), so the action under way is the minute's first event at or after the instant: carry/takeOn/holdUp → `carrier` (actor, his side), pass/cross/cutBack/shot/restart/kickoff → `flight` with the acting side and no carrier, clearance → `contested`, a stoppage → `unknown`. Without such an event the sample's recorded owner, else `transition` → contested, else unknown; sparse historical samples (no players) are always unknown. The default engine records an owner in only 126 of 752 samples (~7.5 s apart), which is why events lead. Pitch resolves it once per animation frame from its one clock (so T349's hold, seeks, hidden/resumed play and match replacement stay in step), mirrors it into React only when its key changes, puts a ball-glyph `소유` badge (glyph only on phones; `볼 소유` accessible name) beside the controlling side's name and draws a theme-gold ring and pointer with a dark edge over the carrier after every shirt.
- at: 261022 v7c3
- verified: 9 possessionCue unit cases; 684 web unit cases; typecheck, ESLint, Prettier, presentation validation, build. Chromium: mobile-match-presentation (360 standard, 390 standard + reduced motion, 1280 pixel; both sides seen as carrier at 16x) and match-core, 9/9; 360 and 1280 captures checked by eye.
- limits: `presentationClock.test.ts` unchanged (the clock is untouched; the hold/seek case lives in possessionCue.test.ts). A carry whose actor is not among the presented sample's players falls back to the side-only flight cue. Firefox/WebKit left to the batch gate and CI.
