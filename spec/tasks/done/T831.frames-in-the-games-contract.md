## impl notes
- `export function* stageFrames(script: MatchScript, sides: readonly [StagingSide, StagingSide], record: MatchRecord, squads: [Player[], Player[]]): Generator<MatchFrame>` yields one frame each time the staging (T828's `advance`) has passed a minute's end, so a caller can stop anywhere (T834 stages ahead in steps); `stageAll(...)` collects them. `record` and `squads` supply ids, names and the numbers below; nothing here settles a fact.
- Read the contract first: `MatchFrame`, `MatchMotionSample`, `PlayerMotion`, `MatchEvent`, `FrameStoppage` (`packages/contracts/src/types.ts`), `frameTimeIssues` and `NEUTRAL_FRAME_PACE` (`packages/contracts/src/match-model.ts`), and how the grid fills them (`packages/engine/src/grid/playback.ts` `buildFrames`) — units are playback units 0–100 along the length and the width, positions as the pitch is drawn (the staging's world frame already holds the change of ends), ball height in the contract's units (`MATCH_MOTION_CONFIG.ball.maxLoft`).
- The clock (→MATCH-12): a script's fixed beats (shots and their sources, booked fouls, incidents, period starts and ends) anchor the match clock at their `clock`; between two anchors staged seconds map linearly onto match seconds. A frame `i` of a period covers match seconds `[start + 60·i, start + 60·(i + 1))`: `startSeconds`, motion `elapsedSeconds` and `ballTimes` are match seconds; dead time is on the clock like running play. Export the stretch rates (`clockRates(script, staging)`) for T840; `STAGING_CONFIG.limits.clockRate` `[0.8, 1.25]` is the limit they are judged by there, not a gate here.
- Sampling: motion samples at least `MATCH_MOTION_CONFIG`'s candidate spacing apart (2 match seconds) plus every kick, reception, restart kick and the minute's end; `ballTrack`/`ballTimes` every staged tick while the ball moves and at each knot, strictly increasing. `velocity` in the contract's convention (read `playback.ts`), scaled by the stretch rate.
- Fields: `players`/`motion[].players` from the staged positions with `state` mapped from what the stage has the player doing — `keeper`, `carry` (holder), `press` (presser, cover), `mark` (a defender on his man), `run` (a cued runner, the last-shoulder role), `recover` (after a turnover while re-forming), `support` (the scripted receiver and showing teammates), else `shape` — and `intent` his current target; `intention` is omitted. `holders` from ball ownership changes; `stoppages` from dead-ball scenes (`dead` when the ball goes dead, `settled` when the last player reaches his place, `kick` at the restart; kinds map `throwIn`, `goalKick`, `corner`, `freeKick`, `penalty`, `kickoff`, a `stoppage` beat as `dropBall`); `events` from stage events and beats; `score`, `metrics` (cumulative `M` rows, minutes and possession as the grid's frames define them) and `xg` counted from the beats performed up to the minute's end through T826's `countScript` on the performed prefix; `side`, `player`, `action` as `buildFrames` chooses the minute's leading action, with its existing label ids; `pace: NEUTRAL_FRAME_PACE`; `onPitch` from the staging's present lines.
- Commentary: build the entry `packages/engine/src/grid/commentary.ts` `describe` takes from a beat and its detail row (cells, kind, outcome, set piece, line-breaking, the script's phase) and call it; where `describe` needs a tag the record does not hold, pass the neutral value that selects its plain phrase. If a notable event has no plain phrase among the existing ids, give it the nearest existing one and list it in the result — adding ids is T839's.
- Not in this task: the worker and the page (T834), the grid's `buildFrames` (untouched), post-match material (T832).
