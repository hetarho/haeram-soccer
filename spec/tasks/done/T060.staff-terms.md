# T060 Owner-chosen terms for managers and coaches, no self-renewal

## goal
Hiring and renewing a manager or coach takes a term of 1–5 seasons at a fixed wage, releases pay 25% per season left, and no manager renews himself.

## acceptance
- [v] `hire-manager`, `hire-staff`, `renew-manager` and `renew-staff` take a term of 1–5 seasons, never past a manager's 70th year; the firefighter signs for 1. The wage is fixed for the term: coach wages follow ability at signing, and the manager's is quote(120 + ability) indexed (→ECON-26).
- [v] Release or replacement pays 25% of the annual wage for each season left.
- [v] Managers no longer renew themselves at the close: on 1 April a renewal reminder covers any manager whose contract ends at the close, and an unrenewed manager leaves at the close (interim, departure choice).
- [v] Delegated coach hiring renews coaches for 2 seasons as now.
- [v] Hire and renew sheets in ClubStaff choose the term and show its total and the release cost.
- [v] Tests: staff.test.ts covers terms, the 70 cap, release per season left and no self-renewal; managerMarket.test.ts covers hiring with a term.
- [v] `pnpm run typecheck && pnpm run test:unit` pass.

## result
- outcome: `hire` (the manager hire, →T051), `hire-staff`, `renew-manager` and `renew-staff` take a term of 1–5 seasons, and the engine refuses any other value. A manager may never sign past his 70th year (`managerTermLimit`: until ≤ born + 70; older saves without an age are capped at 5). A firefighter signs only for 1, and the refusal names the reason. Free agents aged 70+ are no longer offered. With no term given, the earlier lengths still apply: a coach's offered 2–3 seasons, a manager's school length, a renewal of 1 manager season or 2 coach seasons. The owner's chosen term is stored as `term` and dropped when a contract is signed without one. Wages are fixed until the next renewal. Coaches are priced from their current ability and managers at quote(120 + ability), both carried onto the football index (`staffQuote`/`footballScaled` in staff.ts, real multiple = 1 before 1979). The same index now prices candidates, club managers, free agents, signing fees and the interim. Releasing or replacing a paid coach, or replacing a manager who is not an interim, pays 25% of the annual wage for each season left, counting the current one (`releaseCost`, `managerPayoff`). Managers no longer renew themselves: `yearlyStaff` lets an unrenewed manager go at the close (interim, `MANAGER_GONE`). On 1 April, any manager whose contract ends at the close and who can still sign gets the reminder (연봉 · 계약금 · 1–N시즌). With `delegation.manager` the staff renew him on 1 April for 2 seasons (1 for a firefighter) if the club can afford it; that used to depend on `delegation.staff`, and only for firefighters. Delegated coach hires and renewals run for 2 seasons. A renewal at the season boundary now really adds 2 seasons; before, it added 3. ClubStaff exports `TermDial` (a 1–5 season dial; seasons past the limit are disabled and say why) and `TermCosts` (the total wage × seasons + fee, the contract end, and the release cost right after signing). The coach hire sheet chooses the term and names the release cost of the coach being replaced. Coach 재계약 now opens a renewal sheet with a term dial. The manager renewal block (`ManagerRenewal`) and the hire confirmation (`ManagerHireTerm`) come from ClubStaff. The renewal outcome note states how many seasons were added.
