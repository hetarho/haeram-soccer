## goal
The pass network tab shows one side at a time as the owner's reference pictures do: a numbered circle for each player at his average position, bigger the more he passed, and lines between players as wide as the passes played between them with an arrowhead for each direction — readable on a phone and in every theme.

## impl notes
- The owner's reference (261111): the broadcast pass map — circles at average positions sized by passes, connecting lines whose thickness is the passes between two players, arrows for direction. Nothing in the stored form changes (→MATCH-25): `network` already holds `[avgX×10, avgY×10, touches]` a line and `[from, to, completed]` a pair.
- A player's passes for the circle: the sum of his outgoing pairs' counts (completed passes); keep `touches` for the touch text only.
- `NETWORK_VIEW` is a named constant beside the view helpers: `radius { min: 0.018, max: 0.045 }` of the pitch's drawn width, `line { min: 1, max: 9 }` px at the reference width scaled with it, `minPasses: 3`. Lines are drawn under circles, the thicker over the thinner; colours come from the theme's semantic tokens (→WEB-91), the own side's in its kit's base colour with the contrast ink the scoreboard uses.
- Keep what the tab has today: the 1.9:1 pitch, cell names on touch elsewhere, reduced motion, phone widths (→WEB-17/38).
- This task does not depend on which engine filled the material: it works on today's grid matches and on scripted ones.
