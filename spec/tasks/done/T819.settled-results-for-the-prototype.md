- The trace, `aggregate/types.ts`:
  ```ts
  /** What `attributePlayerFacts` split, group by group, when a caller asks (transient, never persisted, →MATCH-26). Count arrays are indexed by the side's `lines`. */
  export interface AggregateAttributionTrace {
    /** Per side, per index of its ledger's `moves`. */
    moves: [AggregateTraceMove[], AggregateTraceMove[]];
    /** Per side (the fouled side), per index of its ledger's `fouled`. */
    fouls: [AggregateTraceFouls[], AggregateTraceFouls[]];
  }
  export interface AggregateTraceMove {
    /** This side's lines. */
    attempts: number[];
    completed: number[];
    broke: number[];
    /** This side's lines caught offside on the group's offside passes. */
    offside: number[];
    /** The opponent's lines that won the ball: interceptions of a pass group, tackles of a carry or take-on group. */
    won: number[];
    /** The opponent's lines that recovered the group's loose balls in their high-turnover zone (empty elsewhere). */
    recovered: number[];
  }
  export interface AggregateTraceFouls {
    /** Booked fouls: the opponent's line that fouled, this side's line fouled, the index of the card in `participation.incidents`. */
    booked: { fouler: number; fouled: number; incident: number }[];
    /** The other fouls of the row: this side's lines fouled and the opponent's lines that fouled. */
    fouled: number[];
    fouler: number[];
  }
  ```
  - `attributePlayerFacts(input, plan, participation, facts, streams, trace?)`: when `trace` is given, `attributeSide` stores a copy (`slice()`) of each split it already draws — the scratch buffers (`own.attempts`, `own.completed`, `own.broke`, `own.split`, `opp.split`) are reused between groups and must not be stored themselves. The loose-ball draw at the end of `attributeSide` runs per pushed group; when tracing, remember each pushed entry's move index beside it so `recovered` lands on its group. A group or row of another period keeps the arrays the caller prepared (the caller passes arrays of the ledger's lengths, each entry with empty arrays; `attributePlayerFacts` creates them when `trace.moves`/`trace.fouls` are empty).
  - Nothing else in `packages/` changes: `playAggregate` and `simulateAggregate` keep calling without a trace. The exporter calls `attributePlayerFacts` again itself with `match.input`, `match.plan`, `match.participation`, `match.facts` and `aggregateStreams(w.seed, match.retry === 0 ? f.id : `${f.id}:retry${match.retry}`)`, and checks its result deep-equals `match.players` before trusting the trace.
- The exporter, `scripts/export-settled.ts` (`pnpm exec tsx scripts/export-settled.ts --out <dir> [--count 10] [--year <y>]`):
  - Fixtures as `scripts/match-parity.ts` builds its core sample: the pairs of `coreFixturePairs()`, each as `{ ...w.fixtures[0], year, round, kind, id: 'settled:' + i, home, away }`, played with `aggregateRun(w, f, 'settled', undefined, { tactics })`. Candidate `i` takes pair `i % pairs.length` and the tactic pair `i % 4` of `[possession, possession]`, `[possession, counter]`, `[counter, possession]`, `[counter, counter]` (production's 4-3-3 and 4-4-2, the two formations the prototype stages; their slot orders equal the prototype's).
  - `--year` defaults to the latest year whose `eraRules(year)` allow no substitute, so every match is played by the same 22 (the prototype stages neither substitutions nor a side a man short). Read `eraRules` in `packages/engine/src/grid/rules.ts` for it; record the year in the result.
  - A candidate is skipped, with a counted reason, when its match went to extra time, has an incident of kind `sub`, `injury`, `red` or `secondYellow`, has a penalty (a shot of kind `penalty` or a foul row with `penalties > 0`), or a line outside the starting eleven appears in `match.players`. Take candidates `i = 0, 1, 2, …` until `--count` are kept; stop with an error after 400.
  - Expansion of a move group into attempts, deterministic and documented in the file's header comment: list the group's attempts line by line in ascending line index, each line's completed ones first; mark the first `broke[line]` completed ones of each line as line-breaking; give the failed ones, in that listed order, the results `offside` × `move.offside`, `intercepted` × `move.intercepted`, `outTouch` × `move.outTouch`, `outByline` × (`move.outByline - move.cornersWon`), `cornerWon` × `move.cornersWon`, `looseRecovered` × `move.recovered`, then `looseKept` for the rest; a failed attempt of a carry or take-on group is `tackled`. `other`: for `intercepted` and `tackled` the opponent's lines of `won` in ascending order, one per attempt; for `offside` this side's lines of `offside` in ascending order, and when that line is the attempt's own actor swap it with the next offside attempt's (when the group has no other, take the lowest other outfield line); for `looseRecovered` the lines of `recovered` in ascending order while they last, else none.
  - A shot's `shooter` and `passer` are its `AggregateShotCredit`; its `source` is the id of a completed attempt of its source group made by the credited passer (for a carry or take-on source: by the shooter), the lowest such id not yet used by another shot; `keeper` is the opponent's slot 0.
  - Fouls: one entry per foul; the booked ones from `booked` with the card's minute, the others by pairing `fouled` and `fouler` in ascending line order.
  - The record rows are read from production, not recomputed: `match.players.sides[side][line].metrics` and `.detail` (`M`, `PD`) and `match.facts.sides[side]` (`M`, `TD`).
  - Players: index `side * 11 + slot`; `position` and `role` from `FORMATIONS[tactic].slots[slot]`, a production role the prototype does not know mapped by position (`GK` → `keeper`, `CB` → `centreBack`, `LB`/`RB` → `fullBack`, `DM` → `anchor`, `CM` → `boxToBox`, `LM`/`RM` → `wideMidfielder`, `LW`/`RW` → `traditionalWinger`, `ST` → `targetMan`); `number` = slot + 1; the ten attributes of the prototype's `Attrs` by the same names from the player's own attributes, rounded. No name is exported (→ARCH-32: identifiers and numbers only).
  - Cells: the ledger's cell is `band * 5 + lane` in the acting side's attack frame. Convert to the prototype's numbering (`cellOf` in `scripts/match-proto/core/pitch.ts`, read it; do not import it) with one pure function and pin it in the test with three reference cells: the acting side's own goal area, the opponent's goal area, and the wide cell on the acting side's own left in the band before the opponent's box.
  - Output is written with `JSON.stringify` (no spaces) plus a final newline; `.prettierignore` gains `scripts/match-proto/fixtures`.
- The file format, `scripts/match-proto/core/settled.ts` (the prototype owns it; the exporter writes the same shape without importing it):
  ```ts
  export interface Settled {
    version: 1;
    /** `s01` … */
    id: string;
    /** Production identifiers, data only. */
    source: { seed: string; fixture: string; year: number; revision: number };
    sides: [SettledSide, SettledSide];
    score: [number, number];
    /** Added minutes of the two halves, as the record states them. */
    added: [number, number];
    /** One row per attempt: [side, group, kind, setPiece, from, to, actor, result, lineBreaking, other]. */
    events: number[][];
    /** One row per shot: [side, minute, cell, kind, header, xg, outcome, corner, shooter, passer, source, keeper]. */
    shots: number[][];
    /** One row per foul: [fouledSide, cell, fouler, fouled, cardMinute]. */
    fouls: number[][];
    /** One row per yellow card: [minute, side, player]. */
    cards: number[][];
    record: { team: [number[], number[]]; players: number[][] };
  }
  export interface SettledSide {
    formation: FormationId;
    style: Style;
    /** Eleven, by slot: position, role, shirt number and the ten attributes in `Attrs` order. */
    players: { position: Position; role: RoleId; number: number; attrs: number[] }[];
  }
  export const EVENT_KINDS = ['short', 'long', 'through', 'cross', 'cutBack', 'carry', 'takeOn'] as const;
  export const SET_PIECES = ['none', 'corner', 'freeKick'] as const;
  export const EVENT_RESULTS = ['complete', 'offside', 'intercepted', 'outTouch', 'outByline', 'cornerWon', 'looseKept', 'looseRecovered', 'tackled'] as const;
  export const SHOT_KINDS = ['open', 'corner', 'freeKick'] as const;
  export const SHOT_OUTCOMES = ['goal', 'save', 'block', 'miss'] as const;
  export const TEAM_ROW = ['goals', 'shots', 'onTarget', 'passes', 'completed', 'corners', 'offsides', 'throwIns', 'goalKicks', 'freeKicks', 'fouls'] as const;
  export const PLAYER_ROW = ['goals', 'assists', 'shots', 'onTarget', 'passes', 'completed', 'tackles', 'interceptions', 'takeOns', 'offsides', 'fouls', 'foulsWon', 'yellowCards', 'saves'] as const;
  export function readSettled(json: unknown): Settled;
  export function fixtureOf(settled: Settled): Fixture;
  ```
  - Codes are indexes into the lists above; a player is a prototype index 0–21; a missing `other`, `passer`, `source` or `cardMinute` is −1; an event's id is its index in `events`, a shot's and a foul's likewise. Cells are the prototype's, in the acting (for a foul: the fouled) side's attack frame.
  - `readSettled` checks the version, every row's length, every code and index range, and throws naming the row; it does not recompute the record.
  - The recount the test makes, to prove the file holds together: per player `passes` = his attempts of the five pass kinds, `completed` those with result `complete`, `takeOns` his take-on attempts, `tackles` and `interceptions` the events naming him as `other` with those results, `offsides` the offside events naming him as `other`, `goals`/`assists`/`shots`/`onTarget` from `shots` (on target = `goal` or `save`), `saves` the saved shots naming him as `keeper`, `fouls`/`foulsWon` from `fouls`, `yellowCards` from `cards`; per side the sums and `corners` = its `cornerWon` events plus its shots with `corner`, `offsides` its offside events, `throwIns` = the opponent's `outTouch` events, `fouls` the fouls it committed. Where production's row differs from one of these simple readings (`goalKicks`, `freeKicks`, or a definition found different in `aggregate/ledger.ts` `sideCounters`), the test states the production definition it uses in a comment and this task's result lists it — T820 counts by the same definitions.
