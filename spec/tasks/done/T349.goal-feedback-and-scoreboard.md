## goal
Make each recorded goal readable at every observation speed through a short presentation hold, a GOAL cue and emphasis on the updated scoring-side scoreboard value.

## acceptance
- [v] Forward live observation and ordinary archived replay stop at each recorded goal, update the score once, show GOAL with the scoring side and the retained scorer when available, emphasize that side's score and resume from the same instant at the selected speed. Conceded goals identify the opponent; shootout conversions do not trigger an ordinary-score cue.
- [v] The visible hold uses GOAL_FEEDBACK_CONFIG.holdMs independently of 1x–16x. Consume the earliest crossed goal before advancing farther, including multiple goals in a minute or display update; do not leak the next goal's score, player positions or chart data, discard goals, or accumulate a catch-up jump during the hold.
- [v] Rerenders and speed/visibility changes never duplicate a cue. Seeking clears it and skips intermediate goals; replaying from before a goal permits a new cue. Archived pause stays paused, hidden documents retain the remaining visible hold, and final-whistle/navigation/playback replacement clean up transient state. The final recorded goal receives its complete cue before playback finishes.
- [v] GOAL and restrained score emphasis fit at 360/390 CSS px and desktop widths in both themes without covering controls. Reduced motion shows static feedback for the same hold. Score emphasis uses text/shape as well as colour, and the cue introduces no focus trap or repeated assistive announcement on rerender.
- [v] Add isolated `goalFeedback.test.ts`/`presentationClock.test.ts` cases: `stops at the earliest crossed goal at every speed`, `two goals in one minute keep separate running scores`, `hold does not advance the running clock`, `speed change and rerender do not repeat a cue`, `seek clears without celebrating skipped goals`, `replay can cross a goal again`, `pause survives the hold`, `hidden time does not consume the cue`, `final goal completes before finish`, `shootout is excluded`, and `feedback leaves settled match facts unchanged`.
- [v] Update `mobile-match-presentation.spec.ts` and `match-core.spec.ts` with phone/desktop, high-speed, archived pause/seek and reduced-motion journeys using compact recorded fixtures. Run affected unit tests, ARCH-30 task checks, presentation-resource validation and build; name the browser cases left to the batch gate.

Define GOAL_FEEDBACK_CONFIG here with holdMs initially 1000 and a restrained two-pulse score emphasis; reduced motion has no pulse.
- Reuse retained goal ordering and running scores from `ui/moments.ts`, refining shared extraction there if necessary. Use recorded event timing when present; sparse archived records use their existing goal-frame boundary without inventing sub-minute actions or scorers. - Keep transient feedback outside game saves and the engine. Existing speed controls remain reachable during the hold; speed changes apply on resumption. Seeking must preserve the pause contract of WEB-23. Do not add a new live pause control, new preference, sound, camera cut or player-celebration simulation.
- Use current locale resources and semantic presentation tokens for the cue and emphasis. The hold belongs to the WEB-94 transition, while presentationAdvance keeps WEB-32's uniform running-time mapping.

## result
- outcome: `ui/goalFeedback.ts` derives goal instants from the frames' running score (shoot-outs never count): an observed minute's goal sits at its scoring event (shots and direct free kicks both carry `outcome: 'goal'`), a sparse archived minute at the first sample of its frame; scorers come from `recordedGoals` by running score. One pure `advanceGoalFeedback` owns the 1x clock, the next unreached goal and the held cue's remaining real ms (GOAL_FEEDBACK_CONFIG holdMs 1000, two pulses); `seekGoalFeedback` passes goals up to the target without a cue. Pitch keeps that state in its clock ref and mirrors only the cue id and goals shown into React; the scoreboard shows the presented running score (no longer the minute's settled score), boxes the scoring side's value with a pulse (static under reduced motion), and one polite live region shows GOAL with the scoring side and scorer. `finished` waits for the last cue. Locale keys `match.goal.cue|side|scorer`.
