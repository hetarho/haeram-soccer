## goal
Turn an observed grid match's trace into per-minute frames — positions, ball flight, events with commentary, labels and pace — in the shape design §2.4 defines, without touching any football outcome.

## acceptance
- [v] `grid/playback.ts` exports `PlaybackEvent`, `PlaybackFrame` (exactly the design §2.4 `MatchEvent`/`MatchFrame` shapes, under these local names until T218 moves them to contracts), `PLAYBACK_CONFIG` (ball speeds, flight curve and loft constants moved from `MATCH_MOTION_CONFIG.ball`/`integration`), `buildFrames(result, setup, seed)`.
- [v] `grid/commentary.ts` exports `describe(entry, ctx): string` (design §10.3) using `cellName`, the entry's tags and only terms listed in `docs/MATCH-BASELINES.md` as verified; never `골든 존` or `존 5`.
- [v] `playback.test.ts` cases: `builds one frame per played minute including added time`, `samples 8 positions and 80 ball points per minute`, `keeps everyone on the pitch inside 0–100 units and mirrors the second half`, `keeps the ball at the carrier while he carries`, `lands every flight at its recorded end`, `labels added time as 45+n and 90+n`, `averages pace to 1 and slows minutes with shots and set pieces`, `shows ten players after a red card`, `does not change the record` (record from `playGrid` with and without building frames is identical; frames built twice are identical).
- [v] `commentary.test.ts` cases: `names the cell of a pass`, `describes a through ball into the space in behind`, `marks receiving between the lines`, `names cut-backs, headers, cards, substitutions and offside`, `uses near and far post by the ball's side`.
- [v] `pnpm run typecheck`, `pnpm run lint`, the test files pass.

## impl notes
- Inputs: `GridResult.trace` (T211) — entries carry period, t, kind, side, actor/receiver/opponent roster indexes, from/to (engine frame), outcome flags, cell, tags summary and the 22 positions; and `setup.rosters` for ids/names.
- Frames (design §10.2): group entries by match minute (`floor(t/60)` within the period plus the period offset; added minutes continue counting past 45/90 and get labels `45+n`); `score`/`metrics`/`xg` cumulative at minute end (replay the tally cheaply: track goals/shots/passes from entries); `players` = `PlayerMotion` for the players on the pitch in units (`toUnits(p, period)`), `velocity` from the last two samples, `state` per §10.2, `intent` = target in units if recorded, else position; `onPitch` ids.
- Motion samples: 8 per minute at even times; positions linearly interpolated between the two trace snapshots around each sample time.
- Ball track: 80 points per minute; between entries, the ball follows the entry's flight from `from` to `to` (quadratic curve, bend and loft from `random(`${seed}:${f.id}:ball-decisions`)`, rules of MATCH-12), sits at the carrier during carries/take-ons/hold-ups, rests on restart spots during dead time.
- Event `t` = seconds into the minute; `text` = `describe(entry, ctx)`; `ctx` gives names (`squads` lookup by roster index → `player.name`) and the ball's side for near/far post.
- Frame `action`/`player`: the minute's last notable entry, Korean label list of design §10.2; `player` = slot of that actor (keep the old meaning for the inspector).
- Pace (design §10.4): weights 0.6 / 1.0 / 1.6, then divide by the mean.
- Second half and second extra-time half are mirrored in units (design §1.1).

## result
- outcome: `buildFrames(result, setup, seed)` turns an observed grid match into one `PlaybackFrame` per minute of each period, added minutes included (`45+n`/`90+n`, `minute` capped at the period's end like `Incident`): 8 motion samples of the players on the pitch (positions interpolated between trace snapshots; a passer stays on the ball until he kicks, a restart's taker walks to the spot within 0.9 motion s), 80 ball points (MATCH-12 quadratic flights with bend and loft from `${seed}:${fixture}:ball-decisions`, the ball at the carrier's feet while he has it, resting on restart spots), events in the §2.4 shape with `describe` commentary, cumulative score/metrics/xG equal to the record at the end, the longest-possession side, the minute's leading label and slot, `onPitch` ids and pace (0.6/1.0/1.6 over their mean); the second half is mirrored. The observed trace now records receiver, duel opponent, fouled player, landing point, pass kind, restart spot and dead time, shot result/xG/header, incidents (cards after their foul, substitutions, injuries, both whistles), the actor's cell and tags, the roster index, target and motion state of every slot and `Tally.running()` (inbox). `describe` uses `cellName`, near/far post by the ball's side and only ✓ terms (라인 사이, 뒷공간, 컷백, 세컨드 볼, 라인 브레이킹, 측면 전환, 역습, 등지고 받은). `PLAYBACK_CONFIG` holds the ball speeds, flight curve, loft and styles that `MATCH_MOTION_CONFIG.integration`/`ball` now read.
