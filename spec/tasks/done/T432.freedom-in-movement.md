## goal
On revision 2, let players leave their slots by their manager-given freedom for football reasons, loosen every side's shape in the situations real football loosens it, and swap flanks and roles, with the trade-off showing in results (MATCH-17 r26).

## acceptance
- [v] Behind `modelAtLeast(setup, 2)` only: a player's intention target may move from his slot by up to `FREEDOM_CONFIG.drift` metres scaled by his `freedom` (T431), for an overlap or underlap, a forward dropping into midfield in the build-up, a full-back stepping into midfield or across to the far side, a winger coming inside; a teammate takes over the vacated zone and the rest defence holds (coordination, MATCH-17).
- [v] Situational disorder: after a turnover, when behind after the 75th minute and straight after a set piece, every player's slot pull weakens by `DISORDER_CONFIG.loosen` and the shape re-forms within `DISORDER_CONFIG.reform` seconds.
- [v] Position swaps: wingers switch flanks and a full-back and his winger rotate when both freedoms exceed `SWAP_CONFIG.threshold`, each doing the other's job until they swap back.
- [v] Tests: `intentions.test.ts` "a free full-back overlaps beyond his winger while a low-freedom one stays", "a forward with freedom drops into midfield in the build-up", "disorder loosens after a turnover and re-forms"; `coordination.test.ts` "a vacated zone is covered", "wingers swap flanks when both are free"; revision 1 and the pre-feature path digests unchanged; every record valid.
- [v] Measured on 20 observed revision-2 fixtures per tactic against revision 1: the share of matches in which a full-back or wing-back plays past the far half of the width (the owner's left-back on the right wing) and the mean distance from slot by freedom quartile, plus goals, xG, passes, box entries and MATCH-29's tactic ordering per match, written in the result.
- [v] exit: when the timebox ends, keep the version whose movement shows the owner's examples with every record valid and no MATCH-29 tactic ordering moved inside → outside, record its figures and every residual difference for T410's re-read; if none qualifies, keep the neutral hooks with freedom off and record why.
