## impl notes
- Why: T700's screen (`artifacts/benchmarks/T700/growth.md`): pooled over 지역 유지 and 자수성가 축구팬's 50 seasons below the top flight, signings suiting a higher division carry 90.1% of the XI's points above the league's NPC median and are paid 0.40 of the wage their ability suits; priced at it the paid wage bill would go 28–34% → 50–76% of revenue (p90 92–108%) while 재정 기조 표준 refuses only 7 of 166 at the signing, so the price binds a season later through the wage bill ceiling, renewals, improved-contract asks and runway sales. On the rules in force every lower tier reaches and wins the top flight (중견 8/8 reach, 6/8 win; 지역 유지 8/8, 5/8; 자수성가 6/6, 3/6), outside LEAGUE-13's bands.
- One rule for every club (CLUB-25 r61, ECON-36's same scales): the switch lives in `marketWageAt`, the only door every wage takes (books.ts:239, dynamics.ts:66, world-market.ts:68/86, free-agents.ts, world.ts:142 and `marketWage`). `marketWageUnits` and `ordinaryWageUnits` keep their explicit division: `marketWageAt` hands both the fitted division, so W80 and the spread read the same division. The 85–90 interpolation in `starredWageUnits` (`MARKET_CONFIG.star.globalFrom/globalFull`) stays: with the fit on a player from OVR 77 is already on division 0 and it never acts; with it off it is the released rule CLUB-25's "until then" keeps.
