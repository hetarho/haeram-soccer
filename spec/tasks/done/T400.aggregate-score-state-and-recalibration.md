## impl notes
- MATCH-22 r22 allows at most six segments and no per-minute chance loop (a world season settles about 10,000 fixtures). The segments are one simulated match under MATCH-26: never independent draws stitched together, never a fixture whose path depends on timing.
- T369 found finishing variance alone cannot move the relationships without hurting the curve and goals; the residual is the score state (T369's option a, triggered by T372's parity).
- MATCH-28: the grid reference is authoritative only where it holds MATCH-23's targets; where the refreshed grid still misses a band, fit the aggregate to the target and record the grid's miss, never copy a known defect.
- T326's internal guards (`assertLedger`, `assertParticipation`) stay on.
