## goal
Every club has a derived 단장, chief scout and six coaches, and each post's candidates are only its free agents and other clubs' holders (compensation one annual wage) — no per-season generated candidates.

## acceptance
- [ ] `clubStaffMember(w, club, role)` names any club's holder of a post, derived from seed, club, role and that club's holder count, ability around the club's strength (±8) inside the coach bands, age and potential per STAFF-16, a trait from the role's list, a coach wage; nothing is stored until his count changes; a former player placed by STAFF-43 overrides the derived holder while employed.
- [ ] `World.staffMarket` stores per-post free pools, per-club per-post `[count, since]` and the refresh stamp; it is optional, schema-validated and round-trips through save/load; absent means seeded on first need (as `marketOf` does for managers).
- [ ] Every 4 league rounds and at the close (the `refreshManagerMarket` call sites): each free agent signs elsewhere with 25% from his own stream; pools refill to at least 3 with new free agents (levels 45–85, coach age/prime bands); pools hold at most 6 (마당발: at least 4, at most 7, new free agents +5 ability). Our replaced/released holders and unrenewed expiries join their post's pool; retained former-player coaches count toward the pool and keep their identity (STAFF-27).
- [ ] `staffCandidates(w, role)` / `staffMarket(w, roles)` return the post's free agents (`source: 'free'`) and the six ablest holders of that post in our league and the division above (`source: 'club'`, `club`, `compensation` = one annual wage); the old `drawCandidates`, its cache and the `${playerClub}:staff:...` ids are gone.
- [ ] Hiring a club holder pays fee + compensation, increments that club's count (it names its next holder) and records his move; hiring a free agent removes him from the pool. Delegated staff (STAFF-15) include compensation in affordability and take a free agent first at equal standing; the 단장 post uses the same market (STAFF-20).
- [ ] STAFF-43 placement puts a former-player coach in his department's post at one club nobody owns, replacing its derived holder; a post already holding a placed former player is skipped; no vacancy check remains.
- [ ] Takeover and founding seed the pools; the worker view's `coaches` keeps its role map with the new fields.
- [ ] New `packages/engine/src/club-staff.test.ts` covers derivation stability, refresh odds and fill/cap, 마당발, compensation and count increment, delegated choice order, STAFF-43 replacement; staff, staff-market, staff.hiring, coaching-market, coaching-candidates and owner-traits tests are updated and pass.
- [ ] calendar.progression, season and full-model-throughput equivalence tests run and pass or are re-recorded with the reason; golden and prepared worlds regenerated as T685 did.
- [ ] typecheck, lint, format:check and the changed/affected unit tests pass.
