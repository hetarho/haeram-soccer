# T947 Prodigies (1–2 a year, 60 at 14, past 80 by 16) and war-gap intake only to the standing size

## goal
Each summer the world draws one or two prodigies (about 1.5 a year) aged 14 near 60 OVR with potential 88–96, placed at a club weighted by academy level and stature (a club nobody owns: its squad; the owner's club: its academy), who grow three times faster until 18 so one can pass 80 by 16; and skipped war summers only refill squads to their standing size.

## acceptance
- [v] Unit tests: over many seeded summers the yearly prodigy count is 1 or 2 with mean about 1.5; age 14, OVR 57–63, potential 88–96; placement weighted by academy level and stature (higher-level clubs more often); a prodigy in the owner's academy appears in its youngest age group (CLUB-16) and is never generated for a representative club; a prodigy's growth is ×3 with ×3 the season limit until 18 and normal after; on a seeded world a prodigy reaches 80+ by 16 in a clear share of cases (report the share over at least 50 prodigies on a 3-season run of the growth rule).
- [v] A skipped war summer brings youth only to the standing size (NPC_SQUAD.size, lines and a goalkeeper): a 1930→1946 run on the T916 seed opens 1946 with squads near the standing size (report mean and max; was about 39), ageing, development and retirement unchanged; wartime and the wartime-employment tests (T922 coaching overlaps) still pass.
- [v] The day-loop equivalence tests (calendar.progression, season, full-model-throughput) pass; world-hash fixtures this intended change moves are regenerated and named in the result.
- [v] Task-tier checks pass through `vibe check --tier task`.

## result
- outcome: New packages/engine/src/prodigies.ts. `PRODIGY` holds twoChance 0.5, age 14, ovr 57–63, potential 88–96, growth 3 until 18. `prodigyGrowth`, `prodigyWeight` = (academy level + 1) × (1 + max(0, stature)/10), and `drawProdigies(w, year)` on the stream `<seed>:prodigy:<year>`. Each draw picks the count, then per prodigy the club by weight over the playable associations' clubs, the owner's included (representative clubs, full registrations and a full academy excluded), then a slot of the club's formation, the OVR and the potential. His attributes shift so his OVR lands on the drawn value, and his wage is re-quoted. The player carries `prodigy?: true` (contracts types.ts + schema.ts, optional like `graduate`). A club nobody owns takes him as a squad player (`<club>:y:<year>:prodigyN`, graduate, contract 1–5 from his own stream). The owner's club takes him into its academy (`<club>:academy:<year>:prodigyN`, unpaid, U-17 at 14). `closeNpcSummer` calls `drawProdigies` once after the youth intake, so every played close and every war-gap summer draws one year's prodigies. Growth: training.ts round growth (`roundGrowthGain`, own and NPC) and the season's growth (`developAnnually`) multiply both the rate and its limit (0.12 a round, 3 a season) by `prodigyGrowth`; academy.ts `developAcademy` does the same to the academy cap 0.3. War gap: `intakeNpcYouth(…, refillOnly)` takes no academy count, so a skipped summer (`matches=false`) refills only the standing size, the lines and the goalkeeper, at the bench centre (T946's refill rule). Ageing, development, retirement and the contracts that stand still are unchanged.
