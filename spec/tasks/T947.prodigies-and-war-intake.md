## goal
Each summer the world draws one or two prodigies (about 1.5 a year) aged 14 near 60 OVR with potential 88–96, placed at a club weighted by academy level and stature (a club nobody owns: its squad; the owner's club: its academy), who grow three times faster until 18 so one can pass 80 by 16; and skipped war summers only refill squads to their standing size.

## acceptance

- [v] A skipped war summer brings youth only to the standing size (NPC_SQUAD.size, lines and a goalkeeper): a 1930→1946 run on the T916 seed opens 1946 with squads near the standing size (report mean and max; was about 39), ageing, development and retirement unchanged; wartime and the wartime-employment tests (T922 coaching overlaps) still pass.
- [v] The day-loop equivalence tests (calendar.progression, season, full-model-throughput) pass; world-hash fixtures this intended change moves are regenerated and named in the result.

## result
- outcome: New packages/engine/src/prodigies.ts. `PRODIGY` holds twoChance 0.5, age 14, ovr 57–63, potential 88–96, growth 3 until 18. `prodigyGrowth`, `prodigyWeight` = (academy level + 1) × (1 + max(0, stature)/10), and `drawProdigies(w, year)` on the stream `<seed>:prodigy:<year>`. Each draw picks the count, then per prodigy the club by weight over the playable associations' clubs, the owner's included (representative clubs, full registrations and a full academy excluded), then a slot of the club's formation, the OVR and the potential. His attributes shift so his OVR lands on the drawn value, and his wage is re-quoted. The player carries `prodigy?: true` (contracts types.ts + schema.ts, optional like `graduate`). A club nobody owns takes him as a squad player (`<club>:y:<year>:prodigyN`, graduate, contract 1–5 from his own stream). War gap: `intakeNpcYouth(…, refillOnly)` takes no academy count, so a skipped summer (`matches=false`) refills only the standing size, the lines and the goalkeeper, at the bench centre (T946's refill rule). Ageing, development, retirement and the contracts that stand still are unchanged.
