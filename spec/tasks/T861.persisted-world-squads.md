## goal
Every club of the eight playable associations nobody owns holds a stored squad of persisted players (founded in 1930 from today's generation); matches, market, scouting and the takeover read those players instead of a squad redrawn each season. Representative clubs keep generated squads.

## acceptance
- [ ] `World.squads?: Record<string, Player[]>` (contracts) holds each playable-country club nobody owns's persisted players; the owner's club keeps `w.players`; representative clubs have no entry.
- [ ] `historyWorld` founds every playable club's squad once in 1930 from the generation in force (`npcSquad` + the founding extra places of `NPC_SQUAD.size`), each player with a wage (→CLUB-25/48) and an `until` season drawn 1–5 seasons ahead from his own stream.
- [ ] `squad(w, club)` for a playable club nobody owns returns its matchday 18 in `squadPlaces` order chosen from `w.squads[club]` by →CLUB-6 (best 실효 OVR per formation slot, then the bench by line minimum), skipping unavailable players (→LEAGUE-15); representative clubs still use `npcSquad`. Matchday selection is cached and invalidated on any squad change, as `invalidateNpcSquads` does now.
- [ ] A player's attributes, potential, personality and identity never change at a season rollover by regeneration; `continuousSquad`'s regeneration, `replacement` and people `origin` slots are no longer used for playable clubs (removed or confined to representative clubs).
- [ ] The takeover moves the bought club's persisted squad into `w.players` (removing its `w.squads` entry); own purchases, loans, sales and returns move the same `Player` object between `w.players` and `w.squads[club]`; the free-agent pool still holds players without a club (→CLUB-49).
- [ ] Market, scouting and recruitment reads (`world-market.ts`, `scout-search.ts`, `transfers.ts`) list persisted players by `w.squads`, with identical candidates on repeated reads.
- [ ] Unavailable lists (→LEAGUE-15) no longer clear at a playable club's new season; an injury and an unserved ban carry over by person.
- [ ] Loading a checkpoint without `squads` persists its current generated squads once (`continuousSquad` of the loaded season) before activation; no other migration.
- [ ] Save packing keeps `squads` lossless and the century profiles inside →SAVE-4/13 (`apps/web/src/adapters/persistence.budget.test.ts` and the long-career persistence tests pass; add a column/packing path in `packing.ts` if plain packing exceeds the budget).
- [ ] Tests: `packages/engine/src/persisted-squads.test.ts` — founding squads exist for every playable club and none for representative clubs; a player keeps identity and attributes across a rollover; the matchday 18 follow CLUB-6 and skip an injured starter; a purchase moves the same object; a checkpoint without squads loads them once. Existing affected unit tests and goldens updated (`update-golden --write` as the golden tooling documents).
- [ ] `pnpm run typecheck`, `pnpm run lint`, `pnpm run format:check` and the changed unit tests pass.

## impl notes
- `NPC_SQUAD` config in `world.ts`: `size` = 22 persisted players per club (18 generated places + 4 founding extras drawn as bench at the place-age ladder's next cohorts); T866 may tune it.
- Founding uses today's `npcSquad` (age ladder, spread setting in force) so 1930 starts as today; nothing else draws a squad for a playable club after 1930.
- This task does not age, develop, retire or refill players — T862 does. Keep the season close working (squads simply persist; players age by `born`).
- People registry (`w.people`) stays the record of cross-role careers and former own players (→HIST-14, WORLD-10); it stops being the source of who plays for a playable club. Read affiliation from `w.squads`; keep `retainPerson`/`openEmployment` calls where a person record is required (own players, coaching entry).
- NPC players carry zero `career`/`season` metrics; their record arrives in T865 (→HIST-23).
- The match model's NPC inputs keep the 18-place squad shape so MATCH contracts hold; do not change match-engine code.
