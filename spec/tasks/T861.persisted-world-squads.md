## goal
Every club of the eight playable associations nobody owns holds a stored squad of persisted players, founded on the world's first day from today's generation; matches, market, scouting and the takeover read those players instead of a squad redrawn each season. Representative clubs keep generated squads. The view, the helpers' per-club carry, the packed save and the cost reads follow in T867.

## acceptance
- [ ] `World.squads?: Record<string, Player[]>` (contracts) holds each playable-country club nobody owns's persisted players; the owner's club keeps `w.players`; representative clubs have no entry.
- [ ] `foundSquads(w)` (new `packages/engine/src/persisted-squads.ts`) founds every playable club's squad once, whenever `w.squads` is absent: in `historyWorld` on the world's first day (`historyWorld`'s `year` — 1930 in production, a later year in a test world) from the generation in force (`npcSquad` + the founding extra places of `NPC_SQUAD.size`), each player with a wage (→CLUB-25/48) and an `until` season drawn 1–5 seasons ahead from his own stream.
- [ ] One live record a player (→WORLD-11): an id is live in exactly one of `w.players` (status `active`), one `w.squads[club]` or `w.freeAgents`.
  - a sale or loan of ours puts his attributes, potential, personality, age and identity into `w.squads[buyer]` as the live record; the row left in `w.players` (status `sold`) is his record with us and nothing reads it as a player of the buyer.
  - a purchase, a loan in, a return and a free signing bring the live record into `w.players`; over a departed row of the same id it replaces that row and keeps the row's `career` and `graduate`, as `signFreeAgentAtClose` does now.
  - no path draws a persisted player's attributes from a club's strength again: `identity()` over a generated place, `continuousSquad`'s regeneration, `replacement` and people `origin` slots are removed or confined to representative clubs.
- [ ] `squad(w, club)` for a playable club nobody owns returns its matchday 18 in `squadPlaces` order chosen from `w.squads[club]` by →CLUB-6 (`lineup` for the eleven, then the bench by line minimum): always 18 while the club holds 18 players. The choice is cached per club and made again only when that club's players change, on a season's first day and at a window's close (→WORLD-16); one club's change never clears another club's choice. Availability is not part of the cached choice: `startingSquad` swaps an unavailable starter for his fixture with the best available of the bench, then of the players outside the 18, and leaves him in place only when nobody is left (→LEAGUE-15). Representative clubs still use `npcSquad`.
- [ ] A player's attributes, potential, personality and identity never change at a season rollover by regeneration.
- [ ] The takeover moves the bought club's persisted squad into `w.players`, each with his contract, and removes its `w.squads` entry; it is filled to 20 only when it holds fewer (→CLUB-1). The free-agent pool still holds players without a club (→CLUB-49).
- [ ] Market, scouting and recruitment reads (`world-market.ts`, `scout-search.ts`, `transfers.ts`) list persisted players by `w.squads`, with identical candidates on repeated reads.
- [ ] Unavailable lists (→LEAGUE-15) no longer clear at a playable club's new season; an injury and an unserved ban carry over by person.
- [ ] People registry: a player whose coaching grade `materializePeople` retains today (4 or higher) is enrolled when he enters a squad (the founding here) and his moves keep going through `movePerson`; `materializePeople` draws no generated squad for a playable club. The 1 April announcement of registered people is unchanged.
- [ ] `crossWar` moves the `until` of every `w.squads` player, and a loan's end, by the seasons skipped (→WORLD-14/16).
- [ ] A load and a fixture helper read the same players as the host: `worldSchema` (`apps/web/src/adapters/schema.ts`) validates `squads` (it strips an unknown key silently) and `FIXTURE_READ_KEYS` carries `squads`, whole for now; T867 replaces the whole carry with per-club changes and the plain save with a packer. `fixtureReplica.test.ts` and a save → load unit test keep a squad player deep-equal.

## impl notes
- Owner execution override for pw86 (261113): during implementation run affected unit tests only; all other verification, including spec lint, typecheck, lint, i18n, formatting commands, build, integration/e2e, whole suites and performance/storage measurements, is deferred to the coordinator's final integrated pre-main-push gate. Preserve the original gates and register exact deferred commands in result limits. For this run an acceptance check for a deferred check/measurement records its prepared implementation and registered handoff only, never a claimed pass; the coordinator must satisfy it before publishing.
- `NPC_SQUAD` config in `world.ts`: `size` = 22 persisted players per club (18 generated places + 4 founding extras drawn as bench at the place-age ladder's next cohorts); T866 may tune it.
- Founding uses today's `npcSquad` (age ladder, spread setting in force) so the first season starts as today; nothing else draws a squad for a playable club after it. `foundSquads` is the one path for a new world, a test world founded on a later year and (T867) a loaded checkpoint without squads.
- IDs: a founding extra's and every later entrant's id starts with his first club's id (`originClub` and `nationalityOf` read it) and never repeats a place or vacancy id.
- Measured 261113 on this host (9,922 players of 451 clubs): one `lineup` pass over every club costs 106 ms against a 1.36 s season. Choosing the 18 on every round or clearing every club's choice on one move would multiply a season's time, so the cache is per club and follows the acceptance's three moments only.
- `rating`'s `npcBase` and `npcLineups` caches assume a squad fixed for the season; key them on the club's chosen 18 so a change of players or of attributes (T862) cannot leave a stale sum.
- This task does not age, develop, retire or refill players beyond the interim line — T862 does. Contracts, books, strength and the market are T863/T864.
- People registry (`w.people`) stays the record of cross-role careers and former own players (→HIST-14, WORLD-10); it stops being the source of who plays for a playable club. Read affiliation from `w.squads`.
- NPC players carry zero `career`/`season` metrics; their record arrives in T865 (→HIST-23).
- The match model's NPC inputs keep the 18-place squad shape so MATCH contracts hold; do not change match-engine code.
- Until T867 lands, `squads` reaches the UI thread through the view's rest spread, travels whole to every fixture helper and is saved unpacked: correct but costly. Do not optimise any of the three here.
