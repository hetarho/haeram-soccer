## impl notes
- Put NPC lifecycle code in a new `packages/engine/src/npc-careers.ts`; parameterize the shared growth math in `training.ts` (a pure function of player, age, ground, coaching, youth work, focus and the rounds settled) so own and NPC players share one formula.
- Wherever a growth rule reads a player's adaptation (PLAYER-16's 0.90), a player of a club nobody owns reads his club's 조직력 (→PLAYER-1).
- `CLUB_LEVELS` and `NPC_YOUTH` are named configs defined here (founding levels by tier and stature; youth count 2–5 and potential range by academy level); T866 tunes them. Pick starting values on the invariants test's world so each division's mean rating stays ordered and near its founding level.
- The 1.6 is this task's share of T867's season-time budget. Measured 261113 on this host: a growth pass over every player under 27 costs 28 ms and an XI pass 106 ms against a 1.36 s season, so 46 per-round passes of both would cost 4.5 seasons; the four-round cadence and no re-choice on growth are the rule, not an optimisation to revisit.
- Retirement, youth, development and ageing run for squads only; free agents keep CLUB-49's own ageing.
- Contracts, books, strength and the market are T863/T864; here squads shrink by retirement and grow by youth.
