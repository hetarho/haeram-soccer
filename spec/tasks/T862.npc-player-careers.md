## goal
Players of clubs nobody owns live the owner's players' career rules: per-round and annual development and ageing at their club's levels, CLUB-3 retirement, youth intake each summer keeping squads at size, and the war gap's skipped summers.

## acceptance
- [ ] `Club.levels?: { academy: number; ground: number; coaching: number }` exists for playable clubs nobody owns, founded in 1930 from division and stature (`CLUB_LEVELS.founding`); T863 moves them with the books.
- [ ] Each league round's settlement applies PLAYER-15/16 growth to every persisted NPC player with his club's `ground` and `coaching` in place of the owner's training ground, staff and squad investment, the club manager's youth work and the default training focus; the close applies `developAnnually`'s ageing/decline (PLAYER-17) the same way. Own-player results are unchanged (existing training tests pass).
- [ ] CLUB-3 retirement applies to every persisted NPC player (age chances, keeper offset, starter and minutes factors, 41 cap) decided from his own stream; NPC decisions make no inbox item and no news (news rules of T865 apply later); a retiring NPC whose coaching aptitude qualifies is enrolled as a person (retainPerson with his playing club history) so STAFF-23/24 coaching entry still finds him.
- [ ] Each summer every club nobody owns takes in youth (`NPC_YOUTH`: count and potential by `academy` level, ages 17–18, positions the squad lacks most, names by WORLD-12) until its squad reaches `NPC_SQUAD.size`, before the transfer window; a golden generation comes only from these draws.
- [ ] The war gap (1939/40–1945/46) runs each skipped summer's ageing, decline, retirement and youth intake once per season with no matches and no window (→WORLD-14).
- [ ] Tests: `packages/engine/src/npc-careers.test.ts` — a young NPC with potential headroom grows over a season and a 33-year-old declines; retirement chances follow CLUB-3 on a fixed stream; youth refill restores `NPC_SQUAD.size` with a goalkeeper; a qualifying retiree becomes a coaching-entry person; the war gap ages squads seven years and keeps them at size. Affected existing tests and goldens updated.
- [ ] `pnpm run typecheck`, `pnpm run lint`, `pnpm run format:check` and the changed unit tests pass.
