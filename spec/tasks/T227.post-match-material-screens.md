## goal
From the final analysis or a fixture list, an own match of this season opens its shot map, heat maps, pass network and tactical report on the pitch.

## acceptance
- [ ] `MatchMaterial.tsx` (lazy) with four tabs: 슛 지도 (dots sized by xG, filled for goals, headers marked, both sides attacking right), 히트맵 (a selector: our side, the opponent, each own player; 40 cells shaded by touch share), 패스 연결망 (own players at average positions, lines between pairs with width by passes), 전술 해설 (sentences built from `tacticalReport`: e.g. `오른쪽 하프스페이스로 12번 진입했어요`, entries by lane bar, regains and losses by third, set-piece shots and goals).
- [ ] Opens from `MatchReport` (button `경기 자료`) and from `FixtureNotebook` rows of own matches; a match without `material` (earlier seasons) shows `이 경기의 지도는 보관하지 않아요 — 선수의 시즌 히트맵을 확인하세요` with a link to the player sheet of the top touch-holder.
- [ ] Pitch drawn at 1.9:1 like the theatre; cell names appear on touch/tap (`cellName`).
