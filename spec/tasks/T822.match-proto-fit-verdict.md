## impl notes
- from review/match-engine-split-261110 F5
- Follow T791's chain rules; nothing outside `scripts/match-proto/` and `artifacts/match-proto-fit/` changes. The page is not opened in a browser by this task and no browser automation is run (→ARCH-30); say so in the result.
- What is being decided, so the timebox is spent on it: the owner has settled the method (ideation/match-script-and-staging); production planning starts from this task's result. Exactness is already proven by construction in T820 and by counting in T821. The open questions are (1) how much the script must invent between recorded events and whether that reads as football, (2) whether the staging can perform a real result's density without strain, (3) what it costs, (4) what should change upstream — in the aggregate path's ledger or attribution — rather than in the writer.
- Order of work (keep a short log with clock times in the artifacts README):
  1. baseline reports of the ten matches; the three hours start here.
  2. anything that stops a match from being staged to its end, or breaks exactness: bugs, fixed with a unit test.
  3. the order of touches inside a minute, the largest lever on `fit.links`, `fit.linkMetres` and `fit.miscontrols`. Candidates, one at a time: choose the next touch by the reception cell and the receiver's nominal place together instead of the cell alone; look one touch ahead so a pass is not received in a cell nothing leaves from; spread a minute's touches over possessions by the number of possession-ending events each side has in it, so neither side runs out mid-minute; let a touch move to the neighbouring minute when its cell has no partner (never a shot, its source or a booked foul); order a possession from deep to high cells when its events allow.
  4. who receives: a completed pass's receiver is fixed by the next touch, so order is also casting — read `events.reception` and how far receivers run, and prefer orders in which the next actor's nominal place is in or beside the pass's `to` cell.
  5. the clock: `fit.clockRatio` and `fit.stagedSeconds`. Levers: `fit.hold`, `fit.dead`, `stage.maxExtraHold`, and how a minute's time is shared; a minute with more beats than it can hold shows in `maxOverrun`.
  6. strain in contests: `fit.stretch`, `fit.late`. A scripted winner far from the ball is an ordering matter first (cue him earlier through the order), a `stage.*` value second.
  7. the picture: the ten naturalness metrics and the contact metrics on full matches, read against T818's final values on grammar scripts.
  8. findings and delivery, leaving at least thirty minutes for them.
- Findings to write down in the README, each with its numbers over the ten matches (these are what production planning reads):
  - what the script invented per match: `link` carries (count, total and mean metres, the longest), `miscontrol`s, connective restarts against restarts delivered by a recorded pass, orphans; and the same per ninety minutes for the baseline writer.
  - where the invention comes from in the record: cells that events leave more often than the ball arrives there (and the reverse), the difference between the two sides' possession-ending events, minutes whose recorded events cannot be joined without a `miscontrol` — in a form that says whether the aggregate path's flows would need to be drawn as chains for a natural script.
  - events whose recorded actor is far from where his role stands (count per match, the worst ten as role, cell and metres) — whether the attribution's choice of players needs the pitch in mind.
  - restarts: how many throw-ins, goal kicks and free kicks the record implies against the recorded passes that could deliver them.
  - cost: `fit.writeMs` and `fit.stageMs` per match (median and worst), the page's bytes at `viewer.matchStride`.
  - what the ten matches did not contain and production must add: substitutions, dismissals and injuries, penalties, extra time and shoot-outs, formations other than 4-3-3 and 4-4-2.
  - the verdict in one line per question (1)–(4) above, as measured values, not as a recommendation to change the method.
- One change at a time; a round's score is read on `s01`–`s05` as the sum of `fit.linkMetres` and the count of `fit.miscontrols` going down without `events.reception`, `events.contest` or `kinematics.*` getting worse. There are no limits for the fit's cost numbers: they are measured, and the owner and planning judge them.
- If the only way to lower a number is to break T820's principle (an unrecorded pass, an event moved off its minute, another player credited), do not: write the number down with that explanation. That is the finding.
- If the criteria themselves need changing, or a change to the aggregate path looks necessary, that is a planning change for the owner (update-ssot), not something to decide here; this task changes no file under `packages/`.
