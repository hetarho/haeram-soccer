## goal
After every fixture the world applies what happened in it: substitutes' adaptation and fatigue by minutes, injuries from the match for every club, cards into bans, and bans served.

## acceptance

## impl notes
- Own lines: `saved.players` = record lines whose ids belong to own players (already filtered by id).
- Starters vs substitutes: the first `lineCounts[ownSide]`… lines of the own side in record order are starters for the first 11 that have no `sub` incident as `other`; simplest rule: a line is a substitute iff its id appears as `other` in a `sub` incident.
- Slot of a substitute for `gainLevelFactor`: the slot of the player he replaced (from the incident's `player` → that player's slot in the XI order).
- `fatigueCost(p, tactic)` stays in strategy.ts.
- NPC injury days: reuse the CLUB-23 band table (`INJURY.bands` in careers.ts) with the same stream key.
- Dedupe: `recordMatch` already returns early for an own match already stored; NPC fixtures settle once (fixture `score` set once) — keep `serveBans`/`applyDiscipline` inside the non-dedupe branch.
