## impl notes
- CI evidence: 41930383191 (webkit and firefox), 42385052171 (firefox 4 failed; webkit 3 failed, 1 flaky): `expectHomeBounds` fails `scroll` with 2 / 4 while `documentScroll` is 0 and the document fits; the page is 구단주 사무실 with the 우리 팀 오버뷰 dialog open and its 창 닫기 focused.
- The trace of 42385052171's webkit 360×740 case (artifact `e2e-webkit-1`) records `view-scroller` at 0 before the 오버뷰 click and 2 in the click's own snapshot. Its log: "element is not stable" three times, then "scrolling into view if needed", "done scrolling". So the offset appears inside `openClubOverview`'s click, right after the office page's entrance animation.
- Two candidates: (1) the click handler in `ClubNavigation.tsx` calls `event.currentTarget.focus()` without `preventScroll` (WebKit and Linux Firefox do not focus a clicked button, so this focus can scroll; `Dialog.tsx` already focuses with `preventScroll: true`); (2) Playwright scrolling a target still settling from the page's enter animation (`App.tsx` plays translateY 10 px → none for 280 ms on the scroller), which a `settle(page)` before the click in `openClubOverview` would rule out.
- Local runs on this macOS machine (PREVIEW_BUILD=1, Firefox and WebKit) pass all four, so verify on CI's browsers; Chromium is the only local gate browser.
