# T1066 Unlock management after a stop under load

## goal
Under load the game can stay locked after the owner stops automatic play or changes the progression mode. Every office control stays disabled with "자동 진행 중에는 현재 날짜의 정보가 아직 계산되지 않아 이 영역을 볼 수 없습니다.", and `자동 진행 시작` is shown but disabled, for minutes. Main CI 45389461640 failed `tests/e2e/progression-settings.spec.ts:201` (the 위임 설정 `부재 중에도 업무 계속` checkbox stays disabled for 150 s after `자동 진행 정지`) and `tests/e2e/second-career.spec.ts:292` (`progression-mode` stays MANUAL 30 s after choosing AUTO at a season close). Locally with `CI=1`, two workers and other load, these failed 2 of 6 runs on 0.8.25 (22a22cf0f), 3 of 8 on the same code with T1062 reverted, and 0 of 8 on 0211dd2a0 when the host was quieter, so the lock predates 0.8.25 and depends on load. WEB-97 locks management only until the admitted step finishes and the computed days ahead have been shown. Find why the lock outlives that and fix it, so a player never needs a reload after stopping.
