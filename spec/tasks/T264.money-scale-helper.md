## goal
Multiplying an amount by a decimal factor goes through one rounding-safe helper, so a tuning value that is not a short decimal can never crash a money check.

## acceptance
- [ ] primitives.ts exports `scaleBy(amount: string, factor: number, precision: bigint): string` = `ratio(amount, BigInt(Math.round(factor * Number(precision))), precision)` (half-even like `ratio`); it throws on a non-finite factor or a precision ≤ 0.
- [ ] These sites call `scaleBy` with their current precision: signings.ts:155 (`feeShare`, 100n — today without `Math.round`), contracts.ts:364 and :648 (1000n), ops-squad.ts:130 (1000n), staff.ts:290 (1_000_000n), finance.ts:125 (10000n), :182 `scaleOperatingExpenses` (100n) and :580 parachute (1000n), transfers.ts:776 (1000n).
- [ ] Composite ratios are left untouched (sponsors.ts:126-129, settlement.ts:229, finance.ts:566, transfers.ts:829, contracts.ts:389), and academy.ts:404 keeps its truncating `/ 100n` (not in touches).
- [ ] `packages/engine/src/primitives.test.ts` cases: "scaleBy matches ratio for representable factors", "scaleBy rounds a factor like 0.29 instead of throwing", "scaleBy rounds half to even at the precision", "scaleBy rejects NaN, Infinity and a zero precision", and a fast-check property "scaleBy(x, f, P) equals ratio(x, round(f·P), P) for integer x and f in [0, 4]".
