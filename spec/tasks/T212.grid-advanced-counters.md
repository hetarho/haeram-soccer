## impl notes
- All rules in design §9.2, computed in the acting side's attack frame (`attackFrame`), with cells/bands/`inBox` from `pitch.ts` and xT from `CELL_BASELINES`.
- Box entry: the pass/carry ends `inBox` and started outside it. Front-of-box entry: ends in band 5, started in band < 5.
- Line-breaking: `decide` already marks `lineBreaking` on the decision; count it when the pass completes.
- PPDA: `buildUpPasses[side]` += 1 for every pass attempt of `side` starting at x < 70; `pressActions[side]` += 1 for every tackle or interception by `side` at x ≥ 35 in `side`'s frame (that is the opponent's two deeper thirds).
- High turnover: the ball won by `side` at x ≥ 70 in `side`'s frame (= the opponent's defensive third); count `TD.highTurnovers` and `PD.highTurnovers` for the winner.
- xT added: `round(max(0, xT(end) − xT(start)) × 1000)` per completed pass or carry, to the passer/carrier (team TD gets the same).
- Restart counters: corners, throw-ins, goal kicks, free kicks (direct and indirect), penalties — to the side taking them.
- Fouls: `PD.fouls` for the fouler, `PD.foulsWon` for the fouled; `TD.fouls` for the fouling side.
