## goal
Lower divisions' best players move up and surplus players move down for fees, at football's rates: a buyer ranks targets by slot gain for what they cost, reads its means from its season wage budget, skips players who would refuse it after the first settlement day, and may buy a higher division's listed players; the sale answer explains the runway rows.

## acceptance
- [ ] Unit tests: with two candidates of equal slot gain the cheaper (lower-division, priced on his own division) ranks first, and a cheaper candidate with a slightly smaller gain can outrank a dear one by the value rule; a buyer's affordable level follows its season wage budget (not cash plus backing), so a rich-but-small club does not read 99; a lower-division club can buy a higher division's listed player (surplus, blocked, final season, aged 30+) for a fee and the move reports reason `listed-down`; an unlisted higher-division starter is never bought down; from a window's second settlement day a target whose answer would refuse is skipped without spending a bid, the first day still bids blind.

## impl notes
- From the T950 phase-1 diagnosis (coordinator measurement 261118): targets rank by raw slot rating and the first success settles; `affordableLevel` (npc-market.ts) reads npcWageLimit's whole envelope (cash, backing, transfer balance) and saturates at 97–99, so the search band does nothing; a higher division's player comes down only on loan, so every downward flow goes through releases and loans; top-3-up sat at 5–13% under every constant sweep; (war refills are T952's).
- Value rule: score = slot gain ÷ (fee + `NPC_MARKET.valueYears` × annual wage) normalised by the buyer's wage budget, with `NPC_MARKET.valueWeight` blending raw gain and value (starting 0.5); constants named for T950.
- Means: the affordable level is the highest rating whose market wage at the buyer's division fits its season wage budget (books' projected operating income less operating costs, plus a runway share of cash above debt: `NPC_MARKET.cashRunway` seasons, starting 3); npcCanPay (books.ts) is unchanged and still decides each commitment.
- Down moves: lower-division buyers may target a higher division's listed players only; the seller's answer and personal terms as for any NPC bid; contested players unchanged.
- Sale-answer copy: T948 changed wagePressure (delegation.ts) to the runway test; sale-answer.ts:139-146 still reports the retired sales-line wording — reassigned here from T948's review (owner-market lane). Touches exceed five on purpose: copy and translations change with their consumer.
- Refused pairs: under T945's decision A a refused buyer re-asks the same top target every settlement day and, with one bid a day in the 1950s, one refusal can hold a need for the whole window. From a window's second settlement day a buyer skips a target whose answer under this window's draws, read with today's factors, would refuse it (what it learned from the first day's refusals or an enquiry), so its later bids go to players who can say yes; the first settlement day still bids blind so refusals happen; measure the effect.
