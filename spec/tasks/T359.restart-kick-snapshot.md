## goal
An observed candidate match records a snapshot at each restart's kick and starts the restart's intentions there, so playback draws the walk-in and the delivery as the engine played them instead of one straight move.

## acceptance
- [v] The candidate trace records every player's position at a restart's kick (the instant `restartTaken` runs) in addition to its ordinary samples, and the intentions sampled during a restart's preparation are the restart's, not the previous play's.
- [v] Facts are unchanged: the trace change must not move any draw, so the facts digests in `grid-throughput.test.ts` and the candidate rounds digest in `full-model-throughput.test.ts` stay as they are and only the observed positions digests (darwin-arm64) are re-recorded with a comment; the pre-feature path is unchanged.
- [v] `grid/playback.test.ts` adds `playback passes through the recorded kick of a corner` and `restart preparation samples show the restart's intentions`, using a compact corner scene, both attacking directions and a crossed free kick; same-seed reruns reproduce exactly.
- [v] `tests/e2e/set-piece-defence.spec.ts` inspects the walk-in at the recorded kick at phone and desktop sizes (left to the batch run, ARCH-30); `docs/MATCH-ENGINE-DESIGN.md` §4.7 drops the playback limitation. Task checks and build follow ARCH-30.

## impl notes
- From T348's limit (3): non-actors are drawn in straight lines between snapshots (T275's known limit); a restart's walk-in is drawn as one move ending as the delivery lands, so the kick itself is never shown as recorded, and samples during a restart's preparation show the previous entry's intentions. T348's engine side (defenders' jobs through the delivery) is correct; this is recording and presentation only.
- The extra snapshot is presentation data: record it without drawing, reading a stream or reordering any floating-point operation that feeds play, so the facts digests prove it changed nothing. If the trace format gains a field, keep old traces readable (settled matches keep their recorded traces; no history is replayed).
- T286's continuity evidence includes T348's correction, so this lands before T286.

  - Trace: a restart prepared on the clock (every restart but a period's kick-off) carries `TraceEntry.kick` (`TraceKick`: elapsed, positions, slots, targets, states, intentions).
