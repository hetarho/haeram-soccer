## goal
An observed candidate match records a snapshot at each restart's kick and starts the restart's intentions there, so playback draws the walk-in and the delivery as the engine played them instead of one straight move.

## acceptance
- [ ] The candidate trace records every player's position at a restart's kick (the instant `restartTaken` runs) in addition to its ordinary samples, and the intentions sampled during a restart's preparation are the restart's, not the previous play's.
- [ ] Facts are unchanged: the trace change must not move any draw, so the facts digests in `grid-throughput.test.ts` and the candidate rounds digest in `full-model-throughput.test.ts` stay as they are and only the observed positions digests (darwin-arm64) are re-recorded with a comment; the pre-feature path is unchanged.
- [ ] `grid/playback.test.ts` adds `playback passes through the recorded kick of a corner` and `restart preparation samples show the restart's intentions`, using a compact corner scene, both attacking directions and a crossed free kick; same-seed reruns reproduce exactly.
