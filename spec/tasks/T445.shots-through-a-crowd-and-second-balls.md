## goal
On the candidate model, a shot is blocked first by the outfielders in its path to the goal, its xG reads those bodies, and a block, a parry or a loose save falls where it would and is contested by whoever reaches it first, an attacker who wins it near goal shooting again (MATCH-35 r28).

## acceptance
- [v] `BOX_CONFIG.shot` sets the block: every outfield opponent within `shot.lane` (1.5 m) of the triangle from the ball to the two posts may block it, each with `shot.block` (0.35) × a nearness factor (1 at the shooter, falling to `shot.far` 0.4 at the goal line) × (1 + (위치선정 − 55) × `SHOT_CONFIG.block.skill`); the draws come in order of distance from the shooter before the keeper's, and only a shot nobody blocks meets the keeper. This replaces the miss-then-block order of `resolveShot` on the candidate model.
- [v] xG on the candidate model reads the same bodies: the recorded and the decision's xG is the chance's xG × the chance that nobody in the triangle blocks it, so recorded xG stays the chance's goal probability for the average finisher and keeper (MATCH-11) and goals − xG still measures finishing; the keeper's part of the resolution (`goalChance` after no block) reads the xG without the bodies, so nothing counts them twice. The decision's shot quality weight (`shotQuality`, T370) reads it, so a carrier passes up a blocked view more surely with better 판단력.
- [v] Second balls: a block falls `shot.rebound.block` (4–9 m) back toward the shooter from the blocker, a parried save or `then: 'loose'` save `shot.rebound.save` (5–10 m) out from the keeper, wide of the goal by the shot's side, drawn from the match stream; the loose ball goes to the player `reachOnPath` (T441) finds first among both sides (in place of `looseWinner`'s distance weights there), and an attacker who wins it inside the box shoots at once (a first-time shot, no carry) when his shot weight allows it.
- [v] Tests: `box.test.ts` "two defenders in the shot's path block it more often than one", "a shot with nobody in its path is never blocked"; `resolve.test.ts` "xG falls with bodies between the ball and the goal", "blocks come before the keeper"; `engine.test.ts` "a parried shot falls in front of goal and is played by the player who reaches it first", "a rebound won near goal is shot at once"; every new record passes `validateFullMatchRecord` (blocks stay shots, MATCH-3/27); candidate digests re-recorded where they change, with the measured change in the test comment; the pre-feature path is unchanged (its digests pass untouched).
- [v] The result reports, on one calibration round against the pre-change source (the next unused round in docs/CALIBRATION.md), per team a match: shots, the blocked share of shots, on target, saves, xG per shot, big chances, rebound shots and goals, shot place shares, xG and goals (reported, not gated: MATCH-28 r23).
- [v] Task checks (ARCH-30): typecheck, lint, format:check, `vitest run --changed`; the release-on unit files this touches pass under `vitest.release-on.config.ts` as well.

  - `grid/resolve.ts`:
    - `shotBlockers(s, side, from)`: the outfield opponents ahead of the ball and within `lane` m of the triangle ball–posts, nearest first (ties by player ID). Each chance is `block` × nearness (1 → `far`, by the fraction of the way along the line to the goal's middle) × (1 + (위치선정 − 55) × `SHOT_CONFIG.block.skill`), within 0–1.
    - `unblocked(blockers)`: the product of 1 − chance.
    - `resolveShot` on the candidate: one draw per body in that order, the first success a block (`blockThen` corner/loose split kept). Then the keeper: §7.4's goal/save/miss on the chance's xG without the bodies, with no block after a miss. The outcome's `xg` (big chances) is chance × `unblocked`; `recordedXg` is that × `levelFactor`, capped. The pre-feature branch is untouched; `SHOT_CONFIG.block` stays its block.
    - Rebounds: `blockRebound` (4–9 m back toward the shooter from the blocker) and `saveRebound` (5–10 m out from the keeper, `wide` m sideways a metre out toward the shot's side). Both draw from the match stream.
    - `secondBall`: reads `reachOnPath` for both sides on the path from the blocker or keeper (left out) to the landing point, kicked when the shot reaches him. The smaller `along` wins at its reach point; equal points go by more spare, then the defending side, then ID. With nobody reaching it, the soonest runner to the resting place (`reachSeconds`) wins it there. `ShotOutcome` gains `via`, `at` and `winner`.
    - `resolvePenalty`'s parry uses the same second ball on the candidate. Penalties read no bodies.
  - `grid/decide.ts`: `actionMix`'s `shotQuality` xG is chance × `unblocked`^(1/τ), τ the carrier's `temperature`. A sharp head passes up a blocked view more surely; at τ 1 it is the share itself.
  - `grid/engine.ts`:

    - `applyShot` hands a candidate second ball to its `winner` at `at`; an attacking winner in the box sets `rebound`.
    - The next decision of that player is drawn as ever. A drawn shot is first time: no jitter, no carry, no wind-up, as T444's finish. A dead ball clears `rebound`.
    - `motionOf` flies a second ball's shot to `via` (a `touch` knot) and on at pass speed to the winner.
