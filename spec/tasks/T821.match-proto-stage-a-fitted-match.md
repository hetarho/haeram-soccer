## impl notes
- from review/match-engine-split-261110 F5
- Follow T791's chain rules; the page still shows no authored prose (identifiers, digits, symbols). It is not opened in a browser by this task (→ARCH-30).
- The rule this task enforces in staging: for a `bound` script nothing the record names may change — outcome, actor, receiver, opponent. Today's staging keeps the first three and may replace the opponent (`recast`) when the scripted man cannot get there; a bound script forbids that, because the record credits that tackle or interception to him.
- `stage/perform.ts`: for `script.bound` the loop runs to the last beat; `options.duration` still cuts when given (tests use it, cutting between beats: a beat that starts is finished). The playback's `duration` is the last frame's `t`.
- `stage/contest.ts` `resolveCut`: with `state.bound` (set by `createStage` from the script) the scripted opponent's own plan is used; when his margin is over `stage.cutSlack` the lunge and then the slower ball (as today's last branch does) are applied to him alone, `stretch: true`, `recast: false`. `ensureOwner` already lets a late man collect a ball that lies; when he is still not there at the end of the flight, the event gets `late: true` (a new optional `StageEvent` field) and the ball is his once he arrives.
- `stage/carry.ts` `performCarryLost`: with `state.bound` the scripted tackler is never replaced: after `stage.tackleGrace` he gets the lunge for `stage.tackleExtra`; if he is still out of `stage.tackleRange` the carrier stops (order: his own position) until he arrives, `stretch: true`, and `late: true` when that takes more than `stage.settleMax`.
- New handler `carry:loose` (`stage/carry.ts` `performMiscontrol`): the holder takes his hold, then a touch that leaves the ball loose `PROTO_CONFIG.stage.heavyTouch` (new, 3) metres toward the named opponent, who collects it through `ensureOwner`; the event pushed is the new `StageEventKind` `'loose'` with `actor` and `other`. No tackle or interception event is pushed for it.
- Restarts and halves: a connective restart beat is an ordinary restart-kind beat and goes through `withRestart` as today. Kick-offs after goals and at the half come from the script as `kickOff` beats; before a half's kick-off the dead-ball scene already walks both sides to their kick-off places. After the last beat of each half the stage pushes a `whistle` event and holds a dead ball for `stage.restart.delay.kickOff` seconds (full time included).
- The match clock: `Frame` gains optional `clock` (match seconds). While a beat is performed the frame's clock runs from the beat's `clock` at one match second per staged second and never passes the next beat's `clock`; in a dead-ball scene it stands still. So the clock is the record's time (a shot recorded in minute 63 is seen in minute 63) whatever the staging's own seconds are. `Playback` gains optional `stride` (stored frames per staged tick, default 1).
- Viewer and build: `PROTO_CONFIG.viewer` gains `matchStride: 2` and `matchSpeeds: [1, 5, 20]`. `build.ts --match <id>` reads `fixtures/settled/<id>.json`, stages `writeFitted(settled, 'fit')`, computes metrics on the full-rate playback, then stores every `matchStride`-th frame (events keep their times; `hz` becomes `hz / stride`). The HUD shows `clock` when the first frame has one: `mm:ss`, as `45+m:ss` and `90+m:ss` past the halves' ends; the speed buttons are `matchSpeeds` for such a playback. Nothing else in the viewer changes.
- `check/fit.ts`:
  ```ts
  /** The record's rows counted from a playback: its performed beats through `countScript`, with each contest's winner read from the stage event (`intercept`, `tackle`, `loose`) of that beat instead of the beat's `opponent`. */
  export function countPlayback(playback: Playback, settled: Settled): Counted;
  export interface FitMetric { id: string; value: number; pass?: boolean; detail?: string }
  export function fitMetrics(playback: Playback, script: Script, settled: Settled, timings: { writeMs: number; stageMs: number }): FitMetric[];
  ```
  The fit lines, in this order (only the first three are judged; the rest are measurements for T822 and for planning):
  - `fit.exact`: the number of cells in which `compareRecord(settled, countPlayback(...))` differs; passes at 0.
  - `fit.performed`: script beats not in the playback; passes at 0.
  - `fit.recast`: contested events with `recast`; passes at 0.
  - `fit.stretch`: share of contested events with `stretch`; `fit.late`: events with `late`.
  - `fit.links`, `fit.linkMetres`, `fit.miscontrols`, `fit.connectiveRestarts`, `fit.orphans`: `fitCost`'s numbers (`orphans` = set pieces plus free-kick shots).
  - `fit.stagedSeconds`: the playback's duration; `fit.clockRatio`: staged live seconds per match second, the median over match minutes (1 = the staging keeps the clock; 2 = a minute of the record takes two to perform).
  - `fit.writeMs`, `fit.stageMs`: wall-clock milliseconds of `writeFitted` and of `performScript`, measured in `report.ts` (not in `fit.ts`, which stays pure) with `performance.now` — `report.ts` is a CLI outside `stage/`, the one place besides `viewer/` where the chain's determinism rule allows a clock, and the two numbers are printed, never fed back. `README.md`'s determinism rule is reworded to say so: no clock outside `viewer/` except `check/report.ts` timing its own run.
- `check/report.ts --match <id>`: prints `match=<id>`, `score=`, the fit lines (`id value ok|FAIL|-`), then the 31 metrics. Showcase quotas are not printed for a match. The 31 metrics run unchanged on the full playback; expect several to move (a fitted script was not written to please them) and record them as they are.
- Expected strains to record, not to fix here: the baseline writer of T820 joins events by nearest cell only, so receivers may have far to run (`events.reception`), scripted winners may arrive late (`fit.stretch`, `fit.late`) and `fit.clockRatio` may sit well above 1. If a bound script cannot be staged to its end at all (a handler waits forever, a player cannot reach a point), that is a bug to fix here with a unit test; an ugly but complete match is this task's expected result.
- Not in this task: improving the writer's order or the staging's naturalness for fitted scripts (T822); substitutions, dismissals, penalties, extra time.
