## goal
Make each NPC market settlement walk one presorted listing order per line and slot instead of refiltering and re-sorting the line pool for every buyer and need, producing the identical world faster.

## acceptance
- [ ] `npcMarket` builds, at most once per settlement and lazily, the order of each line's listings for a need's slot (rating for that slot descending, then the settlement `tie`, then `compareIds`), and for each need walks it: skip the buyer's own listings, moved players and listings `npcCanSell` refuses as they are reached; stop at the first listing whose slot rating is below `need.best + NPC_MARKET.upgrade` when the need has a slot. Club stature comes from one per-settlement map in the buyer sort, contender filter and offer sort. Formulas, funding, registration, offers, tie order, observer calls and stored facts are unchanged (deterministic; worker, task stage).
