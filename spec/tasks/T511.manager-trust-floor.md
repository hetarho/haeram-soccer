## goal
A manager who sits at trust 10 or less warns the owner, then may resign each settled league round, and leaves for certain at the season close at trust 0.

## acceptance
- [v] `TRUST_FLOOR_CONFIG = { warn: 10, chance: 0.1, zeroChance: 0.25 }` in ops-staff.ts; new exported `settleManagerTrust(w)` runs once per settled league round, called in season.ts `playRound` right after `settleRound(w)` (league rounds only, never cup/europe days).
- [v] Not interim and trust ≤ warn without `trustWarned`: sets `m.trustWarned = true`, adds event `manager-trust-warning` (title `${m.name} 감독, 사임 고민`), pushes an inbox item of kind `staff-report` saying he is considering resigning and that a 공개 신임 can help; no resignation draw that round.
- [v] Not interim, trust ≤ warn, already warned: resigns with `zeroChance` at trust 0, else `chance`, drawn from `random(\`${w.seed}:trust-floor:${m.id}:${w.year}:${w.round}\`)` only, so other draws and match results stay put; resignation uses the existing private `resign(w, '구단주와의 신뢰가 무너져 사임했습니다.', 'resigned')`.
- [v] Trust above warn at a settled round deletes `trustWarned` (a later fall warns again).
- [v] `yearlyStaff`: before the retirement/expiry checks, a non-interim manager at trust 0 resigns with the same reason; retirement/expiry do not run for him afterwards.
- [v] Delegated manager post: the existing `resign` path appoints a successor at once (→STAFF-18) — covered by a test.
- [v] Tests in new `packages/engine/src/staff.trust-floor.test.ts`: "warns once on first reaching the floor without resigning", "resigns at the draw's odds on later rounds and never as an interim", "clears the warning above the floor and warns again", "a trust-0 manager leaves at the close before renewal", "a delegated post appoints a successor at once", "the draw leaves other streams unchanged" (same seed, a manager above the floor vs. a hidden draw — compare match scores).
- [v] Existing staff, managerMarket and manager-history-capture unit tests pass. Fast checks: typecheck, lint, format:check, unit tests of changed files.
