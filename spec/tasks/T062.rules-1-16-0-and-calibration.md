## result
- outcome: blocked for update-ssot: no constant can make the net-result band (ECON-29) hold. Apart from wages, the only costs are hosting costs and facility upkeep, which follow prices, plus small signing fees. Income follows the football index, so these other costs fall from 13.2% of income in the 1950s to 0.8% in the 2000s. With the wage bill at most 70% of income, the net result stays at about 29% or more; the band asks for −5% to +10%. Done so far: rules 1.16.0 in versions.ts accept 1.15.0, and writer activation moves a 1.15 save's engine, catalog pin (2026-demo-1→2026-demo-2) and revision, with its single sponsor already loaded as its slot's contract. `scripts/calibrate-economy.ts` plays 60 seasons from the 1950 founding with every delegation on. The first 50 seasons give the career figures; seasons 51–60 are the 2000s. It covers each country × build (default, 트레이딩 머신) × facility mode (none, greedy: buy a level whenever cash still covers 13 rounds of costs). It prints the ECON-29 table for both facility modes, ECON-9 own league home attendance by division, an income/cost/net breakdown by decade and by division, and the CLUB-25 wage ratios. `--out`/`--in` keep and re-report careers. Measured on 16 careers (ENG ESP FRA GER, seed `economy-<code>-0`), with no facility buying | greedy facility buying:
