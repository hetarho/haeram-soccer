## goal
Correct the measured asymmetry between attacking placement retention and defensive release, keeping both sides' set-piece jobs coherent through the delivery and second ball.

## acceptance
- [v] Re-run the recorded probes in `docs/benchmarks/set-piece-motion-261022.md`: 37 default corners move both sides, with mean 10 m groups 6.43 attacking/2.16 defending; the isolated default placement changes 6/9 → 6/1 after ordinary movement. Identify the exact preparation, resolution or release stage responsible; inspect the candidate independently (52 corners: 5.38/5.44), rather than assuming a stationary team or a whole-team point collapse.
- [v] Preserve and update defensive marking/cover and goalkeeper response through preparation, delivery, the aerial contest and the second ball. Attackers and defenders follow continuous engine-recorded routes; no renderer-only defensive motion, teleporting, duplicate marking assignment or lingering set-piece job after a turnover or restart.
- [v] Add small cases in `grid/shape.test.ts`, `grid/coordination.test.ts` and `grid/restarts.test.ts`: `corner defenders retain and update their marks after the kick`, `cover follows a displaced runner`, `keeper responds to the delivery`, `second ball releases obsolete restart jobs`, and `dismissed players never receive a marking assignment`. Assert observable movement only when the fixture changes the relevant target.
- [v] Extend `grid/movement.test.ts` and `grid/playback.test.ts` with `both sides retain continuous recorded paths through a corner` and `defensive placement is not discarded before its job ends`, including bounded travel, both attacking directions, both corner touchlines, a delivered free kick, the delivery boundary and the second ball. Repeated same-seed runs reproduce exactly; settled historical facts remain unchanged under the existing activation policy.
- [v] Add `tests/e2e/set-piece-defence.spec.ts` using the compact recorded scene to inspect both sides through the delivery at phone and desktop sizes. Run affected unit tests and ARCH-30 task checks plus build; retain this browser spec for the batch gate.

## impl notes
- From `ideation/set-piece-defence-and-goal-feedback`; this is a correctness task, not a timeboxed calibration exercise. T325 finishes its bit-identical baseline work and T345 its tactical changes before this task changes candidate football; T323 then measures the resulting reference.
- Measurement source and limits are `docs/benchmarks/set-piece-motion-261022.md`; use its recorded coordinates and stage calls as the regression starting point. The baseline reproduces group retention and dispersal, not the original unsupported claim that only attackers move. Do not treat ordinary box crowding as a defect merely because several players are near one another.
- Trace `setPieceTargets` → `restartPlaces`/`prepareRestart` → `restartTaken` → ordinary coordination. Initial placement already includes `scene.marks`, while `holdSetPiece` and the current `restartTaken` call retain attacking participants; inspect that asymmetry rather than assuming it proves the sole cause.
- Extend the existing shared scene/coordination types and intention priorities in their owning modules. Reuse SHAPE_CONFIG and the movement rules; avoid a second restart simulation or presentation-side invented positions. Carry the corrected lifecycle into the candidate under existing prospective activation; preserve settled/queued legacy matches, and document the default-path limitation until activation instead of silently resimulating history.
- Use compact state fixtures instead of simulating a season. Unit assertions should prove responses to changed runner/ball targets and valid job release, rather than requiring every defender to move at every instant.

## result
- outcome: On the candidate model the defending scene now keeps its set-piece jobs after the kick, for as long as the attackers who went up hold the box (candidate model only; the pre-feature path is unchanged). Details and figures are in docs/MATCH-ENGINE-DESIGN.md §4.7.
  - Probes rerun on c2f5aa9 (main + T325 + T345). The default path matches the 70eec53 record: 37 corners, 6.43/2.16; the isolated placement goes 6/9 → 6/1. The candidate gives 43 corners, 5.40/5.19; the recorded 52 corners, 5.38/5.44, came from 70eec53 before T345. Neither side was stationary, and no snapshot had 7 teammates within 5 m.
  - The responsible stage is the release, not preparation (`setPieceTargets`, `restartPlaces`/`prepareRestart`) or resolution (`resolveCross`).
    - Default path: `holdSetPiece` keeps only `scene.up`; `playGrid`'s `moveAll(s, 8)` then walks the defenders, who hold nothing, to `shapePlace`.
    - Candidate: `restartTaken` at the kick ended every restart place on both sides (`arrival`) and re-committed only `scene.up`. `planCoordination` then re-paired runners only while the attackers kept the ball. The zonal and edge defenders and the keeper went to the shape during the flight. Isolated stage: defending group 9 → 6 after the 3.2 s delivery; the scene drifted 6.2 m (3.2 s) and 12 m (5.7 s).
  - The fix: the candidate preparer moved bit-identically from engine.ts into `prepareOnClock` (movement.ts, be45045). It now calls `defendSetPiece` (intentions.ts) after `restartTaken`, giving committed jobs until the kick + `setPiece.stay`:
    - each man-marker marks the runner his place names; `setPieceTargets` now names that runner (`marks[].opponent`) and the keeper;
    - each zonal or edge defender guards his spot (`cover` with a new `Intention.zone`; `guardPoint` sends him goal-side of an opponent within `setPiece.zone` 3 m);
    - the keeper guards his goal on the line from its middle to the ball: 0.25 of the distance, at most 4 m out, at least 1 m off the line, within 4.66 m of the middle.
    - The jobs end at a turnover, a restart, an urgent press or mark, a player leaving (`departed`) and expiry. A mark also ends with its runner's hold; a guard once the ball is more than `setPiece.area` (30) m from the goal line (`secondBallOver`).
