## goal
On the candidate model, an open-play pass is intercepted only by an opponent who can reach its path before the ball has passed, at the point he reaches, and a pass nobody can reach is lost only at its receiver (MATCH-31 r28).

## acceptance
- [ ] New `grid/lanes.ts` exports `LANE_CONFIG` and `reachOnPath(s, side, from, to, kickAt)`: for each opponent on the pitch, the first point along the ball's path from `from` to `to` (engine frame, the ball at `GRID_BALL_CONFIG.pass` speed from `kickAt`) he can get within `LANE_CONFIG.reach` (1.2 m) of, or at full stretch within `LANE_CONFIG.stretch` (2.2 m) of, before the ball gets there, with the time he has to spare; he starts from his position and velocity at the kick, reacts after his read of the pass (the T324 read, `GRID_MOVEMENT_CONFIG.read` × his 위치선정 share of the wind-up, the same read T426 restored) and runs within `GRID_MOVEMENT_CONFIG` acceleration and his top speed with fatigue. It returns the earliest such opponent, or none.
- [ ] `resolvePass` on the candidate model: the lane term of the completion chance is `LANE_CONFIG.risk` × the summed spare-time weights of the opponents `reachOnPath` finds (in place of `laneRisk`'s exp(−d/2)); on failure, the earliest reaching opponent intercepts at his reach point when he gets within `reach` with at least `LANE_CONFIG.sure` (0.25 s) to spare, and deflects it loose at that point when he gets there only at full stretch or with less time; with no reaching opponent the ball is lost at the receiver (the existing loose ball weighted by `receiverLoose`) or out. The tactic build-up shares (`DUEL_CONFIG.candidate.intercept`) now split only failures that have a reaching opponent. The pre-feature path is unchanged (its digests pass untouched).
- [ ] `choosePass` on the candidate model reads the same reach: its `risk` term is the `reachOnPath` weight of the option's path, so a carrier with better 판단력 (the existing temperature) more surely avoids a lane an opponent reaches.
- [ ] The ball's recorded flight ends at the interceptor's reach point and he is there when it arrives (playback and the knots agree, MATCH-12).
- [ ] Tests: `lanes.test.ts` "an opponent 10 m off the line cannot reach a 20 m pass", "a defender standing in the lane reaches it at once", "a late read reaches the path later"; `resolve.test.ts` "nobody cuts out a pass he could not reach" (over the interceptions of three observed candidate fixtures, every interceptor is within `LANE_CONFIG.stretch` of his reach point when the ball arrives) and "a pass whose lane nobody reaches is never intercepted"; `decide.test.ts` "a reached lane is chosen less often"; every new record passes `validateFullMatchRecord`; candidate digests re-recorded where they change, with the measured change in the test comment.
- [ ] The result reports, on one calibration round against the pre-change source (the next unused round in docs/CALIBRATION.md), per team a match: passes and completion, interceptions, tackles + interceptions, the share of failed open-play passes intercepted in the lane, deflected, lost at the receiver and out, PPDA and high turnovers, final-third passes, box entries, shots, xG and goals (reported, not gated: MATCH-28 r23).
