## impl notes
- MATCH-18 r21 keeps the drawn call: T368, T374 and T375 tried calling every offside position with runners holding the line, and each time press's high line held without risk (MATCH-29 inside → outside), so the honest positional rule is no longer the target. Do not restore the held line or T374's deeper line here.
- Today's `isOffside` (`restarts.ts`): chance = `base` 0.03 − (판단력 − 60) / `decisions` 360 − linebreaker 0.1 + highLine 0.05, clamped to [0.012, 0.7]; T368 measured ~23.4 receptions beyond the line and ~1.6 calls a team, so the mean call rate is ~7%. Reaching 1.8 calls needs a mean of ~8% with the lowest quarter ≥3× the highest; with the min clamp at 0.012 the slope must be steeper than today's (360 points per unit) or the clamp lower. Pick the shape (linear in 판단력 or a ratio form) on the screens and write it in the setting's comment.
- More calls cut through balls and box entries a little (each call ends the move); MATCH-30's through-ball, box-entry and xT rows stay outside under the drawn call and are not this task's target — record them, do not chase them.
- The aggregate path draws offsides from failed passes into the final third (`config.ts` `offside: { forward, through }`) and attributes them by `AC.offside` line weights with `ROLES.offside` on `pace`; MATCH-26/27 need its offsides to stay one settled fact per player and team. T372 measures full parity later; here read the aggregate's offsides a team and the 판단력 split only.
