- Use predeclared equivalence margins and power checks. Mean agreement alone is insufficient; rare red cards, high scores, player scoring concentration and metric relationships need their own evidence.
- Do not fit player/club-specific seed exceptions or generate cosmetic independent values to populate the missing fields.
- T281's fallback settles the searched plan with the fewest score-disagreeing red-card answers after the last retry and lists them in `unreconciled`; measure its frequency and bias, and add a cup-rule (120-minute) forced-exhaustion case with tied plans to `aggregate/engine.test.ts`.
- Measurement conditions (decided at T320): both sides of a tactic pairing start from the same pre-match squad fatigue (the condition's neutral value), and a forced tactic changes only in-match behaviour and its in-match cost, so the freshness ordering measures the tactic's own cost; T320 left grid-bands' forced tactics moving only the press load's skill effect while press-style clubs carry fatigue 32 whatever they play. Read T320's `## result` for the names to call (`tacticPairingVerdicts`, `describeTacticPairings`, `MatchSample.freshness`, `inMatchFatigue`, `sideFreshness`).
- The aggregate adapter has no tactic override, so `scripts/match-parity.ts --tactics` (T320) measures core pairs on each club's own tactic with uneven venues; add a forced-tactic option to the aggregate measurement (same pairings and venues as grid-bands' tactics family) before reading MATCH-29. If the participation planner's fatigue counting changes, call `inMatchFatigue` there and keep `aggregateFreshness` in step.
