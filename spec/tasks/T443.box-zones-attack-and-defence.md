## goal
On the candidate model, when the ball is wide in the final third or in a cut-back zone, the attackers take the near post, the far post, the penalty spot and the edge of the box with runs timed to the delivery, and the defending side drops goal-side to protect the same zones (MATCH-33 and MATCH-34 r28, open play).

## acceptance
- [ ] New `grid/box.ts` exports `BOX_CONFIG` and `boxScene(s, side)`: the scene is on while the ball (the side's attack frame) is in a wide lane from `BOX_CONFIG.wideFrom` (70 m) up or in a cut-back zone (MATCH-14), and names the zones by the ball's side: `nearPost` (3.5 m out, 3 m inside the near post), `farPost` (3 m out, 1 m beyond the far post), `spot` (the penalty spot, 11 m out) and `edge` (19 m out, central), each a point in the attack frame.
- [ ] Attack: `boxScene` assigns at most one attacker per zone, nearest suitable first with ties by player ID: forwards to the near and the far post, a midfielder whose role makes box runs (the existing box-run roles plus 섀도 스트라이커 and 공격형 미드필더) or the far-side winger to the spot or the free post, another midfielder to the edge; the rest defence (`COORDINATION_CONFIG.rest`) never goes. Each takes a committed box run toward a load point `BOX_CONFIG.load` (4 m) short of his zone and attacks the zone itself once the carrier's decided action is a cross or cut-back (read at the decision, as the defenders' read in T426 is), so he arrives as the ball does; on the candidate model these runs replace the `box` run's `[boxX, 30 + 8u]` target while the scene is on.
- [ ] Defence: while the opponent's scene is on, the defending back line drops between `BOX_CONFIG.defend.line` (5.5–11 m from its goal) and narrows: the ball-side centre-back guards the near post, the other centre-back the middle, the far full-back or wing-back tucks in to the far post, and one holding or central midfielder drops to the edge or the spot (whichever the attack has filled). Markers of runners keep goal-side, between the runner and his zone. The shape releases when the ball leaves the scene's area or is cleared, the line stepping up together; set pieces keep §4.7's defence.
- [ ] Tests: `box.test.ts` "the scene names near and far post by the ball's side", "the scene is off with the ball central outside the box"; `intentions.test.ts` "with the ball wide in the final third the forwards attack the near and far post and a midfielder the penalty spot", "runners wait at their load points and attack the zones when a cross is decided", "the rest defence stays home"; `coordination.test.ts` "the defending line drops goal-side with a centre-back at the near post and the far full-back at the far post", "a holding midfielder drops to the edge", "markers stay goal-side of their runners"; every new record passes `validateFullMatchRecord`; candidate digests re-recorded where they change, with the measured change in the test comment; the pre-feature path is unchanged (its digests pass untouched).

## impl notes
- `grid/box.ts` is the shared module for T443–T445 (`BOX_CONFIG.delivery` comes in T444, `BOX_CONFIG.shot` in T445); zones are points, so T444 can aim deliveries at them and T445 can drop second balls near them.
- The jobs are intentions given out by `planCoordination`/`applyCoordination` (one snapshot, cost ranking, ties by player ID, named streams). Attackers keep the box run's `committed` priority; defenders take `danger` priority only where §4.5's danger rule already gives it, otherwise tactical.
- Movement only: no decision table, duel or xG changes here. The delivery still draws its target the existing way until T444.
- If bands.test's coarse guards fail, widen them only to the measured level with this task's evidence in the comment; T437 restores them before T286. Do not request a decision for that.
- The aggregate path is untouched; T410 re-reads parity on the released model.
