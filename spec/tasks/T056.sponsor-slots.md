## goal
Commercial income comes from contracts in five slots that open by era, each valued at signing and fixed for 2–5 seasons.

## acceptance
- [v] `sponsors.ts`: offers per open slot (→T050 `sponsorSlotOpens`), valued at signing from division, reputation, last finish, following (diminishing returns) and the football index, × the slot's share (10/25/45/15/10%); kinds guaranteed, performance-linked, exclusive and inflation-linked; terms of 2–5 seasons; 경기장 명명권 only at facilities 10+.
- [v] Payments per contract follow league payments (→ECON-14); bonuses per slot (kit: promotion or title; main: league wins and draws); a relegation lowers every running contract's remaining seasons by 30%.
- [v] Delegated business signs each empty open slot's best guaranteed offer at once and reports it; otherwise a slot empty at a season's start, or a contract in its final season on 1 April, posts a `sponsor-ending` or sponsor reminder (→ECON-21).
- [v] 후원 tab lists every open slot with its contract (kind, annual, bonus, end) and offers; closed slots say when they open (→WEB-39). Amounts use `<Money>` (→T053).
- [v] Ledger lines `sponsor:<slot>` are named in financialBreakdown.
- [v] sponsors.test.ts:
  - ENG in 1975 offers no shirt-front slot and in 1979 does;
  - an amount stays fixed through its term, and renewal revalues it;
  - relegation cuts the remaining seasons by 30%;
  - a migrated 1.15 sponsor (→T051) pays on to its end.
- [v] `pnpm run typecheck && pnpm run test:unit` pass.

## result
- outcome: Commercial income now comes from contracts in five slots (`w.sponsors`, one per slot), implemented in the new packages/engine/src/sponsors.ts. `sponsorSlots(w)` (the view's `sponsors`) lists every slot with its opening year and catalog status, its contract, or its offers while it is open and empty. A slot is open from its catalog year in the club's country (stadium also needs facilities 10+). `sponsorOffers(w, slot)` values one offer per kind from `commercialValue`: the division value × reputation (×0.75–1.25) × last finish (×0.9–1.1) × following ((fans/20000)^0.2, no ceiling) × build and synergy sponsor effects, on the football index, × the slot's share. A slot without a bonus offers no performance-linked kind. Terms are stable 3, performance 2, exclusive 4 and indexed 5 seasons. `sign-sponsor {slot, offer}` signs an open, empty slot; amounts stay fixed (indexed follows prices) until the contract ends at its term, and a new contract is valued anew. Each league game pays every contract's installment (no retroactive games) into one accrued `sponsor:<slot>` ledger entry per season. The main sponsor's results bonus (win 1/games, draw 1/3 of that) books as `sponsor:shirt` 메인 스폰서 경기 보너스. At the season boundary a kit contract that ran through the season pays its bonus for a promotion or title (`sponsor:kit`). A relegation keeps round(remaining × 0.7) seasons of every running contract (`sponsor-cut` event), and ended contracts record `sponsor-end`. With business delegated, the staff sign every empty open slot's stable offer the same day, with one finance inbox report. Otherwise the season start posts one 후원 계약이 비어 있어요 reminder naming the empty slots, and 1 April posts a `sponsor-ending` item for each contract in its final season. financialBreakdown names 지역 후원, 용품 후원, 메인 스폰서, 경기장 명명권 and 소매 스폰서. The cash projection carries every contract and the main sponsor's results bonus. An exclusive contract in any slot blocks merchandise campaigns. The 후원 tab shows each slot (share, bonus rule) with either its contract card (kind, current annual, next installment, bonus, end, seasons left) or the offer cards with a sign button per offer; a closed slot says the season it opens (marked 추정 when estimated) or the facility level it needs. All amounts use `<Money>`. The engine no longer writes `w.sponsor`, and refuses the old `sponsor` command.
