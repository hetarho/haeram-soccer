## impl notes
- areas move from `{ slot, dials }` to `{ key, label, icon, question, card?: BuildSlot, rows: RowKey[] }` with `RowKey = DialKey | DirectionKey`; a row helper `rowInfo(w, key)` returns label, question, option names, the index in force and a detail builder, so StrategyBoard maps rows without per-kind branches beyond the detail.
- BoardDraft gains `direction?: Partial<Direction>`; `draftChanges` adds `{ kind: 'direction', key, from, to }` after the dials; `draftCommands` sends them last; `withDirection` mirrors `withPolicy`.
- the people panels' row lines reuse the WEB-71 "line naming its value that opens its home" pattern already used for 영입 기조 (useBoardRequest can carry an area to open without a draft: add `area?: AreaKey` to BoardRequest).
- delete apps/web/src/ui/criteria.ts and the editors in ClubStaff.tsx and the roster's talk-rule section; keep the "what the staff would do today" previews, now from the derived criteria.
- option names on screen are the SSOT's Korean names; 선호 학파's any reads 상관없음 and the schools use STYLE_INFO labels.
