# IDEATION club-direction
> st:converted@261014 | One place where the owner sets the club's direction, the delegated staff decide by it, and every choice of one kind looks and behaves the same.

## vision
- Core value (owner): simple controls that branch into trillions of different clubs — few, uniform choices whose combinations decide the outcome.
- Problem (owner, 261014): the controls grew one feature at a time, so which control moves which part of the game is scattered, and 운영's two former halves were merged only as tab labels — buttons, card shapes and behaviour still differ (유소년: the 유스 정책 card row and the 유소년 투자 dial).
- Today a direction is expressed by three different things in three tabs, unrelated to each other:
  - strategy cards, 6 slots × 5 (운영 › 운영 전략, →ECON-22) — change simulation numbers and light synergies
  - dials, 5 × 5 steps (same board, →ECON-16, →ECON-17) — change spending and simulation numbers
  - staff criteria (사람 › 선수 talk rule →CLUB-26, 이적시장 sale bar →CLUB-18, 감독 manager criteria →STAFF-18 (11 fields), 코치진 hiring criteria ×7 →STAFF-15; 훈련 › 유소년 the youth director's fixed rule →STAFF-13) — steer delegated decisions only
  - so choosing 성골 유스 does not change how the youth director promotes, and the talk rule ignores the 라커룸 문화 card except through goodwill.
- Target: owners who set a course and watch the club live it (level 4 idle play) as much as owners who take every decision.
- Process (owner): finish the whole design before building, then change in small slices instead of one large rewrite.
