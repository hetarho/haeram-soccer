# IDEATION match-engine-rebuild
> st:ready@261026 | Rebuild the match engine so every player's choice comes from his role, his place on the pitch and the situation, drawn by probability.

## vision
- Problem: observed matches do not read as football — the ball sits with a defender and a moment later is a shot; shots leave from odd, distant spots; a forward suddenly plays the ball back to a deep full-back or wing-back. The owner wants a full engine rebuild, not patches.
- Target: the owner watching their club's matches, and every number those matches feed (player statistics, xG, records, scouting impressions).
- Core value: each touch is a decision a player in that role, at that place, under that pressure would plausibly make; the result follows from those decisions, so what is seen and what is counted are the same football.

## explored
- [o] Owner's direction: a probabilistic decision engine — per role, and per situation and pitch position, each player has weighted choices (pass, carry, dribble, cross, shoot, hold …) resolved by duels.
- [o] Current-engine diagnosis (packages/engine/src/match.ts duel chain, motion.ts playback; checkout da97fe0) ← shared ground before choosing a direction
  - The result engine knows no positions: three abstract zones (build-up, midfield, final third) and per-line slot pools; the pitch picture is a separate layer (MATCH-12) that places players by formation anchors and flies the ball to whichever slot was recorded.
  - Each minute first draws which side has the ball (team possession chance) and whether the minute holds a chance (team chance rate); neither depends on where the ball is.
  - A chance minute plays 1–3 quick passes (1–5 under possession) from wherever the ball is, then the shooter is drawn from the whole outfield by line weight (FW 3, MF 1.1, DF 0.2 × role/trait), and the event is labelled final third.
  - Pass direction is a draw (forward by tactic share, back 16%, otherwise square) and the receiver a draw from that zone's pool; the final-third pool includes the flank defenders who attack (overlapping full-backs, wing-backs).
  - The pitch plays only the last 5 duels of a minute inside 8 motion seconds; possession redrawn the next minute is shown as an invented restart.
  - Measured 261019 on 120–200 observed own matches (seeds measure/measure2, 4-3-3 and 3-5-2): 46–51% of minutes with our shot start with the ball in our own half, with 2–4 duels before the shot; about a third of shots leave from more than 30 pitch units (~32 m) from the goal line and 9% from the own half, at the shooter's drawn position; 30–39% of forwards' completed passes go to full-backs or wing-backs; 49% of minutes open with a restart; 25% of duels are never shown.
- [o] Engine resolution: a pitch grid — the ball and all 22 players occupy real cells, and the carrier picks his action from a probability table by role × cell × pressure × tactic, then a duel resolves it; the pitch view draws the engine's own positions ← removes the root cause (no positions) while staying calibratable and fast enough
- [x] Continuous agent simulation (every player moves every moment on real geometry, like Football Manager) ← the most natural picture, but the hardest to build and calibrate and far too slow for the other fixtures
- [x] Keep the three-zone chain and repair only the rules behind the symptoms ← cheap, but the engine would still not know where anyone is, so picture and result would keep drifting apart
- [o] Time advances per touch (a few seconds per action); the ball changes sides only through an event — tackle, interception, ball out of play, shot, foul — never by a per-minute possession draw ← lets the view show every action and ends invented restarts
- [x] Keep one-minute possession blocks with position-aware choices inside ← less change to records and view, but the ball would still switch sides without a duel at minute boundaries
- [o] Must hold: the current win/draw/loss and goals calibration (MATCH-2, e.g. a +20 favourite 79/14/7), the processing speed of the ~10,000 other fixtures a world season (the unwatched path may stay a summary on the same scoring curve), and every recorded statistic (MATCH-3/13: xG, key passes, big chances, PPDA, field tilt …).
- [o] The rebuild starts new careers as the previous feature activation did (SAVE-18): earlier saves are not converted ← one engine to maintain; the new attributes need newly generated players anyway
- [x] Earlier careers keep playing on the old engine ← keeps their data, but two engines would raise the cost and testing of every later feature
- [o] Decision tables are anchored to real football statistics — shot, pass and dribble shares by location, scoring probability by cell (e.g. expected-threat grids) — with roles, traits and tactics bending them ← makes "shots from odd places" measurable against real data instead of feel
- [x] Hand-tuned tables first, adjusted by watching ← fast start, but realism would rest on feel
- [o] Name the grid with real football spatial concepts — pockets between the lines, half-spaces, Zone 14, channels, cut-back zone and the like — so the engine produces richer material (commentary, statistics, heat maps, tactical explanations) ← owner's request 261019
- [o] Vocabulary and data research 261019 is the material for cell names, tags, role habits and calibration targets (sources: IFAB Law 1, FIFA EFI 2023, Spielverlagerung, Coaches' Voice, Hudl StatsBomb, Opta definitions, Karun Singh xT, Korean media Footballist/Best Eleven/Xportsnews):
  - Fixed zones follow pitch markings. Five lanes (FIFA: left/right channel, left/right inside channel, central channel; KO 측면 · 하프스페이스 · 중앙) bounded by the box and six-yard-box lines, y 13.84/24.84/43.16/54.16 of 68 m; thirds at 35/70 m of 105 m. Named cells: Zone 14 (KO 아크 정면, centre just outside the box), the box, six-yard box, penalty-spot area, the D, cut-back zones (inside the box either side of the six-yard box, near the byline), near/far post, the half-space at the box edge, the wide final-third corner. "Zone 5" and "golden zone" are not standard (single or no source) — avoid as names.
  - Relational spaces move with the opponents and are tags computed each touch, not cells: between the lines (KO 라인 사이) and its pockets, in behind (KO 뒷공간), the CB–FB channel, rest defence (KO 레스트 디펜스), the second-ball zone (KO 세컨드 볼).
  - Situation tags: phase (build-up, progression, final third, counter-attack, counter-press, settled attack — KO 지공 · 역습 · 게겐프레싱; FIFA also has an "in contest" loose-ball state); under pressure = an opponent within about 4.6 m (StatsBomb); body orientation (half-turn, back to goal — a pressing trigger); overload, isolation, overload-to-isolate, third-man run, switch of play; line break through/around/over a unit (FIFA), packing (opponents bypassed); block height high/mid/low.
  - Every game role (PLAYER-5) has a documented spatial habit usable as its decision bias — e.g. inverted full-back steps into midfield in build-up, mezzala drifts to the half-space and runs the CB–FB channel, false nine drops into the pocket/Zone 14, traditional winger hugs the touchline and crosses, inverted winger cuts in to shoot, poacher lives on the last defender's shoulder and in the six-yard box. FM-only role definitions are game convention, not analytic consensus.
  - Grid size: Singh's xT uses 16×12 cells (not 12×8); analysts trade resolution against data noise (about 13×10 is the largest a few seasons of data support). Candidate: a landmark-aligned grid of about 5–7 lanes × 8–10 depth bands (40–70 cells) so every cell maps to a named zone.
  - Real event-data baselines 261019, collected in docs/MATCH-BASELINES.md (FBref/Opta EPL and Big-5 2023-24; StatsBomb open data EPL 2015/16 and Wyscout public dataset 2017/18 computed per cell on a 12×8 grid): about 93 possessions a team, median 3 passes, 12.5% ending in a shot; 1.4–2.4 s per action; passes 35/50/15% forward/lateral/back, by position (a striker 22/53/26, a centre-back 44/50/6); shot probability per decision under 0.1% outside the attacking quarter, rising to 25–83% centrally near goal; shots 7–8% six-yard box, ~50% rest of box, 32–41% outside, mean distance ~15–17 m; crosses 17 a team at ~25%; set pieces 20–28% of goals plus penalties ~8%; restarts a match: throw-ins 35–46, corners ~10, goal kicks ~16, fouls ~22.
  - Current engine against them (measured 261019): shooter median ~25 m and a third beyond ~32 m (real 30 m+ is 4–10%); forwards' completed passes 30–39% to full-backs or wing-backs; restarts ~27 throw-ins, ~7 goal kicks plus wide shots, ~12 corners; one possession block a minute instead of touch-length possessions.
  - Korean commentary terms verified in Korean media: 하프스페이스, 라인 사이, 뒷공간, 아크 정면, 컷백, 니어/파 포스트, 레스트 디펜스, 세컨드 볼, 수적 우위, 아이솔레이션, 등지고 받다, 라인 브레이킹, 지공/역습, 게겐프레싱; unverified: 포켓, 하프 턴, 서드맨, 커버 섀도, 존 14.

## shape
- Format migration 261026: previous converted status restored to ready pending a coverage audit; historical questions and decisions are preserved. Parked during the module-boundary refactor; this restoration authorizes no product changes.
- flow (draft): kickoff → the carrier stands in a named cell with his tags (phase, pressure, body orientation, between the lines, overload …) → his decision table — the real-data baseline for that cell and his position, bent by his role, traits, the tactic and the situation — picks an action and a target → a duel on the players' attributes resolves it → the ball reaches a teammate's cell, is lost, goes out or is fouled → a set piece or restart from the real spot → … until full time. Off the ball all 22 move cell to cell by their formation's shape for the phase (in possession, out of possession, transition), their role's habit (e.g. inverted full-back into midfield) and the tactic's block height, shifted toward the ball.
- v1 in: grid engine with per-touch time and event-only possession changes; fouls, free kicks, corners and penalties; the four tactics deepened; spatial commentary, post-match shot map/heat maps/pass network, new statistics with season and career totals, post-match tactical report; calibration against the current MATCH-2 curve and the real baselines; the unwatched path kept fast on the same scoring curve.
- v1 also in: cards and suspensions under one English-style rule, era rules for substitutes and cards, in-match substitutions by the manager and in-match injuries; decisions and composure attributes in 실효 OVR for newly generated players; current-season maps and season heat maps; own-league fixtures on the grid engine if the measured cost allows, else on the probabilistic path; cards, suspensions and injuries drawn in unwatched fixtures too.
- v1 not: continuous agent physics; new tactical instructions; converting earlier careers.
