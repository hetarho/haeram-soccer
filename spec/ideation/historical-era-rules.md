# IDEATION historical-era-rules
> st:open@261118 | Bring back period football's own rules as flavour once the modern-feeling world is settled

## vision
- Problem: the world now behaves like today's football in every era (free transfers at expiry, one distress ladder, one market rate band), while the game spans 1930–2010s.
- Target: owners who enjoy football history, without making early decades feel alien to today's fans.
- Core value: era flavour that changes how a decade plays, never a set of rules that makes the game unrecognisable.

## explored
- [o] Modern behaviour in every era for now: free transfer at contract expiry (CLUB-28), one financial distress ladder (ECON-24), one NPC books band (ECON-36) ← today's fans play the game; strict history felt alien
- [x] Strict 1950s band for starters lost by fee (0.8–1.5 a top-flight club a season) ← the modern-like 1.7–1.9 measured on 0.8.11 is fine for players
- [?] England's retain-and-transfer system before 1963 (a club could hold an out-of-contract player)
- [?] Compensation fees for out-of-contract moves before the 1995 Bosman ruling (the removed 50%-of-value rule)
- [?] Era scope of financial sanctions: administration points from 2004 in England, UEFA FFP from 2011/12, PSR from 2015
- [?] The maximum wage's effect on moves before 1961 beyond CLUB-48's star pay spread
- [?] Era-specific market and level bands (WORLD-16 upward moves, NPC_MARKET era table) once the modern bands hold

## shape
- flow: the modern-feeling world lands → each era rule is tried as a flavour switch on measured worlds → adopted only where it keeps play recognisable
- v1: none yet / not: anything that blocks a career or makes an era's market unplayable
