# IDEATION club-history-reboot
> st:converted@261007 | Observe matches and statistics, experiment with football and business decisions, and build a fictional club's long history.

## vision
- [o] Core value: observe statistics, try different football and business strategies, and accumulate meaningful club history.
- [o] Preserve simple, observable match simulation with moving players and ball, match events, and detailed match/player statistics.
- [o] Preserve player growth, casual progression, fast-forward, season records, and historical standings.
- [o] Marketing income, sponsor contracts, manager hiring, and player recruitment are core gameplay areas.
- [o] Manager personality determines the response to owner tactical demands, including acceptance, reluctant compliance, conflict, anger, and possible resignation.
- [o] Difficulty is based on the founding club's initial capital.
- [o] Country/year price changes and currency units evolve from past to present using actual monetary and price history as reference.
- [o] The first release focuses on a limited set of European countries participating in European club competitions.
- [o] Champions League, Europa League, Conference League, and their necessary predecessors appear and evolve at historical dates; actual participants/results are generated by the fictional world.
- [o] Complete an interest-building web demo first; plan Flutter commercial release afterward.
- [o] Web progression saves in localStorage; later Supabase functionality is limited initially to an optional hall of fame/ranking service.
- [o] This stage produces the architecture and delivery plan only; application implementation follows the spec/task workflow.
- [?] Audience proposal: football fans who enjoy club stories and long careers with little repetitive management.

## shape
- [o] Concept flow: new world → found a club → enter the lowest supported professional division → observe matches/statistics → try football/business decisions → observe outcomes → accumulate club growth and history.
- [?] Proposed onboarding: select association → enter club name/location/colors → choose initial-capital difficulty → display world-generation progress → validate competition membership → show founding record → begin the first season.
- [?] Proposed season loop: inspect metrics → hire/recruit/invest or request a tactical change → manager response and agreement/conflict → observe actual matches/business results → compare → adjust → archive → resolve promotion/relegation and personnel renewal → continue.
- [?] Proposed v1: selected European domestic pyramids/cups and founding, representative external European clubs, historically introduced European club competitions, initial-capital difficulty, observable matches, metric comparison, marketing, sponsors, manager personality/requests/conflict/resignation, player transactions, finance with historical prices/currencies, fast-forward/delegation, squad renewal, season recaps, historical browsing, save/load.
- [?] Proposed v1 exclusions: full domestic systems/founding for all UEFA associations, special cross-border/non-professional founding structures, real-club databases, reenactment of actual football results, online competition, complex extra contract clauses, mandatory live match viewing, national-team and non-European continental competitions.
- [o] Web delivery boundaries, storage recovery, stack, verification, deployment, and Flutter migration are owned by →ARCH.
- [?] Rule-reference policy: freeze a modern domestic baseline and a historical European-competition timeline per world; do not silently rewrite saves when external sources change.
- [?] History policy: preserve seasons, players, manager tenures/conflicts/departures, transactions, contracts, campaign outcomes, and decision-linked metrics; detailed replay retention is a separate decision.
- [?] Observation policy: watching speed/view changes must not reroll completed outcomes or duplicate statistics.
- [?] Economic policy: respect payment timing, recurring costs, sponsor exclusivity, and non-duplicated income.
- [?] Historical-economy policy: link price/currency changes to game dates, maintain purchasing-power comparisons, and keep original amounts intact.
- [?] European-history policy: connect European Cup→Champions League and UEFA Cup→Europa League lineage; retain discontinued competitions and original season names.
