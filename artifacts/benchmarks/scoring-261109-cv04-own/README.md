## 3. What it shows

- **The box's poor shots are MATCH-16's clear chances, which r56's in-box weighing does not reach.** Of `Perf`'s 2.58 carried box shots a team with nobody between, 2.37 (92%) are clear chances where he decided (`B` 90%, `ctl` 90%): MATCH-16 makes him take them, `actionMix` raising the shot to its clear-chance floor whatever the in-box term reads, and r56 weighs the alternatives only outside a clear chance. They are struck against a keeper already out to him — his narrowing leaves 6.7% of the chance as struck — at xG 0.008, while the decision read 0.145; read as it would be recorded, the chance where he decided is 0.013, and a free teammate in the box or at its edge would have had a better one 61% of the time (best 0.092). He carries only 0.7 m after deciding, so the gap is the keeper's position where he decided, not the stride.
- **So `a` and `b` move the shot mix around the box's chance, not the chance.** They take shots 13.3 → 12.2–13.2 a team, goal-area shots 16.0 → 14.2–15.5% and possessions ending in a shot 16.8 → 15.6–16.6%, rows 28 → 28–31/37 (`Pabp` 31), with goals 2.43–2.55 and entries 26.1–26.7; the box's open-play xG a shot stays 0.056–0.061 (`Perf` 0.057) and the carried shots with nobody between 2.37–2.54 a team at xG 0.006–0.009. The box's conversion on 300 matches (6.3–7.3%) sits in the ±0.4-point noise cv04-ent measured.
- **The chased carrier's choice (`c9`) takes a quarter of them and lifts the chance only a little.** On `Perf` it takes the carried shots with nobody between 2.58 → 1.96 a team, the box's xG a shot 0.057 → 0.063 and with `a`+`b` 0.065 (600 matches each; conversion 6.9 / 7.6%), but the rounds and squares end as goal-area chances (16.0 → 17.5–18.7% of shots), goals rise to 2.69–2.83, and the 1.74–1.82 clear chances left are struck at xG 0.007–0.008 — mostly free carriers with the keeper out, which MATCH-16 r53's clause (a chased carrier) does not cover and cv04-sho's `c9f` (r53 for a free carrier too) already showed breaks MATCH-29.
- **MATCH-29 breaks with every lever that cuts shots, as since cv04-sit.** Every candidate sits beyond `B`'s interval (−0.187 to −0.271 against its lower bound −0.170), `a` alone too (−0.190), where `Perf` itself holds by 0.002 (−0.168); the receiver-valuing pass (`p`) widens it (−0.220 / −0.233) and `c9` most (−0.221 / −0.271, with 3.2 goals and 0.08 hat-tricks a press v counter match). Fewer, better-weighed shots lower press's goals against counter's deep block more than counter's behind press's line.
- **No candidate meets every screen bar.** None reaches the box's xG a shot of 0.07 (best `Pabc9` 0.065 over 600 matches), so none earned the full read (held-out, season share, stress case, MATCH-28's core).
