# T989 comparison evidence

Baseline: 4a9a550ad61a3eeec41ce5de0aa93883f69d5403. Candidate engine source: 38868f558f775e70cdf3d94a32beb1f76f59a182. Node 24.22.1, Linux x64, Intel Xeon Silver 4214. The benchmark A/B reports record machine load and their own commands.

`day-differences.json` repeats the benchmark's `perf-261027` 1950 fresh career, cash override, and 60 daily steps. Its canonical world digests exactly match `ab-day.json`; recursive comparison of all world fields identifies only NPC season player metrics and detail. The recipe is `day-comparison-recipe.txt`.

`compare-1950.json` and `compare-1980.json` start from the same baseline world lived from 1930 to the stated year, seed `t989-lived`, with an ENG top-flight takeover and the measurement driver's cash override. Both sides then advance independently, comparing the full in-memory world at each season's last round. `season-comparison-recipe.txt` and `side-recipe.txt` preserve the executed logic; the latter extends `remove-day/tools/side.ts`, bundled against each source tree with esbuild and the checkout's dependencies. The role summaries inherit the source driver's position grouping, 900-minute minimum and three-decimal rounding; they are not exact unrounded rates or a complete role-level analysis. A difference exceeding 0.001 plus 0.1% of the displayed base necessarily exceeds the requested threshold even after accounting for rounding. The saved per-season hashes cover scores, tables, scorer/goals/assists/minutes, discipline, squads and Elo; recursive differences cover the remaining in-memory fields.
